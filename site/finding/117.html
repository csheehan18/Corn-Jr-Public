The whole-passage reader ships: 526 of 721 at runtime

The result, as the finding states it

The whole-passage adapter reader ships, and the runtime reads 526 of 721 at 199 correct silences in 23 ms a passage — three right answers over the replay's 523, every one of them the harvest's bf16 trunk against the runtime's fp32 one

What it cost

0 s of new scoring on record; it reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m_v4_ft4g). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

Run 2026-09-05 on the frozen corn_jr_260m_v4_ft4g trunk. No weight of the trunk moves and none is trained here: the adapter and its head are checkpoints/reader_whole_causal_s1.pt, the file finding 112 trained and finding 116 re-read. corn/extract.py's reduce, grounded, det_repair and _canonical, corn/gate.py, every scorer and every frozen eval row are untouched, and the server's reader is still generate. This is docs/PLAN-READER-PORT.md — wiring, guards, verification — and it contains no new experiment.

The hundred-and-twelfth finding measured a whole-passage reader that existed only inside scripts/train_reader_whole.py: an adapter bound onto a trunk in the training process, a head that never left that file, and a harvest of spans and margins replayed by probe_harvest_paired.read_at_bar. Finding 116 re-read it at 523 of 721 under the repaired grounding rule. What neither could say is whether Corn-Jr can answer through it, because between that harvest and a live turn sit corn/model.py (which had no adapter and no attention argument), corn/reader.py (which had no whole-passage head), corn.extract.read_passage (which always cut), harness/backends/read.py and harness/serverconfig.py. This file closes each of those and then reads the whole thing end to end.

What ships

adapter + head checkpoints/reader_whole_causal_s1.pt one file, both attention causal, read off the file's own args extent rule d = 2.0, radius 3 unchanged reduce one window, so one vote unchanged threshold -1.174 when he speaks at all

corn/model.py gains LoRALinear on c_attn, c_proj, c_fc and c_proj of every block — base held by reference, never written, off unless a forward turns it on — a causal: bool = True argument threaded to CausalSelfAttention.forward as is_causal = T > 1 and causal, and load_adapter, which keys a file to the trunk's sha256 and refuses a mismatch. corn/reader.py gains WholeHead (the trainer's arithmetic, ported) and WholeHeadReader (a run in read_passage's sense), with the reply-rendering half both readers share factored into _Speaks so there is one place a threshold is compared. read_passage gains whole, which is the cut not being made. Reader(reader="head_whole") builds it; harness/serverconfig.py gains reader_adapter and reader_whole_threshold. generate is still the default.

Three things are decisions rather than readings, and are recorded as such.

  • WHOLE_THRESHOLD is -1.174, the mean of the two cross-validated fold values (-1.094 / -1.254) under the bar protocol — right subject to the fold's share of 200 correct silences — exactly as READER_THRESHOLD is the mean of -1.980 / -2.061. It is not the same quantity as READER_THRESHOLD: that margin is a stacked verifier's logit and this one is max s - s_null off the head's own softmax, so the two carry separate config fields, separate validators, and a guard asserting they are not each other.
  • Seed 1 ships, on finding 87's reasoning. Seed 2 reads 517 against seed 1's 523 on Population A and Population A is the population the arm is measured on, so choosing between them by that number would be selecting on the test population.
  • The adapter names its trunk. train_reader_whole.py now writes the trunk checkpoint's sha256 into every file it saves, and scripts/stamp_adapter.py put it into the two checkpoints finding 116 was measured on without retraining them (both read 985240095e80, the ft4g trunk). A low-rank delta on the wrong weights loads without error and reads nonsense, and the trunk will move — the social workstream is fine-tuning it — so this is the expected accident rather than an exotic one.

(a) The runtime, end to end, at the shipped constant

scripts/probe_reader_runtime.py, finding 87's instrument, extended with a --reader argument: the real Reader handed (question, passage) 1,003 times, whatever comes back scored by probe_reader_passages.judge — score_extract_tight on reduce's output, the scorer every arm on this population has been read on, unchanged. Both readers run through it on the same day on the same card.

gate on (recorded) precision 64.8%, acc|att 0.699, attempt rate 45.6% gate CV precision 56.9%, acc|att 0.641, attempt rate 59.5% head (runtime) precision 58.8%, acc|att 0.671, attempt rate 81.0% head_whole precision 72.0%, acc|att 0.812, attempt rate 89.9%

the hybrid fallback fired on 90 passages for head and 0 for head_whole

526 right at 199 correct silences, against the plan's bar of 523 and 198 "to within one passage each". Correct silence is within one. right is three over, and the whole of (b) is why — it is not a wiring difference, and the bar is met under the numerics the bar was set in.

The window head is the control that makes that claim checkable, and it is the reason both arms were run. --reader head through the same instrument on the same day reads 392 right at 199 correct silences, which is finding 116's replay of that arm to the passage — 392 and 199 there, 392 and 199 here, nothing apart. The window head's harvest was written by a probe running the trunk the way the runtime runs it; the whole arm's was written inside a training process. So read_passage, reduce, judge and the threshold comparison reproduce a replay exactly when the replay's trunk is the runtime's, and the three passages are the one thing that differs.

(b) The three passages, traced

Six passages read differently in the runtime from the replay at the same constant threshold. They net to +3 right and +0 correct silence:

class replay -> runtime the replay's span margin t = -1.174 answ wrong -> silent '~ 0.116 mm' -1.1417 crossed t answ wrong -> right 'sometime in 2018' +0.3902 span moved answ wrong -> right 'Archaea' -0.5118 span moved answ wrong -> right 'Florida' +0.0829 span moved unans silent -> wrong 'Cadillac Mountain' -1.1777 crossed t unans wrong -> silent 'Commander in Chief' -1.1402 crossed t

Three are margins within 0.04 of the threshold falling on the other side of it; three are spans changing on passages nowhere near it. Both are what a small perturbation of the head's inputs looks like, and the rest of this section is where the perturbation is.

It is not the head. On identical states, the ported WholeHead scored per row against train_reader_whole.BatchScores' padded batched enumeration — the path the harvest was actually written through — differs by 0.00007 at a score scale of 96.55, moves the margin by 0.00001, and moves no argmax. On canned states scripts/check_extract.py holds the port bit-identical to the trainer's over 520 pairs with the argmax exact.

It is the states. The harvest reads them through train_reader_whole._trunk_states under torch.compile(dynamic=True) and bf16 autocast; the runtime reads them through corn/model.py's own fp32 forward, hooked. On one batch of five passages the two differ by 4.006 in h, which carries through to 1.247 of score and 0.106 of margin.

Population-wide, the live fp32 reader against the harvest, 1,002 passages read on both paths (the 1,003rd is over the 512 block on both and silent on both):

spans string-identical 985 98.30% un-extended argmax identical 986 98.40% worst margin gap 0.19005 median margin gap 0.021235 mean margin gap 0.029195 margins landing on the other side of t 3

Sixteen of the seventeen differing spans are the head's own pick moving, not the extent rule breaking a tie the other way.

And the control that closes it: the same wired path with the trunk under the harvest's bf16 autocast reads 524 right at 198 correct silences — inside the bar on both columns, and inside finding 87's own tolerance of the replay. The port passes its bar under the condition the bar was measured in, and reads three more under the condition the runtime actually serves.

Two things this rules out. TF32 is not it: train_reader_whole.py:839 sets allow_tf32 = True and the runtime does not, and turning it on in the runtime changes nothing at all (526 / 199, median gap 0.021269). Nor is the fp16 store: there is no store here — unlike finding 87's (a), where the comparison was a harvested fp16 cache against a live fp32 trunk and the worst margin gap was 0.0015. This gap is 130 times that, because bf16 autocast rounds at every one of seventeen layers where a store rounds once at the end.

So the runtime is the more precise of the two paths and reads three more right answers than the file it is checked against. That is stated as what it is rather than banked: the honest number for this arm on Population A remains finding 116's 523, measured under the harvest's numerics on a population, and 526 is what the runtime does with the same weights. Nothing about the ordering of the two arms depends on which is quoted.

(c) Paired against the window head, through the runtime

Both arms' verdicts come from the same instrument on the same passages, so the comparison is paired rather than two cards side by side. McNemar:

against head (runtime) net lost won p joint bit +134 62 196 2.2e-17 right answers +134 27 161 2.0e-24 correct silences +0 35 35 1.0

It buys 134 right answers and spends nothing. That is the difference between this arm and the window head that shipped in finding 87, which bought 107 right answers over the gate and spent 27 correct silences: the whole-passage reader's willingness lives in the same softmax as its pick, so raising one did not cost the other. Correct silence is 199 for both arms and 35 passages move each way underneath that, which is the honest shape of a "no change" — the net is zero and neither half is nothing.

The species the windows caused are gone rather than reduced: the hybrid fallback fired on 90 passages for the window head and 0 here, because a single reply cannot disagree with itself. The whole apparatus of votes, aggregation and disagreement fallback is dead code on this path, kept rather than switched off because a dead branch that is the same code is a fact about the input.

(d) Latency, per passage, both arms on the same card

The clock is around Reader.__call__ and nothing else — the load, the scoring and the two gate replays are outside it. 1,003 passages, idle 3080, fp32:

arm mean median p90 max total head 42.7 ms 40.1 ms 67.0 ms 132.6 ms 43 s head_whole 23.2 ms 23.0 ms 25.0 ms 131.0 ms 23 s

1.84x on the mean and 2.7x at p90, and the shape is the point rather than the ratio. The window reader is one forward per window over one to eight windows, so its cost is the passage's length and its p90 is 67 ms; the whole reader is one forward whatever the passage is, so its median and its p90 are 2 ms apart. The two maxima are both about 131 ms and are not a passage: a separate eight-passage run of the whole arm read 130 ms on its first passage and 20-30 ms on every one after, so it is a warm-up both arms pay once. That is why the means separate and the maxima do not.

So the arm that reads 134 more right answers is also the cheaper one, and it is cheaper for the same reason it is better: there is one read of the passage instead of eight reads of pieces of it.

(e) The guards

scripts/check_model.py is new and is check_all.py's sixty-eighth. It proves a negative, and it proves it against a copy of the arithmetic rather than a description of it: _before writes out the pre-port block and attention forward, and a seeded trunk is held torch.equal to it in three configurations — adapter absent, adapter present and off, adapter present and on with b at the zero it is initialised to — with two controls that stop those from being vacuous (a non-zero b must move the forward, and causal=False must move it). It also holds corn.model.LoRALinear equal to train_reader_whole.LoRALinear on a drawn delta, and drives load_adapter against a matching trunk, a different trunk and an unstamped file.

scripts/check_extract.py grows the whole arm's sections:

  • The head is the trainer's, score for score, on canned states with no checkpoint near it: bit-identical over 520 pairs, the argmax exact, the null equal, and the margin max s - s_null as load_reader_harvest computes it. The bar written into the guard is the plan's 1e-4 of scale; what it actually reads is 0.
  • The whole path asks once, about the passage uncut: the head is called once where the window reader is called three times on the same fixture, the window it is asked about is the passage byte for byte, the one reply comes back through the real reduce as "1 of 1 windows extracted it", a passage a hair below the threshold abstains with the runtime's own decline behind it, the hybrid fallback is unreachable, an empty passage is still empty rather than one empty window, and a gate over a whole passage is refused rather than applied to it.
  • The constants are held equal: ServerConfig().reader_whole_threshold against corn/reader.py's, head_whole is a value --reader takes, and the two thresholds are asserted not to be each other.
  • Layering is unmoved: corn/reader.py now imports corn.model as well as corn.gate and corn.toolgen, all three inside the runtime closure. scripts/check_layering.py is green.

scripts/check_read.py gets the one-window read: one prompt where the cut would have made several, and the window named is the passage.

The full sweep reads 68 guards and two fail: check_churn.py and check_copyable.py. Both fail identically at the commit this session started from (56c33733) in a clean worktree with the same messages and counts, so neither is this session's — finding 116 §3 reports the same two for the same reason.

One guard that is not here, and why. Finding 87's (a) is scripts/check_reader_popa.py: every window of Population A through the wired head against the harvest, spans string-identical at a bar of 99%. The same guard for this arm would read 98.30% and be red, and it would be red for the reason (b) sets out — the harvest's trunk is not the runtime's. A guard that sits red is a comment (rule 7), and a guard whose bar is set to pass a known numerical difference is worse than none, so the comparison is reported here as a reading instead. It becomes a guard the day this arm's harvest is regenerated by a probe that reads the trunk the way the runtime does.

Limits

One trunk, one seed, fp32, one population. The threshold is a constant of a checkpoint and a population and not of the model: the next trunk gets its own train_reader_whole.py run and its own bar-protocol read, and the adapter is retrained with it — which is what load_adapter's fingerprint is for. Nothing here is a live session; finding 87's (f) read fourteen real questions through the provider and this arm has not been through that, and the reader only ever sees what retrieval hands it either way. Nothing here measures packs, which is where the extraction gains were expected to transfer and where no reader number exists yet. The scorecard still has no reader in its path at all (finding 87 (d)), so there is no --reader head_whole column on any card and the frozen rows are invariant to this session by construction. And the server default is unchanged: generate until the owner flips it, as it was for head.
