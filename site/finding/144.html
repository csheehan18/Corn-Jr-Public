The parse and depth · 26 September 2026

Showing the request step by step fixes short chains, not long ones

The result, as the finding states it

Arm S's stepwise views land in the null band at both seeds: they make the short chain reliable and cure the view read as social, and they do not train the one decision a long chain needs at every step — opening a nested group inside the view — which the payload shows 78 times

What it cost

7.1 min of scoring on the 1 card first cited here (nano20m_v3_fw_steps). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

PLAN-PARSE-WEIGHTS arm S, read 2026-09-26. Two nano cells (nano20m_v3_fw_steps, seeds 1337 and 2337) against arm F at the same seeds. Pre-registration: configs/mix_v3_funcworld_steps.py, committed at ee66a09 with the one-shot side gate added at c12275a, both before either seed trained. Arm F's file byte-identical, 7,596 stepwise views (a request from a nested group's carrier on, and the gold line for exactly what is shown) added on top at G2's weight. Every pre-registered reading is null or held; the lines say where the views worked and why they could not reach the gate.

The pre-registered read

scripts/probe_stepwise_depth.py --per 12 --brief --show, honest (no answer key in the loop), window 50. Arm F re-run through the same command in the same sitting reproduces its pre-registered band to the level — 77/420 and 128/420 — so the instrument is deterministic and the comparison is like-for-like. Levels right in order, depths 6-15:

depth carriers F s1 S s1 F s2 S s2 6 5 24/60 27/60 33/60 30/60 8 7 17/84 29/84 26/84 34/84 10 9 12/108 30/108 25/108 29/108 15 14 24/168 37/168 44/168 31/168 pooled 77 0.18 123 0.29 128 0.30 124 0.30 full chains 2/48 4/48 6/48 6/48

Gate (>= 0.42 at both seeds): not met. Both seeds sit inside F's band, 0.18-0.30 — the pre-registered null: the step does not train from views at this size; the runtime carries depth alone.

The rest, in the order the pre-registration lists them:

kill tool.copy 120/120 120/120 (F 102, 120) not fired side stepwise arity 30 29/30 27/30 (F 29, 30) held, seed 2 on the line one-shot ladder nest2 struct 16 12 (F 15, 18) <= 18 at both: null

Committed: evals/results/nano20m_v3_fw_steps{,_seed2}{,__ladder}.json, the item files, and the four __stepdepth.log files (S and F) with every step's line, __steparity.log beside them.

What the lines say

Every step line in the four logs, 305 / 256 on F and 162 / 160 on S, counted by species (a scratchpad count over the logged lines, which the probe truncates at 170 characters — a bound):

species F s1 S s1 F s2 S s2 step read as social[...] at its start 87 3 2 0 a carrier written inline, not opened 82 57 20 53 ... as the view's first nested carrier 34 55 13 42

The views cured one miss. A view opening on a carrier ("the pair, ...") was read as social on 87 of arm F seed 1's steps; every view in the payload opens on exactly such a carrier, and S seed 1 does it 3 times. F seed 2 never had the problem. That is consistent with seed 1's whole rise, 0.18 to 0.29, and with seed 2's flat 0.30 — not tested item by item.

They did not cure the other, and it is one decision. On S, 55 of 57 and 42 of 53 inline misses are the first nested carrier after the one the view opened on — he opens the view's group, writes its first member, and writes the next carrier's words flat:

shown the pair, the capital of Selunnddia and two things, the capital of Kelenndburg and ... S s1 ask[the pair, ask[the capital of Selunnddia] and two things, ask[the capital of Kelenndburg]] ...

On deep chains (6-15) the share of steps carrying that miss rose at both seeds: F 70/234 and 11/187, S 46/107 and 43/107.

The payload shows that decision rarely. Read off the golds with the probe's own tree reader: of 7,596 views, 1,026 (13.5%) contain a nested group inside the view at all, and 78 carry the probe's shape — a nested group as the last member, opening on a bare carrier. Arm F's file has 122. So the arm took the one decision a long chain asks at every middle step from 122 instances to 200, in a 139M-token run, beside 6,570 views that show an opened carrier followed by flat members. The three "inline" hits the counter finds in the payload are false positives (a filler before an opened carrier). Nothing in the bytes teaches the flat write; the bytes simply do not teach the open.

What moved that the gate does not pool

Depths 3-5 — chains the corpus's own depth covers, whose last view is short — were read by the same command and are not in the gate:

depths 3-5 F s1 S s1 F s2 S s2 levels 37/108 68/108 57/108 65/108 full chains 8/36 19/36 11/36 18/36

Up at both seeds. The step does train where one view holds the whole rest of the chain; past that, every middle view needs a nested group opened inside it, and that is where both seeds stop. Not pre-registered and read after the gate; a direction.

One-shot, the depth probe's chain column (carrier levels reproduced in an unbroken run from the top) is higher on S at all eight depth-by-seed cells from 6 to 15 (e.g. depth 8, 0.00 and 0.33 to 0.67 and 0.67) — small counts over twelve items a cell, recorded not read. The ladder's one-shot nest2 side gate is null, and outer4 fell (23 and 16 against 31 and 20, the number word kept 8 and 29 of ~52): the views do not lift one-shot structure.

Off-target card rows moved a lot (search.route_base 91 to 189 and 176, tool.selection 68 to 124 and 139); arm G2 moved the same row 84 to 159 between its own two seeds, so at nano these rows are not read from one arm.

What it does not say

It does not say the nested open is untrainable. It says 78 instances of it did not train it. A payload whose views are cut so a nested group falls inside them would ask that question; this pre-registration licenses no third seed and no second arm, and the owner decides whether one is worth it. Nano, one synthetic world, greedy decodes, a hand-built instrument with no pin of its own (rule 28): every stepwise number here is a bound.

Licenses what the table says: the runtime carries depth alone, as finding 143 already licensed. The views are not recommended for the 1.0 corpus on this evidence. What stays the weights' decision under the windowed runtime — whether a carrier inside the view opens a group — is now named and counted, and is the thing any further arm on it would have to target.
