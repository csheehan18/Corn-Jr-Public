Understanding at small scale · 20 October 2026

Trained on invented names, he learned to copy one, never which

The result, as the finding states it

At 20M, a fine-tune on 60,000 described-menu lines (an invented name beside each property's description, the request phrased by meaning) teaches him to answer with one of the menu's names — 197-200 of 200 fresh training-format lines — and not which one: the right name on 0.21-0.30 of them against a chance of 0.25, and every held read at chance on all three starts (ruler 0.23-0.25, held kinds 0.17-0.26, held phrases 0.20-0.28); the pick does not move with the request; the pre-registered row "the link does not form at this dose", with finding 186's plain read 0.02-0.05 lower

What it cost

the build 18:48-19:05 on 2026-10-20 (ac14986); the chain — three starts read, three fine-tunes of 142, 124 and 124 s, three reads — 640.0 s; the four-column re-read of six checkpoints 214.2 s and the in-distribution read 21.9 s; all contended (the card at 38-41%, a bound).

The question (owner, 2026-10-20: "yes let's do it", on finding 186's proposal). Finding 186: he does not read a described menu, though the descriptions alone pick the field at twice chance. Can a slice built on GENERATORS' rules teach the link — the name beside the description the request means — and does it carry to kinds and words he never trained on? Pre-registered at ac14986 (configs/mix_v3_described_ft.py).

The arm. corn/describegen.py: 20 trained kinds, 80 properties, two descriptions and four trained phrases each; an invented name per property drawn fresh for every line (56,821 effective answer values); no request sharing a content stem with any description in its menu; the answer in each slot 25%. scripts/check_described.py (guard 100) holds it from the bytes. Three fine-tunes from finding 186's starts, mix_v3 as replay, the payload 0.93 passes, 650 iterations.

(Held reads: the registered calibrated score. In-distribution: scripts/probe_described_indist.py, 200 fresh lines in the training format, greedy-decoded.)

Every gate fails on every start, the installs gate included: the pre-registered row is "installs below its gate — the link does not form at this dose". The prediction (installs and carries 3 of 3, transfers 2-3 of 3) was wrong on all three.

He learned the format and not the choice. On the training format he writes a menu name 98-100% of the time, from every slot (slots 0-3 written 42/51/50/54, 72/36/37/54, 63/47/36/54), and the right one at chance. On the probe's held frames 54-69% of decodes write a menu name, against 0 of 903 on every start. Nothing points at which: the request does not move the pick.

An instrument suspicion, cleared

One decode on the first fine-tune wrote the gold name ("a tent that sheds a heavy shower" -> kraglek) where the calibrated read scored a miss, and the calibration — the request's score minus the score with it taken out — was suspected of subtracting a real signal. The probe gained three columns to test it (raw, the pick with the request; prior, the pick without it; writes, the decoded word). On the fine-tunes raw reads the same count as prior on six of nine rows and within three on the rest (ruler 32/32, 23/23, 22/22), both at chance: the request does not move his rate of picking right, so the calibration was not hiding a reading of it, and that decode was the menu-only favourite landing on the gold. Finding 184's lesson again: one decode is an anecdote; read all of them before blaming the instrument. The in-distribution read, which needs no calibration, says the same thing.

The cost gate (names within 0.04) fails on _compiled (-0.05), and desc-only falls on two starts: a payload that teaches nothing still costs the reads it sits nearest. tool.copy_wide moves both ways, by up to 18 of 300.

Why, and what it licenses

The step was not one he could take. The answer needed two moves in one token: match the request to a description by meaning, which at 20M is two-times-chance and not reliable (finding 186's desc-only, 0.43-0.49), and then copy the name standing to that description's left. GENERATORS: "dose amplifies a step; it does not create one", and a step that is not a copy does not form by fine-tune (findings 175, 179). With the meaning half too weak to supply a gradient that points anywhere, the loss could only be lowered by learning the half that is free — an answer is one of these names — and that is what he learned.

  • A described menu is not, by this fine-tune at 20M, a lever on the meaning wall. The format installs; the link does not.
  • The two halves can be separated, and should be next. Finding 180 made a computed step into a copy and it installed 864 of 864: a request that restates its description word for word makes the link pure binding, with no meaning in it. If that installs, binding is his and meaning is the wall; if it does not, the binding itself does not form at this dose.
  • The vehicle is open. Finding 74's re-ask needed the mix by four times where its fine-tune read a quarter of it; pretraining with the payload in the mix is the other vehicle, about 15 min a nano seed.

What it does not settle. Any size above 20M; a longer fine-tune or a second pass; the pretraining vehicle; a curriculum from verbatim to meaning; whether a menu of two, where the meaning read is easiest, forms anything.
