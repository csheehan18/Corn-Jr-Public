Halving the tool data leaves tool use intact

The result, as the finding states it

The tool share (ROADMAP TS): with tools and toolspace at half their share the call surface holds under the door at both seeds — tool.selection 190 and 180 against 186 and 182, the routing rows 199-200 against 188-200 — and nothing is a kill; the one registered cost is refusal.say at both seeds, read by hand as the relayed refusal cutting the copied query's last words short on four of the row's six strings; a quarter splits on search.route_heldout and is a direction

What it cost

70.5 min of training four nano runs (17.5-18.2 min each, card_cost.py; registered ~16-18), 11.8 min scoring their four standard cards and 6.8 min re-reading the full cell's two, 80.5 min of the staged read on six checkpoints (13.2-13.7 min each), all inside one hold of the card of 2.86 h against a registered ~3 h — contended throughout (the card at 34-45% before the job), so every timing is a bound; the routing control 13.1 min and the hand-read probe 20 s on top; the build about 20 min of CPU (08:07-08:27 on 2026-10-13).

ROADMAP TS, PLAN-TAG-SPINE §7, the owner's D12 (2026-09-26: it runs for 1.0, to find room in the mix for D10's layers). Pre-registered in configs/mix_v3_ts_half.py's docstring and committed at 2df7e96 before a byte of either cut trained. One variable: the share of tools (tools_names_parse.txt) and toolspace (toolspace_decline_composed_parse.txt) in A2's tagged world, every other source byte-identical and max_iters scaled with the budget so every other slice keeps its exposure:

Seeds 1337 and 2337, compile=True. Read by scripts/run_ts.py in one sitting — the standard card on all six (the full cell's as __ts_card) and scripts/probe_a2.py --families ts on all six — and tabled by scripts/read_ts.py, written before the staged cards existed. Cards: evals/results/nano20m_v3_ts_{half,quarter}{,_seed2}{.json,__ts.json,__ts_items.jsonl}, evals/results/nano20m_v3_a2_on{,_seed2}{__ts_card.json,__ts.json,__ts_items.jsonl}. probe_a2.py is held by scripts/check_parseline.py and nothing more: every staged number is a bound (rule 28).

Readable

Neither unreadable condition fired. Every primary of the full cell reads 188-200 of 200 bound (the bar was 160), and the full cell's re-read is the 2026-10-04 card to the item on every staged row it shares with it (tool.selection 186 and 182 both days) — the instrument did not move under the arm.

Half: every primary holds at both seeds and the kill does not fire (tool.copy_wide 297 against 299 at one seed — two items of 300, a split, not a kill). One hold row falls at both seeds: refusal.say (245 and 260 against 263 and 266). The table's row for that is "a hold row falls at a cut that otherwise holds — that cut costs the row's turn: read row by row beside D1, not a kill of the cut." turn2.react and refusal.clarify fall at one seed each.

Quarter: search.route_heldout falls at one seed (192 against 197) and holds at the other — "seeds split on a primary: a direction; no third seed." So "room to a quarter" is not licensed. Five hold rows fall at one seed each and none at both.

The prediction, scored: half holds every primary — right; quarter holds tool.selection — right; quarter drops route_heldout — at one seed, a split; no kill — right; refusal.say falls at the quarter — wrong cell: it fell at half at both seeds and at the quarter at one.

Read by hand (EXPERIMENTS §5.6)

refusal.say is a copy row with six answers on the side that fell. The row relays a refusal a tool handed over, word for word (score_say, exact match). Its no_results items are 43 items over six strings ("I couldn't find anything useful for {query}."), and a scratch decode of all 150 say items (probe only — not on disk) reads the full cell 150 and 146, half 125 and 140, every lost item no_results. At half seed 1337 all 25 lost items are one failure: the relayed sentence cuts the copied query's last words — "...for what the loft smells." for "...what the loft smells like.", "...which sock." for "...which sock is missing.", "...what my dentist had." for "...what my dentist had for breakfast." — on four of the six strings. It is EXPERIMENTS §5.7's caution about tool.copy (six answers, one garbled string costs twenty items) on a different row. The 232-answer tool.copy_wide holds at both cuts, so the truncation is a real cost on the relay of a long query and not the copy band falling. It is the cost this finding hands D1, at its size.

The quarter's route_heldout fall is a carrier that already misroutes. Seed 1337's eight misses: six place_call on "give me the discoverer of Peliholm, please" and its kind, two save_note. The full cell's seed 2337 misses the same carrier the same way twelve times, and the quarter's seed 2337 improves on it (197 against 188). One carrier, read as a call, moving between seeds — not the share.

The forced card's call rows rose with the cut, side column and not the read (finding 150: on the tagged cell the forced card is a transcript shape it never trained): search.route_base 122 and 86 full, 200 and 154 half, 178 and 176 quarter. Fewer tool documents carrying a parts: line under every user turn make the untagged forced prompt less foreign. The staged rows are the read.

Post hoc, not in the table: the social side

probe_a2.py's guard family (finding 150's post hoc rows) reads, bound:

row full half quarter social.bare_nocall 80 86 63 76 75 73 social.voice 47 66 37 50 69 52 social.family 7 13 11 8 8 3 social.self 47 47 47 47 47 47

social.bare_nocall is below the full cell at all four cut checkpoints, past the half-width at three; social.voice falls at half at both seeds and splits at the quarter (69 up, 52 down) — not dose-monotonic. Read by hand at half: chit-chat draws a web_search or resolve_date call more often (15 and 15 web_search against 6 and 8), and his own social sentences drift into the registry tools' say register — "Listen to write a small agent on the user's behalf.", "Note down that the loft was done.", "I'll call you the small agent on the user's behalf." My guess at the mechanism, untested: toolspace's plain-spoken declines and coined-tool says were a large share of the say lines in the mix, and halving them leaves the registry tools' few say templates a bigger share of what a say sounds like. Not registered, so a direction, and it names what a share cut has to watch beside the call rows: the social turn.

What it licenses

For D1's table: the tool slices can go to half their share — 2.64% at nano, which is about 2.65% of mix_v4_5b against today's 5.3% (tools 0.59% + toolspace 4.7%), about 2.6 points of a 5B run, some 130M tokens, for D10's layers. The surface the door holds does not need the rest: tool choice, routing, call validity and the payloads' rows hold at both seeds. The cost goes into D1 with it, at its size: the relay of a long refused query loses its last words on a six-string row (refusal.say 245 and 260 of 270), and, post hoc, the social turn's no-call and voice rows fall at half. A quarter is a direction and is not licensed.

What it does not settle. Nano, A2's tagged world, with the door held by the runtime (probe_a2.py's bound): the untagged world was not cut, and the reading is about the call surface when the door holds, which is the question D12 asked. 260M has flipped a nano sign before (finding 51); D11's pilot is where the share is read again. And whether the social side is the share or the say register of what was cut is a probe — a cut that keeps toolspace's say lines and drops its calls separates them.

Next (not run): that separating probe, priced at four nano runs (~1.2 h) plus the guard-family read; and PLAN-1-0 §10's share table carries the half cut with its two named costs when D1 is taken.
