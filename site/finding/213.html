The reply judge · 22 October 2026

A small dose matching how the owner talks moves him furthest

The result, as the finding states it

Short-reply OpenAssistant exchanges — people asking an assistant things, best-ranked answers of 60 words or fewer, 252,008 tokens read twice in place of the same 504,016 tokens of SODA (Apache 2.0) — make the owner's own typed messages less surprising to the 5M at both seeds: 4.937 and 4.958 nats a token against the control's 5.218 and 5.105, under the pre-registered 4.992. Narrative and web hold (0.4890 / 0.4898, 0.9539 / 0.9563, over the web line at seed 2 only). The pre-registered row is (r), against a predicted (n): a seventh of finding 211's EmpatheticDialogues dose moves his register as far (4.880 / 4.963 there). It does not move the 148 stand-in messages (3.833 / 3.862 against 3.987 / 3.828), which EmpatheticDialogues did: each source moves the register it carries, and the owner writes questions and requests. Replies stay level or below at 5M (68 and 47 of 148 by a blind hand read against 70 and 55; 47 is the lowest of any 5M arm read, inside the control's spread)

What it cost

OASST2 download (54 MB), scripts/prepare_oasst.py (a first cut without the rank filter paired prompts with off-topic replies, caught by hand), the mix and the pre-registration 16:30-16:40 on 2026-10-22 (24c35aa); the chain 16:40-16:52, 12.2 min (contended, a bound); the blind hand read of 592 replies ~10 min. ETA 15 min of card, actual 12.2.

The question (owner, 2026-10-22: Apache 2.0 stays, so EmpatheticDialogues is out; "Do the short reply"; option (A), the set at its own size). Finding 212: OpenAssistant's prompts are the closest text to the owner's own messages of any source read (bigram 426); its long answers teach reciting.

Read by hand

The replies are the control's kind; the OASST arm's openings lean to questions back ("What do you mean?", the commonest three-word opening at seed 1 "what do you" x12), and its failures are the familiar ones. Seed 2's 47 comes from more stock "That's great!" to bad news (a scratched car, a burned dinner) and the self-doubt line; read beside the base pair's own 15 of spread, it is a direction to watch at the next size, not a cost by any registered rule. The knowing matcher splits by seed, unchecked by hand.

What it licenses

  • The match of register matters more than the volume: a small set of people asking an assistant moved his expectation of the owner's messages as far as seven times as much everyday-news dialogue, and moved only that register. The 1.0 conversational plan should carry text in the register the owner actually writes in — questions and requests — under a licence the Apache weights can carry.
  • Short-reply OpenAssistant is that text, at 0.25M tokens: it goes in the 1.0 conversational plan as a share (Apache 2.0, attribution in the model card). More of its kind is the next search, not a heavier repeat.
  • Replies are still a size question (findings 208-209): read the reply reads again at 20M with this share in, and watch seed 2's direction there.

What it does not settle. Any size above 5M; whether a larger share of the same kind moves the register further; the owner's register beyond 69 messages.
