Forcing copied search queries breaks follow-ups; keyword shortcuts catch few chats

The result, as the finding states it

The query slot buys nothing on this base and costs three chain follow-ups in four, and the social hatch catches five utterances in thirty

What it cost

0 s of new scoring on record; it reads 2 cards scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m, corn_jr_260m_v4). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

A harness audit rather than a training run: checkpoints/corn_jr_260m_v4/ckpt.pt driven live on 2026-08-29 from a working tree carrying session 12's uncommitted repairs (framed and the frame-loss floor in corn/agent.py, the ASCII fold in corn/grammar.py). 140 logged turns on the primary server (--all, rows in Saved Sessions/turns.jsonl, sessions 20260829T19*–T2*) plus two unlogged controls on the same weights and the same flags: --no-grammar on port 8766, and port 8767 with only ("web_search", "query") removed from RUNTIME_COPY_SLOTS — schema, enum narrowing and both contact slots left armed. The isolated control is what makes this a finding rather than session 12's observation: --no-grammar moves the schema and the copy slots together, and they turn out to have opposite signs.

The question the audit was asked was whether the harness is holding the model back. It is, in exactly one place, and everywhere else it is the only thing between the user and the pretraining register. Three layers intervene between the weights and the user, and they need separating before either half can be read:

layerwhat it doescontrolled by
A. the grammarconstrains what he may emit — schema, enum narrowing, copy slots--no-grammar, RUNTIME_COPY_SLOTS
B. the guardsdiscards what he did emit — social_rejection, recall_rejection, search_rejection, the frame-loss floornothing; always on
C. the hatchesdecide the turn before he runs — conversational_reply, identity_reply, temporal_request, recheck_request--restrict, which adds rather than removes

Layer C is why the authorship columns read as they do. Over the whole 151-row log as it stands: the model authored the spoken words on 60 turns, the tool's say on 70, the runtime's own sentence on 21, and 10 of 109 tool calls were written by the runtime rather than decoded.

A: the query copy slot forbids the composition the same corpus teaches

Four chain follow-ups, each the second turn of its own conversation, armed against the isolated control:

query slot armedquery slot off
what about Sweden? after Norwaythe capital of Norway ~~masked 2the capital of Sweden
what about Brazil? after Japanthe population of japan ~~masked 1the population of brazil
and Hungary? after Polandthe currency of poland ~~masked 2the currency of poland
what about Chile? after Peruthe capital of Peru ~~masked 2the capital of chile

0 of 4 armed, 3 of 4 off. The Hungary row fails identically on both sides and is therefore the weights, not the slot — which is the control doing its job, because without it all four would have been charged to the grammar.

The mechanism is contiguity, and it is structural rather than tunable. span_ok requires the value to occur as a contiguous run of the source, and corn/agent.py's set_source binds that source to the whole conversation below the declaration ("prior turns stay in, deliberately"). A chain query is by construction not contiguous anywhere: the capital of comes from the previous turn, Sweden from this one, and no span of either contains both. So no composed query can ever be legal, the slot bites at the join, and greedy after the bite lands on the one string that is a span — the previous query sitting in the history. Session 12 read this as a case-folding bug and fixed a real one; the fold changed which carrier reproduces it, not whether it reproduces.

And the rescue it was added for does not reproduce on this base. The slot's justification is harness/server.py's own note: corn_jr_260m handed look up Ada Lovelace wrote {"query": "ada-lovelace-definition-definition-… until the budget ran out. Eleven degenerate queries — five bare proper names including Vercingetorix and Zbigniew Brzezinski, a colon syntax, two vocative-prefixed requests, an embedded the thing about the Antikythera mechanism, and I want you to search Rachmaninoff — are byte-identical armed and unarmed. The slot never bit once. Its documented cost was measured on the v3 base and the v4 base does not need it.

The schema half is pure rescue and the same controls say so. Unarmed, --no-grammar produced {"query": "the capital of Finland"} with no closing brace and a <|say|> spliced inside the call, and {"query": "and Hungary?""} — both not_json, both executing nothing. Layer A is not one decision; it is a schema that pays for itself and one slot that is now paying only costs.

C: the hatches are exact-match, and the misses are the dangerous part

conversational_reply, identity_reply and temporal_request all test whole normalised utterances against frozen sets. Coverage over a battery of phrasings a live user produces:

social 5 of 30 identity 11 of 15 temporal 9 of 16

The dominant social miss is saying his name, and the guard turns it from an example into a property. morning and good night are both in the pools; morning corn and good night corn are not. Over all 275 phrases in the five pools, 0 survive being addressed by name, and a trailing-vocative strip recovers all 275 (scripts/check_hatches.py §3). On the battery that is 13 of the 25 social misses, taking coverage from 5/30 to 18/30 — but the pool-wide number is the one that says how much one strip in _normalised is worth.

The vocative is not only a social problem — it poisons web_search and nothing else.

look up the capital of Iceland -> "the capital of Iceland" corn look up the capital of Iceland -> "corn look up the capital of Iceland" corn, look up the capital of Iceland -> "corn, look up the capital of Iceland" text Grace Kim that I'm on my way -> correct with and without the vocative note down that the bins go out tonight -> correct with and without the vocative

send_text and save_note extract a bounded entity and are immune; web_search's query copies a run of the sentence and the vocative is sitting at its front. strip_affixes carries "look " and cannot see it behind a name.

What a miss costs, over all 34 of them run live. Fifteen of the 25 social misses routed to decline — hey corn answered "Nothing I've got covers that." Seven produced ungated speech, of which three were unusable: bye corn gave a repetition loop with <|endoftext|> in the transcript and the say tripled, and hope you're well gave "You're not well. You need to see a doctor." The remaining 8 of 34 reached the live search provider, and 4 of those 8 came back with a confident irrelevant page served as the answer: thanks corn → Food Network's Thanksgiving corn recipes, thank you corn → "It's Corn" thank-you cards, what happened on Wednesday → the plot of Netflix's Wednesday season 2, what did I do on Friday → Marvel trivia about a character called Friday (and, on an earlier run of the same turn, a Reddit post about what people do on Friday nights — the wrong answer is not even stable). The other four searched honestly and returned no_results.

That is the shape worth naming: a hatch miss is not a degraded answer, it is a private or self-referential question leaving the machine. who made you, how do you work and can you tell me what you can do all went to the internet.

The counter-finding, which the same battery produced and which matters for what the hatches are for. remind me what I did yesterday misses temporal_request, falls through to the router, and the model chains resolve_date → recall_day correctly on its own. The hatch is not purely a crutch and the weights are not empty behind it; its coverage is a floor that is in places below what he can already do.

B: the guard fires three times in twenty, and only inside C's coverage

Twenty hatch-caught social turns (hey, how's it going, thanks, bye, morning, four draws each):

guard fired 3 / 20 — 2x "tool register: find anything", 1x "names Lynn" his own words served 17 / 20

By reading rather than by ruler, about 13 of those 17 are not usable: hey → "I'm sorry, but I don't have any questions."; bye → "Ah, that's a beautiful song."; how's it going → "Pretty good. I'm just getting ready for my next class."; thanks → "You're welcome. I'm glad you enjoyed it." That is a judgement and is marked as one; the 3/20 is not.

One of the leaks is a single missing entry rather than a class:

social_rejection("I don't have anything useful on morning.") -> None

TOOL_REGISTER carries "find anything" and not "have anything", so web_search's empty-result sentence reaches the user as a reply to a greeting — the exact family the constant's own docstring says it exists to catch, one phrasing further out.

And the structural point, which no entry fixes. social_rejection is called only from inside the social hatch. Outside it there is no guard on speech at all, which is why rough week, honestly came back as invented personal history ("I've been having a lot of personal issues and I haven't been able to focus on work") and do you ever get tired? as "sometimes I get tired". Guard coverage is exactly pool coverage, so layer B can never be better than layer C, and widening C is what widens B.

What this corrects

  • Session 11's and session 13's reading of the temporal chain. what did I do yesterday completing resolve_date → recall_day in 9 ms with 0 ms to first token was temporal_request, runtime-authored, no decode. Any live read of the chain has to check the author column before crediting the weights — though see the counter-finding above, which credits them on a turn the hatch missed.
  • Session 13's first-pass reading of the greetings. hey and morning both hit the hatch. hey's good reply was the pool after the guard rejected his attempt (fell_back: unfinished); morning's bad reply was the guard leaking. Opposite mechanisms, and neither is coverage.
  • Session 12's sticky-query attribution. The fold was a real repair to a real carrier and is not withdrawn; it is not the whole mechanism, and the contiguity argument above says no fold could have been.

Instrument facts found while auditing

  • The session log cannot price any of this. turn_record's transcript holds what was served, so on a runtime-authored turn the model's own emission is discarded unrecorded. author: "runtime" says an intervention happened and never what it cost — every number above had to come from an A/B against a second server. A model_draft column would make these rates countable from the rows, which is the argument frame_loss already won.
  • The cp1252 guard is in the wrong scope. harness/client.py reconfigures stdout inside main(), so anything importing Client/run_turn inherits the UnicodeEncodeError that guard exists to prevent — hit here on złoty. The CLI is fine; embedders are not.
  • strip_affixes carries "look " but not "search for ", so search for Nikola Tesla reaches the provider with its verb attached, on both sides of the grammar A/B.

Limits

n=1 on every live cell — one checkpoint, greedy tool decode, no seed spread, and the social pools sample at speech_temperature=0.3 so the 20-turn guard battery is four draws of five utterances rather than twenty independent ones. The three coverage numbers are properties of frozen sets and a hand-written battery: they are exact for that battery and say nothing about the true distribution of what the owner types. The 3-of-4 chain recovery and the 0-of-11 rescue are the two numbers this finding actually rests on, and both are byte-comparisons against a control that differs by one slot.

Nothing here licenses a corpus change. The vocative, the missing TOOL_REGISTER entry, the hatch widths and the query slot's removal are all runtime decisions and all the owner's; scripts/check_hatches.py and check_grammar.py §9 record the present numbers so that a repair moves a guard rather than moving silently.
