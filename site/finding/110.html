Of four cheap rescoring ideas, only one vote per mention helps

The result, as the finding states it

The four free replays the audit ranked first: agreement between the two readouts and gate willingness add nothing at the silence floor that the margin does not already carry, per-bucket thresholds fit the folds and give it all back, and one vote per occurrence is the only one positive on both seeds, +10 / +6 at pooled p = 0.03 and under its per-seed bar on one

2026-09-04. Replay only — no GPU, no decode, no weight read, no checkpoint opened. scripts/probe_free_replays.py (new) over nullpr_s{1,2}_popa.json, pairs_built_s{1,2}_popa.json, crossfit_harvest_ft4g.json (the gate's willingness and forced reply per window), multiwindow_ft4g.json (the generator's off+off verdict per passage) and the bpe8k tokenizer. Two seeds, both at the bar protocol, extent on, hybrid reduce, each at its own cross-validated threshold, which reproduces the shipped 389 / 199 and 388 / 196 before anything is read. evals/results/free_replays.json. corn/reader.py and corn/extract.py are untouched.

0. What ran and why now

READER-AUDIT.md Part 3 ranked its levers by expected passages per GPU hour and put four zero-GPU replays at the top — L1's admission half, L2, L3, L4 — with the sentence "the free replays first". The sessions after it ran L6, L7, L18 and the representation plan's A1–A3 and skipped past these. Each carries a pre-registered bar and kill in the audit, quoted below unchanged; every arm here is a rule over the admitted-window mask and nothing downstream of the mask differs from the shipped path.

One instrument is shared. A window's admission score becomes margin + w · feature for L1 and L2, or a threshold drawn from the window's bucket for L3, or the shipped mask with duplicates silenced for L4; the weight, the thresholds and the bucket thresholds are chosen per fold under the bar protocol (max right holding the fold's share of 200 correct silences) and applied to the other fold. The grid for L1 and L2 starts at w = 0, which is the shipped rule, so the CV can only leave it for something that beats it in-fold. Every arm is paired against the shipped verdicts on the same passages and read once more with the willingness control of finding 101 §7 — the shipped head shifted to the arm's attempt rate — so a re-tune cannot pass as an arm.

1. L1's admission half: agreement between the head and the generator

The two gates the audit set both clear. Re-read at seed 2's own constant (−1.6730, where the head reads 388 rather than 409 at seed 1's −2.02):

head generator both union union − head seed 1 (−2.0202) 389 279 235 433 +44 seed 2 (−1.6730) 388 279 231 436 +48

The build gate of ≥ 40 clears by 4 and 8. The seed-1 row is the audit's uncommitted read of 2026-09-03 to the passage, now in a script. The generator-only wins split 21 / 23 and 25 / 23 head-silent / head-wrong. The ceiling the audit asked for before the arm could run — head silent, generator right, and the head's argmax on a gold window already the gold — reads 13 and 19 against a floor of 12, so the arm was licensed on both seeds.

Agreement is a real signal and the verifier already prices it. The decline-banned forced reply keys (_span_key) to the same span the head speaks on 40.8% / 40.3% of the 3,893 windows. As a raw bit it separates "the spoken span is right" at AUC 0.693 / 0.694 over the answerable windows. Added to the margin in finding 94's cross-fitted read of "this window carries the gold" it reads −0.0030 / −0.0001.

The arm, two-feature bar-protocol CV (margin + β · agree ≥ t; grid β ∈ {0, 0.25, …, 4}):

right wrong a.silent | u.silent attempt acc|att (β, t) per fold s1 shipped 389 188 144 199 0.800 0.674 s1 + agree 403 150 168 200 0.767 0.729 (1.00, −1.217) / (2.00, −0.706) s2 shipped 388 172 161 196 0.777 0.693 s2 + agree 389 158 174 198 0.759 0.711 (0.00, −1.598) / (0.75, −1.203)

Paired on right: +14 (21 / 35, p = 0.081) and +1 (8 / 9, p = 1), pooled +15 at p = 0.10. The bar was ≥ +10 on both seeds at pooled p < 0.05; it is missed on seed 2 by nine passages and on the pooled p. One of seed 2's folds chose β = 0 — the shipped rule — which is the CV saying agreement bought nothing in-fold on half of one seed.

What is consistent is not right. Wrong falls by 38 and 14, the attempt rate by 3.3 and 1.8 points, acc | att rises 5.5 and 1.8 points. The rule the folds found on seed 1 — β = 2 at t = −0.7 — is in effect "a window whose two readouts disagree must clear a threshold 1.3 logits stricter than the shipped one; one where they agree may be 0.7 looser", and it mostly refuses. The willingness control says it is not only a re-tune: at the arm's attempt rate the shipped head reads 380 / 173 / 211 and 381 / 167 / 211 (right / wrong / correct silences), so the arm holds +23 / +8 right and −23 / −9 wrong for −11 / −13 correct silences at equal willingness. It moves answerable passages from wrong to right and pays for it on the unanswerable half; on the joint bit that is +12 on seed 1 and −5 on seed 2.

Closed as an arm aimed at right, under the bar it was written with. Not banked and not closed as a precision shape: −38 / −14 wrong at a lower attempt rate is the same category as finding 101's window features and is the owner's call under the standing rule. It would cost a generative decode of every window at read time — the forced harvest is one decline-banned decode per window — which the runtime does not do on the head's path, so unlike finding 101's five scalars it is not a re-pointing.

2. L2: gate willingness beside the verifier margin

Killed at the cheap read. Finding 94's protocol — the 580 answerable passages carrying both a gold-bearing and a no-gold window, 2,498 windows, cross-fitted logistic by passage parity, AUC for "this window carries the gold":

seed 1 seed 2 margin 0.8000 0.8147 margin + willingness 0.8064 0.8195 Δ +0.0064 / +0.0048 willingness alone 0.7445 0.7445

The kill was Δ < +0.01 on either seed; it fires on both. Willingness alone reads 0.74, which is finding 83's 0.79 on a harder label, so the signal is there and the margin carries it: corn/gate.py's presence question and the verifier's are the same question asked of the same trunk.

Finding 92 §2's silent-set read — passages the reader was silent on, the should-have-spoken (answerable, the argmax on some gold window right) against the correctly silent — is the one place willingness moves a number, and it moves it on one seed:

seed 1 (71 / 199) seed 2 (79 / 196) max margin 0.7129 0.7376 max margin + max willingness 0.7718 +0.059 0.7450 +0.007 max margin + willingness at argmax 0.7708 +0.058 0.7395 +0.002

+0.059 on seed 1 is the largest silent-set delta on the record and seed 2 reads it at +0.007; under the audit's two-seed rule it is one seed. The arm was run anyway (margin + γ · willingness, γ ∈ {0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32}): +1 (3 / 4, p = 1) and +5 (9 / 14, p = 0.41), joint −2 / −5, one fold on each seed choosing γ = 0. At the arm's attempt rate the shipped head reads +2 right on both seeds — the arm is a re-tune. Constant 17 of the audit (the gate never enters admission) is measured rather than assumed, and it does not matter.

3. L3: per-bucket thresholds under the bar protocol

Eighteen buckets — the spoken span's answer type (name / number / other) × the question's label (ROLE / no label / other label, probe_question_shape) × window position (first / later) — each with its own threshold, found per fold by coordinate ascent from the fold's global bar t (a bucket's move is kept only if the fold's silence share still holds and right rises; three rounds) and applied to the other fold.

Killed on both of its conditions. Held out: −2 (19 / 17, p = 0.87) and −5 (10 / 5, p = 0.30), joint −15 / −3. In-fold, the ascent raised right to 212 / 215 and 199 / 209 on the training folds, against the 193–196 a fold the shipped threshold reads on the same passages, and gave every passage of it back on the other fold. The fold-agreement condition (no bucket's two thresholds further apart than twice the global fold gap, 0.16 / 0.29) fails on ten of eighteen buckets on seed 1 and five on seed 2; three buckets have one fold at +∞ (silence the bucket) and the other inside the shipped range, other × ROLE × later sits at −1.98 on one fold and −4.82 on the other, and the buckets under ten windows (number × ROLE × first, other × ROLE × first) never move off the global value because they cannot. The 502-passage folds are being fitted, which is what the audit's kill was written to detect. Constant 10 (one threshold per seed) is not where the admission errors are: finding 91's 27-point spread across question labels is real and a threshold cannot spend it.

4. L4: dedupe with a tokenizer-defined occurrence

Finding 97's dedupe keyed one vote per distinct span text and said its definition could not be the eighty-fifth finding's because the harvest has no character offsets. With bpe8k loaded, windows() reproduces every one of the 3,893 harvest windows and each window's start in the passage is recoverable; the argmax's token position in the pairs harvest (ij, on the escaped window's tokens, mapped back through the JSON escaping) locates the spoken span inside the window when its text occurs more than once. An occurrence is then a character position in the passage, and two admitted windows that read the same occurrence count once while two occurrences of the same text count twice.

The tokenizer resolves finding 97's objection and changes one passage a seed. 93 / 91 passages speak one span text from two different occurrences, so the two rules do disagree about the mask; at the vote they disagree about the verdict on one passage a seed.

right wrong a.silent | u.silent attempt acc|att paired right masks moved s1 shipped 389 188 144 199 0.800 0.674 s1 dedupe text 398 178 145 199 0.799 0.691 +9 (9 / 18, p = 0.12) 266 s1 dedupe occ 399 177 145 199 0.799 0.693 +10 (8 / 18, p = 0.076) 252 s2 shipped 388 172 161 196 0.777 0.693 s2 dedupe text 393 165 163 196 0.774 0.704 +5 (10 / 15, p = 0.42) 232 s2 dedupe occ 394 164 163 196 0.774 0.706 +6 (8 / 14, p = 0.29) 220

The text rule reproduces finding 97 to the passage (+9 at 9 / 18, +5 at 10 / 15). Occurrence: +10 / +6, pooled +16 over 1,442 passages at p = 0.029. The willingness control shifts the shipped thresholds by 0.01 and reads the same silences: at equal willingness the rule converts 10 and 6 wrong answers into right ones and touches nothing else — a ranking correction, not a willingness move, which is what a fix to a double-counted vote should look like.

Neither the bar nor the kill. The bar was ≥ +8 a seed at pooled p < 0.05: the pooled p clears and seed 2 is two passages under. The kill was pooled p ≥ 0.1 or either seed negative: neither. It is the audit's row 12 (dedupe, open) moved from p = 0.12 / 0.42 on the text rule to p = 0.03 pooled on the occurrence rule, positive on both seeds in three readings now (finding 85 at JOINT, finding 97 at SHIPPED, here), and it is the only one of the four replays that moves right the same way on both seeds.

5. The bars, in one table

lever bar read kill verdict L1 ≥ +10 both, pooled p < 0.05 +14 / +1, pooled +15 p = 0.10 under the bar closed on right; precision shape open L2 ΔAUC ≥ +0.02 both, then ≥ +12 a seed +0.0064 / +0.0048; +1 / +5 ΔAUC < +0.01 either: fired closed at the cheap read L3 ≥ +12 a seed, folds within 2× the gap −2 / −5; 10 / 8 buckets out < +8 either, folds apart: fired closed at the kill L4 ≥ +8 a seed, pooled p < 0.05 +10 / +6, pooled +16 p = 0.029 p ≥ 0.1 or negative: not fired open, positive twice, under the per-seed bar on seed 2

Three of the four say the same thing findings 92, 94, 101 and 107 said from other directions: the verifier's margin already carries what the gate, the generator's agreement and the question's shape know about admission. The audit's constants 16 (the forward's own outputs) and 17 (the gate) are now measured on the admission side and neither moves a number at the floor. The fourth is not an admission arm at all; it is the vote counting one piece of evidence twice.

6. What this cannot say

L1 is the free-decode form of the generator, not the generator that reads 279. The forced harvest decodes every window with the decline banned; the audit's own read says that replayed with every window admitted it reproduces off+off on 667 of 1,003 passages. The union uses off+off's real verdicts; the agreement bit uses the forced replies. A generator allowed to decline would agree less often and the arm might read differently; that arm needs a decode of every window at read time either way.

The two-feature CVs choose one weight and one threshold on ~500 passages. Finding 92 measured the threshold's own fold wander at ±7 passages; a second parameter widens that, and the fold values here disagree (β = 1 against 2 on seed 1, 0 against 0.75 on seed 2), so the reported right deltas carry more noise than the shipped single-threshold read. That cuts both ways and is why the pooled McNemar is quoted beside every per-seed number.

L3's ascent is greedy and a joint optimum over eighteen thresholds could be higher in-fold; it could not be more held-out, because the in-fold gain it found was already given back entirely.

L4's pooled p treats the two seeds' passages as independent trials when they are the same 1,442 passage-reads under two heads; it is optimistic, as finding 101's was. The per-seed p-values (0.076, 0.29) are the honest ones, and they are not significant.

Nothing here is selected on Population A. Every weight and threshold is chosen on one fold and read on the other; the reports are held-out.
