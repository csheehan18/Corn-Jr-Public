The depth-4 failure is a position never seen, not missing recursion

The result, as the finding states it

The depth-4 zero is a position the corpus pinned at zero, not an absence of recursion: a parse line at index 3 holds a group in 0 of 2,236 blocks, he predicts a fourth level 96 of 96 when the deep cut is given, and the identical cut scored at line 2 instead reads 8 to 27 of 96 on four checkpoints and survives being padded back to the original length

What it cost

19.1 min of scoring on the 4 cards first cited here (nano20m_v3_funcworld, nano20m_v3_funcworld_rope, nano20m_v3_funcworld_rope_seed3, nano20m_v3_funcworld_seed3); it also reads 2 cards scored for an earlier finding (nano100m_v1, nano20m). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

A diagnostic pass over findings 133 and 135, read 2026-09-14 with no training and no new corpus. scripts/probe_depthpos.py, four reads: the corpus's own conditional off the bytes; the continuation prior; the level-3 cut re-scored at line 2; and that shift padded back to length. Checkpoints are rung 2's own — nano20m_v3_funcworld and _seed3 (learned positions), _rope and _rope_seed3 — with nano100m_v1 as the never-saw-a-parse floor and the structure cards of finding 133 re-read beside. Card: evals/results/nano20m_v3_funcworld_rope__depthpos.json. The instrument is probe_structure.div_read unchanged, so every number here is comparable to an l3 cell of a structure card.

The question

Finding 133 read parse.w_depth4 at zero on greedy at every seed and its amendment put the deep-cut preference at B's floor at level 3, the one nesting training never showed. Finding 135 raised the depth-3 dose from 0.081 to 0.202 of turns and moved that level nowhere, and read it as exposure is not the lever. Finding 133 had already read size is not the lever at 100M. Both were taken as evidence about recursion. Neither asked what the corpus says about the position the read scores.

What the corpus says, off the bytes

A block of d consecutive parts: lines is a tree of depth d, so its line at index k holds a group exactly when k < d. Counted:

file line 1 line 2 line 3 funcworld.txt 12105/29950 = 0.4042 2236/12105 = 0.1847 0/2236 = 0.0000 dose2.txt 12164/29358 = 0.4143 3896/12164 = 0.3203 0/3896 = 0.0000 dose3.txt 12056/28853 = 0.4178 5527/12056 = 0.4584 0/5527 = 0.0000

Zero. Not rare — zero, across 2,236 consecutive examples with no counterexample, and depth 4 is the holdout, so every span he has ever seen at line 3 is a leaf. parse.w_depth4's level-3 cell asks him to write a span the corpus gives no support at that line.

And the dose arm could not have moved it. Raising the depth-3 share moved P(group | line 2) from 0.1847 to 0.4584 and left P(group | line 3) at exactly zero in every cell while adding 3,291 more blocks whose line 3 terminates. Finding 135's observed shape — levels 1 and 2 climbing monotonically with the dose, level 3 at the floor — is what that predicts. Its null is true of the exposure it varied and silent about the one that governs the row.

The floor says training moved him, and the direction is down

parse.w_depth4 level 3, deep_div of 96, from the structure cards:

nano100m_v1 never saw a parse 59/96, 52/96 — a coin nano20m funcworld trained depths 1-3 3 to 19/96 nano100m funcworld the same, bigger 4/96 — and 88/96 at level 1

A model with no knowledge of the world is at chance. Training on depths 1 to 3 drives it to near zero, and the larger, better-fitting model goes lowest of all while reading highest at level 1. A capability is not lost by training; a constraint is acquired. This is the signature of a learned regularity, and it is why size did not help — 100M fits the ceiling more sharply.

He predicts the fourth level he has never seen

After a completed parts: line the corpus writes either another one or move:. The mass on each, per line index, split by whether the gold tree continues (--read prior, nano20m_v3_funcworld_rope):

line gold tree items prefers parts: logp parts logp move 1 continues 471 471/471 -0.001 -13.530 1 ends 161 9/161 -9.161 -1.740 2 continues 255 243/255 -0.123 -9.302 2 ends 216 5/216 -7.877 -0.767 3 continues 96 96/96 -0.083 -6.630 3 ends 159 19/159 -6.622 -0.811 4 ends 96 0/96 -5.709 -1.405

Given the deep cut at line 3, he wants a fourth parts: line 96 of 96 times — at a depth the corpus contains zero times — and stops correctly at line 4, and stops correctly at line 3 when the tree genuinely ends. The other trunks agree: 93 of 96 at _rope_seed3, 68 of 96 at the learned-position seed, which is the weakest of the three and still above half.

So the recursion signal generalises to the unseen depth, exactly as finding 133's rank read found the labels do (96 of 96 at the innermost level). What fails is narrower than either: writing the long, conjunction-carrying span at line 3 that makes a fourth level necessary.

The identical decision, one line earlier

A depth-4 tree's lower three levels are a depth-3 tree, which is a trained shape. Re-presenting the level-1 group's span as the whole utterance puts the identical level-3 decision at line 2 — same gold line, same flattened alternative, same divergence token, same spans; only the line index moves. deep_div of 96:

checkpoint at line 3 at line 2 padded to length nano20m_v3_funcworld 3/96 24/96 22/96 nano20m_v3_funcworld_seed3 3/96 9/96 — nano20m_v3_funcworld_rope 7/96 29/96 29/96 nano20m_v3_funcworld_rope_seed3 19/96 45/96 41/96

A confound, caught, and what it costs the headline

The first cut of the shift rewrote line 1 into level-1 label conventions (find->ask, send/note->do) on 41 of 96 items, and read 6.3 to 29.2 of 96 over six checkpoints. Split by subset, the gain sat almost entirely in the 41 — the intervention, not the position. Rerun with line 1 verbatim it is the table above, smaller and noisier, and the two subsets still differ:

orig -> shifted relabelled (41) untouched (55) nano20m_v3_funcworld 2 -> 18 1 -> 6 _seed3 1 -> 5 2 -> 4 _rope 5 -> 22 2 -> 7 _rope_seed3 10 -> 28 9 -> 17

Every cell rises, 8 of 8, but do-rooted trees recover far more than ask-rooted ones and this finding does not explain why. The probe reports both subsets by construction now, and --relabel keeps the old arm readable beside it.

The decomposition

Against what he does opening a group at line 2 on trained items (parse.w_pairing l2 on the same four: 47, 52, 57, 60):

8/96 the depth-4 level-3 decision, as scored 27/96 the identical decision, at line 2 54/96 an in-distribution group at line 2

The position accounts for about 41% of the gap; the content of a depth-4 construction's innermost spans accounts for the rest. Two failures, not one, and neither is an absence of recursion.

What it licenses

  • The guard, built with this finding: check_funcworld.py section 6 reads the conditional off the corpus and fails on any eval row whose deepest cut has zero support at the line it is scored on unless that row is declared in ZERO_SUPPORT with what takes it, and on a declaration that has gone stale. The silent failure it names: a row that asks for a structure the corpus gives no support at the position it is scored, whose refusal is read as a capability limit. It would have fired before C5's four runs.
  • The wide-shape arm C6 is not the next arm on depth. More depth-3 shapes leave P(group | line 3) at zero; it can answer the shape question at the depths that have support and cannot move the row it is aimed at.
  • The arm this proposes instead: a depth profile with support past 3, so the conditional has no cliff, with specific nestings held out at depth 4 rather than the depth itself. Two rows and the gap between them is the result — a trained depth-4 nesting against a held-out one, both floored on B. That makes "generalise to an unseen nesting" a question about shapes, which is what PLAN-TAG-SPINE §5 lever 3 intended, rather than about emitting a zero-support structure.
  • The stepped protocol as mechanism, not fallback: re-prompting a group as a fresh utterance resets the line index and recovers about 41% of the gap, measured rather than assumed.

What it does not say

It does not say the recursion generalises. It says the instrument could not ask: two of the three components reach the unseen depth (the labels, 96 of 96 in finding 133; the continuation, 96 of 96 here) and the third is scored where the corpus has no support. Whether he cuts a nesting he was not shown, at a depth that has support, is untested and is what the proposed arm exists for.

Nor does the shift isolate one variable. It moves the line index, the utterance length and the remaining tree depth together; the padding arm removes the length and the other two cannot be separated on checkpoints that have never seen a group at line 3. Their separation needs the arm.

The subset asymmetry is unexplained, --read prior is one item set on three checkpoints, and every number here is nano at 20M with one 100M floor.
