Extraction training works, mostly by declining answerable questions

The result, as the finding states it

The slice separates on all three rows, and he bought it by declining three fifths of the answerable half

What it cost

3.5 min of scoring on the 1 card first cited here (nano20m_v3_extract); it also reads 2 cards scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m, nano20m_v3). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

configs/nano20m_v3_extract.py ×2 against nano20m_v3 ×2. One source changes: the extraction slice enters at 5% and web donates it, 49.3773% → 44.3773%. Every other source is mix_v3 verbatim, total held at 130,934,381, no repeat anywhere. Trained 2026-08-24/25, ~1,160s per seed.

27 of 27 pre-existing rows reproduced identically on both control seeds before anything was read. The control checkpoints are from 2026-08-19 and were re-scored rather than retrained, so that check is what makes the comparison a comparison; it is reported first here because it was run first.

The result that is not in doubt: confabulation is gone

extract.span failures control arm invented 105 -> 7 / 12 wrote_own 55 -> 3 / 4

That is the fifty-fifth finding's defect answered. The window said "19-year-old Jourdan Miller" and the untrained model said "21-year-old Jourdan Miller" — composing over a topic and supplying the number itself. 105 of 200 replies were that. Now 7. extract.brevity at 395-398/400 says the same thing from the other side: the replies became answers rather than paragraphs.

The 12 reads 13 under today's scorer (added 2026-09-07, finding 127 §4). The grounding rule changed at e46ac9ee and invented is "failed grounded", so one seed-2 reply moved out of wrong_span and into invented when the cards were re-derived from their saved generations. No verdict changed side and the pass count is untouched; the numbers above are what the scorer of the day said, and the claim they support — 105 to single figures — does not depend on the item.

Nothing about that is explained by the caveat below. An abstention and an invention are both failures on extract.span, so trading one for the other moves the row by zero. The row moved by 25-31 items, and invented fell by 98.

The caveat: he abstains on three fifths of the answerable half

extract.span failures, arm abstained = 117-120 of 200

So on windows that do contain the gold, the commonest reply is "I couldn't find anything useful." The plan pre-registered exactly this as the failure mode with no runtime floor:

A model that abstains on everything passes WS-C one hundred per cent of the time. Abstention is the incentive-compatible cheat, and no runtime check will catch it — only extract.abstain scored against extract.span will.

It is not the pure cheat. The degenerate case is 100% abstention on both halves, scoring 0/200 and 200/200; this model discriminates:

P(abstain | unanswerable) 70-79% P(abstain | answerable) 59-60% ~15-19 points

Real signal, and weak. Pooled into one number these two rows read 174-197/400 and look like a triumph, which is why the plan said they must never be pooled and why that instruction turned out to be worth having.

The honest summary of the behaviour change is that the failure mode moved from inventing a fluent wrong answer to declining to answer. That is a large safety improvement and a modest capability one, and the slice's balance is the first suspect: answerable and absent went in 50/50, and 24,239 of the 48,478 transcripts teach that the correct reply to a window is a miss sentence.

The falsifiable prediction could not be tested here

continued_payload was pre-registered as the cheap falsification — 69/400 on the frozen corn_jr_260m, and the claim was that a corpus containing the shape for the first time should collapse it.

control seeds 0 and 24 arm seeds 0 and 0

The two control seeds disagree by more than the whole effect. The row has no stable baseline at nano and the arm's range sits inside the control's, so by the rule this arm is read under, it does not separate. The 69/400 was a 260M number and a 20M control does not reproduce the regime. The envelope hypothesis is untested, not confirmed — it needs the 260M, where the failure actually exists at a readable rate.

This is the thirtieth finding's lesson arriving on a new row: a floor is a property of the arm it is scored on, and a prediction inherited from a different model size is not pre-registered, it is transplanted.

The regression watch caught something on one seed

tool.copy control 118-120/120 arm 100/120 and 120/120 tool.copy_wide control 281-291/300 arm 293-297/300 (UP)

Seed 1 lost 20 items to wrong_answer; seed 2 was perfect. The ranges overlap at 120 so the rule says no separation, but the arm's floor is 18 items below the control's and this is precisely the trade the arm pre-registered as its risk — "a slice that teaches the model to select from an observation operates on exactly the machinery that makes it copy one", the conversational slice having taken refusal.say from 172/162 to 30 of 270.

tool.copy_wide rising argues against a general collapse of deference, so this reads as one seed rather than a trend. It is not clean, and at 260M it is the first thing to look at. Two seeds cannot tell a wobble from a trend, which is the twenty-third finding's shape and is a limit of this arm rather than a result from it.

Everything else held

temporal.copy 353/353, temporal.route 399/400, schema.conform 191-195/200, refusal.say 267-268/270, chat.engage 100/100, tool.selection, turn2.first and turn2.second all overlapping. tool.validity rose 143-152 → 158-159.

lm web moved 0.8839-0.8867 → 0.8902-0.8923, about +0.006. Pre-registered as the donation being paid for rather than as a regression, and it landed under the +0.010 the forty-fourth finding's linear reading predicted from a 5-point cut.

What this does and does not license

The execution order says "260M only after the pilot separates from its control". It separated, on all three rows, with the corpus's largest single behavioural defect collapsing. That is the bar.

What it does not license is reading 34-39/200 as an extraction rate. The row is an upper bound and says so in its own docstring; three tightenings were tried against it and rejected in the fifty-fifth finding. What moved is movement.

The open question this arm creates rather than answers: is the abstention rate a property of the class balance or of the model? The balance was pre-registered at 50/50 before the slice existed, so changing it is a decision to be taken out loud rather than a knob to turn after seeing a scorecard.
