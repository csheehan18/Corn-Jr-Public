The passage reader ships: 389 of 721 right, up 107

The result, as the finding states it

The reader head ships, and the runtime reads what the replay said it would to the passage

What it cost

0 s of new scoring on record; it reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m_v4_ft4g). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

Run 2026-09-03 on the frozen corn_jr_260m_v4_ft4g trunk. No weight of the trunk moves, no second checkpoint is loaded, corn/extract.py's reduce and grounded, corn/gate.py, every scorer and every frozen eval row are untouched. This is PLAN-EXTRACTION-READER.md R3 — wiring, guards, verification — and it contains no new experiment.

The eighty-sixth finding measured a reader on Population A by replay: a harvest of spans and margins, replies rendered by the probe, reduce called directly. What it could not say is whether the runtime reads the same way, because between that harvest and a live turn sit harness/backends/read.py, corn.extract.read_passage's own cut and prompt build, the trunk run live in fp32 rather than replayed off an fp16 store, and a second reduce call for the fallback. This file closes each of those and then reads the whole thing end to end.

What ships

span head checkpoints/reader_built_s1.pt where the answer is verifier head checkpoints/nullpr_s1.pt whether it is spoken extent rule d = 2.0, radius 3 where the span ends reduce hybrid (finding 84) how N windows become one threshold -2.02 when he speaks at all

corn/reader.py is new and holds all five: SpanHead (the built arm's pair scorer — pointer term, LayerNorm per layer chunk, width embedding, bilinear product, hidden 512 over the concatenated block outputs at layers 8, 12 and 16), VerifierHead (the pair-rows null head — the say marker's row, the argmax pair's own two rows, the three standardised scalars), extend, and HeadReader, which is a run in read_passage's sense: N prompts in, N worker-shaped replies out. harness/backends/read.py gains Reader(reader="head") and the hybrid's second reduce call; harness/serverconfig.py gains reader, reader_threshold and reader_extent_d. generate is still the default.

Two owner decisions are recorded as decisions, not as readings.

  • The threshold protocol is the bar's own shape — the t that maximises right on the training fold among those holding that fold's share of 200 correct silences — and not the joint optimum every earlier Population A number was quoted at. The eighty-sixth finding is why this is decidable rather than a preference: the joint optimum sits in a band up to 158 thresholds wide across which right and correct silence trade by up to thirty passages, so joint does not choose. The two cross-validated fold values are -1.980 / -2.061 and the shipped constant is their mean, -2.02, the way train_null_head.py ships a frozen file's mean.
  • Seed 1's heads ship. Seed 3 read higher on Population A (400 right at 201 silent against seed 1's 389 at 199) and Population A is the population the arm is measured on, so choosing the seed by that number would be selecting on the test population. Seed 1 is the seed the workstream ran first and the one whose verifier bar (f) passes on both files.

The one row that differs did not move its verdict. extract.span reads pass 97 of 200 on both cards; what changed is the why breakdown, because the scorer gained two reporting categories in an earlier session and 49 wrong_span is now 34 wrong_span + 10 near_miss_clip + 5 near_miss_det. The eight new rows are evals/'s own growth since that card was written — docs/ENVIRONMENT.md's known confound, that a card written after an eval rebuild carries rows the ones before it do not.

And the plan's expectation that the extract.* rows would move as a new column beside the old is wrong, for a reason worth writing down: the scorecard has no reader in its path at all. corn/evals.py scores single-window items by decoding the trunk directly; it never calls read_passage, never builds a Reader, and importing it loads corn, corn.evals and corn.temporal and nothing else — no corn.reader, no harness. So there is no --reader head column to write on the card, the frozen rows are invariant to this session by construction, and the population that can see the arm is Population A, which (b) reads. If the head is ever to appear on a card, the card needs a whole-passage row it does not have.

(e) The guards

scripts/check_extract.py grows a boundary-reader section, and every check in it runs with no checkpoint anywhere near it:

  • The ports are the trainer's, score for score. corn/ may not import scripts/ (rule 29), so corn/reader.py carries copies of two heads and five helpers; the guard loads CachedHead(built)'s and NullHead's own state_dict into them on canned states and asserts the outputs are bit-identical, the pair enumeration equal tensor for tensor, the three scalars equal, and the two state dicts key for key the same. Same for the token map: corn.reader.token_bounds and window_region against corn/spangen.py's on a prompt whose window carries a quote and a backslash, and corn.reader.bucket against harness.backends.read.bucket. This is corn/gate.py's arrangement and it is why the copies are safe to have.
  • The extent rule on hand cases: a longer span within d is spoken; one that scores past d away — the extension that walks into the next entity — is refused; equal-length extensions tie to the smaller a; nothing past three rows is reachable; d = 0 is the argmax. And the rule against probe_extent_frozen.extend, the function the eighty-fifth and eighty-sixth findings measured, on five canned score patterns: same span every time.
  • The threshold's direction: a window exactly at -2.02 speaks, one a hair below it declines and the passage abstains with the runtime's own decline behind it.
  • The hybrid fallback fires on the disagreement reason and on nothing else: two admitted windows extracting different grounded values fall back to the surest one; a passage nothing was extracted from still abstains; an agreed read never reaches the fallback.
  • Flag off is the path that shipped — not "similar": Reader's own call and a direct read_passage return the same (answer, reason) tuple for the same canned worker, and with reader="generate" the head is never constructed at all.
  • The decline on every refused window: all 200 unanswerable items of evals/extract.jsonl pass score_extract_abstain under decline_text(question, window).
  • The constants are held equal: ServerConfig().reader_threshold and .reader_extent_d against corn/reader.py's, the way gate_threshold already is, plus the three nonsense configs validate() must refuse.
  • Layering: corn/reader.py imports corn.gate and corn.toolgen and nothing else from the package, both inside the runtime closure, so the reader moves with the harness when the repo splits and drags no generator with it. scripts/check_layering.py is green.

scripts/check_reader_popa.py is (a) as a guard, and is check_all.py's sixty-seventh — the count was 66. The full sweep reads 585 s over 67 guards, 91 s of it this one, and three guards fail: check_churn.py, check_copyable.py and check_social.py. All three fail identically at HEAD (b72ad908) in a clean worktree with the same messages and counts, so none of them is this session's: check_churn's is the same extract.span_tight re-derivation that (d) sees as a new row, and the other two belong to the slice work another session has in flight. Reported here rather than left for the next reader to attribute.

Limits

One trunk, one seed of the shipped heads, fp32. Population A is nq-dev's own paragraphs and the questions are NQ's; the live session is fourteen questions and is not a population. The verifier's training saw the fold heads' argmax rows and at inference it reads the deployed head's, which is the stacking rule's usual cost, accepted rather than removed (finding 86's limits). The threshold is a constant of a checkpoint and a population and not of the model: the next trunk gets its own scripts/probe_reader_passages.py run. (a)'s tolerance is a reading on this trunk and this harvest and would have to be re-read on another. Nothing here measures packs, which is where the extraction gains were expected to transfer and where no reader number exists yet.
