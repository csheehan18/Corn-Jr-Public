Training on whole passages lifts extraction from 54% to 71%

The result, as the finding states it

A rank-32 adapter on the trunk, reading each passage whole under causal attention, reads 513 and 510 of 721 on two seeds against the shipped 389 and 388, and the bidirectional flip without an adaptation phase reads 267 after one epoch, under the frozen reader it was meant to beat

What it cost

0 s of new scoring on record; it reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m_v4_ft4g). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

scripts/train_reader_whole.py, 2026-09-04/05. Two seeds of the arm that counts, same recipe, each at its own article split and read against its own shipped harvest; one epoch of the one that does not. corn/reader.py, corn/model.py and the conversational checkpoint are untouched: the adapter and the attention flip are bound inside the training process, the base weights are held by reference and asserted to take no gradient, and nothing in corn/ was edited. evals/results/whole_causal_s{1,2}.json, {reader,pairs}_whole_causal_s{1,2}_popa.json, the epoch-0 reads whole_{causal,bidir}_ep0_s1.json and their harvests; checkpoints/reader_whole_causal_s{1,2}.pt (gitignored, with per-epoch files).

1. The bidirectional arm first, because it went first and it failed

The plan's breakthrough variant is is_causal=False on the reading forward. Run first, seed 1:

epoch 0 held-out pooled 0.3522 nulls 0.8034 positives 0.1584 exact-pair (null out) 0.1958 Population A, bar protocol, extent on, epoch-0 checkpoint: right 267 wrong 258 silent 196 | u.silent 193 att 0.728 acc|att 0.509 paired vs shipped: right -122 (lost 177, won 55, p = 4e-16) gold in the ten 472, ranked first 193 (shipped: ranked first 494) species: AGGREGATION 64 -> 13, REFUSED_RIGHT 78 -> 15, RIVAL 63 -> 210

The whole-passage mechanism worked and the reader did not. The vote losses vanished as the plan said they would, and what replaced them is a picker that finds the passage's entities and confuses them (RIVAL 210): another actor from the same film, a date near the right date. A causally pretrained trunk read with full attention has every state perturbed by keys it never attended, and a rank-32 adapter did not rebuild them in one epoch. The run was stopped at epoch 1 to spend the card on the control, which is the decision this finding rests on; its epochs 1 and 2 are unread.

The one passage of Population A whose prompt exceeds 512 tokens is silent and counted (popa_silent_unread 1); it is answerable, so it is one of the 82.

4. Three replay checks made before any of this ran, all null

On the shipped heads' RANK_2_10 windows (the gold in the ten and not first), over pairs_built_s{1,2,3}_popa.json: the winner sits later in the window than the gold on 38 / 45 / 40% and earlier on 45 / 42 / 44%; gold and winner contain a question content word equally often (39 vs 41, 55 vs 57); neither is systematically longer. The pick's error on the frozen states is not a shallow feature, which is consistent with what moved it being the weights and not another read of them.

5. Deviations from the pre-registration, and costs

  • The product term is projected first. PairHead reads C (h_i * h_j) on the raw 3,072-dim rows; with the trunk in the graph that filled the card at step ~100 and thrashed the allocator (no step logged in ten minutes). The head here reads C ((P h_i) * (Q h_j)) at width 512, the same low-rank family, six times narrower on the pair axis. Peak memory 5.1 GB.
  • W1 was not run first. The plan sequences the frozen whole-passage read before the adapter; §8 allows starting W2 on §0.4's argument, and findings 107–109 had already made it. --frozen exists in the script and is unrun; the causal-adapter epoch-0 read is the nearest thing to W1's number on the record.
  • Seed 1 was first read at 505, and that number was a chimera. The per-epoch record held head.state_dict(), a live view of the weights, so when seed 1 selected epoch 1 the in-process final read used epoch 1's adapter with epoch 2's head. Found when a re-harvest from the disk checkpoint (serialised at epoch 1, correct) differed on 124 passages; the record now copies. Every seed-1 number here is from the disk checkpoint. Seed 2 selected its last epoch and was unaffected; the fold arms of finding 113 had the same mix and were re-read from disk.
  • The bidirectional arm has one seed and one epoch. Its number is a reason the control was run, not a result about the flip.
  • Costs, as measured: first bidirectional attempt lost to the memory thrash (~15 min); bidirectional epoch 0 ~23 min; causal seed 1 ~80 min including an epoch-1 slowdown from a Population A harvest run beside it (do not do that — 195 s instead of 40 s for the harvest and ~5 min lost on the run); causal seed 2 ~67 min on an idle card; items cache 46 s once, reused.

6. What this does not say, and what is next

It does not say the flip is dead: --mntp-steps adds an adaptation phase — masked next-token prediction under full attention through the frozen lm_head, LLM2Vec's remedy — and is unrun. It does not ship: the port is a guarded causal argument and adapter loading in corn/model.py, a whole path in HeadReader, and check_model.py's byte-identity guard with the adapter off, as PLAN-READER-WHOLE.md §4.1 and §6 specify. And the frozen-eval regression rows (extract.jsonl tight / abstain through this head) were not read.

7. On disk

scripts/train_reader_whole.py (new): --bidirectional, --frozen, --mntp-steps, --resume (per-epoch files carry the optimiser from the causal run's epoch 0 onward), --load for a replay harvest, writes both harvest shapes and prints the paired bar-protocol read. Items cache at F:/corn-jr-data/states/whole_items.pkl (87k prompts tokenised and aligned, regenerable in 46 s).
