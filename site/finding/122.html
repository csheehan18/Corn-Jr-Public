The second social turn installs and lifts the first

The result, as the finding states it

The second social turn installs and lifts the first: declared abstention reads 111 of 112, the consolation row 11 of 28, nothing reissues under a social history, the turn-2 rows do not move, and the one cost is that the router now speaks on questions about his life and half of what it says is SODA's

What it cost

9.7 min of scoring on the 3 cards first cited here (corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social7, corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social7__historygrid-0905ctl, corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social7__toolgrid). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

Why a second turn lifts the first is the shape: 905 of the 1,821 social transcripts now carry two social exchanges, so the payload holds 2,726 social moves answered without a call where arm 5b's held 1,821, on the same token share — and the second exchange sits where the corpus never showed one, a <|say|> followed by another social <|user|>, which is what the declared-social context looks like from the inside of a conversation rather than from its first line.

4. The one cost, and it is decision 8's

social.self rose 13 -> 18, and the species under it moved:

social.self why arm 5b called_web_search 31 spoken leak 1 (life) arm 7 called_web_search 16 spoken leak 9 (digits 5, i work 2, history class 1, names 1) social.self_heldout arm 5b web 7 leak 1 arm 7 web 3 leak 4

The router now speaks on twice as many questions about his life, and about half of what it says when it speaks is a SODA life — "I'm 23", "I work ...". No second-turn transcript carries a life claim (every reply passed life_claim before it was trained); what the class taught is the willingness to speak on a short second-person question, which is finding 88's mechanism a third time, and the sentence he speaks is the one SODA gives him. At runtime the 31 trained carriers never reach the router (4c's hatch answers them from the pool), so the live exposure is the 16 held-out phrasings, four of which now get a life instead of a search. That is exactly §10 decision 8's residue — the unguarded routed say — larger than it was, and the arm makes the case for the router-side rule the decision names (a question with you/your and no request) rather than for a payload, which §3 rule 1 forbids. Recorded, not repaired.

5. Reading

§6c's first reading, with a footnote that is not this arm's to fix. The second social turn installs, lifts the single-turn rows with it, spends nothing on the gates, and the history does not leak. Arm 7 is the best checkpoint on the record on every social row and the base arm 8 stacks on. The footnote is social.self's species, which is a runtime question (decision 8) that every arm from here on will make more urgent, because every one of them makes him more willing to speak.

Limits

One seed. The rows that moved most are 112 items; the second-turn rows are 12 and 36; the self species is 9 lines of 47. The reader proxy is at a fixed threshold.
