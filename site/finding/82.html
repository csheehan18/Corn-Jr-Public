Scored fairly, the boundary model trails plain generation

The result, as the finding states it

The extent headroom was containment, the real repair is span_loose - span, and the span head is worse than generation once the column can tell a region from an answer

What it cost

0 s of new scoring on record; it reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m_v4). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

One read-only replay on the frozen checkpoints/corn_jr_260m_v4/ckpt.pt, run 2026-09-01 over generations already on disk. No training, no GPU, no forward pass. scripts/probe_recut.py, record evals/results/recut.json, replicated on both evals/extract.jsonl and the article-decontaminated evals/extract_phrase.jsonl.

What was being tested

docs/READ-COMPARISON.md identified the largest headroom anyone had found: of the 65 attempted-and-wrong items on the frozen eval, 27 already share a gold content word — right neighbourhood, wrong extent — against 38 disjoint. A perfect re-cut reads span 121/200, joint 242 against greedy's 215, acc | att 0.591 → 0.761. It was the only route on the record to the conditional-accuracy wall the eighty-first finding's thread never moved.

The design under test was a re-cut: start from the string the generator actually said, locate it in the window, change its extent once. It fixes the two things that killed the previous designs by construction — it never fires on a window the generator declined, so the abstain row is preserved without a gate (probe_spanhead's defect); and it makes one decision, not N sequential ones (probe_shrink's defect).

The central result, and it is a control rather than a bar

extract.span credits containment, so padding a span with its neighbours buys the row without repairing anything. The control is degenerate: blindly extend every located assertion by the full (L, R), no trigger, no selection, no model.

On span the blind pad equals the oracle at every width, and at (5,8) it reads 131 / joint 252 / acc | att 0.824 — better than the "perfect re-cut" the design was justified by. The 122 / 243 / 0.767 target, and the 121 / 242 / 0.761 in READ-COMPARISON.md that produced it, are padding. That number is mine and it is retracted here.

_length_verdict does not catch it: RECITED_FRACTION is 0.8, so a reply must reach 80% of the window's content words before it counts as recited, and a nine-word pad of a 36-word window is nowhere near it. The recitation guard is calibrated for whole-window recitation and is blind to local padding.

The column that works, and the number it gives

exact — credited by score_extract_span and asserting nothing beyond the gold: every novel content word (not question, not source, not FRAME) is a gold word. It is score_extract_span's own near_miss_clip test run as a requirement rather than as a forgiveness, and padding cannot buy it.

Greedy reads 94 / 93 / 89 on span / sized / exact — a generated sentence averages 4.3 content words against a 2.3-word gold, so containment and identity nearly coincide and the three columns agree. Only span-emitting policies come apart on them.

On exact the honest re-cut ceiling is +16 items, not +27. Split by what greedy did:

Not one of the 36 wrong_span failures is reachable by re-cutting its own assertion. And extract.span_loose — strict passes plus both near-miss buckets — already reads 110 against span's 94 on every card. The repair ceiling is span_loose - span, a quantity that has been printed beside the row all along. The other 11 of the 27 were containment.

The partition itself is sound and turns on exactly one item: FRAME-stripped it is 27 / 38, and counting FRAME words as overlap gives 28 / 37, the extra being extract-0087 whose entire overlap with its gold is the preposition "of". Locating both strings in the window confirms it — 25 of the 27 are at distance 0, all 11 near_miss_clip and all 5 near_miss_det at distance 0, and 23 of 36 wrong_span more than five words away.

One rule survives, and it buys the class the repo calls orthography

Model-free and structural. DET prepends the window's own determiner when the assertion is immediately preceded by the/a/an.

DET's zero breakage is structural, not lucky: determiners are FRAME words, so prepending one cannot add a novel content word and cannot cost exact. It replicates on the decontaminated phrase eval — fires 12, fixes 3, breaks 0, joint(exact) 58 → 61. Pooled across both evals it is 8 fixed, 0 broken, exact binomial p = 0.0078.

And it recovers the class the scorer deliberately declines to credit. score_extract_span's own note: "NQ writes golds like 'the Ramones' and 'The aorta' where the article is orthography, not content." The one repair that survives its control converts near_miss_det and nothing else. NAME and NUM, which would reach the 8 real near_miss_clip items, break more than they fix.

ftxclause reads the best span of any arm and emits almost no answers. Its replies average 17.96 content words against the frozen model's 4.93, its median reply is 17 for a gold averaging 2.3, and what it emits is the window's sentence:

gold Jourdan Miller reply The winner was 19-year-old Jourdan Miller from Bend, Oregon.

gold July 4, 1776 reply On July 4, 1776, the Second Continental Congress, meeting in Philadelphia, declared the independence of "the United States of America" in the Declaration of Independence.

Its span_loose is 100, equal to its span, where its own control ftxmask reads 114 against 96 — a diagnostic signature, because a reply long enough to carry the whole sentence can no longer clip the gold, so both near-miss buckets empty into the strict row.

The risk was named in advance and the chosen instrument could not see it. configs/mix_v4_ft_xclause.py read (d) watches extract.brevity for exactly this — "sentence replies are longer ... the model's replies on the frozen eval may lengthen" — and names the failure state, "Regression (abstain or brevity falls apart)". extract.brevity reads 383/400, a mild-looking 17-item cost. Its test is len(said) < n_window, and the mean window is 36.2 content words, so a 17.96-word reply passes it by a factor of two. The guard is a whole-window guard, like RECITED_FRACTION, and both are blind at sentence scale. The same config also states outright that "a clause reply that carries the gold plus its sentence still passes strict containment" — and then selects the more permissive span_loose as its primary.

Nothing was banked on the arm: the eighty-first finding's thread had already discarded it on decontamination (openers +0, abstention 37/53 → 13/53 at p = 3e-06). What this adds is the mechanism. It was not only a willingness knob; it changed the shape of the emission from an answer to a sentence, and the row that made it look like the best arm on the record is that change being credited.

It also corrects one cell of my own invariant table: ftxclause's 0.602 is not the top of the band, it is an arm that stopped answering. On sized | att the four answering arms read 0.570 – 0.593, a 2.3-point band as before, and the apparent counterexample was recitation.

What it settles

  • The extent headroom is 16 items, not 27, it is span_loose - span, and it was already on the card.
  • extract.span is not a safe row for any policy that emits window text. probe_shrink.py discovered this about itself and built answer_sized; probe_spanhead.py never did, and neither did the re-cut framing. probe_recut.answer_sized is probe_shrink's, unchanged, so the files agree.
  • wrong_span is not a repair problem. Zero of 36 are reachable by re-cutting the generator's own assertion, which retires the "right neighbourhood, wrong extent" reading of that bucket entirely.
  • The trigger remains the blocker, and the structural agreement filter does not avoid it. The verifier question is untouched.
  • acc | att did not move. The one policy that survives its control takes it from 0.591 to 0.629 on the frozen eval and 0.453 to 0.509 on the phrase eval, entirely on determiners.
  • extract.brevity needs recalibrating. It compares the reply to the window when the question is whether the reply is an answer. Against the gold rather than the window it would have read ftxclause at 21/166 instead of 383/400 and the arm's shape change would have been visible on the card. This is a one-line change to a shipped row and is the cheapest thing on this list.

What shipped from it

extract.span_tight — the third rung of a ladder read off one decode:

extract.span_loose strict pass, or a gold missing only its article, or a gold stated in part extract.span the gold's content words are in the reply extract.span_tight ... and the reply is answer-sized

Three things were considered and rejected, and the reasoning is the finding as much as the row is.

  • Mutating extract.brevity instead. That was the recommendation this work started from — "one line, on a shipped row" — and it is wrong. Changing a threshold in place silently rewrites every card in evals/results/, which is the argument score_extract_span makes for its own frozen criterion, and extract.span_loose is the precedent for the companion instead. Brevity stays at 383/400 and its docstring now records why it cannot see any of this.
  • Scoring the new row on both classes, as brevity does. There is no gold to size against on an unanswerable item, and the canonical decline "I couldn't find anything useful here." is seven content words, so the natural cap of 6 would fail 116 of 200 correct refusals. It is also unnecessary: recitation is not a miss sentence, so extract.abstain already reads it directly — ftxclause 121 → 66 with 22 explicit recited.
  • Unifying probe_reduce.is_span. The claim that three probes held one rule in three copies was wrong and is corrected in answer_sized's docstring: probe_shrink.answer_sized and probe_recut.sized were identical and now import the shipped one, but is_span sizes the gold as a FRAME-stripped set and disagrees on 26 of the 200 golds (the Ramones budgets 6 there and 8 here). Measured, it is invisible on every arm that emits answers — 93 / 96 / 94 / 65 either way — and moves only ftxclause, 21 against 16. Left alone rather than unified, because its numbers are on disk and the choice decides nothing.

The cards in evals/results/ are not backfilled; the row appears on the next run, and the five values above are computed offline from the retained generations and recorded here.

What it does not settle

The 8 near_miss_clip items are real repairs with no trigger, and nothing here shows one cannot exist — only that neither the span head's geometry nor the two typed structural rules is it. And exact is stricter than anything the card reports; it is a control column, not a candidate row, and the three are printed together for that reason.
