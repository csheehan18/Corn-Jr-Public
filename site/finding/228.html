The fields stall is not the word-pieces, and not hidden in him

The result, as the finding states it

Three reads of why the 20M answers 76 to 78 of the ruler's 141 fields where a count of the same text answers 95. Split by piece count, his shortfall from the book is the same whether the gold label and the phrase's content words are one piece or several (-4.5 and +0.6 points where 15 was asked): the cut into pieces is not why. A book counted over his own 8,192 pieces, the ruler never read, answers 83 fields at the registered window and so does one tied table of 512 numbers a piece, the shape of his word table, where his own trained table answers 58 to 61 by the same read — a direction on both rows, and 83 is inside the spread of seven 20M runs (76 to 87). A ridge reader from his state to the book's 512-number home, fitted on words the ruler never uses, answers 70 and 72 fields at the middle of the net and 77 and 80 at its best depth: what he holds is about what his framed answer already gives, so it is not sitting in him unread. One thing nobody predicted: the piece book answers more fields the further it looks — 83, 86, 86 and 91 at windows of 5, 10, 20 and 40 pieces — while its wishes fall (33, 30, 30, 28 of the first 96), and the wide-window knowledge does not squeeze (79 tied at 512 numbers).

The fields are far-apart knowledge: each step out adds items (83, 86, 86, 91), the wishes go the other way, and what the wide window adds needs the whole table (91 whole, 79 tied at 512 numbers a piece, where the narrow window loses nothing to the same squeeze).

What it licenses

By the pre-registration this is its fourth outcome with two directions in it: X fails, R fails, B1 and B2 are directions.

  • The pieces are not why he stalls. Whole-word tokens, or a whole-word signal at a word's last piece, are not the fix for this.
  • It is not in him unread. Finding 223 said so with a cosine and this says so with a trained reader on words it never met. On the fields the 20M's shortfall is knowledge he does not hold in any form read so far.
  • A counted table in his word table's shape is a real but modest start. It holds 83 where his own holds 58 to 61, and 83 is a good 20M, not a better one. The one-hour arm that starts him from it is a test of sooner (the ruler at 10% and 25% of the run), not of higher.
  • The lesson stands, and it has a shape now: distance. The count answers more fields the further it looks, and he is paid only for the next piece. Where he misses, three separately trained 20Ms give the noun's stock label — the phrase a few words back has not moved him. This is elimination plus a clue, not a measurement of him.

What it does not settle. How far back he actually reads: his loss with his context cut to the last 5, 10, 20 and 40 pieces, beside the 260M's, is the direct read, and nothing here made it. The word book by window on the fields (finding 221 printed its windows for the wishes). Whether a table started from the count stays there through training, or is pulled back to what next-piece prediction wants.

Amended 2026-11-14 — the direct read was made

"How far back he actually reads" was read in finding 232, on 4,800 held-out meetings of pairs the book ties and pairs it does not, beside the 260M. He does not fall short from afar: a tied word 9 to 16 words back is worth 0.28 and 0.29 nats to the 20Ms and 0.33 to the 260M, and with the context cut the pieces 11 to 40 back are worth the same two thirds of the 260M's that the pieces 2 to 10 back are. So the fourth licence above — "the lesson stands, and it has a shape now: distance" — holds for the count (the text's ties barely fade over thirty words, which is why a wider window answers more fields) and not for him: distance is not where he falls short of a model that reads the fields. Where he does is two to four words back.
