The right answer is among the top ten on 90% of misses

The result, as the finding states it

The gold is among the head's own ten on nine tenths of the residue, so the ninety-third finding's fifty-eight per cent ceiling was an artefact of conditioning on admission, and perfect window choice with a perfect pick from those ten reads eighty-three per cent

scripts/probe_reader_rank.py and scripts/audit_refused_wrong.py, 2026-09-03. Replay only — no GPU, no decode, no weight read, no tokenizer, and no new harvest — over nullpr_s{1,2}_popa.json joined to pairs_built_s{1,2}_popa.json. evals/results/rank_s{1,2}.json and evals/results/refused_wrong_audit.jsonl.

The pass that was going to be run already exists on disk. The plan for this session was one no-grad inference pass per seed to write the head's top ten pairs per window. pairs_built_s{1,2}_popa.json has carried them since 2026-09-02 — top_k = 10, span text, score and token offsets, plus the extent rule's around grid — for the same 1,003 passages and the same 3,893 windows. The join is checked and not assumed: ids align, window counts align, and the argmax span text matches on 3,893 of 3,893 windows on both seeds. So the whole of what follows is arithmetic, and no GPU was spent.

1. The gold is in the head's ten on nine tenths of the residue

For every answerable failure, where the gold sits among the ten candidates the head ranked on that passage's best gold-bearing window, admitted or not. score_extract_tight on the rendered reply decides "is the gold", which is the ninety-third finding's own test.

REFUSED_RIGHT's RANK_1 is by construction — the species is defined by the head's span on that window being the gold — and AGGREGATION's nearly so. The load-bearing rows are the other four, and they are RANK_2_10: 152 of 332 and 166 of 312 of the residue are passages where the gold is a candidate the head ranked second to tenth on a window it had.

This does not contradict the ninety-third finding's §2, and the difference between them is the result. That read said the gold is reachable in the ten on 26.8% and 31.4% of the residue — but it conditioned on admitted windows. This one conditions on the best gold-bearing window whether or not it was admitted. Both are true, and the gap between 27% and 89% is the size of what admission is hiding. The candidate list is not the bottleneck; getting the window past the threshold is.

2. So the fifty-eight per cent ceiling was an artefact, and the real one is eighty-three

The ninety-third finding put ORACLE_PICK at 58.1%/59.0% and concluded that "the entire choose better among what he produced family is capped twenty-six points short" of 85%. ORACLE_PICK fixes the pick on admitted windows; ORACLE_WINDOW fixes the window and leaves the pick. Neither is the ceiling on everything downstream of candidate ranking. Their conjunction is.

JOINT (finding 93's point) seed 1 seed 2 baseline 367 50.9% 368 51.0% ORACLE_PICK 419 58.1% +52 425 58.9% +57 ORACLE_WINDOW 460 63.8% +93 442 61.3% +74 ORACLE_JOINT 597 82.8% +230 584 81.0% +216 GUARD (ECHO_BLOCKED 22) 699 96.9%

SHIPPED seed 1 seed 2 baseline 389 53.9% 409 56.7% ORACLE_PICK 449 62.3% +60 493 68.4% +84 ORACLE_WINDOW 444 61.6% +55 449 62.3% +40 ORACLE_JOINT 559 77.5% +170 573 79.5% +164

This section's two claims are withdrawn where they were made, 2026-09-03, and the ninety-sixth finding replaces them. ORACLE_JOINT adds the best gold-bearing window to the real mask and leaves every rival window in the vote, so it is not "the ceiling on everything downstream of candidate ranking" — admitting only that window reads 93.2% and 92.9% at SHIPPED, fourteen points above its 77.5% and 79.5%. And 58% is not an artefact: it is a correctly computed oracle over a different conditioning set, which is what §1's own paragraph about 27% and 89% says and what this section's heading overstated. The heading and the file name are left as they were written, because a finding is a record of what was claimed and this note is the correction. Everything below is unchanged and reproduces.

ORACLE_PICK and ORACLE_WINDOW reproduce the ninety-third finding exactly at its own operating point — 419/460 and 425/442, to the passage — which is what licenses reading ORACLE_JOINT off the same code.

82.8% and 81.0%, against the owner's 85% = 613 of 721. ORACLE_JOINT reads 597 and 584: 16 and 29 passages short of the target, not 194. The two oracles are strongly super-additive (+230 against +52 and +93) because each one alone is blocked by the other's failure — fixing the pick on a window that was never admitted buys nothing, and admitting the right window buys nothing if the head then speaks its rival.

The caveat the ninety-third finding wrote for ORACLE_WINDOW applies here unchanged and is not smaller for being repeated. An unanswerable passage has no gold-bearing window, so the force fires on the answerable half only and the correct-silence column is unchanged by construction, not by merit; a real window scorer would lift the attempt rate on both halves. ORACLE_JOINT runs at attempt rate 0.972 and 0.969. It is a ceiling and it is not a forecast.

3. The outranking signal survives its control

The ninety-third finding's §5 — a no-gold window outranks every gold-bearing one on 2.8% of passages he gets right against 47.8% of the residue — is near-tautological as stated, because a passage he gets right usually got there through an admitted gold-bearing window. The control is tight_on: whether the head's span on the best gold window is the gold at all, which is a property of the reading and not of the outcome. At SHIPPED, over the 580 answerable passages carrying both kinds of window:

seed 1 seed 2 all 148 of 580 25.5% 129 of 580 22.2% he got it right 10 of 300 3.3% 18 of 313 5.8% residue 138 of 280 49.3% 111 of 267 41.6%

P(outrank | tight) 72 of 412 17.5% 57 of 398 14.3% P(outrank | not tight) 76 of 168 45.2% 72 of 182 39.6%

tight & right 10 of 294 3.4% 18 of 306 5.9% tight & residue 62 of 118 52.5% 39 of 92 42.4% not tight & right 0 of 6 0.0% 0 of 7 0.0% not tight & residue 76 of 162 46.9% 72 of 175 41.1% Fisher, right vs residue within tight p = 5.9e-30 / 1.4e-15

It does not collapse. Within the tight set — where reading the window is not the problem — the split is 3.4% against 52.5% and 5.9% against 42.4%, as large as the uncontrolled contrast and significant on both seeds.

Two things the control does show that the original read could not. First, outranking and misreading co-occur: 45.2% against 17.5% and 39.6% against 14.3%, so cross-window ranking failure and within-window reading failure are correlated and are not two independent budgets to be added. Second, the residual structural link is still there and is named rather than waved at — a right verdict does not strictly require the gold window to outrank every empty one (under agree several windows vote), which is why the cell is 3.4% and not 0%, but it comes close, and the honest unconditional number remains 25.5% and 22.2%.

4. There is no memorised answer prior

For every RIVAL and every REFUSED_WRONG/wrong_span passage, how often the span he spoke is a short answer in data/raw/spans_nq.jsonl — 82,932 training questions of which 58,509 carry a short answer, zero id overlap with Population A, checked — against how often the gold is.

53.8% and 51.3% against a null of 50%, p = 0.62 and 0.91. The head does not speak the commoner training answer. A RIVAL is not a memorised frequent answer displacing a rare gold.

Seed 2's REFUSED_WRONG cell reads 74.1% at p = 0.019 and is not a result. Seed 1's same cell reads 53.6% at p = 0.85, and the two seeds' RIVAL cells disagree in sign (54.1% against 39.2%). One cell of six clearing 0.05 on one seed with the other seed flat is what noise looks like at n = 27, and the house rule against single-seed numbers is why it is printed rather than claimed. The tie column is large because most golds are not training answers at all.

The ninety-fourth finding put the hand lexicon on the admission decision. This puts it inside the window: on RIVAL and REFUSED_WRONG windows, restrict the head's ten to spans following an anchor and take the restricted argmax. **Pre-registered before the run: worth building only if it recovers

= 25% of those windows on both seeds.** The gap counting as "follows" was

2.2% and 3.8% against a bar of 25%. Missed by an order of magnitude, at every gap. The failure is the ninety-fourth finding's, in the same shape and worse: the lexicon fires on one window in ten here against one passage in three there, because a window is a smaller target than a passage. Where it does fire it is right 21% and 32% of the time, which is again respectable and again irrelevant at that coverage. The frame is an admission signal, not an extraction prior, and §2 says admission is where the passages are anyway.

6. The label-noise floor under 85% is about a quarter, and this is a model audit

100 REFUSED_WRONG passages, random.Random(0) over the seed-1 harvest's JOINT set (136 passages; SHIPPED holds 93, too few to draw 100 from), each labelled by reading the question, the gold and the passage:

UNIQUE the gold is the answer and no other span is 77 77% EQUIVALENT the gold is right and so is another span 15 15% EXTENT right referent, arguable boundary 6 6% WRONG not the answer the passage gives 2 2%

the gold is not the unique correct span on 23 of 100

EQUIVALENT is the bulk of it and it is one shape. The question asks for a set and NQ's annotator gave one member: three meninges and the gold is dura mater; four commonwealth states and the gold is Kentucky; five Warriors championship years and the gold is 1947; four layers of the Earth "in order" and the gold is the crust. A reader naming a different correct member is scored wrong. The two WRONG cases are cleaner: the Charles Bridge passage says it was built as a bow bridge and the gold Stone is lifted out of "the Stone Bridge in Regensburg", a different bridge; the possibilism passage says Sahlins used the concept and credits the theory to Strabo.

This is a model audit and not a human one. The labels were assigned by Claude reading question, gold and passage, blind to what the head said on that passage. That removes the obvious circularity and does not remove the model-judgement one, and the boundary between EXTENT and UNIQUE in particular is a judgement call made about twenty times. The owner should spot-check 20 before this number is quoted as a data fact; audit_refused_wrong.py --check 20 prints a seeded sub-sample for it.

Spot-checked 2026-09-04 (docs/SPOT-CHECK-REFUSED-WRONG.md). A second model rater agreed with the labels above on 17 of the 20, disagreeing only toward less unique (one EQUIVALENT → WRONG, two UNIQUE → EXTENT). The owner then ruled on the five disputed or non-unique rows: agreed on one, overturned four, and every overturn moved the gold toward less unique or wrong — 169 is WRONG where the question asked for countries and the passage says 216; the FBI Director's pre-2004 superior is WRONG on a present-tense question; two when golds that add a season or a range are EXTENT because "a year is enough". So the model audit was not generous to the reader, and 23 of 100 is a floor. The fifteen agreed UNIQUE rows were not individually ruled on. Three rules from the rulings — recency on present-tense questions, a year answers when, a different quantity is wrong — are the lenient ruler's principles from here.

And it is a floor on one species, not on Population A. 23% of REFUSED_WRONG is 23% of 28% of the residue. It does not license subtracting 23% from anything, and the correct use of it is that a 100% reader does not score 100%, and nobody had measured by how much. The 22 ECHO_BLOCKED passages (699/721 = 96.9%) are the other known deduction and are disjoint from this one in kind.

What this cannot say

It cannot separate rank 11 from a gold the pair enumeration never contained. Both read as OUTSIDE_TEN, because separating them needs the tokenizer and this file loads none. It does not change §1's plurality, which is what the arm gate turned on, but "10.8% is rank > 10" is not a claim this file makes — the claim is "10.8% is not in the ten".

Every oracle in §2 is handed the answer. The ninetieth finding's locality rule had a plausible mechanism and reached a negative fraction of its bound. 83% is what perfect information buys, and the last four findings are a record of real mechanisms reaching small fractions of such numbers.

§3's control reduces the outcome-conditioning and does not eliminate it, as §3 says in its own last paragraph.

§6 is one species, one seed's harvest, one operating point and one model auditor.

Two seeds, one trunk, one population, Population A under hybrid, at both operating points, with each read's point stated.

What follows

The pre-registered gate on the two training arms did not fire, and they should not be written. The session's plan said to write Arm B1 (a bidirectional encoder between the cached states and the pair scorer) and Arm B2 (listwise passage-level training for the verifier) only if rank > 10 were the plurality of the residue on both seeds. It is 10.8% and 11.2%, and the plurality is RANK_2_10. More than that, B1's stated rationale is contradicted by §1: it was justified by the trunk being causal, so no start state sees its span or right context, which would put the gold outside the enumeration. The gold is inside the enumeration nine times in ten. Building a richer state to fix candidate generation would be aimed at 11% of the residue.

What §1 and §2 name instead is admission, and it is the same lever the ninety-third and ninety-fourth findings converged on from the other side. The gold is in the ten and the window is refused, or admitted and outranked. That is ORACLE_JOINT's +230 and it is where the ninety-fourth finding said a generated frame belongs.

Re-size the plan's arms at SHIPPED before running any of them. PLAN-READER-RESIDUE.md's four arms are sized off the JOINT table, where AGGREGATION is 31 and not 66 and REFUSED_RIGHT is 113 and not 77. A2 — the score-aware reduce, aimed at AGGREGATION — is the arm whose target doubles at the operating point that ships, and it is the one arm in the plan that costs no GPU.

Left unexplained: AGGREGATION is 55 and 51 RANK_1 at SHIPPED — passages where the head ranked the gold first on a window it admitted, and reduce returned something else. That is a larger RANK_1 population than the plan's whole AGGREGATION species at JOINT, and no read here says whether the outvoting window is a repetition of one rival or several different ones.
