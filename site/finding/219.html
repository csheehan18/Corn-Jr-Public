The reply judge · 24 October 2026

A 5M reading only conversation answers far more messages

The result, as the finding states it

The same 6.95M-parameter model, the same 130M tokens read once, with every token conversation — 55% SODA dialogue, 40% SODA exchanges in the one-off reply frame, 5% EmpatheticDialogues and short-reply OpenAssistant (research only) — answers 34 and 36 of 100 fresh hand-written one-off messages by a blind hand read, against 26 and 20 for the 5M that reads the usual mix (half web, 15% conversation): +8 and +16, 42 replies passing only under it and 18 only under the control (sign test p = 0.003), the pre-registered row R. With the judge-picked look-ahead on top it reads 41 and 41 (+7 and +5 over its own greedy, 22 to 10, p = 0.05 — L holds at seed 1, misses by 2 at seed 2), +15 and +21 over the control's greedy and +17 and +14 over the control's own look-ahead (47 to 16). It is not yet a coherent 5M by the registered bar (60 of 100 at both seeds, R+ and L+ not met). The gains are in plans (1-2 of 8 to 6-7), feelings, good news and bad news; advice, opinions and the short and vague stay where the control is. The price: narrative and web text cost twice as much (1.03 and 1.41 nats a byte against 0.49 and 0.95); held-out SODA costs less (0.456 against 0.558)

What it cost

the framed exchanges (scripts/prepare_chat_pairs.py, 6 s), the mix (44 s), the hand check of the stream, the 100-message exam, the configs and pre-registration 17:23-17:32 on 2026-10-24 (408482c); the chain 17:32-17:56, 23.7 min (two runs 273 and 290 s, two judges ~340 s each, 800 replies, contended at ~40%, a bound); the blind hand read of 800 ~15 min. ETA ~1.5 h, actual ~1.2 h.

The question (owner, 2026-10-24: "Can we do an only chat mix purely for research on this? Like a one off pure research question of can we get a coherent 5M model in one off chatting"; "should we do the judge picker combo that helped us initially just applied to this"). The control reads about 20M tokens of conversation — ~3 a parameter, once — and half its reading is web text; TinyStories (Eldan and Li 2023) found coherence under 10M parameters follows a narrow reading of the kind the model must write. Not a 1.0 mix (the 1.0 direction is broad reading, finding 185; the corpus's EmpatheticDialogues licence keeps it to research). configs/nano5m_chatonly.py's docstring is the pre-registration.

The instrument. A fresh exam (scripts/reply_exam2_messages.py): 100 everyday messages hand-written 2026-10-24 in the 148's ten kinds, no overlap with the 148 or the practice set, read by no model before. Eight versions in one blind file; findings 214-218's rule and reader. It reads harder than the practice set: the control's greedy 26 / 20 here against 31 / 33 there.

Read by hand

What chat reading fixes. Plans most: "That sounds like a great idea! I'm sure you'll love it there." to a move, "That's great! What kind of cake is it?", "Why do you want to learn to swim?" — the control answered most of these with "What? Why?" or a wrong object. Feelings and news turn the right way more often ("Oh no! I hope you're not too upset." to a phone in the bath, "I'm so sorry, Jaydon. That must have been really tough for you." to a grandad's death). A few advice replies now carry an answer ("You'll need to replace them." to smelly shoes).

What it does not. The stock pull is still there and has moved: "That's great! I'm sure you'll do great." becomes the commonest line, and it lands on bad news ("That's great news! I'm really happy for you." to a rent rise, "Oh, wow! That's amazing! How did you find it?" to losing five pounds). Short and vague messages get SODA's scene-openers — "Hi, I'm looking for a new car." to "Hello!", "So, what do you think of the new project?" to "Alright then." — because SODA's dialogues begin with a scenario, not a reply to "Meh.". Opinions get "Yes, I am." to a question about e-books. Advice is still almost empty: no reading of chat supplies how to save for a holiday.

The look-ahead on this model adds where the stock line is the miss: "Oh no! I hope you're not too upset." where greedy wrote a cheerful line, "What kind of cake?" where greedy wrote "That's great, son!". On the control it does not replicate on this exam (24 / 27 against 26 / 20, 16 to 11): the look-ahead's gain is surest where the model's candidates are good enough to choose between.

What it licenses

  • Reading is the biggest single lever found for the 5M's one-off replies. Same size, same budget: 26 / 20 to 34 / 36, and 41 / 41 with the look-ahead — +15 / +21 over the control's greedy, the largest gain of any 5M arm in findings 202-219.
  • The remaining failures are named and each has a lever: the stock line on bad news (the judge's mood read; a judge on hard negatives, or move tags), the short and vague (no such messages in SODA — a slice of short replies to short messages), the scene-opener habit (SODA's dialogue starts; exchanges cut from mid-dialogue only), and advice (knowledge: retrieval, or size).
  • Reading more of it is the next cheap read: each exchange was read once; four passes cost ~20 min a run (the 2x2 of the owner's plan).
  • For 1.0: the share and form of conversation in the mix move one-off replies at fixed size — a direction to carry to the D1 share table, read at the pilot, not a 1.0 mix (this one gives up stories and the web).

What it does not settle. More passes; the four named fixes; any size above 5M; the app's full prompt; multi-turn conversation.
