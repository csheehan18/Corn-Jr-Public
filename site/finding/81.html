The answer score measures willingness to answer, not confidence

The result, as the finding states it

The isolated score is a willingness meter and not a confidence meter, ranking windows is the one thing it buys, and every read-time route to the span is now closed

What it cost

0 s of new scoring on record; it reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m_v4). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

Eight read-only probes on the frozen checkpoints/corn_jr_260m_v4/ckpt.pt, run 2026-08-31 in one session. No training, no corpus change, no card touched; inference only, fp32, and every number below is against that one checkpoint at n=1. The thread began as a design conversation about decomposing extraction into stages a 260M model can each do, and it ends having closed every stage except the first.

scripts/probe_enumerator.py, probe_detector.py, probe_series.py, probe_scan.py, probe_scanread.py, probe_rescue.py, probe_tiebreak.py, with records in evals/results/.

The central result

The isolated ask — cut a window to one candidate, ask the ordinary extraction question — produces a score whose meaning had never been separated. Split by what the ordinary read of the same window produced, on 186 gold-bearing NQ dev windows:

comparisonAUC
correct answer against abstained0.8522
correct answer against wrong span0.5288

The score knows whether he will speak. It knows nothing about whether what he says is right. That single dissociation retroactively explains five separate failures in this session and one before it: detection reads 0.79 because detection is engagement; selection reads 0.63 and cannot beat generation; the hedge collapses because it was built on a number that predicts talking; and the seventh vehicle's PMI calibration failed because it was normalising the wrong quantity.

Everything the score is good for is downstream of that sentence.

What ranking buys, and it is real

Item-level score — the maximum isolated score over the candidate list — ranks windows. Cross-document AUC 0.7925; within one passage 0.8175, which was pre-registered to come in lower on the reasoning that windows of one article share topic, entities and register. It came in higher. Holding the question and the article fixed cancels item-level noise that a cross-document comparison carries, so ranking inside one document is this score's best case, not its worst.

Over 100 NQ dev passages, 443 windows, top-1 gold-bearing 80.0% against 44.7% chance.

Read into answers (probe_scanread.py, same forward passes for every policy):

policy answered rate reads/item random 21 21.0% 1.00 first 40 40.0% 1.00 scan 44 44.0% 1.00 readall 42 42.0% 1.59 cascade 47 47.0% 1.55 oracle 49 49.0% 1.00

Reading in score order beats reading in document order by 5 items at equal cost (1.55 reads against 1.59) — the pre-registered primary, passed at exactly its bar and therefore suggestive rather than settled. scan against first is +4 and fails its bar, so reading only the top window is not supported.

And the ceiling retires the premise. oracle is 49 and first is 40: window choice can buy 9 items of 100, because on an NQ paragraph the answer is usually near the front. The other 51 are extraction.

Every read-time route to the span is closed

Selection. Top-1 over the isolated score covers the gold 44/200 against greedy's own 94/200, on a candidate list whose covering ceiling is 117. Letting him generate is better than twice letting him choose. The enumerator was not the constraint: base-to-widened moves candidate AUC by 0.0096 against a 0.03 falsification while rivals per window go 4.80 → 9.28.

The hedge. A three-band take/hedge/quiet policy separates cleanly on detection — 84.0% / 49.0% / 18.0% carry the answer, 152 of 190 splits clearing a pre-registered 15-point bar. Joined to the retained generations it is right 19.5% end to end, and 19–24% at every split tried. The wording ladder, fixed in the file before the run, calls everything under 40% an abstain wearing a hedge's clothes and refused the sentence five times of five. And the whole band policy loses to the generator it wraps: best joint 198 against greedy's 215.

The rescue. The largest single failure on a window that provably contains the answer is that he declines it — 51 of 186, against 40 wrong_span, 9 invented, 8 near-miss. On those windows isolation endorses the gold 15.0% and rivals 1.1%: he refuses the window and refuses every candidate in it standing alone. The control is what makes that readable — the same procedure on windows he answered correctly reads 96.4% gold / 12.7% rival.

The tiebreak. On wrong_span isolation does discriminate — gold 57.1%, rival 19.5%, a 37.6-point gap on the bucket AUC 0.5288 says is invisible — but unique is 0, so it narrows ten candidates to about three and stops. Two tiebreaks over 33 ambiguous windows, both below the null model:

group n firstwin random perm toggle wrong_span 33 33.3% 39.4% 20.7% 24.2% ok 114 57.9% 36.8% 92.2% 60.5%

The perm dissociation is the finding and it is not what the column was built to show. Permutation majority recovers the gold 92.2% where the ordinary read is already right and 20.7% where it is wrong. Reordering the window's sentences does not perturb his answer, it confirms it. So whatever the sixty-sixth finding's "first value of the right type" describes, it is not a positional bias reordering can cancel — the error is a stable property of the (question, window) pair, and perm underperforms chance precisely by reproducing his own mistake four times.

That closes a family rather than a probe. Majority-over-perturbations, self-consistency and sample-and-vote all assume the error is variance to be averaged out; here it is signal. The sixty-eighth finding said this from the seed side and this says it from the input side.

What he actually does, and the shape hypothesis

Handed a window containing the answer he is right 78/186 = 41.9%. Restricted to windows whose answer is a short typed entity the enumerator can reach, 56/101 = 55.4%.

The gap between those two numbers is the one live lead. Against the ok group, the abstained group is enumerable 39% against 72%, carries answer_type: other at 37% against 19%, and its golds run 2.14 words against 1.85. The enumerator hunts capitalised runs and digits; he emits short typed spans. Both instruments fail on the same property — the answer is a phrase rather than an entity. 31 of the 51 abstentions and 19 of the 40 wrong_span items sit there: 50 of 108 failures.

That reframes the abstentions. A model declining because the answer is not the shape it emits is doing something score_extract_abstain credits, and it is a statement about what the corpus taught rather than about what the runtime hands over.

Corrections to the record

STATUS.md's "an overlapping candidate is enumerable 175/200 but the exact gold only 109/200" does not reproduce. Against probe_isolation.candidates — the list the isolation pipeline actually runs — it reads 97 exact / 157 overlapping / 101 covering, and the cap is not the cause: the mean list is 5.07 long and uncapping moves nothing. The pair was read off a different candidate list. probe_verifier.py's PRIOR carries the same two numbers and its read (d) will disagree with itself when run. This is probe_scorer.py's own warning arriving: two candidate lists over the same weights give numbers that cannot be pooled.

The operative containment test is score_extract_span's, which asks for the gold's content words rather than string equality — a candidate spanning Jourdan Miller from Bend covers gold Jourdan Miller. Widened, the enumerator reaches 144/200 (held out 141) at 37.5 asks an item, against 101 at 5.1. That ceiling is now moot for selection and still bounds anything that picks.

Limits, and two specification errors of my own

n=1 on one checkpoint. The populations are NQ dev paragraphs at WIDTH 64 / STRIDE 32 — 4.4 windows a passage, which is the shortest possible test of a scan and the reason first is a strong baseline. probe_detector's band table is 50/50 answerable by construction; at the top-100 cut the detector is 42% sensitive with an 8% false-positive rate, so precision falls to 36.8% where one window in ten carries the answer and 21.6% at one in twenty. A real page is the second regime and nothing here measures it.

probe_scan's read (b) asked top-3 to beat chance by 20 points without checking that 20 points existed — chance is 86.4% at 4.4 windows, leaving 13.6 — and an earlier draft printed "the scan does not have an instrument" off that unreachable bar. It is reported VOID and the verdict keys on the primary alone. probe_enumerator's units variant first read identical to base on every row because _dedup discarded 1538 degrees Celsius as overlapping 1538, which deleted the extent fix the variant existed to price. Both are the same defect as an under-powered run printing a verdict, and all four probes now guard against it.

Nothing here licenses a corpus change on its own. What it licenses is a question — whether the failure concentrates on phrase-shaped answers — that is now measured rather than inferred, and that no vehicle so far has been aimed at.
