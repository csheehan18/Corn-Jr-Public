Branch choice falls to chance when condition words flip the meaning

The result, as the finding states it

Branch rebuilt with flip words takes the table away and nothing forms in its place: at one pass and 2.2% of the mix the pick is at chance on the traces it trained on (202 of 400, a median margin of 0.01 nats), the result does not reach it, and what he writes is a fixed taste in place phrases ("a spot to eat" picked 89% of the times it is offered, "somewhere to eat" 26%)

What it cost

19.1 min of training one nano run (1,146 s, contended — the card at 37-40%, a bound), two reads at 18-19 s, one copy card 9 s, the control read 15 s, the free-decode hand read 9 s and the own-trace reads ~20 s on the card; the build 13:26-13:34 on 2026-10-14, the chain 13:34-13:54, the read 13:54-13:57.

The branch rung rebuilt to GENERATORS.md after findings 169-172, at the owner's direction — the same words with a different meaning, so that memorising the wording cannot work — pre-registered in configs/mix_v3_funcworld_branchflip.py's docstring at 6a9265e before a byte trained. corn/branchflipgen.py: eight families in contrast pairs, where one flip word moves the gold ("X if it's raining, otherwise Y" / "X if it's not raining, otherwise Y" / "X unless it's raining, in which case Y" ...), filled from word lists into 2,997 wordings, none seen more than 11 times (finding 169's payload: eight wordings at ~928 each). check_branch.py section 8 re-derives every gold by rule from the words (0 wrong of 7,424) and holds every single key at 50.2% or less on the gold's position. Twenty traces were read by hand before training, every pick right. One change from finding 169's arm: the requests; the same world, menus and dose (7,424 traces, 2.2% of the mix, 5,844 iterations), arm E2 underneath. One seed, by the owner's call: a corpus that teaches the rule installs it at any seed, so a miss is the data's. Cards: evals/results/nano20m_v3_fw_branchflip__branchflip{,_items}.json*, __branch{,_items}.json*, __copywide.json; the control nano20m_v3_fw_filter_ev2__branchflip.json.

Read by hand — the free decode first

hand_flip.py (scratch, probe only — not on disk): he writes the pick line himself, greedy, on every prompt of 40 sampled quads across the four cells, each printed beside its request and result. 160 lines, 0 malformed, and right on exactly half in every cell — 20 of 40, 20 of 40, 21 of 40, 18 of 40. The lines say why: in most quads he writes the same continuation on all four prompts — whatever the result, and wherever the phrase sits in the menu. Row 126 writes "a spot for a pint" four times, 138 "a place for drinks", 130 "a room for the night". Over all 432 rows (the full-option card), 276 quads name one place on all four prompts, and the share of times each phrase is picked when offered is a ranking:

a spot to eat 0.89 a spot for a pint 0.81 a room for the night 0.54 a place to sleep 0.45 a place for dinner 0.44 a place for drinks 0.43 somewhere for lunch 0.41 somewhere for a drink 0.33 somewhere to stay 0.31 somewhere to eat 0.26

GENERATORS §5's signature: a fixed ranking, here over the continuation phrases. It is what is left when no decision formed. The phrase is drawn independently of the gold, so it scores half and moves nothing.

On its own training traces

The registered rows are held-out, so the question that settles the cause is whether anything formed where it trained. Scored on 400 payload traces from the front of the file (own_traces.py, scratch, probe only — not on disk; the validation split is the tail):

run right of 400 median margin (nats, wrong minus gold) flip-word branch, seed 1337 202 +0.01 finding 169's branch, seed 2337 367 +2.27 (installed) finding 169's branch, seed 1337 229 +0.16 (did not)

Nothing formed, not even on the traces it trained on. The rebuild did the first half of its job: there was no table to fall back on, and none formed. But at one pass and 2.2% of the mix the rule did not form either, and the binding now asks for more than finding 169's did. Finding 169 needed the result crossed with one of eight wordings. This needs the result crossed with where the condition attaches and whether a flip word turns it over. The dose that got finding 169's easier binding to form at one seed of four forms nothing here.

The copy row, a side column: tool.copy_wide 297 of 300, inside the 20M family's spread (finding 170).

What it licenses

By the table, the data is still wrong — and the own-trace read says which way: not memorisable (fixed), not learnable at this dose either. Two levers follow from GENERATORS, and the owner's reading ("it should install regardless of luck") is the standard either has to meet:

  • Supply the hop in the bytes (D5). Let him write the binding himself, next to the request where the flip words are: a line after the parse naming the continuation the condition selects. Then the pick is a short read of his own line and the result. Every rung that installed did so on a short, copyable hop (findings 147, 151, 168's named field).
  • More distinct evidence (D8, finding 159). Fresh traces rather than passes — several times the 7,424, each wording still near ten sightings — so the rule has the volume a harder conjunction needs.

The first changes the format and is a D7 question. The second changes only the dose.

What it does not settle. Whether a second seed would differ — by the owner's standard it should not matter, and the own-trace read leaves nothing for a seed to rescue. Also whether the flip families are equally hard, which the per-family tally above (6-12 prompts a family) is too thin to say.
