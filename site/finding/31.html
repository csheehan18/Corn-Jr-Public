The gain has no sharp threshold, and the fix was already cheap

The result, as the finding states it

There is no knee, and the intervention was already cheap

What it cost

0 s of new scoring on record; it reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (nano20m_v1). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

The twenty-ninth finding widened the temporal slice's left edges 6 → 24 and took the treated bucket to 88/88; the thirtieth confirmed at eight checkpoints that the gain is confined to the edges the pools contain. Nothing had been run between 6 and 24, and with transfer ruled out, the only thing left that could make the intervention cheap was where inside that range the gain sits.

configs/mix_v1_mid.py is the bracket: the base pools plus the first half of what the wide pools add, in file order — a rule rather than a hand-picked list, because the wide pools were written after seeing which edges failed and a second subset chosen by hand would inherit that freedom twice. It lands date_past at 12 left edges. Two seeds, everything else byte-identical to both existing arms, 130,279,711 train tokens to the token.

The rule covers 76 of the 88 carriers the wide pools cover. That is not a choice — it is a fact about which left edges are common in ordinary English, and it is the whole finding.

Twelve edges buys the items it covers exactly as completely as twenty-four does. The treated bucket reads 74–75 against the wide arm's 72–76 — a complete overlap — where the baseline reads 38–57. And scripts/effect.py puts the two arms in overlap on every row and every bucket, including newly covered at 82–86 against 84–88. There is no knee between 12 and 24 because there is nothing between 12 and 24: doubling the edges again buys nothing measurable.

So the gain is proportional to coverage, and coverage is skewed. Half the added templates carry 86% of the recoverable items, which means the twenty-ninth finding's intervention was already cheap and its cost was being measured against the wrong thing. The in-distribution control is exact — temporal.route frozen reads 398/400 on both seeds, the same number nano20m_v1 reads — and lm temporal spans 0.0954–0.0978 against mix_v1's 0.0939–0.0973 and mix_v1_wide's 0.0976–0.0985, overlapping both. Widening the left context is free at either dose.

The bucket that was supposed to test transfer cannot

wide-only is the twelve carriers the mid pools leave untrained, and on a mid checkpoint it is a treatment's own control sitting in the same row of the same scorecard — which is why the config pre-registered it as the interesting outcome. It does not work, and the way it failed is worth recording.

Seed 1337 read 11/12, which is transfer: three items gained on edges the model was never trained on. Seed 1338 read 8/12, exactly the baseline. And the baseline itself spans 8–12 across four seeds, so one mix_v1 checkpoint scores full marks on the bucket with no intervention at all. A twelve-item group whose untreated range touches its own ceiling cannot separate anything, at any seed count.

The config predicted n=12 would make a positive "a reason to run seeds rather than a finding." That was right about the seeds and wrong about the remedy: the second seed did kill the reading, but no number of seeds fixes a bucket this size. The transfer question at 12 edges is unanswerable as instrumented, and answering it needs eval carriers built to sit in that bucket rather than the dozen that happen to land there.

Those carriers now exist, and nothing has been scored on them. evals_wideonly is a fourth arm out of scripts/paraphrase_evals.py, built the same way as the other three — utterances only, every other field byte-identical to the frozen set — with every temporal carrier taking a left edge the wide pools contain and the mid pools do not. That puts 398 of the 400 items in the bucket instead of 12, across all fourteen such edges at 9–49 items each; the two left over are the date_today items, which carry no slot and so have no edge to place. The dozen that happened to land there were one carrier on one edge (I'd like the notes {p}{e}), which is the whole of why the bucket read its own ceiling. check_wideonly_edges fails the build if an edge is absent from the wide pools — that is a held-out edge, and evals_heldout is where it belongs — or present in the mid ones, where it would be treated on both arms and the pair would agree whatever the model did; no carrier may be a wide training template either, since the treated side of this comparison is the checkpoint that read them.

The reading is three-way and every checkpoint it needs is already trained: mix_v1 (four seeds) and mix_v1_mid (two) are both untreated on these edges and are the transfer test, mix_v1_wide (four) is treated and is the positive control. If the control does not separate, the arm has no sensitivity and a flat transfer result means nothing — which is the failure this bucket has already had once, at n=12, and the reason the control is stated before the result rather than after. The arm's seed spread is unmeasured, so nothing may be read off it against a paraphrase-arm floor; that is the thirtieth finding applied to an arm that postdates it.

What replicates

temporal.copy reads 352/353 on the mid arm's first seed under paraphrase — the third checkpoint in the project to move on that row, after one mix_v1_wide seed frozen and one mix_v1 seed under paraphrase. Three of ten checkpoints now, one item each. The thirtieth finding's retirement of that row as definitionally invariant holds up.

tool.selection spreads 16 frozen over two seeds here (157 and 141), which is inside the 44 and 36 the two four-seed families gave and one more corpus family agreeing that the row is not readable.

What this does not settle

  • Two seeds are a range and not a floor, and the ranges above are doing the work. The mid arm's treated bucket overlapping the wide arm's is the claim, and two points against four is the weakest form of that comparison this project has run.
  • Nothing below 12 was tried. The knee could be at 8 or at 7; all this shows is that it is not between 12 and 24. The base pool's 6 is the only point known to be insufficient.
  • The rule chose the edges, and it chose well by accident. A different half of the wide additions would cover a different number of carriers, and how much of the 86% is the rule and how much is English is not separated here.
