The checker needs cross-fitted labels; fallback rules are exhausted

The result, as the finding states it

The verifier label has to be cross-fitted, and cross-fitted with the span head's confidence it passes the verifier bar; the extent rule buys eight for nothing and the reduce family is closed

docs/PLAN-READER-RESIDUE.md's four arms, run or retired 2026-09-02, plus the reads that file's species table made possible once the span head's own score was put beside the null head's logit. Replay wherever a number is a verdict; one no-grad pass over the popa cache (20 s a seed) for the pair scores; minutes of GPU for the verifier head. corn/extract.py, corn/gate.py, every scorer and every frozen row are untouched.

The plan said A2 first because it was free, A1 next because it was minutes, A3a third, A4 on the owner's decision, and put the arithmetic of every bar being met at 500 and 496 of 721. This file is what the arms actually read, and three of the four come out differently from their pre-registration — one for a reason the plan could not have seen without the number this file had to harvest first.

The 22 that cannot be won are two things, and one of them is a defect

The plan attributes all 22 to grounded's anti-restatement rule. Rendering It's {gold}. into every gold-bearing window of the 721 and asking grounded why it refuses:

gold-bearing windows 1,211 accepted 1,162 echo: no novel word 29 -> 10 passages where every gold window refuses novel words not contiguous 20 -> 12 passages where every gold window refuses

Twelve of the 22 are the contiguity test, not the restatement rule. grounded strips frame, question and source words and then asks the remaining words to be contiguous in the window. It's Stephen A. Douglas. loses a to FRAME and stephen douglas is not a run of any window; Mandate of Heaven loses of; best-of-seven, revenge and karma, Coton in the Elms, Rugrats in Paris: The Movie, Tandi, in Lahaul the same. The test was written for 1538 2862 — two window numbers that never appear together — and it also refuses every multi-word gold with a function word inside it.

The repair is a relaxation and nothing the rule accepts today is refused by it: if the novel words fail the current test, take the reply's run from its first novel word to its last, function words left in place, and accept when that run is a contiguous run of the window. 1538 2862 still fails (the run is 1538 2862). check_extract.py's seven grounding guards pass under it. What ships and what does not

  • The extent rule at d = 1.5, in the probe, both seeds paired, seed 1 at p = 0.0078 with nothing lost. It lives in the replay over pairs_built_s{1,2}_popa.json; wiring it into the reader's read path is R3's, beside hybrid, and both are read-time rules on the span head's scores that touch no weight.
  • scripts/harvest_popa_pairs.py, the pass that makes every read-time extent or ranking rule a replay, span-checked against the built harvest.
  • --label verifier and --hits-cache in train_null_head.py, --n-folds/--fold in train_reader_cached.py, and scripts/crossfit_hits.py — the cross-fitted A1, with the in-fold label's 95% recorded as the reason it exists, one seed read, the second pre-registered.
  • Nothing in reduce, grounded, any scorer or any frozen row. The contiguity repair is measured, its guards are checked, and it waits on the owner with rule 4's cost attached.
  • The threshold protocol is the owner's call and it is now the largest free variable in the null-head arms. bar matches PRIMARY; joint is what the record uses; R-T's one-SE rule is measured as unusable.

Limits

One trunk, two head seeds, fp32, Population A only for every paired claim; the frozen and phrase files are quoted where the trainer prints them and arbitrate nothing here, for the eighty-fourth finding's reason. The A3a (d, t) search over six values of d and ~3,900 of t on a 502-passage fold is a wider search than a single t, and the fixed-d rows are the honest ones for that reason: d = 1.5 is quoted because both seeds' curves put it there, not because a fold chose it. The margin-feature arms' hybrid reads are new reads of harvests already on disk, made under a reduce chosen after those harvests were written; the threshold is cross-validated on each read as it always was, and nothing about the heads changed. The verifier head's two seeds are one run each, selected on a label this file argues is not the verifier label; their numbers are a data fact and not an arm. The grounded repair is measured through a monkeypatch of the probe's reduce path and not through a changed corn/extract.py, and the guard run is the seven cases check_extract.py names, in the scratch replay rather than the guard file.
