Routing and declining · 18 August 2026

Training second-turn declines changed nothing and cost tool choice

The result, as the finding states it

The turn-2 no-call class moved turn-2 abstention by exactly nothing, and took 17 items of tool choice with it

What it cost

0 s of new scoring on record; it reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (nano20m_v1_chat). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

Two seeds of configs/mix_v1_chat2.py against the two nano20m_v1_chat seeds. The intervention is data/raw/chat2.txt — 94,844 two-exchange no-call transcripts — at a 25th of one pass, 318,873 tokens, 0.24% of the budget. The corpus rationale, the 89.3% it refuses and the overlap that bounds it are in the mix config.

This is a negative result with a cost attached, and it is the outcome the config pre-registered as "the reflex is upstream of the corpus class".

The control passed, so the rest is readable

register.conv — baseline [77, 82], arm [79, 80] of 200. Fully overlapping. The twenty-fifth finding priced this row at exactly this dialogue share against a 1.0-point floor, and a 0.24% perturbation moving it would have meant a broken build rather than a hypothesis. Total dialogue tokens are 26,186,876 in both corpora by construction.

Turn-2 abstention did not move. At all.

turn2.second's 19 out-of-scope items are the only ones on that row where the right answer is no call. called_anyway is the failure:

baseline s1baseline s2arm s1arm s2
called_anyway19191919

Nineteen of nineteen, in every cell, on both sides. The class the corpus was missing was added, in the position it was missing from, and the behaviour it was supposed to buy is at complete floor before and after. There is no seed spread to argue about and no margin to size: the number is the ceiling of the row and it did not change by one item.

That is the third time this project has added a no-call class and watched the reflex survive. The thirty-fourth finding's absent failed at 128 hand-written refusals; the thirty-fifth succeeded at turn 1 and is the reason this arm was worth running; this fails at turn 2. The pair the thirty-fifth finding's config anticipated — "it would be the second no-call intervention to fail and the pair would be the finding" — has arrived, and the honest reading is that turn-1 and turn-2 abstention are not the same behaviour: one moved off floor on a 5% slice and the other does not move on a slice occupying the same structural position.

And the tool choice got worse, decisively

turn2.second overall:

pass168/200166/200147/200149/200

Ranges [166, 168] against [147, 149] — non-overlapping, margin 17, within-arm spreads 2 and 2. This clears the spread that produced it by more than eight times, which none of the thirty-sixth finding's four controls managed and which makes this the readable number on the scorecard.

The failure mass is wrong-tool, not over-abstention, and the distinction is checkable because score_selection has no separate reason for declining an item that wants a call — a model that says something instead of calling produces no parseable call and scores unparseable. That bucket moves +4 and +3. The rest is tool choice:

So the slice did not make him quieter at turn 2. It made him worse at picking which tool, while leaving the one behaviour it targeted untouched. 0.24% of the budget bought a 17-item regression on a row eleven findings paid for.

turn2.first says nothing, and says it loudly

Baseline [159, 160], arm [161, 174]. The margin is 1 against a within-arm spread of 13. INSIDE SPREAD — no claim, in either direction.

Worth recording anyway: the arm's spread on this row is thirteen times the baseline's. Two seeds cannot tell a genuinely less stable arm from an unlucky draw, and the thirtieth finding's rule is why this is a note rather than a result.

The register gap narrowed, and this is the one place the thirty-sixth finding earns out

turn2_reply, talk − tool distinct-3 at an identical length histogram, differenced against the same matching at turn 1:

baseline s1baseline s2arm s1arm s2
matched DiD+0.0573+0.0547+0.0135+0.0123

Baseline [+0.0547, +0.0573] against arm [+0.0123, +0.0135]: margin 0.0412 against within-arm spreads of 0.0026 and 0.0012. That is a separation of more than fifteen times the spread — far cleaner than anything the thirty-sixth finding could establish about the gap's size, and readable only because that finding first showed the gap survives length matching. Quoting the raw cells here would have been quoting a length difference: the arm's median reply after a tool preamble falls from 11 words to 8.

Which cell moved is not established. The absolute numbers point at the tool cell rising toward talk (0.8680/0.9154 → 0.9066/0.9381) rather than talk falling (0.9456/0.9500 → 0.9449/0.9520), which would make this the one thing the arm improved. But that comparison is arm-against-baseline within a cell and is not length-controlled, and the median dropped by three words in exactly that cell — which is the confound the thirty-sixth finding exists to police. The narrowing is established; its mechanism is not. terminated is 200/200 in every cell of every checkpoint, so nothing collapsed.

What this does not settle

  • The dose. 1/25 of one pass is one point. But chat2.txt shares 100% of its openings and 99.3% of its first exchanges with chat.txt, so a bracket above ~1/8 would be testing duplication as much as the class. A larger dose needs chat2 re-cut from a region chat.txt does not use — a corpus rebuild, not a weight change.
  • Whether the cost is the class or the duplication. At this dose ~3,767 openings go from one use to two. That is 1.9% of chat.txt, and the pool moves from 1.19 uses per distinct reply to ~1.21 — well inside the bar — but the arm that would separate "the turn-2 class hurt tool choice" from "re-reading 1.9% of turn 1 hurt tool choice" has not been run.
  • Whether turn-2 abstention is reachable at all. Three interventions, one success, and the success was at turn 1. Nothing here says a bigger turn-2 slice would work; the 19/19 is equally consistent with the reflex sitting upstream of any corpus fix, which is what the thirty-fourth finding concluded about absent.
