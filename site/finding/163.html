Capping real openings at half keeps both consolation styles; greetings drop

The result, as the finding states it

The mix arm (C5's real openings capped at half a family) reads "real only" by its table: on a fresh draw of real openings it writes consolation 89 and 94 of 100 and share 94 and 88, within 10 of the real arm, and its hand-written consolation holds at 15 and 15 of 24, within 5 of A3b — the first slice to keep both kinds of consolation — but installs reads 77 and 73 under the registered 80 because greeting falls to 5 and 6 of 20 at both seeds, with its exposure and its word evidence level with the other arms, a cause not found; beside, on the hand-written far phrasings it writes 40 and 46, at or above every arm read so far, and its state reads 54 and 51 against A3b's 47 and 48

What it cost

922 s and 904 s of training (two nano runs at 5,337 iterations, contended — the card at 44% before the chain, so bounds); probe_move.py 58 s and 62 s; the inside read over three arms 124 s; the fresh held-out read over six checkpoints 73 s; the chain 2,143 s end to end, 15:01-15:37 on 2026-10-11; the build (the generator's cap, the guard section and two mutants, the configs) and the pre-registration commit 14:54-15:01; the greeting reads (sightings, word counter) under a minute on CPU.

Pre-registered in configs/mix_v3_a3b_mix50.py's docstring and committed at e96201c before a byte trained. Finding 162: C5's deck does not deal real openings beside the hand-written carriers, it replaces them (97.5% of consolation lines, 99% of share), and each arm then reaches its own kind of line — the real arm unseen real openings, A3b the hand-written idioms. With the chance of a real opening capped at one half per family, does one slice hold both? The one variable is corn.socialgen._carrier's chance, capped at 0.5 (generate(soda_cap=); absent, every earlier slice regenerates byte for byte, check_moveline.py sections 8 and 10); in what trains, consolation is 48% SODA's and share 49%, the other families as the real arm's. Weighted at A3b's 2,510,963 tokens, so budget, targets and iterations are A3b's and the real arm's to the token (section 12, two mutants caught).

The fresh draw (scripts/probe_real_heldout.py, card evals/results/move_inside_mix50__soda_heldout.json) is 100 consolation and 100 share SODA lines that none of the three payloads holds (6,844 and 21,029 never dealt), every arm re-scored on it; finding 162's draw excluded two payloads, so A3b and the real arm move by a few items against its numbers.

Unreadable first, and it is not: the shuffled reader 12 and 19 (stop 30); the three families the cap leaves alone (wellbeing, thanks, ack) 57 and 52 of 60 (bar 45).

Real reached — holds at both seeds: consolation 89 and 94 against the real arm's 87 and 95 (bar: within 10), share 94 and 88 against 97 and 95.

Idioms held — fails at both seeds, on one clause: consolation 15 and 15 of 24 against bars of 14 and 13 (A3b's 19 and 18 less five) — held; and installs 77 and 73 against a bar of 80 — missed.

By the table the reading is "real only": one gate at both seeds, the other at neither. My written guess was "both", with consolation near its bar; the consolation clause landed there (15, 15), and the arm missed on a family the guess did not consider.

The cause of the miss: greeting, with the evidence level

Every point under the bar is greeting's — 5 and 6 of 20, against A3b's 11 and 15 and the real arm's 10 and 9; consolation and the untouched families account for the rest at A3b's level. Read by hand at both seeds: "hi" and "howdy" written ack, "good evening" ack, "what have we got going on today" and its variants wellbeing — where A3b writes greeting on most.

Two causes checked and dropped, CPU, probe only:

  • Exposure. Counted to the budget, the twenty greeting phrasings are seen exactly 137 times in A3b's slice, 158 in the real arm's and 144 in this one; "hi" inside 343, 419 and 443 greeting lines. Level.
  • The word evidence. A word counter fit on each arm's slice labels the twenty greeting items right 18, 18 and 17 times. One difference is real — "hi" now also opens SODA's wellbeing, consolation and share lines ("Hi, how are you?", "Hi. I'm..."), 281 lines in this slice against none in A3b's — but the real arm has more of them (354) and holds greeting at 10 and 9, so it does not explain this arm on its own.

So the data carries greeting as well as before and this net uses less of it: finding 161's readout layer, on a family the cap does not touch. Why here is not found. The two seeds share one draw of the slice (generator seed 65), so a draw effect would show at both; a second draw is the test.

On the hand-written far phrasings the state reads above A3b's and the real arm's — R_fixed 54 and 51 against 47 and 48 for both, at the top of the 46-55 every arm read in finding 161 (card evals/results/move_inside_mix50__fixed.json) — and he writes 40 and 46 there, at or above every arm read so far (finding 161's 12-40). Two kinds of speech in one slice carry more words for each move than either alone, and finding 161 read the state as holding what the training words carry: this is that, in the direction it predicts, by +7 and +3 and unregistered. On the fresh real openings the state holds consolation on 76 and 71 against the real arm's 80 and 82 and A3b's 27 and 29. Routing holds (route.social below A3b's at 2337 only).

What it licenses

  • For C5 and D1: the cap keeps both kinds of consolation and the real reach — the result the arm was built to look for — and costs greeting at this draw. The registered licence for "real only" ("half is not enough for the losing kind") assumed the loser would be the capped family; it was not, so it does not apply as written. Before --soda-cap 0.5 goes to C5: a second draw of the mix (another generator seed, two nano runs, about 35 min of card), to read whether greeting's fall is the draw or the cap.
  • For finding 161's rule: a slice with more kinds of words for each move gave the state more reach on far phrasings than either kind alone — a direction for the rule, not a test of it.

What it does not say

That the cap costs greeting — one draw. That greeting's evidence is unchanged in every sense: a word counter is one reading of it. Anything at 260M. Two seeds of one draw, one floor seed, a linear reader — every number a bound.
