Looping until it sounds right: he learns to always keep looking

The result, as the finding states it

G6's loop rung as the owner defined it, "until it sounds right", by fine-tune from arm E2's three starts: he learns to write none on every batch — 189-192 of 192 answer batches, every start, the free line and two scoring reads agreeing — so three gates fail and the fourth passes vacuously; the starts never wrote none at all; and inside, his lean to stop does not follow the wish (the swap twins at AUC 0.38-0.49) while the soft read under it ranks the right place first on 47-60% of answer batches against a chance of about 42%

What it cost

three fine-tunes of 650 iterations at 20M (144, 143, 143 s, contended — the card at 35-38%, a bound), three loop reads at 18-19 s and three copy cards at 8 s: one chain of 509.1 s, 13:19-13:28 on 2026-10-20, registered ~9 min; the zero-training control 57 s; the three-way read on six checkpoints 399 s and the inside read on six 90 s; the build 12:57-13:18 on CPU.

The owner's go, 2026-10-20: "let's move on to loop", and of three definitions offered his pick was "until it sounds right" — a stop rule the runtime cannot check — over "keep looking until" a checkable condition and "do it for each". Pre-registered at bf6c994 (configs/mix_v3_loop_ft.py) after a zero-training control read and before a byte trained.

The world (corn/loopgen.py). A request with a soft wish and a loop frame ("keep looking till you find somewhere cosy"); batch after batch of two or three places; on each his pick: because[<the answering place's description>] (D-A1.20) or pick: none; on none the runtime fetches the next batch, up to four, so no line of his is one the runtime could compute. The answer on batch 1, 2, 3 or 4 or nowhere, a fifth each. The lexicon and holdout are corn/filtergen.py's — no description shares a content word with any wish, 24 of 72 phrasing x family pairings never train — and no place in a trace could answer the wish but the answer. scripts/check_loop.py holds it, every description's concept found by matching filtergen's templates; three mutants each fail their check. The recipe is the branch fine-tunes' (findings 179-183), the loop payload in the branch payload's place: 1.68 passes.

The registered read

scripts/probe_loop.py, every batch's options scored in full, earlier batches' gold picks before it; a trace right when every batch is:

run train (gate 0.85) no answer, all none swap (0.80) held (0.60) of 240 (gate 0.85) of 48 of 148 of 120 start ev2 23 0 59 8 start ev2s2 22 0 66 12 start ev2s3 23 0 55 7 loop ev2 48 48 0 24 loop ev2s2 48 48 0 24 loop ev2s3 48 48 0 24

Every trace right is a trace with no answer. Early stops 0, misses 192 of 192 answer batches on the trained rows at every start. Installs, reads it and by meaning fail at three of three; gives up passes at three of three and means nothing — he never stops. The prediction (the first three gates at three of three, by meaning at one) was wrong.

The instrument, suspected and cleared

The first fine-tune's six free decodes held one where he wrote the gold (pick: because[a rooftop hostel]) and the registered read had picked none — and the read sums each option's loss over its whole line, which favours the two-token pick: none over an eight-token description. So the read was rebuilt (probe_loop.py --split, committed): the decision at its one token (pick: none against pick: because), the place among the because[...] options by full loss, and his greedy line on every batch. All three agree: the free line writes none on 189, 192 and 192 of 192 answer batches. The length bias accounts for 3, 0 and 0 of 436 answer batches; the one decode read by hand was one of the three. The collapse is his, not the instrument's.

Inside: is the judgment there and outvoted?

scripts/probe_loop_inside.py (committed), per batch d = loss(pick: none) - loss(pick: because) — the stop lean, no length term — and, on answer batches, whether the right place ranks first among the places:

run AUC answer vs none batch right place first train held swap train (of 192) held (96) swap (148) start ev2 0.44 0.43 0.59 87 37 67 start ev2s2 0.44 0.53 0.45 91 37 76 start ev2s3 0.40 0.39 0.52 76 41 71 loop ev2 0.64 0.63 0.49 94 46 77 loop ev2s2 0.62 0.66 0.38 116 51 101 loop ev2s3 0.63 0.64 0.45 90 46 75

After the fine-tune his lean to stop is a little higher on the batches that hold the answer (AUC 0.62-0.66) — and not on the swap twins (0.38-0.49), where the batches are the same and only the wish moved. What separates an answer batch from the others on the trained rows is not the wish; the batch's place in the trace is the candidate it leaves. The soft read under the decision — forced to choose among the places on an answer batch — ranks the right one first on 47-49% at two starts and 60% (swap 68%) at 2337, the start finding 148 found reading the wish at pretraining; chance is about 42% (two or three places).

The copy row, a side column

tool.copy_wide 298, 264, 286 against the starts' 298, 295, 293 — the second start's fall again, its fifth arm today (finding 180's digit species, still owed a cause).

What it licenses

  • Loop's stop rule is rung 3's soft read, and at nano the soft read is not there to stop on. Near chance at two starts of three and partial at the third — finding 148's split, unchanged by a fine-tune that taught the format. The batch decision had nothing to read, and fell to the payload's majority answer (none, 71% of decisions): the starts always stopped, the fine-tunes never do. GENERATORS §1 with the input missing rather than a shortcut present.
  • The none decision now has two readings on record, both a constant: never written at pretraining (findings 148-149, these starts), always written after a fine-tune where it is the majority. Neither is a decision.
  • For the ladder: the iteration format (the runtime fetching the next batch after none) is unread, because nothing above it formed. The owner's choice is between testing loop's control on a stop the runtime can check — which splits the format from the soft read, as branch did — and taking the soft read itself as the work, which is a meaning hop findings 148, 156 and 167-168 found to be the wall at nano.

What it does not settle. Whether loop's control installs on a checkable stop; whether the soft read forms at 260M or with real descriptions; whether balancing the payload's none share changes the constant's sign without making a decision; and the second start's copy species.
