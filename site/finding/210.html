The reply judge · 22 October 2026

Real everyday dialogue fits the owner's messages best; Reddit worst

The result, as the finding states it

Of four conversational sources read on CPU, EmpatheticDialogues talks most like the 148 everyday messages of the reply read: a word-bigram model fitted on a million words of each source's messages scores those messages at perplexity 142, against 180 for SODA (what the mix reads now), 288 for OpenSubtitles and 359 for PEC (Reddit r/happy and r/offmychest) — and it is twice as varied as SODA on its own held-out messages (171 against 86; SODA is the most formulaic of the four). By hand it reads as real people texting about their day ("Oh man I hate when that happens...did you dry it off or change to a new pair?"). PEC is long, confessional, often off-thread, carries slurs, and was lowercased and tokenised by its authors, so it is unusable as text; subtitle lines that follow each other are rarely a message and its reply. EmpatheticDialogues is small (17,844 training dialogues, 1.26M words) and CC BY-NC 4.0

What it cost

licence and source search, downloads (28 MB, 252 MB and a 100 MB prefix of 3.7 GB, to F:/corn-jr-data/convcand/), scripts/probe_conv_sources.py and the hand read of 80 pairs, 15:15-15:30 on 2026-10-22. No card. ETA 45 min, actual ~25.

The question (owner, 2026-10-22: avoid SODA's style; find a source in his register). Finding 209: the 5M finds the owner-register messages far more surprising than its own openers, and the conversation it learns from is scripted, name-heavy SODA.

Read by hand (20 pairs a source)

  • EmpatheticDialogues: first-person everyday moments — a drink spilled before work, a baby's shots, waiting for a friend at the airport — and replies that answer them with a follow-up ("Where are you going?", "How nice! Are you going out or are you going to have a nice night in?"). Typos, lowercase, "haha"; real crowdworkers, not a model. Some heavy topics (a partner's affair).
  • SODA: polished and earnest ("I'm just concentrating on staying calm and relaxed. It's helping me to stay focused"), set roles (professor, fortune teller), names in the lines.
  • PEC: casual Reddit, but posts run long, replies drift off the post, and it carries slurs and politics; lowercased and tokenised ("i 'm", "do n't") beyond repair.
  • OpenSubtitles: the only source rich in short lines (35% four words or fewer), but consecutive lines are usually one speaker or a cut ("It'll give 'em a little scare." -> "I am Father."); without speaker turns it is not conversation.

What it licenses

  • EmpatheticDialogues is the first candidate for the owner's register, as a share beside SODA, not in place of it: ~1.7M tokens against SODA's 19.5M in the mix. Its licence is non-commercial; the owner does not plan to monetise Corn-Jr (2026-10-22), and RELEASE must list it with its terms if it enters the corpus.
  • Pass on Reddit as read here (PEC; other subreddits through the raw dumps are untested, and nothing here argues for chasing them) and on subtitles as conversation.
  • The target itself is a stand-in: the 148 messages are written in the owner's register by the assistant, not by him. A sharper read needs messages the owner writes himself.

DCLM, read beside (owner question, 2026-10-22). The same bigram read on 1M words of sentences (3-40 words) from the DCLM shards on disk (finding 190's): ppl owner 606, ppl own 2,075; median 14 words, 0.06 with a question mark, 0.16 first person. Web pages — install notes, product descriptions, fan fiction — the furthest of any source from the owner's register; a knowledge-side source (where finding 190 found it adds nothing measurable), not a conversational one.

The owner's own messages, counts only (2026-10-22). The owner offered his sessions; under his counts-only rule (2026-10-09) scripts/probe_owner_register.py reads the typed > lines of Saved Sessions/*.txt and prints only totals — no message was read or written. 69 unique messages: median 6 words, 0.38 four words or fewer, 0.38 with a question mark, 0.06 first person — against the stand-in set's 8, 0.11, 0.32 and 0.61. He writes to Corn-Jr as to an assistant, short questions and requests, not personal news; the 148 stand-ins over-represent the latter. Bigram perplexity of his messages: ED 718, SODA 938, subtitles 954, PEC 1,099, DCLM 1,386 — no source fits closely, ED still best. Sixty-nine messages from sessions that were largely testing him; a bound, not a portrait.

What it does not settle. Whether an EmpatheticDialogues share moves the reply read (a 5M arm); other real sources; the owner's own register.
