Giving memory-lookup calls real results turns silence into recitation

The result, as the finding states it

Finishing the chain converts silence into recitation, and the hard zero that held at every size goes to 26 of 26

What it cost

8.5 min of scoring on the 2 cards first cited here (nano20m_v1_daylog, nano20m_v1_daylog_seed2); it also reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (nano20m_v1). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

nano20m_v1_daylog and nano20m_v1_daylog_seed2, 2026-08-26. R5 of docs/PLAN-P3-RULERS.md — the one training run in that plan, pre-registered in configs/mix_v1_daylog.py on 2026-08-18 and never run until now. Two nano seeds, one source path changed, every weight identical, scored against the frozen evals/ set. The control is nano20m_v1 at four seeds.

What the arm changes, and it is one line

temporal data/raw/temporal.txt -> data/raw/temporal_daylog.txt

The corpus contains 90,293 recall_day calls and zero observations for any of them — 55% of the largest generated slice stops mid-chain, and no other slice carries one either. So the only thing the corpus had ever taught about the day log was how to ask for it, and temporal.route at 398/400 was scoring exactly that. --daylog finishes the chain: the tool answers and the model says the answer back.

The hard zero

row 260M (finding 51) control, 4 seeds arm, 2 seeds daylog.open 4/90 0, 9, 3, 0 / 90 25, 16 / 90 daylog.targeted 4/84 0, 7, 0, 0 / 84 12, 7 / 84 daylog.empty 0/26 0, 0, 0, 0 / 26 26, 26 / 26

daylog.empty has been 0/26 on every scorecard this project has ever written — 58 cards on disk carry the row, across nano and 260M, treated and untreated, and not one of them passed a single item. Both arm seeds score 26 of 26. Finding 51's sentence was "daylog.empty is a hard zero at every size this project has trained"; it was a corpus absence, and one file closes it completely.

daylog.open clears the control's four-seed range on both seeds (arm 16-25 against control 0-9). daylog.targeted does not: the arm's weaker seed reads 7/84 and the control's best seed also reads 7/84, so on the row that requires picking the right entry out of several, this arm is inside its own floor and cannot be said to have moved it.

The failure changed species, exactly as the config predicted

checkpoint daylog.open failure vocabulary 260M (finding 51) no_say 63, confabulated 23 control seed 1 empty 90 control seed 2 confabulated 81 arm seed 1 trained_topic 48, confabulated 17 arm seed 2 trained_topic 67, confabulated 7

Silence is gone. The untreated failure was an empty continuation — he emits the call, receives the observation, and says nothing — and there is not one such item on either arm seed. What replaced it is trained_topic: a well-formed read-back carrying a topic from the training pool instead of the one in the payload.

got "You got me to set a reminder to pay the gas bill, mid-morning." want "That was the day you got me to set up the push develop."

That is the limit configs/mix_v1_daylog.py pre-registered before the run, in those words: the slice carries 67 distinct topics over 132,628 entry uses — 1,980 uses each — and DATA.md §11 puts the failing regime at 2,096 uses per carrier. The prediction was that this corpus would teach the shape of a reply and not the reading of a topic out of a payload, that trained_topic would dominate if so, and that the fix is composing the topic space rather than abandoning the slice. All three hold. Confabulation — inventing a topic in neither pool — falls to 17 and 7.

So the arm bought the half nothing taught (that a <|say|> follows a log at all, and that an empty day is said to be empty) and did not buy the half its own topic cardinality made unlearnable. Both halves were named in advance.

Everything pre-registered as holding, holds

row control, 4 seeds arm, 2 seeds pre-registered temporal.route 398, 398, 398, 400 397, 397 hold, floor 3 temporal.copy 353/353 x4 353, 353 hold, floor 1 schema.conform 189-193 / 200 193, 193 hold tool.selection 112-156 / 200 156, 164 hold tool.validity 157-175 / 181 169, 167 hold register.conv 78-81 / 200 86, 86 hold

temporal.route holding is the informative one. The swap costs a 46% cut in routing examples at equal tokens (the answered chain is longer: 91,613 transcripts against 164,263), and the config registered that as a confound in advance — a fall would not have been attributable to the day log. It did not fall. 397/400 on both seeds says routing is bought well below 121,318 examples, which is a supply fact worth more than the row it came from.

One row sits outside its control range on one seed: tool.copy reads 92 and 100 of 120 against a control of 100, 100, 120, 114. The arm's second seed lands on the control's own minimum, and nothing in this swap touches the tool slice, so the honest reading is that four control seeds spanning 20 items is too wide a floor to call an 8-item difference with — the nineteenth and forty-third findings' lesson, arriving on a new row.

lm.temporal is not comparable here and must not be quoted as a regression. It reads 0.0940-0.0973 on the control and 0.1267-0.1342 on the arm, because the arm is scored against a different temporal text — the answered chains are the file. A source swap moves its own lm row by construction.

What it settles for the freeze

docs/PLAN-5B-SUPPLY.md §7 listed daylog completion as the cheapest open question before mix_v4_5b, and it is now read rather than open: the line delivers a real behaviour at the share it already has, with the residue named and cheap (compose the topic space, corn/daylog.py's only structural change, no new markers). What it does not settle is whether one file can carry both the sayvar and daylog repairs — they are one generator with two flags and that combination is still unmeasured — nor whether the line is worth its share, which was never this arm's question.

Limits

Two seeds against a four-seed control, at nano. The 260M numbers in the first table are finding 51's and are not a treated/untreated pair with this arm — they are the untreated baseline at a different size. daylog.targeted is inside its floor and is reported as such. And the arm's own topic pool is the thing its dominant failure is made of, so the next reading of these rows should come from a composed-topic slice rather than from another seed of this one.
