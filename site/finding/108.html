The reader is sensitive to prompt wording, but no wording does better

The result, as the finding states it

The states the span head reads are not prompt-invariant: half the argmaxes move when the question is written once and four fifths when the JSON goes, yet no other prompt form lands the gold on fifteen per cent of the misses, and every one loses more right answers than it gains, so A2 closes on its go bar

scripts/probe_prompt_forms.py, 2026-09-04. Four prompt forms over Population A's 3,893 windows, one forward each at the shipped layers, scored by the shipped, untrained span heads reader_built_s{1,2}.pt on the same candidate pairs; about four minutes of GPU in all. evals/results/prompt_forms.json. Nothing trained, nothing shipped, corn/reader.py untouched.

0. The arm and what was pre-registered

PLAN-READER-REPRESENTATION.md A2, READER-AUDIT.md L16 and L17. The head's states come from one prompt: the question on the look up line and again in the call's query, the window escaped inside a JSON payload in the obs slot, the say marker last. The trunk was trained to speak after that string; whether it is the string whose mid-stack states best separate a gold from its rival was an assumption. Three variants: noquery, the payload without its query field (the question once); prose, the window as bare text in the obs slot, no JSON; exemplar, two worked turns of the same shape in front, drawn from spans_nq.jsonl's positives by content-word Jaccard on the question and never from the passage's own article.

The cheap read, as the audit wrote it: read the shipped heads off each variant's states. Kill if every variant moves under 5% of argmaxes — the states are prompt-invariant and the lever is dead. Go for a variant that moves ≥ 5% and lands the gold on ≥ 15% of the RANK_2_10 windows (the gold in the shipped head's ten and not first) on both seeds, after which that variant is harvested for training. Finding 107's anchor added the third column: RANK_1 windows the shipped head had right, and how many the variant loses.

The states are not prompt-invariant. Writing the question once moves the argmax on half the windows; taking the window out of the JSON moves it on more than four fifths; two exemplar turns in front move it on a quarter. The first kill does not fire on any variant. The representation the head reads is strongly contingent on the envelope it was harvested under — the doubled question and the escaped payload are part of what the states encode.

No variant reaches the go bar, and none is close on the column that matters. noquery lands the gold on 11.7% / 10.7% of the misses against a bar of 15%, and loses a third of the windows the shipped head had right. prose lands 5.7% / 5.0% and loses four fifths. exemplar is the least destructive — it changes the least — and lands 8.5% / 13.1%, losing 14% / 10%. Net over gold-bearing windows every variant is negative on both seeds, from −16 to −559.

3. What the read can and cannot say

It can say the lever is alive as a representation question: states this sensitive to the prompt mean a head trained on noquery or exemplar states would be a different head, not the same head with noise. It cannot say that head would be better. The shipped heads were fitted to base states, so a loss on every variant is the expected sign of distribution shift and not evidence about the variant's ceiling; the audit's go bar was written knowing that, which is why it asked only for 15% of the misses to land and not for a net gain. The variants missed even that.

What would answer the ceiling is the arm itself: harvest the training positives under a variant prompt (772 s, 38 GB, which F: does not have without the deletions PLAN-READER-REPRESENTATION.md §0.4 names), train the built head, chain and read at the bar. The pre-registration does not license it on these numbers, and this workstream's last six arms of "train a head on different inputs" (100, 101, 102, 103, 106, 107) each landed within noise on the population. It is not run here.

4. Verdict

A2 closes on its go bar, both seeds, all three forms. L16 (a) and (b) and L17 are closed as cheap reads; the prior finding 98 put against exemplars (the trunk copies them rather than abstracting from them) is consistent with exemplar moving the fewest spans and landing the gold least on seed 1. The one result worth carrying forward is the invariance one: the shipped head's inputs depend on the JSON envelope and the doubled question, so any future change to worker_prompt re-harvests and re-trains every head, which corn/reader.py should say and does not.

5. On disk

scripts/probe_prompt_forms.py (new): the four forms with their own candidate-region locators, the control's reproduction check, and the three columns per seed; --forms selects a subset. evals/results/prompt_forms.json.

6. Limits

The shipped heads are the instrument, so every variant is read under distribution shift; a trained head per variant is the arm, not this read. exemplar used two exemplars chosen by Jaccard from a 30,000-window sample of the positives and no relation matching beyond word overlap. prose puts the window in the obs slot with no envelope at all, which the trunk never saw in training, so its collapse is the least informative of the three. The RANK_2_10 denominators differ by seed because the shipped heads' tens differ.
