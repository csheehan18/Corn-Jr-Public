A fallback rule buys 35 answers, but refusals still dominate

The result, as the finding states it

The fallback reduce buys thirty-five right answers at the same conditional accuracy, and the refusal still owns half of what is left

docs/PLAN-EXTRACTION-READER.md §2.2's second reduce, specified and run 2026-09-02 on the null-headed reader's recorded Population A harvests. Replay only: no GPU, no decode, no weight trained, corn/extract.py and corn/gate.py untouched. hybrid passes its pre-registered PRIMARY on both seeds and ships; the gain is attempt rate at held conditional accuracy and is reported as that.

The eighty-third finding ends with a supervised null head that reads 336 and 329 answerable right of 721 on Population A and beats both the ungated generative null and the gated runtime. This file is what happened next: the residue was partitioned, the partition named an arm, that arm ran and missed, and the other arm — the one the partition ranked third — is the one that worked.

Where it starts: the null head against the arm it would replace

Carried here from the eighty-third finding because every number below is a delta on it. scripts/probe_reader_passages.py reads (e) and (f):

arm joint net vs null head s1 net vs s2 gate, recorded on at t = -0.082 486 +64 (97/161, p = 8.1e-05) +59 (96/155, p = 0.00024) gate, cross-validated by this protocol 509 +41 (97/138, p = 0.0089) +36 (105/141, p = 0.025) null head s1 / s2 550 / 545

509 is the number the claim is made against — the same two-fold CV the reader's own threshold comes from, so neither arm gets a threshold advantage. The win is on the reading half: right 336/329 against the gate's 282, while correct silence falls 13 and 11.

And the frozen eval is saturated for this comparison and cannot arbitrate it. Its cross-validated folds disagree by 0.85 and 0.84, its two head seeds reach the same joint from opposite ends (tight 102 against 76, abstain 153 against 177), and every arm in this file lands inside that spread on it. On 200 items with a threshold that unstable the frozen file is measuring where the fold fell. Population A, at 1,003 passages, is the ruler for anything threshold-dependent from here, and the frozen file is a regression instrument and not an arbiter.

The owner table, under one rule, stated

scripts/probe_reader_residue.py partitions the answerable residue exclusively and in order — NULL (no gold-bearing window admitted), LOCATION (one was admitted and the head pointed elsewhere on all of them), REDUCE (one was admitted, the head pointed right on it, the passage is still wrong or silent).

The plurality depends on the gold-window predicate, so the predicate is named and argued rather than assumed. Three are available:

(A) corn.extract.span_of(answer, window) THE RULE THIS FILE USES the whole gold as a contiguous run of the window's content words. Marks 1,211 of Population A's 2,794 answerable windows. (B) every window NOT A RULE span_of(window, answer) is not None. span_of returns a bool and False is not None is True, so this marks 2,794 of 2,794. (C) corn.spangen.label_window(...) == "positive" THE STRICTER RULE (A) and the gold aligns to a bpe8k boundary inside the candidate range, is at most MAX_SPAN_TOKENS, and round-trips — i.e. the pair enumeration has a slot for it. Marks 1,170 of (A)'s 1,211 (96.6%); 707 of 721 passages keep one, against (A)'s 721 of 721. The 41 windows (A) keeps and (C) drops are no_verbatim 20, escaped 10, unaligned 7, too_long 4.

(A) is the rule because it is conservative against the conclusion drawn from it. (C) marks fewer windows gold-bearing, so under (C) strictly more passages fall to NULL and fewer to LOCATION and REDUCE: NULL's plurality can only grow, and at most 14 passages can move on that account. Reporting the permissive rule makes the plurality a lower bound on itself.

(B) is a bug and it flips the plurality, which is why the rule is written down at all. Reproduced in this repo, under (B) seed 1 reads NULL 133 / LOCATION 175 / REDUCE 77 against (A)'s 183 / 136 / 66 — the first is LOCATION-plurality and would have named a different next arm. A predicate that never consults span_of cannot distinguish a refused answer-bearing window from a refused empty one, so every admitted window counts as a missed location. The two tables are the same arithmetic over two predicates and nothing else; the fold mapping and the scorer are identical.

NULL holds the plurality on both seeds, every one of the 721 answerable passages has a gold-bearing window (so retrieval costs none of this), and the three owners have three different verdict shapes: NULL is four fifths refusal, LOCATION four fifths a wrong answer from the right window, REDUCE four fifths windows disagreeing into silence. The pre-registered reading named the margin-feature null head, it ran, and it missed. Four arms (the specified nn.LayerNorm(3) and a per-feature standardiser as its pre-registered control, two seeds each) took held-out balanced accuracy from 0.7570/0.7381 to 0.8698/0.8494 and held-out AUC from 0.8352/0.8204 to 0.9383/0.9240 — every selection bar passed — and read Population A answerable right 337, 322, 353, 340 against a bar of 371. A tenth of held-out presence accuracy bought between −7 and +17 passages, and the re-partition said why: NULL's share went up on three of four arms while LOCATION and REDUCE fell. The better detector converted mislocations into refusals rather than into right answers. That arm's failing branch is what sends this file to the reduce.

Read (g): the second reduce, specified because the plan never was

§2.2 promises reduce (unchanged first; score-aware second, §3.5). There is no §3.5 in PLAN-EXTRACTION-READER.md — the pointer is dangling and the second reduce had never been specified anywhere. It is specified in probe_reader_passages.py's docstring, as a flag on the probe, because §2.2's other half is binding: corn/extract.py::reduce is not edited, and all three modes call it rather than reimplementing it.

agree the runtime's own, and the default. Every admitted window speaks. top only the highest-margin admitted window speaks; every other window declines. reduce still runs and takes its one-reply branch, so grounded, _span_key, _canonical and det_repair all apply. hybrid agree, and only when it returns None with reduce's own "{n} windows disagree (...)" reason, fall back to top. "no window extracted an answer" is deliberately not caught.

Pre-registered before the run: PRIMARY is answerable right >= 371 with unanswerable silent >= 200, plus a positive McNemar against the CV'd gate at p < 0.05. The choice rule, also written first: hybrid ships if it meets PRIMARY on both seeds, because it keeps reduce's agreement semantics; top ships only if hybrid fails on a seed and top passes on both.

hybrid meets PRIMARY on both seeds and top does not, missing on seed 2 at 368 against 371. The choice rule fires as written and hybrid is the arm that ships: it is both the semantically conservative option — it only acts where reduce's agreement rule has already failed to produce an answer — and the one that replicates. It reads 371 right / 207 silent / joint 578 on both seeds, which is the tightest seed agreement anything in this workstream has produced on this population.

And the gain is attempt rate at held conditional accuracy, exactly as read (g) pre-registered that it would be. acc | att goes 0.675 and 0.676 to 0.664 and 0.666 — down — while the attempt rate goes 69.1% and 67.5% to 77.5% and 77.3%. A score-aware reduce cannot improve conditional accuracy on a window the head already read right; all it can do is stop a right reply being abstained away, and that is what it did. This is a reduce result and not a reading result, and it is worth shipping anyway: 35 and 42 more right answers at the same conditional accuracy are 35 and 42 more right answers, and INDEPENDENT-READ.md §5.1's rule is that the number is printed beside the one that would show what it was, not that it is discarded.

It is still not 85%.

Step 3: the selection slice does predict Population A's nulls, and the topical class is the easy one

The worry this read exists to test: train_null_head.py selects its epoch on a held-out slice of the training file, and if that slice's nulls are not the nulls Population A hands the runtime, the selection metric is about the directory. corn/extractgen.py's two kinds are carried through the harvest — absent_window is a null drawn from an answerable passage, absent_topical from a passage nobody could answer — and --kind-report splits the held-out slice by them. No span-head forward, so no GPU.

held out by article, at each head's own selection threshold seed null kind n null n pos AUC bal.acc TNR 1 absent_window 2855 4964 0.8263 0.7452 0.7282 1 absent_topical 2117 4964 0.8472 0.7730 0.7837 2 absent_window 3067 5127 0.8053 0.7217 0.6828 2 absent_topical 2173 5127 0.8417 0.7611 0.7616

absent_topical reads 0.8472 and 0.8417, well above the 0.75 that would have triggered a topical-only arm, so that arm was pre-registered as conditional and is not run. More than that, the result is the opposite of the worry: absent_topical is the easier class on both seeds, by 0.021 and 0.036 of AUC and by 0.056 and 0.079 of true-negative rate at the selection threshold. The head is better at refusing a window from a passage nobody could answer than at refusing an empty window from a passage that has an answer somewhere else.

Which relocates the 68 and 66 unanswerable passages spoken wrong. They are not a class the head cannot recognise — it recognises this class best. They are the tail of the class it recognises best, and the harder class is the one Population A's answerable half is full of: a passage with four windows and the answer in one of them offers three absent_window nulls, and those are what NULL's 185 and 184 are made of. absent_window is where the next null-side gain is, and the selection slice can see it.

Two artefacts found and fixed, both in a scratch replay rather than in the repo

An advisor's read-only replay reproduced this file's recorded reads exactly and then diverged in two places. Both are reproduced in-repo here so the difference is a measurement rather than an assertion, and neither touches any number above.

The predicate. span_of(window, answer) is not None marks every window gold-bearing; see (B). It moves the seed-1 owner table from 183/136/66 to 133/175/77 and flips the plurality.

The fold mapping, and it is Step 4's answer in one line. The replay counted 6 passages on seed 1 and 7 on seed 2 scored right with zero admitted windows at their fold's threshold, which should be impossible: with no window admitted every reply is a decline, reduce returns None, and the verdict is silent. It is an artefact of the replay's fold mapping and not a path in the probe. probe_reader_passages.cross_validate returns its verdicts alongside ordered, the fold-reordered rows; the replay then ran fold_split on ordered rather than on the file-order rows, which re-alternates by class in the new order and hands 502 of 1,003 passages the other fold's threshold. Under the correct mapping the count is 0 on both seeds; under the replay's it is exactly 6 and 7. probe_reader_residue.thresholds_by_id splits the original rows and always did.

What ships and what does not

  • --reduce hybrid is the arm the choice rule selected. It lives behind a flag on the probe. Wiring it into corn/extract.py::reduce is a runtime change that R3 owns and this file does not make.
  • Nothing about the reader changed. No weight, no scorer, no frozen row, no threshold constant. Every number here is replay over harvests that were already on disk.
  • The margin-feature null head does not ship. It is a better presence detector that reads no more passages, and its cards stay on disk as the measurement of that.
  • The topical-only null arm is not run, its trigger having not fired.

Limits

One trunk, two head seeds, one span-head arm (built), fp32. Population A is NQ dev's own paragraphs at 64/32; hybrid's +35 and +42 are measured there and nowhere else, and the frozen and phrase files are saturated for this comparison by the argument at the top of this file rather than being evidence against it. The three reduces share one candidate-threshold list and one CV protocol but choose their own thresholds, so the three rows of a seed are not the same policy read three ways; the fold values are printed for that reason. hybrid's fallback fires only on reduce's disagreement reason, so a passage whose windows produced nothing grounded is untouched by it and stays NULL. The acc | att fall of 0.011 and 0.010 is inside nothing in particular — it is small, it is in the direction the pre-registration named, and it is quoted rather than tested. R2's requoted ceiling of 505/508 assumes LOCATION is entirely fixable, which is an upper bound and not a forecast.
