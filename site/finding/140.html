The parse and depth · 16 September 2026

Depth-4 failure is position alone; moved to line one it works

The result, as the finding states it

The depth-4 zero is a position and nothing else: the identical cut that reads 3 to 19 of 96 at line 3 and 9 to 45 at line 2 reads 71 to 89 of 95 at line 1 on six W seeds and 88 at 100M, survives padding back to length, and sits where trained depth-3 shapes sit, so the content is not the block and a position-free expansion format is the arm on depth

What it cost

0 s of new scoring on record; it reads 8 cards scored for an earlier finding (nano100m_v3_funcworld_rope, nano20m_v3, nano20m_v3_funcworld and 5 more). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

Read 2026-09-15 with no training, off PLAN-PARSE-WEIGHTS §7.6's owed read. scripts/probe_depthpos.py --read shift2,pad2, new arms on finding 136's own instrument, on the six rung-2 W checkpoints, nano100m_v3_funcworld_rope, and B (nano20m_v3, never saw a parse world) as the floor. Cards: evals/results/<run>__depthpos2.json, with finding 136's shift re-read on the same checkpoints for one table.

The question

Finding 136 found that a parts: line at index 3 holds a group in 0 of 2,236 corpus blocks, and moved the failing level-3 decision of a depth-4 tree one line earlier by presenting the level-1 group's span as the utterance: 8 to 27 of 96 on the mean against 3 to 19 in place. It read that as position about 41% of the gap and the innermost content the rest. But line 2 is itself a low-support position — the corpus holds a group there in 0.18 of lines against 0.40 at line 1 — so the residue could be position too. This read moves the decision two lines: the level-2 group's span becomes the whole utterance, and the identical decision — write the depth-2 group's span whole rather than cut it into its members — sits at line 1, in a tree that is a trained depth-3 shape.

The instrument is probe_structure.div_read unchanged: at the one token where the gold line and its flattened alternative part, which is likelier. No length enters; chance is a coin. The scored line is now line 1, so where its labels are not level-1 legal (find, send, note inside a do group) both gold and flat are relabelled to level 1 and those 41 items are reported apart, finding 136's own control. pad2 pads the shortened prompt back to the original's length with whole in-distribution turns, so the token offset and the line index are separated as 136 separated them.

What this settles

The depth-4 zero is a position. The same spans, the same decision, the same fork token, read at the one line index the corpus has never shown a group at, are refused; read at the index it shows most, they are cut at the trained rate. Finding 136's "position about 41%" was the one-line shift landing on another under-supported index; two lines removes the gap. Nothing in the content of a depth-4 tree blocks him — finding 137 said copy burden did not, and this says the pairing and the carriers do not either.

The arm this licenses, PLAN-PARSE-WEIGHTS arm F: a format in which every expansion is the same local step at the same position. The transcript writes each group's expansion as its own block — the group's span re-presented, then the parts: line of its members — so depth is a loop of identical steps, each at index 0 of its own block with support at every level, and the runtime walks the tree (PLAN-DECOMPOSE arm D's stepped protocol, made what the corpus teaches rather than a fallback). C7 (PLAN-TAG-SPINE §11), which trains a table one row deeper, is no longer the arm on depth: a table of depths reads zero at the row it lacks whatever its length.

What it does not say

The instrument is the one-token fork, not a greedy decode of the line; parse.w_depth4 on the card stays at zero until a format he was trained on is scored, and a greedy read of the line-1 cut is owed beside this one. The relabelled subset carries finding 136's caveat that relabelling is an intervention; the untouched subset is the number to quote. One checkpoint at 100M.

Instruments. probe_depthpos.py gains --read shift2,pad2; eight cards in one serialised background run, about a minute each, no timing read.
