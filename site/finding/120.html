Consolation replies barely move and cost tool routing

The result, as the finding states it

The consolation family moves 1 to 6 of 28 and pays six items on each half of the routing pair, every loss called_web_search rising through its own mood-prefixed balancing class; and the in-session rater keeps 1,873 of 17,459 lines for a pool 1.44x wider at 1.18x the words, so arm 6 does not run by its own bar

What it cost

9.7 min of scoring on the 3 cards first cited here (corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social5, corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social5__historygrid-0905ctl, corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social5__toolgrid). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social5, 2026-09-05. PLAN-CONVERSATION §6a on arm 4's recipe: init_from ft4g, data/raw/social5.txt at arm 2's share, 1,200 iterations in 1,506 s, best at 1,000 (val 0.5875), social per-source loss 0.7435 -> 0.38. Full batched card, grid A table-backed, historygrid depth 1 under --multi, the ft4g adapter read as a proxy (finding 118). Beside it, the CPU half of arm 6: scripts/rate_social.py in its subscription form — every one of 17,459 candidate lines rated in-session by this session, no API key, the decisions file kept beside the rated file.

0. What arm 5's payload was

Six families where arm 4 had five. scripts/mine_social.py finds 2,945 distinct consolation replies over the whole of SODA; the generator keeps 21 — 2,768 die on the lexicon, and a consolation-only register of twenty words (CONSOLATION_EXTRA, measured) is what took it from 5 to 21; a fifty-word list bought 24 with three scene lines. A SODA reply to a low mood asks what happened, which is the scene. So §6a's own rule applied: the family rode at its natural share, 55 draws over 20 distinct in 1,821 pairs, not oversampled into finding 34's regime. Three revisions from arm 3's generator rode with it on their own merits (the exclusion fix, the scene rules, the vague-tail carriers), the greeting hello-opener share was bounded at 30% rather than anchored (141/470 against arm 3's 278/443), and the balancing class §6a asked for was built: _with_mood put one of LOW's short forms in front of a route draw's own request — "rough day, call Priya" -> place_call — 31 transcripts. Guarded: calibration 0 refused of 104 pool lines; check_social.py green with a seventh section.

decline did not move. No life claim moved (life: i got a 1 on both). What moved is web_search on turns that should be spoken — the routing prior leaning toward a call on a short second-person opener. That is §6a's second pre-registered reading, "the pool widened onto a neighbour", and the neighbour is the router.

The mechanism is the balancing class, and it is named rather than inferred. Fifty-five transcripts answer a mood; thirty-one put a mood in front of a request and answer it with a call. The second class is the sharper signal of the two — a call is a longer, more structured continuation than a one-line say — and its opener has the social move's own shape. Arm 1's diagnosis (finding 88's family: ping me later is hey with a verb on it) ran the other way: there, short second-person requests were spoken instead of declined; here, short second-person moves are called instead of spoken. Same boundary, opposite push, and the push came from the one class this arm added that no arm before it had.

3. Reading, and the owner's decision

§6a's second reading, with the cause named. The consolation moves did what they could from twenty lines; the class built to balance them taught the router more than they taught the speaker. The pre-registered response is "read the species before the next arm", and the species says the mood-prefixed calls. The owner took the recommendation to cut the class rather than dose it: corn.socialgen.MOOD_BALANCE is off, and arm 5b — arm 5's generator, replies and seed with that one switch flipped — is the one-file re-run whose readings configs/mix_v4_ft_social5b.py pre-registers (routing pair back inside arm 4's band and low_family holding at 6 is the class being the whole cost; the pair still down with low_family holding is the moves themselves widening the basin, and the family's share becomes the variable). Arms 7 and 8 are regenerated on the 5b basis and stack on whichever of 5b's readings fires.

4. Arm 6, closed by its own bar — the rater confirms the lexicon

§6b priced a rater as an API batch job; there is no key on this machine and none was wanted. The rater is this session: scripts/rate_social.py --chunks cut the 17,459 candidates (every mined line whose words sit in the 453-word probe lexicon after every mechanical rule but the shipped lexicon and the family tag), views narrowed them to the 11,014 the generator's rated path could use, and every one was read against §6b's paragraph and VOICE.md — 1,873 kept, 15,586 refused, 0 unrated. --assemble wrote data/raw/social_replies_rated.txt with the decisions file beside it.

generator pool arm 5 (lexicon) rated path (lexicon off, tag off) greeting 279 387 wellbeing 213 218 thanks 629 1,063 ack 103 63 consolation 21 54 farewell (untrained) 10 9 distinct, total 1,245 1,794 1.44x distinct words 180 213 1.18x

Against §6b's bar of 3x and 3x, that is 1.44x and 1.18x, and the arm does not run. The bar was written to keep a null arm off the card, and this is the null it foresaw: what a rater refuses is what the closed lexicon refuses — the scene — and the register that survives both judgements is about two hundred words wide. ack shrank under the rater (103 -> 63), because the lexicon had been passing acknowledgements that read as a deed or a promise ("Will do.", "Got it. Thanks."). Consolation widened 21 -> 54, which is the one family where the rater buys something, and it is recorded here for the day the family's share is the variable. The rated path stays in the generator (--rated) with the file, so the question can be reopened without re-rating.

5. Two guards that did not exist this morning

Arm 8's first cut put a history call exchange in front of a social pair whose declaration had never declared the tool it called — toolspace's lesson inverted. scripts/check_social.py now asserts over every transcript in every slice that each <|call|> names a tool its own declaration line declares, reads balance by the last turn (a call in the history is not what the transcript teaches), and takes only social says (a say answering a <|user|> line directly) to the reply filter. All three regenerated slices are green under it.

Limits

One seed per arm, and the numbers that moved are 5-6 items of 112 and 47. Grid A's trained column moved by two at n=176 and the post-history probe by three at n=36. The rater is one reader on one day with a written rubric; the decisions file is the record and a second rater would be the check. The reader proxy is read at a fixed threshold, attempt rate beside it.
