Everyday web text leaves the soft read under loop near chance

The result, as the finding states it

Arm E2's three starts rebuilt with the filtered plain FineWeb as their web source, and finding 184's loop fine-tune run from each, land on the pre-registered row "understands fails": the understanding ruler's wishes read 0.34, 0.38, 0.34 on the 96 items the gate was set on (gate 0.36 at two of three; one passes) and 0.33, 0.35, 0.30 on all 192 against the matched Edu starts' 0.32, 0.31, 0.31 — the everyday web does not move understanding inside E2's mix; under it every other gate fails at three of three — knowledge wishes 14, 16, 21 of 72 (gate 26), the filter read at no start (E2: one), the one start that read the wish on Edu (2337) losing it (held-out 97 -> 73 of 288, with the first-place habit raced out 91 -> 74 against chance 79), loop's fine-tunes writing pick: none on all 192 answer batches at every start, and the soft read under the stop ranking the right place first on 101, 88 and 80 of 192 (gate 125; finding 184: 94, 116, 90; chance about 81); so the question this was built for — was vocabulary the wall — is unreadable from this arm, because the vocabulary it was meant to add never arrived

What it cost

both mixes built on CPU 14:13-14:15 on 2026-10-21; one chain under the card's lock 14:15-15:34, 4,743.9 s, contended (the card at 35-37% before every job, a bound): three nano pretrains of 1,241, 1,241 and 1,242 s, on each start the ruler 18 s, the knowledge probe 34 s and the filter read 40 s, three loop fine-tunes of 161 s each, their loop reads 48 s and copy rows 8 s each, the inside read on six checkpoints 90 s; afterwards the 192-item ruler on arm E2's three Edu starts 53.4 s, a ruler hand read 17.8 s and a hand decode of 24 loop batches 7.7 s.

The question (pre-registered at 2db41ec, configs/mix_v3_funcworld_filter_ev2_plain.py, before a byte trained). Finding 184: loop "until it sounds right" collapses to always keep looking, because the soft read under the stop — does "a pub with a roaring fire" answer "cosy" — is near chance. Every start in that line of work pretrained on FineWeb-Edu at score 4, where the everyday words the soft read needs are rare, and finding 185 showed the plain web raising the understanding ruler. One variable: arm E2's starts, seeds 1337, 2337 and 3337, with the web file swapped for the filtered plain FineWeb (2be3af2's filter) at Edu's weight to the token; then finding 184's loop fine-tune from each, its replay's web the same file. Unreadable if check_webjunk.py, check_filter.py or check_loop.py fails, or a read is missing: all three pass and every read is on disk.

Read by hand before it was quoted

  • The loop line. 24 answer batches (8 per fine-tune, across trained and held-out rows) decoded greedily by an eager loop, not the probe's graphed decoder: pick: none on 24 of 24, as the probe's 3,600 free lines (1,200 a fine-tune, every one pick: none). Forced past pick: because[, the place he names is right on 2 of 8 (plain 2337), 4 of 8 (Edu 2337) and 4 of 8 (plain 1337); the misses read as meaning misses — "homely" -> "a brasserie facing the hills" over "a bistro with cushions and lamplight", "for the little ones" -> "a hotel with woollen throws" over "a hotel that hands out crayons" — and two forced lines blend two places ("a guesthouse with a beer garden", "a hostel with a crackling fire"), which a read ranking listed places cannot credit or fault. The instrument agrees with the text.
  • The filter line. Eight trained items read whole on the 1337 start: the scored read is right on 4; his free line names the right place's description on 2, a wrong place's on 3, and on 3 writes a description belonging to no place ("a canteen with cushions and lamplight" from "a diner with cushions and lamplight" and "a canteen with oak beams"), and the id he writes after it is the described place's on none of the 8. The free line is a side column; the gated read scores each place's whole line and behaved as designed on all 8.
  • The ruler. Twelve field misses on the 2337 start, every one a plain meaning miss ("a recipe for a big family gathering" -> cost, gold servings; "a pokemon that can take a hit" -> attack, gold defense). No hole found.

Against the prediction

Predicted: understands passes at three of three; knows, filter, loop and soft read fail — filter at one of three, loop's installs at none, the soft read 0.47-0.60. Understands failed (one of three), which moves the arm off the predicted row onto "understands fails". Knows held (fails). Filter failed lower than predicted, at none of three. Loop held (installs at none). The soft read held at two fine-tunes and fell below the predicted band at the third (0.42).

Two rows match, and one governs. "Understands fails" and "starts split on any gate" (understands passes at 2337 alone). The first governs: the 2337 pass is two items over the line on the 96 the gate was set on and under it on all 192, inside the read's spread, and the split row is for a gate whose seeds disagree beyond that; every row that would answer the question — vocabulary the wall, or the predicted "the ruler's words are not the rung's" — needs understanding to have moved first.

What it licenses

  • The question is unreadable from this arm, as its own table says. The everyday web was the means; inside E2's mix the filtered plain file moved the understanding read by 0.01, so a flat rung beneath it says nothing about whether vocabulary was the wall.
  • What it does show is that the soft read under loop does not come free from a different web at nano. With the web swapped, the one seed where the filter read formed lost it (why is not named); at three starts the soft read sits at 0.42-0.53, as finding 184's did, and loop's constant is the same constant.
  • For finding 184's open call — a stop the runtime can check, or the soft read itself as the work: this removes the cheap corpus route to the soft read at nano. The checkable stop remains the arm that can read loop's control at this size; the soft read, if it is the work, needs something this arm did not supply — its words in real descriptions, a source aimed at them, or 260M. The call stays the owner's.
  • The filter gate, read with finding 148's habit-free column beside it, is the honest read of this rung: a start with the first-place habit can pass the plain count on position (Edu 2337's 97 is 50 of 81 where the gold is first, 47 of 207 where it is not).

What it does not settle. Whether the unfiltered plain web, or the blend D1 took, moves understanding inside E2's mix; why the 2337 start lost the filter read (a candidate named, not tested); anything at 260M; whether loop's control installs on a checkable stop.
