A left-to-right count still beats him, so order is not it

The result, as the finding states it

Finding 221's ruler-blind book, split by which side of the label a description word stood on, reads the understanding ruler about as well from one side as from half of both. Counting only the meetings with the description word first — the order the ruler asks in, and all a left-to-right reader could ever tally — it answers 82 wishes of 192 and 92 fields of 141; counting only the meetings with the label first, 87 and 90. Six books that keep both orders and half the meetings read 80 to 89 wishes and 88 to 97 fields, so both one-sided books are inside what halving the evidence does. D is dead and S does not hold, as predicted. The two 20Ms that trained on the same tokens read 71 and 73 wishes and 78 and 76 fields: a count that reads in his direction with half the book's evidence is still 9 to 11 wishes and 14 to 16 fields over him. Of the meetings between a description word and its item's right label, 0.53 (wishes) and 0.50 (fields) have the description word first.

What it cost

ETA about a minute; 0.4.

The question (owner, 2026-11-14: "The goal is to figure out how to make understanding 'click' with the data we have", then "Yes, run it"). The count pools both orders of a pair — a line "light goes with weight" is written whether the text said light ... weight or weight ... light — and he predicts in one order only, the one the ruler asks in. Every lesson tried from finding 222 to 231 was in that order too. If the book's lead over him stood in the meetings with the label first, a both-sides lesson on the same text was the candidate. The pre-registration is scripts/probe_book_sides.py's docstring.

The books

scripts/probe_book.py's count, cell and answer, unchanged: PPMI at window 10, every word of the description voting its line to each candidate label, a page and a column for the 20,000 commonest words and the ruler never read. A line's weight depends on its own tally and the two words' counts, so only the cells the ruler touches are tallied: 813 description words by 96 labels (6 of the ruler's 102 labels stand outside the 20,000 and have no column, as in finding 221). 9,036,258 meetings within ten words, 4,540,675 with the description word first and 4,495,583 with the label first. Each meeting is also filed by the earlier word's position mod 4, and a half book keeps two of the four classes. A one-sided or half book's chance tally is half the whole book's, so a pair met equally both ways weighs the same in every book.

Read by hand

73 items change side between before and after (35 right in before only, 38 in after only); 21 were read with the right label's heaviest lines and how often each pair was met either way. Two things show.

  • The lines that decide an item are thin. "a spa retreat with massages every afternoon" -> relaxing rests on spa met 2 times before and 8 after, retreat 0 and 4, afternoon 0 and 5; "two strangers share an umbrella" -> romantic on strangers 0 and 2; "a cruise at dusk for two with champagne" -> romantic on champagne 1 and 0 and cruise 1 and 0; "trekking through jungle" -> adventurous on jungle 0 and 3. Which side wins such an item is which side the two or three meetings happened to fall on. That is the half books' spread seen from inside.
  • Where a pair is met often it is met both ways. woods/scary 24 and 10, warm/sunny 102 and 48, long/length 110 and 81, petrol/fuel 11 and 10, nearly/age 29 and 25, moves/speed 20 and 14. The one lopsided heavy line read is old/year, 296 before and 3,898 after ("year old").

What it licenses

By the pre-registration, its second outcome: order is not what the book has over him.

  • Direction is dropped. A count that only ever tallies the order he reads in, on half the evidence, answers 82 wishes and 92 fields to his 71 to 73 and 76 to 78. The lead is there without a single meeting he could not have used. A both-sides lesson is not proposed on this ground.
  • Two of the count's freedoms are now read and neither is the lead. It looks far (finding 232: he uses far words about as well as a model that reads the fields) and it pools both orders (here). What is left of what the count does and he does not is the first thing finding 221 said about it: a line is written by one meeting and writing on one page cannot change another.
  • The book's own numbers come with a width. Which half of the meetings is counted moves it by nine wishes and nine fields, because items turn on lines met a handful of times. Two books fewer than about nine items apart are not shown to differ by one read each. Finding 221's 91 and 95 stand as the whole book's; its lead over the 20Ms (18 to 20 wishes, 17 to 19 fields) is two widths.
  • Unguarded instrument. The tally is checked against a plain loop and against probe_book.tally, and the whole book against finding 221's numbers, inside the script; no check_*.py pins it, so these are bounds through scripts/probe_book_sides.py.

What it does not settle. Why he stops short of a count on his own text: this removes order and puts nothing in its place. Whether a model can use a meeting in the other order at all (the count says he would not need to). The narrow windows, where a one-sided book loses more than half a book does. And the test finding 227 left standing and nobody has run: a trunk trained with each word's home from the first step.
