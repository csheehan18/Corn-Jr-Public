At 260M the date header helps where at 30M it hurt

The result, as the finding states it

The header that cost ninety-nine items now buys seventeen, and the scorecard and the runtime disagree by forty-eight points

What it cost

3.5 min of scoring on the 1 card first cited here (nano20m_v3); it also reads 2 cards scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m, nano100m_v1). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

No training. checkpoints/corn_jr_260m as it stands, scored 2026-08-24 on the 3080 through python -m corn.evals, 10 m 50 s wall clock — 416 s of behaviour generation and 71 s of variety. Written to evals/results/corn_jr_260m.json. Two live arms alongside it, harness/checkup.py against the same weights at temperature 0 without --multi, differing only in whether decline was offered.

This is the first scorecard the 260M has ever had. There were 222 in evals/results/ before it and not one of them was this checkpoint: findings 48, 49 and 50 all drove corn_jr_260m through harness/, which reports rates over sixteen or twenty hand-written sentences, and the frozen 400-item instrument the rest of the project is built on had never been pointed at it. 260M Run Data/RUN-NOTES.md closes on exactly this — "Whether the nano-scale findings — 34 and 39 above all — reproduce at 260M is the first question the scorecard should answer."

The comparison that is legitimate, and the one that is not

The clean scale contrast is nano20m_v3 -> corn_jr_260m: same corpus recipe (mix_v3, mix_v3_5b), 29.6M -> 260.6M, 8.8x the parameters.

nano100m_v1 is not part of it, and the row it flatters is the one someone will quote. It reads tool.selection 181/200 against the 260M's 171/200 — the smaller model apparently winning at eight times fewer parameters. It trained on mix_v1. mix_v3 folds in WS-E, and the forty-second finding measured what that does: offered an escape he over-uses it. The pair differs by corpus and by policy, not by scale, and it is not a reading.

At nano the <|day|> header costs 99 and 67 items. At 260M it buys 17.

The fortieth finding recovered 90 of the 126 the header was costing and recorded that the residue was "still -29 to -63, so this is about half the effect", with the next doses named. The residue does not shrink at scale — it changes sign, and no further dose was spent.

This is the row HANDOFF.md (now docs/SESSION-ARCHIVE.md) singles out as the reason to distrust scale transfer: "daycross is worse at 91.6M than at 29.6M, so scale transfer is exactly what none of this assumes." That caution was correct as stated and correct to hold; it is now measured wrong at 260M, in the favourable direction, which is the less common way for a caution to fail.

Five rulers are at ceiling

temporal.route 400/400, temporal.copy 353/353, tool.copy 120/120, tool.copy_wide 300/300, refusal.say 270/270, refusal.say_unechoed 55/55. Decontamination reads clean on all four sources against mix_v3_5b's train.bin and val.bin, which matters because the corpus was rebuilt at 5B after the eval set was frozen on 2026-08-10.

The forty-sixth finding's caveat on say_unechoed carries over unchanged: 55/55 is two sentences seen 902 times, not a capability. The fiftieth finding's is sharper and applies to the whole block — tool.copy draws from an invented index, so every deference row in the project is measured in the one regime where nothing in the weights competes with the observation.

Scale bought nothing here, and daylog.empty is a hard zero at every size this project has trained. The breakdown is the part that was not known: it is dominated by silence, not by invention. He emits the call, receives the observation, and says nothing.

That is precisely the class corn/agent.py's fallback exists for — scripts/check_agent.py asserts "a day he failed to read back is read back rather than dropped" — so these rows should understate the assembled system. This run could not confirm that; see below.

The scorecard says 85.5% and the runtime says 37.5%

harness/checkup.py against the same weights, temperature 0, without --multi:

rowlive
routed6/16 (37.5%)
executed6/11
spoke_from_tool10/10
held_back3/5

Against tool.selection 171/200 (85.5%) and temporal.route 400/400 on the frozen set. Ten of sixteen live turns went to decline, including three plain save_note requests, one resolve_date, both recall_day turns and both ask_claude turns.

The live number is not new and should not be reported as a discovery. The forty-eighth finding published routed 4/16 -> 5/16 across its own fixes and wrote the honest headline itself: "the fixes did not teach him to route." executed 6/11 and spoke_from_tool 10/10 reproduce that run exactly. The 6/16 here is not an improvement on its 5/16 — that run used --multi and this one did not, so the two differ by a flag and the difference is one turn.

What is new is the other half of the pair. Until today there was a live rate and no scorecard on the same weights, so the gap could not be stated as a quantity. It is 48 points.

Two explanations were already in the file:

  • The twenty-eighth finding. Routing collapses on wording no generator emits. checkup.py's cases are hand-written sentences; evals/'s 200 are generated. The notefid cross above shows the same effect, same direction, on a frozen set — -8 of 120 there against -48 points here.
  • The forty-second finding. Offered decline he over-uses it, and ABSENT_SHARE is 35% and was never tuned.

Withholding the escape buys back five of the sixteen

The same sixteen cases, same weights, same temperature, --no-decline:

rowdecline offeredwithheld

The forty-second finding's over-declining dominates the live gap, and the twenty-eighth's phrasing effect is at most the remainder. The single clearest receipt is one turn run twice, byte-identical input:

decline offered note down pick up the dry cleaning -> {"name": "decline", "arguments": {}} -> "Nothing I've got covers that."

withheld note down pick up the dry cleaning -> {"name": "save_note", "arguments": {"text": "pick up the dry cleaning"}} -> {"status": "saved", "chars": 24} "Noted."

ABSENT_SHARE at 35%, never tuned, is now the highest-value untuned number in the project, and the bracket HANDOFF.md (now docs/SESSION-ARCHIVE.md) has carried as open item 2 since the forty-second finding has a 260M receipt rather than a nano one.

What it costs is visible in the same table. spoke_from_tool falls from 10/10 to 4/5, and the misses change shape: with no escape he routes to the wrong tool rather than to none, and save_note is the sink — resolve_date -> save_note, ask_claude -> save_note, and "turn the heating up" -> save_note, answered "Noted." This is a policy trade, not a repair, and --no-decline's docstring already says what it is: a measurement, not a setting.

What neither run could settle, and the sharper reason why

Both were started to test whether corn/agent.py's fallback rescues the no_say rows above. Neither answers it, and the second failed for a more interesting reason than the first.

policywhat did I note down todaywhat was I doing on the 14th
decline offered-> decline-> decline
withheld-> resolve_date-> web_search

recall_day is never selected live, under either policy — four attempts, four different wrong destinations. The fallback needs an observation to speak from, and no observation ever arrives.

That makes the daylog block above sharper than it first reads. Those rows score prompt_after: the call and the observation are supplied, and what is measured is the speech that follows. So the scorecard measures a stage the runtime never reaches for that tool, and its 4/90 is an upper bound on a capability whose live rate is zero for a reason the scorecard cannot see. It is the forty-eighth finding's lesson once more — a scorecard that starts after the call cannot see a call that is never made.

One number in passing

Latency over the sixteen turns: median 351 ms / p90 542 ms with decline offered, median 384 ms / p90 570 ms without. VISION §3 budgets the whole round trip at 500 ms. It holds at the median in both arms and at p90 in neither, on a checkpoint whose constraint costs were fixed in the forty-eighth finding — and the arm that routes more is the slower one, because a turn that declines is a turn that does no work.
