Understanding at small scale · 21 October 2026

A model a quarter as wide nearly matches the 20M's understanding

The result, as the finding states it

On finding 189's blend and the 20M's own 128.7M-token schedule, a 6.95M-parameter model (6 layers x 256, lr 3e-3 from a five-point sweep) reads the understanding ruler's fields 0.56 and 0.53 against the 20M blend's 0.55 and 0.54 and its 192 wishes 0.35 and 0.32 against 0.37 and 0.38, the plain lift 0.49 and 0.47 (chance 0.41), no everyday word in its own words at either seed, and a stock-answer pull (0.28, 0.27) between the blend's and Edu's — while its language loss sits about 0.045 nats a byte above the 20M's on all three text rows; at this budget the understanding reads barely move between 5M and 20M, so they are not reading width there

What it cost

22:24-23:13 on 2026-10-21; trainbench 6 s; the lr sweep, seven 1,000-iteration runs, 7.7 min; both base runs with card and four reads in one chain, 19.7 min (training 350 and 354 s); the chats and the held-out word read 18 s; all contended (the card at 37-40%, a bound).

The question (owner, 2026-10-21). The surprise-steering idea is to be iterated at about 5M before anything bigger. Before a mechanism is built on it: where does a 5M on the blend sit on every instrument the arm will be read by — how far above chance, and how far below the 20M?

The shape and the rate. configs/nano5m_v3_webblend.py: configs/nano.py's shape on bpe8k, 6,950,144 parameters (4.7M not in the embedding), ~19 tokens a parameter on the blend's 5,237 iterations. Trainbench: 58.5 ms a step, 420k tokens a second, 1.72 GB peak. The 20M's 6e-4 was suspect at a quarter of its width, so a sweep at 1,000 iterations (validation loss at the end, cosine to a tenth):

lr 6e-4 1e-3 2e-3 3e-3 4e-3 6e-3 8e-3 val 3.770 3.619 3.452 3.379 3.320 3.370 3.621

The run takes 3e-3, one below the short horizon's best, since a short sweep overstates the best rate of a run five times longer. The 20M's rate reads 0.39 worse here.

The everyday words, read by hand: no free answer means its word at either seed — spendy "that you're not going to be able to have a place to live", knackered "that you're not going to be a good boy", and most of the rest echo the word ("the 'sus' mean is the 'sus' mean"). The pick (0.35, 0.45) flatters, as finding 189 said it would.

Ten free chats a seed, hand-written openers (scripts/probe_free_chats.py), read by hand: 8 and 6 of 10 answer the opener in whole sentences ("I finally finished painting the kitchen!" -> "That's great! What made you decide to paint the kitchen?"); the misses answer something else ("Can you believe how expensive groceries have gotten?" -> "It's a great deal, thank you"); neither seed loops.

The held-out word read (scripts/probe_word_surprise.py, 300 words seen 10-300 times in his web text, 8 sentences each the mix never reads, and 60 common words): summed surprise on the word, in nats

run 10-30 30-100 100-300 common rare-word gap 5M 17.94 14.32 11.41 6.66 7.90 _seed2 18.16 14.46 11.28 6.57 8.06

-- rarer is more surprising, in order, at both seeds; this is the floor the surprise-steered arm is read against.

What it says

  • At this budget the understanding reads do not track width. A quarter of the 20M's width and a third of its parameters read fields level with it and wishes 0.02-0.06 lower, inside the 192-item read's spread (finding 189: about +-0.05 a run), on the same text. The language rows do track it: 0.03-0.05 nats a byte worse everywhere.
  • So finding 196's "grows with size" is, below 20M, mostly not size. The size steps it read (20M to 100M to 260M) came with three to forty times the text. Here width moved and text did not, and the reads barely moved. That is the confound finding 196 named, now with one side of it read.
  • The 5M is a fair place to iterate the mechanism. Its floor is above chance on fields, wishes and the plain lift, its chats are sentences, and the held-out word read separates the frequency bands cleanly.

What it does not settle. Whether the 20M's 6e-4 is itself under-tuned (a 20M at its best rate might pull ahead of the 5M on the reads); more seeds (two a side, and the wish spread is about +-0.05); the plain lift at 20M on the blend, which was not read; any size past 20M.
