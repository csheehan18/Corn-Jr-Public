Cutting mood-prefixed search calls removes the whole cost

The result, as the finding states it

With the mood-prefixed calls cut, the routing pair returns to arm 4's band and the consolation row holds at 7 of 28, novel decline reads 31 of 32, and the class was the whole cost

What it cost

9.7 min of scoring on the 3 cards first cited here (corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social5b, corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social5b__historygrid-0905ctl, corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social5b__toolgrid). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social5b, 2026-09-05. Arm 5's generator, replies and seed with one switch flipped — corn.socialgen.MOOD_BALANCE off — so the 31 "mood, then a request -> call" transcripts are gone and the 52 consolation moves ride with the plain balance every family has. init_from ft4g, arm 2's share, 1,200 iterations in 1,505 s, best at 1,000 (val 0.5875). Full batched card, grid A, historygrid depth 1, the ft4g adapter as a proxy. Pre-registered in configs/mix_v4_ft_social5b.py before the run.

The routing pair is back inside arm 4's band (the bar was declared >= 95, bare >= 97; it reads 97 and 97), voice is back to 90, and low_family holds at 7 — one above arm 5 — with one of the four held-out forms still answered. 3. Reading

Arm 5b is the base arm 7 stacks on, and it is the best ft4g-lineage checkpoint on the record: arm 4's rows with the consolation family added (1 -> 7 of 28), arm 2's grid A, every gate inside its floor. The consolation family is still supply-limited — seven of twenty-eight from a twenty-line pool at two uses each — and the row that would move it is the rated pool's 54 lines (finding 120 §4), which is a share question for a later arm and not this one.

Limits

One seed. The rows that decide this reading moved by 4-6 items of 112 against arm 5 and by 0-3 against arm 4; social.self by 3 of 47. Grid A's novel column is 32 items. The reader proxy is at a fixed threshold.
