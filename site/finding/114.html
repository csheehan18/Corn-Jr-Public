Tuning answer length on the new reader changes nothing

The result, as the finding states it

The extent rule's reach and tolerance on the whole-passage adapter move right by one to four passages inside noise on both seeds, and the CLIPPED they remove turns into wrong answers and silences rather than right ones, so the grid is not the lever for the clip

Replay, 2026-09-05, both seeds, no training. Population A re-harvested from the two selected checkpoints of finding 112 with the extent grid stored to radius 8 (train_reader_whole.py --load ... --radius 8, 12 s a seed), then apply_extent at every (d, radius) in {1, 2, 3} × {3, 5, 8}, bar protocol, hybrid reduce, paired by passage against the shipped point (2.0, 3). evals/results/{reader,pairs}_whole_causal_s{1,2}_popa.json now carry the radius-8 grid; the read at radius 3 is byte-for-byte the record's.

0. Why

Finding 112's one cost was CLIPPED, 18 → 32 and 23 → 34: a head with the whole passage in view finds the entity and cuts it short. The extent rule (finding 85) speaks the longest pair within d of the argmax inside a radius of candidate rows; on the window reader it was worth +8 a seed at d = 2, radius 3 and nothing wider (finding 105). Pre-registered before the read: d = 2.0 as shipped, radius 5 and 8 against 3; counts if right is up on both seeds with CLIPPED down; the d grid printed for information.

Every paired right delta is between −8 and +4 with p ≥ 0.08; the largest positive, +4 on seed 2, is +1 on seed 1. The pre-registered cells are up on both seeds — (2.0, 5) by +2 / +2, (2.0, 8) by +1 / +4 — and inside the seed spread, which rule 6 calls not readable.

2. Where the clip goes

At (3.0, 5) CLIPPED falls 32 → 22 and 34 → 27, and wrong falls 126 → 116 and 123 → 116, yet right moves +1 / +4: the widened span is more often a different wrong answer or a refusal than the gold. The extent grid can only extend the argmax by candidate rows; what the head clips on this arm is not a missing last word (finding 86's "Matt" for "Matt Monro") but a different boundary decision the widened neighbourhood does not fix. A tighter d = 1 loses 3 to 8 on both seeds at every radius, so the shipped d = 2 is not too loose either.

3. Verdict

Closed, both seeds, at the pre-registration. The shipped constants (d = 2.0, radius 3) stay. Finding 105's result on the window reader holds on the adapter: the fuller answer is not among the spans the head scores near its argmax, and CLIPPED on this arm is a training-side matter (the loss sums every gold occurrence but says nothing about preferring the longer of two overlapping candidates).
