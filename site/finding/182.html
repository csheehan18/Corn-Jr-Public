Copying the condition's clause first: new sentence shapes read, new words don't

The result, as the finding states it

With the condition's clause cut first, composed clause structures install and transfer at all three starts — the trained cells 0.986-0.997, five held cells 0.979-1.000, every family on his training traces 1.00, "otherwise" and the default-then-late-switch order included; two gates miss, each read by hand to vocabulary the inventory lacks ("but not if": 54-66 of 64-80 misses on finding 180's rows, which read 0.992-0.997 without it) and to a parenthetical condition the cut rule itself attaches forward

What it cost

three fine-tunes of 650 iterations at 20M (144, 143, 143 s, contended — the card at 36%, a bound), two span reads a run (144-145 s and 106-108 s), three slot reads and copy cards at 7-8 s, the real-wording probe: one chain of 1,263.5 s, 11:15-11:36 on 2026-10-20, registered ~17 min (the span reads ran twice the estimate: two greedy lines a prompt); the build 11:02-11:15 on CPU.

The owner's go, 2026-10-20: "do 3" — put finding 181's missing hop in the bytes. Finding 181: with the clause structure composed from parts, the one-slot line if: v -> <the continuation taken when it holds> fell to chance wherever the condition attached backward ("X if it's v") or Y had only "otherwise". Two hops sat inside the one choice: which continuation the condition is attached to, and "otherwise means the other one". So the line became two, each a copy (corn/branchstructgen.py, StructBranch.span_line):

cond: X if it isn't raining the clause holding the condition, run on to the clause its continuation stands in if: not raining -> X that continuation, "not" when negated

The runtime orders the menu from the if: line — what runs when the condition holds first — as A2's runtime already does; the menu and the pick are finding 181's byte for byte. One variable against finding 181: the payload's binding lines (1.88 passes against 1.96, the same weight). Pre-registered at 9a788a2 (configs/mix_v3_bind_ft_span.py); check_branch.py section 17 holds it; finding 180's rows were rewritten into the form with every prompt byte for byte but the binding lines, so the old eight are read on the requests A2 read.
