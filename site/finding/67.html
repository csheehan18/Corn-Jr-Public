Routing and declining · 26 August 2026

The best tool-router never declines; the best decliner never serves

The result, as the finding states it

The checkpoint that routes best never declines, and the one that declines best never serves

What it cost

0 s of new scoring on record; it reads 2 cards scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m, corn_jr_260m_ftextract). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

corn_jr_260m and corn_jr_260m_ftextract, 2026-08-26, measured by harness/toolgrid.py — the R1 instrument of docs/PLAN-P3-RULERS.md, pre-registered the same day. 808 turns per checkpoint, greedy, table-backed (search: table, nothing leaves the machine), against the live server at rev 411f2f1 (dirty). hatched: 0 on every cell — no turn was answered by the runtime, so every number below is the model. No training.

Where it came from

Three failures from the 2026-08-26 live audit had no instrument: decline under-firing on action requests (checkup read held_back 2/5, all three misses sinking to web_search), send_text losing carriers ("send a text to Sam Iversen" → ask_agent), and save_note routing collapsing under date-bearing content. checkup measures these with denominators of three and five; searchgrid measures only turns whose right answer is web_search. The plan's own closing sentence was that running Phase 3 without this grid would leave its motivating failures unmeasured before treatment. The grid was built, and the first thing it measured was bigger than the three failures it was built for.

The see-saw

Serve-cases are grids B and C (600 turns: contact carriers and note carriers); decline-cases are grid A (208 turns: 176 trained decline shapes, 32 novel action requests).

checkpoint decline-cases right serve-cases right base 201/208 96.6% 118/600 19.7% ftextract 88/208 42.3% 386/600 64.3%

Neither checkpoint is deciding. The base declines almost everything that is not a search question — including 298 of 424 real call and text requests and 154 of 176 real notes, phrasings its own corpus taught. The fine-tune moved the mass wholesale the other way: serve-routing tripled and decline collapsed to 82/176 on trained shapes and 6/32 on novel ones. The displacement is directional and names its own cause: ftextract's misses land on web_search (49, 44, 41 across the three grids) and ask_agent (41, 69, 44) — the two tools its fine-tune corpus is about. The p3b2 tool corpora are the first to carry ask_agent transcripts at all (8,376 each; the shipped tools corpora carry zero, counted in PLAN-5B-SUPPLY.md §7), and the tool the base never saw is now absorbing 154 turns of everything else. The fine-tune moved the prior, not the judgement.

Grid A — decline, and why the base's ceiling is not a skill

base trained 172/176 novel 29/32 misses: 6 silent, 1 place_call ftextract trained 82/176 novel 6/32 misses: web_search 49, ask_agent 41, save_note 29

Read alone, the base's column says decline works and the fine-tune broke it. Read against grids B and C it says something worse: the base declines as a default, so its grid A score is the same bias that costs it 482 serve-cases, wearing the one costume where it happens to be right. A decline rate is only evidence of discrimination when the same checkpoint also serves, and no checkpoint on disk does both.

Grid B — the contact carriers, and finding 52 on a second tool family

424 turns: every trained place_call/send_text carrier the corpus teaches, eight fills each from the training book's first names, plus the messageless family measured failing live ("send a text to {c}").

base trained routed 96/400 place_call 89/224 send_text 7/200 ftextract trained routed 302/400 place_call 197/224 send_text 109/200 messageless base 0/24 ftextract 4/24

The base's per-carrier column is the fifty-second finding's shape on a second tool family: call {c} routes 8/8 while give {c} a ring, get hold of {c} and I want to talk to {c} route 0/8 — the corpus teaches the carriers and the weights keep a few. On ftextract the slots are healthier than the routing: kept_message 99/105, kept_contact 249/302 — and shed_overrun is now a counted repair rather than an argument: send_text kept_contact reads 71/109 raw and 83/109 after the shed, twelve texts to the right person recovered at the lookup. The messageless family is a near-total loss on both checkpoints and lands on ask_agent, which confirms the carrier-overlap hypothesis the plan registered ("to {c}" reads as relay) and makes it a corpus line rather than a suspicion.

Grid C — notes under content, the first content-conditioned routing read

176 turns: 22 trained note carriers held fixed, content crossed four ways.

content class base routed ftextract routed plain 5/44 37/44 date_bearing 4/44 16/44 question_shaped 5/44 11/44 real_entity 8/44 16/44

The base is flat — it declines notes regardless of content, so content does nothing to a checkpoint that never serves. On ftextract the interaction is real and large: the carrier is identical and the content halves the routing, with the misses going to ask_agent (44) and web_search (41). A date inside the note text pulls the turn toward the tools that answer date questions; an entity pulls it toward search. The live audit's single anecdote ("note down dentist appointment moved to friday") is a 21-point effect at n=44 per cell, and the cheap generator change — dates and entities inside note text — is licensed by measurement.

What this retires and reframes

The sixty-third finding's sentence — routing installs by fine-tune — survives with one word changed: the routing prior installs by fine-tune. Its evidence was searchgrid, 400 turns on which the right answer is always web_search; on such a grid a checkpoint that reaches for web_search indiscriminately is indistinguishable from one that discriminates, and grid A is the column that separates them. The handoff's (now STATUS.md's) "ftextract is the best of the four on live routing" is therefore a claim about search-shaped turns only, and checkup's held_back 2/5 was the real reading of a real problem at a denominator that could not carry it — it is 88/208 now.

What it licenses, and what it does not

The Phase 3 variable this names is balance: a fine-tune corpus in which served and declined requests are trained against each other, graded on both columns of the see-saw at once, plus three named supply lines — content variety inside note text, the messageless text family, and carrier breadth generally. What it does not license is any claim about which share or mix achieves the balance; that is an arm, and this is its ruler. Both ends of the see-saw now have baselines in harness/results/.

Limits

One seed per side — the seed2 fine-tunes are on disk and unscored (R4), and findings 57 and 61 both caution against reading a two-checkpoint difference as a trend before the spread is known. The fills are authored, not user traffic. Greedy only. Both checkpoints were served the same eight-tool declaration, but only ftextract's corpus contained ask_agent, so part of the displacement is a sink existing for one side and not the other — which is itself the finding's mechanism, not a confound of it, and the seed2 scorecards can say how stable it is.
