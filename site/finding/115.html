A copy-check bug refused answers like 'best-of-seven'

The result, as the finding states it

grounded strips frame words out of the middle of a span before testing that it is contiguous, so "best-of-seven" is tested as "best seven" and refused; on the whole-passage adapter that costs ten and seven right answers a seed against three on the window reader, and it is READER-AUDIT.md row 43 with its size measured

Replay, 2026-09-05, no GPU, both seeds, both readers. Found while asking what AGGREGATION could mean on finding 112's harvests, where every passage is one window and there is no vote to lose: all 16 / 10 of them are passages where the head's span (or its extent-widened form) is handed to reduce and reduce returns nothing, "no window extracted an answer", because corn.extract.grounded refuses the reply.

0. The rule and the defect

grounded (corn/extract.py) accepts a reply if every content word is in the window, the question, the source or FRAME; if at least one word is novel (from the window and not the question); and if the novel words are contiguous in the window:

novel = [w for w in said if w in window_words and w not in question_words and w not in source_words and w not in FRAME] return len(set(novel)) == 1 or longest_span(" ".join(novel), window) >= 2

FRAME is the closed set of function words a framing may add, and it holds of, the, a, in, and. So a span that carries one of those inside it loses it before the contiguity test:

reply said novel tested as passage says It's best-of-seven. best of seven best seven "best seven" best-of-seven It's Stephen A. Douglas. stephen a douglas stephen douglas "stephen douglas" Stephen A. Douglas It's Coton in the Elms. coton in the elms coton elms "coton elms" Coton in the Elms

"best seven" is not a phrase the passage contains, and the right answer is refused. Finding 85 counted this as "12 contiguity defect" among the 22 unwinnable passages on the window reader and the audit carries it as row 43, "contiguity repair in grounded", +3 / +3 right, open, the owner's.

1. The size, on both readers

The variant tested: contiguity measured over the reply's own words from the first novel word to the last, frame words inside that span kept; everything else in grounded unchanged (the no-invention test, the novel-word requirement, the source and question exclusions). Bar protocol, extent on, hybrid, each harvest at its own CV'd threshold, paired by passage:

right wrong silent u.silent paired right AGGREGATION W2 s1 (112) 513 -> 523 126 -> 127 82 -> 71 199 -> 198 +10 (p 0.021) 16 -> 2 W2 s2 (112) 510 -> 517 123 -> 125 88 -> 79 199 -> 200 +7 (p 0.065) 10 -> 1 shipped s1 389 -> 392 188 -> 192 144 -> 137 199 -> 199 +3 (p 0.25) 64 -> 61 shipped s2 388 -> 391 172 -> 175 161 -> 155 196 -> 196 +3 (p 0.25) 50 -> 47

Correct silence is unchanged to a passage on every row. The shipped reader's +3 / +3 is finding 85's number reproduced. On the adapter the same defect is worth three times as much, because the adapter finds the exact multi-word span far more often (other doubled in finding 112) and those are the spans that carry a function word.

2. Why this is recorded and not applied

grounded is on the measurement path: corn/evals.py calls it inside the extraction scorers (line ~2002), corn/agent.py uses it as the runtime's acceptance test, and corn/reader.py and every probe read through it. A change to it is rule 4's case — a scorer change that must re-score two frozen checkpoints byte-identically before it merges, and here it would not be byte-identical by construction, since the change is the point. Finding 85 left the same repair to the owner for the same reason. What is new is the price tag: +10 / +7 on the reader that will ship, pooled +17 over 1,442 passages, and AGGREGATION on the adapter goes to 2 / 1.

Neither the head nor the adapter is touched by this; it is the runtime's acceptance rule refusing what the head got right.

3. What it would take

A change to corn.extract.grounded (the novel slice replaced by the span between the first and last novel word), the scorers re-scored on two frozen checkpoints with the moved rows listed, and scripts/check_extract.py extended with the three cases above as the regression. About an hour, no GPU; the owner's decision under rule 4.
