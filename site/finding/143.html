Nesting at 20M breaks two different ways; too fragile to build on

The result, as the finding states it

Arm G2 held every pair arm F had and the trained nesting fell anyway, this time because he flattens the nested pair into the outer group; two differently matched arms broke the same base two different ways, so nesting at nano is too fragile to carry the arity ladder, while the outer four-carrier installs cleanly at both seeds

What it cost

6.6 min of scoring on the 1 card first cited here (nano20m_v3_fw_ladder_add). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

PLAN-PARSE-WEIGHTS arm G2, read 2026-09-25. Two nano cells (nano20m_v3_fw_ladder_add, seeds 1337 and 2337) against arm F at the same seeds, scripts/probe_ladder.py on evals/funcworld_ladder.jsonl plus the full card. Pre-registration: configs/mix_v3_funcworld_ladder_add.py, committed at 190cef1 before either seed trained. G2 is finding 142's repair: arm F's file byte-identical, 2,096 four-member outer groups added as their own source on top, total tokens and iterations raised by exactly the payload's weight, so every outer pair, triple and nested pair F saw is there to the count (re-read off the union's bytes by check_funcworld.py).

What the lines say

Holding the pairs did what it was built to do. Arm G's invented consumer is mostly gone — nest2 do+ back inside F's range at seed 2 and at the line at seed 1 — and outer pairs read above F at seed 1 and two under at seed 2. The nesting fell by a different road. Where the gold nests a pair inside a group, G2 writes the pair's members into the outer group, or splits the group into two outer ones:

hey no ulterior motive say the capital of Gledaenddor and equally the capital of Bryunlvholm, and lastly the currency of Garorwold GOLD ask[say ask[ask[the capital of Gledaenddor] and equally ask[the capital of Bryunlvholm]], and ask[lastly the currency of Garorwold]] F ask[say, ask[ask[the capital of Gledaenddor] and ask[equally the capital of Bryunlvholm]], and ask[lastly ...]] G2 ask[say ask[the capital of Gledaenddor] and ask[equally the capital of Bryunlvholm]] ask[lastly the currency of Garorwold]

Counted on nest2, among items where he writes no nested group, an outer group one member wider than the gold's (the pair inlined): F 13 and 4, G2 18 and 19, at seeds 1 and 2; the group split into outer groups, F 5 and 9, G2 7 and 7. A bound — a scratchpad count over greedy decodes with the probe's tree reader, not a pinned instrument.

Why, as far as these two arms can say

The payload's four-member groups were built flat (--profile flat4) so that G2 would add no nested pairs: under armf the filter would have added about 610, +25% on the rows being read. That held the nested-pair count at F's 2,467 and lowered the share of groups that hold a nesting, from 0.309 on F (2,467 of 7,990) to 0.245 on G2 (2,467 of 10,086). Arm G did the opposite — its share stayed at 0.301 and its count fell 10%. Each arm held one of the two and moved the other, and each lost the trained nesting, by a different mechanism. These runs cannot separate "the nesting share fell" from "training wide outer groups pulls members outward"; both predict flattening.

The pre-registration named count as the thing to hold and did not name share. That was this session's design choice, and the table did not anticipate it.

What it does say

The trained nesting at nano is fragile, and that is the result that survives both arms. It is 15 and 18 of 96 on arm F to start with (nesting at all on 16 and 23), and two corpus changes, each matched carefully on a different axis, took it to 2 to 11. A question about arity at a nested position cannot be read on a base that moves this easily — a third ladder arm would be asking the same unreadable question a third way.

The outer four-carrier installs, and cleanly. outer4 46 and 42 against F's 31 and 20, 44 and 40 on balanced lines, the number word kept on 40 of 56 at both seeds against F's 17 of 50 and 19 of 45 — and this time without G's cost to the rows beside it. Arity at the level where it is trained is trainable at nano.

What it does not say

Nothing about whether a four-member carrier travels to a nested position: the gate was not read, twice. Nothing at 260M. Nano, one synthetic world, two seeds, greedy decodes.

Licenses moving the nesting question off the corpus and onto the runtime, as PLAN-PARSE-WEIGHTS row F first specified: the stepped protocol, where the runtime re-presents each group's span as its own request and he writes one level at a time — the level these two arms show he learns reliably, and the position finding 140 already reads at 71 to 89 of 95. No third ladder arm.
