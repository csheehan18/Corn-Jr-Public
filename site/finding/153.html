He writes a summary of the conversation state but doesn't use it

The result, as the finding states it

The state line's second seed: he writes the line again, 106 of 120, and the repeat does not fall under it — at seed 2 a line present raises it, 59 to 74 hand-inserted and 66 under his own record, and averaged over four draws seed 1's fall shrinks from 21 to 8; the pre-registered read fails at both seeds, so the record is written and not read

Read by hand

The base's written column is not a record at either seed. The base never trained on the line; asked to write it, it writes tool declarations (schedule_weekly_shared_shared_..., add_evening_backup_shopping_list(item) -- add a new ...), and the second turn degrades ("Getting low.", "Yes, I'm afraid."), refused 99 and 109 of 384. Its low repeat there is a broken turn, which is why the gate reads the base's no-line column.

What this licenses

The pre-registered row it lands on is the second: state.value high, the repeat rate not moved by the record present — the record is written and not read; the awareness claim retires, and the line stays a scored intermediate for the harness (configs/mix_v3_turn2_state.py's table). Finding 134's "a line merely present moves the repeat on the cells trained on it" was one draw at one seed; at four draws and two seeds it does not hold.

For D7 the state line's case is now what it costs and what it records, not what it does to the next turn: 0.95% of the mix (finding 152), written well on corpus says and badly on his own openers at one of two seeds. Who writes it stays H3's choice (SPEC-LANGUAGE §4), and this says nothing in favour of him writing it: the runtime's state_of writes the right value every time. D2 (the runtime reading the line back) is not shown to buy anything at nano.

Limits

Nano, two seeds of two cells; the dependent-only and state-only cells ran at seed 1 alone. The repeat ruler is lexical (finding 134's instruments note) and its cases are six wellbeing openers. The replicates are post hoc and unseeded, as the registered cards were; the probe is seeded from here on. Nothing touches the shipped checkpoint.
