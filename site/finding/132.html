Rotary position embeddings beat learned positions on most tests

The result, as the finding states it

RoPE against learned positions on the dummy world: above on the trained parse at both seeds, three per cent better on three of four language rows, two below on the bound tree, and the held-out rows zero as on every arm before it, so rung 2 trains both trunks at nano and its mid size takes the one the parse prefers

What it cost

3.1 min of scoring on the 1 card first cited here (nano20m_v3_decomp_b_rope). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

PLAN-TAG-SPINE phase C0, the position encoding as an arm (§5 lever 4), read 2026-09-13. Two nano runs, nano20m_v3_decomp_b_rope at seeds 1337 and 1338, on arm B's file byte for byte (data/mix_v3_decomp_b, rule 1) under B's schedule, loss policy and token budget, with one field moved: Config.pos_enc="rope". Pre-registered in the config docstring with B's band, the outcome table, the unreadable outcome, the kill and the fallback before a byte trained. B's two seeds are the control, already scored, nothing retrained. Full cards, the bound partial cards (--role-bind --copy-bind), and scripts/check_decode.py parity on both checkpoints.

The question

Rung 2 asks whether a label learned as a function can be written for a shape he was never shown, and learned absolute positions are the choice the compositional-generalisation literature most consistently finds harmful for exactly that. Ten runs train one trunk. Which? Measured on the world that exists, so the choice is a reading and not a citation, and so a RoPE defect in the decode path is found on a seventeen-minute run.

What was built

Config.pos_enc (learned | rope, rope_theta). Under rope no wpe is built; every attention layer rotates q and k by their position before the dot product, in the training forward and in the graphed decode step alike, so the cache holds rotated keys and decode needs no new path. The trunk is drawn exactly as the learned build draws it — wpe constructed so the generator advances the same, then dropped — so at one seed a RoPE arm against a wpe arm is the encoding alone. pos_enc joined INIT_MUST_MATCH, so a fine-tune across it is refused by name.

Held by scripts/check_model.py, sixteen new checks: pos_enc="learned" spelled out is the pre-flag forward bit for bit; the RoPE trunk is the learned trunk tensor for tensor minus wpe, and the generator is left where the learned build leaves it; the RoPE step — token by token and as one chunk into an empty cache — is the RoPE forward within 1e-5, and the cat-cache forward is too; attention under RoPE is unchanged by a constant shift of every position to 1e-9 and moved by a stretch of the positions. check_decode.py on a RoPE checkpoint: greedy parity 96/96 static, graph, bucketed and compiled; prefix reuse 24/24; the batched reader inert — on the smoke checkpoint before the runs and on both trained ones after.

Cost, measured before the run (corn/trainbench.py, batch 24 x 512, bf16, compiled, 30 timed steps after 5 warm, idle card; probe only — not on disk): 139.1 against 137.6 ms a step, +1.1%, peak 3.18 against 3.00 GB. 29,368,832 parameters against 29,630,976: the 512 x 512 table.

The pre-registered table lands on its mixed row. RoPE is above B's band on parse.span at both seeds (81 and 81 against 77 and 78, B's own spread 1), above it on tool.validity at both, and better on three of four language rows at both seeds with the validation loss lower by 0.066 twice — and it is two below B's low on the tree under the binding at both seeds (43 and 43 against 45 and 51, B's spread 6). By the docstring's own definition of the band, widened by nothing, that is below on one deciding row and above on another, and the row written for it says: rung 2's nano seeds train both trunks, and the mid size takes the one nano prefers on the parse rows. The kill did not fire (the copy band holds at 120, 292, 353); the fallback did not fire (nothing is below at both seeds on the copy band, on parse.span or on the language rows).

Two things beside the table. The bound-tree gap is inside B's own seed spread and lives in the sixteen items whose two tools of one role the label cannot choose between (finding 131), so it is a direction and not a size. And the language rows are the clearest read in this finding: three per cent of nats a byte at both seeds on web, narrative and conversational prose is larger than any arm on this mix has moved them, and it is the reason RoPE goes to the 1.0 corpus's trunk decision (PLAN-1-0 §10) as a measured candidate whatever rung 2 reads.

An instrument fact, found while designing the next world

corn/decompgen.py joins level-1 parts and level-2 children from one joiner table, so a two-question turn is a legal flat pair and a legal composite, and the gold is whichever the generator drew. Counted on the frozen rows (probe only — not on disk): 26 of 120 span items, 33 of 120 entity, 63 of 120 heldout and 96 of 96 depth3 carry a level-1 arity that no reading of the sentence decides. B's span failures are 19 of 43 wrong_arity and its depth3 failures 43 of 96; every parse.depth3 zero since finding 129 was read on items whose flat parse is legal. This does not move any reading in this finding — both trunks were read on the same rows — and it is why the next world marks a composite on the surface (PLAN-TAG-SPINE §5b).

Instruments

  • The trunk-init sameness across the flag, in the guard before anything trained — finding 130's lesson, applied at construction.
  • A smoke run of the real trainer (twelve iterations, compiled, one eval, one checkpoint) and check_decode.py --compile on its checkpoint, before the runs.
  • check_decode.py on both trained checkpoints after, so a decode-path defect could not be read as a model result — the unreadable clause of the pre-registration, which did not fire.

Limits

Two seeds, so directions (rule 6). A nano read on a dummy world whose held-out rows are zero on every trunk, so nothing here says what the encoding does for the thing rung 2 is for; that is C4's question, and it is why both trunks train there. The bound-tree difference is two items at each seed inside a spread of six. The language-row gain is on a 137M-token mix at 29M parameters and is not assumed to survive to 260M.
