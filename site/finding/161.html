His internal state knows more than he writes about move choice

The result, as the finding states it

Probe C, inside him at the move decision: a linear reader on his own state reads 46 to 55 of 100 far phrasings at all eight checkpoints, at the word counter's level and 15 to 35 over an untrained net of his shape, while he writes 12 to 40; six checkpoints read built and overridden and A3b's two read between, so the registered verdict is split — the arms differ in how much of the state the label uses, not in how much is there, and what A3b misses is mostly not in its state

What it cost

140 s on the card for the registered read and 140 s for the post hoc prior-keeping read (both contended, the card at 39-40% before each — bounds); the CPU sweep before the pre-registration commit 188 s; hand and post hoc reads off the card under 10 s; 13:14-13:29 on 2026-10-11 end to end, build included.

Asked by the owner on 2026-10-10, after finding 159's amendment read the far-phrasing limit from the outside — the word counter right on 75-82% of cue-covered items, he on 25-54% — and called it size more than meaning. The outside cannot say where the evidence is lost: never in his state where he decides, or in it and overridden by the label he writes. This reads the inside. Pre-registered in scripts/probe_move_inside.py's docstring and committed at e6f4c64 before any state was read.

The instrument

At the decision position — the last token every move: <value> line shares after the route prompt, which ends on move: (the value token carries its own space; no boundary merged on any prompt) — his residual state is read at ten places: the embedding sum, the eight blocks, ln_f. A linear reader (multinomial logistic regression, balanced classes, L2 1e-2 on standardised features) is fit per place on every distinct user line of the slice that checkpoint trained on (3,985 to 6,854), 80% to fit and 20% to choose the place, rendered as the unseen items are. It reads HELD_OUT_PROBE's 100 phrasings of the five families every arm trains — far phrasings by construction (check_moveline.py section 8). Beside it: his own tag off the committed A3 items file (O), the same protocol on a randomly initialised net of his shape (F, seed 0), the word counter (W), and the reader with its training labels shuffled (S). Card evals/results/move_inside.json.

Neither stop fired: S is 9-20 against a stop of 30, and every decision prefix ends on move:. The verdict is split — built, overridden at six of eight, and the registration needed both A3b seeds in the reading; A3b's two read between (R over O by 7 and 10, under the bar of 15). Read per arm, as registered: A3, cap and rep hold the move in their state and write their default over it; A3b writes most of what its state holds.

The floor is a real floor. F (20-32) sits 15-35 under R at every checkpoint and 21-28 under W, so the move read in his state is something training built, not what any net mixing these tokens carries; the reaches case (F within 5 of W) never arises. The embedding row — at a fixed token, so position and nothing else under learned positions — reads 10-23. By place, the reader is at 39-47 after the first block and level from the third (the per-place rows are on the card): the move is gathered early and carried to ln_f (45-53 there), right under the head that writes the label.

Read by hand, and four post hoc reads (not registered)

The reader's wins are the readable ones (A3b 1337, --hand): "top of the morning" greeting where he writes consolation, "i've had better weeks" consolation where he writes share, "duly noted" ack where he writes consolation. At rep 2337, where he writes open or share on most items, the reader reads ack on "loud and clear", "no argument here", "fair play", "seems reasonable", "no objections" — one item in the other direction ("grateful for that": he thanks, the reader ack).

1. A reader that keeps the slice's prior reads the same (--prior, card evals/results/move_inside__prior.json): R 46-55, every reading and the verdict unchanged. The lead is not the balancing.

2. The default is in his label, not in his state. His commonest label takes 34-66 of 100 at six checkpoints (A3 1337 greeting 44, A3 2337 open 66, cap open 34 and 61, rep open 40 and share 42); the reader's commonest takes 16-25 everywhere. A3b is the arm without a large default (consolation 26, ack 19) — finding 159 read its defaults on the 120 items with farewell in, where they are consolation and farewell.

3. How much of the state the label uses — of the items the reader gets right, the share he also writes right:

A3 on 27/49 19/55 cap 23/49 14/48 A3b 32/47 32/48 rep 18/47 11/48

A3b's lift over A3 in finding 159 (unseen 40 and 38 against 32 and 20) is this column: the label using more of what is there, not more being there (R 47-48 against A3's 49-55).

4. What is not in the state is the same size everywhere. Items both the reader and he get wrong: 44-51 of 100 at all eight checkpoints (A3b 45, 46). On those they write the same wrong label 20-31 times — not an A3b property. By finding 159's cue split: where a cue word points to the gold, the reader reads 18-28 of 28-45 against the word counter's 21-35; where none does, 19-36 of 55-72 against 18-27, at or above it at all eight — differences of a few items, no claim.

What it licenses

  • Finding 159's "size more than meaning" is located, and it has two layers. The words' evidence reaches his decision point as a move he built, at about the word counter's level, in every arm. On top of that, the six non-A3b checkpoints write a default over it — a readout loss of 17-36 items. A3b nearly closes the readout loss; what it still misses (about 45 of 100) is not in its state, linearly.
  • For the move line (D7): the arm that would ride is limited by the state, not the readout. The registered next step for built, overridden — adding the reader's direction and seeing whether his label follows — would buy A3b 15-16 items at most, so it is not the next lever there. The next lever is what the state holds: finding 159's two untested ones, more distinct evidence per cue or a larger net (D11's pilot), and this probe is the read to put on that pilot.
  • For the owner's question — what separates memorising from the idea: on far phrasings the idea is in him at the level of the words and no further, at nano, one pass. The torn-page read (each wording in or out of training on random halves) is the third definition, not run.

What it does not say

That he can use what the reader finds — only an intervention shows that, and it was not run. That the 45 items are absent from his state: a linear reader fit on the trained lines missed them; a nonlinear one or a different place might not. Anything at 260M (finding 51). One floor seed; two seeds an arm; an instrument with no guard — every number here is a bound (rule 28).
