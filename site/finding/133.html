On a synthetic world he generalises labels but not one level deeper

The result, as the finding states it

On a world where the phrasing is useless for the label he cuts phrasings he never saw at every seed, repairs a wrong label after its bound call fails, parses a world he never trained on, holds the right label for a fourth level ninety-six of ninety-six times when the cut is given, and still writes no cut one level deeper than he was shown

What it cost

8.4 min of scoring on the 1 card first cited here (nano100m_v3_funcworld_rope); it also reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (nano100m_v1). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

PLAN-TAG-SPINE rung 2, phase C4, read 2026-09-13. Six nano runs on configs/mix_v3_funcworld.py — the functional world corn/funcgen.py at arm B's pledge, under the repair loss policy — three seeds under RoPE and three under learned positions, token- and iteration-matched to arm B, pre-registered with the outcome table, the unreadable outcome, the kill and the fallback before a byte trained. Floors: B's two seeds and C0's RoPE seed 1 on the same rows. Every card full, every checkpoint through check_decode.py (six of six parity), the bound partials under --role-bind --copy-bind, the label-rank read and the structure-rank read beside the greedy rows. Ten rows new (w_*, rule 2), eight old, the copy band.

The question

Finding 131 opened rung 2 with one question left: can a label learned as what a part does be written for a shape he was never shown. On the old world his label was a bag of words (0.641 held out against a majority of 0.269) and every held-out parse row read zero on every arm. funcgen removed the two shortcuts that could be named and measured — one lookup pool serving ask and find with what follows deciding, and groups marked on the surface so arity is decidable (the old depth3 row was undecidable on 96 of 96 items, finding 132) — put a consequence under every label and a cost under a wrong one, trained depths 1-3 and held out 4, and scripts/check_funcworld.py held the bag-of-words bracket on the bytes: ask against find at 0.524 held out, pairwise chance 0.556.

Two things the table did not pre-register and the rows say plainly.

He repairs. parse.w_repair gives him the wrong parse line, the bound call it licensed and that call's failure, and asks for the corrected line: 30 to 37 of 60 at every seed, from a floor of zero on which the old-world checkpoints answered no_parse 54 of 60 times — they called again. The misses are copy slips on the re-written spans (46 of the 96 RoPE misses), not the label (5). The consequence built into the corpus under the binding is read: a failed bound call is followed by a re-parse.

He parses a world he never saw. The old world's held-out row — carriers in none of W's pools, orders W trains — reads 3 to 11 of 120 on every W checkpoint where every checkpoint trained on that world read 0, and its trained row reads 59 to 64 of 120 against B's 77 and 78 on carriers B memorised and W never met. A label learned as a function on one world's phrasings cuts another world's.

Depth 4: a readout, partly, and an absence, partly

What he writes on a depth-4 item is not blank. Decoded by hand on three items: in two he wrote a legal, shallower parse — the deep group cut into its members at level 1, his first line close to the gold's second — and in all three the forty-token spans carried copy slips ("Zelathgard" became "Zelvik", "once" became "since") and one label confusion (chat/social). Two instruments read that apart from an absence:

The structure-rank read (scripts/probe_structure.py, written after the first card): the gold line against the flattened line, teacher-forced.

This instrument is unpinned and B is its floor. The flattened line carries the same words under more label tokens, so total NLL favours the gold on length alone — B, which never saw this world, prefers the deep cut 72 of 96 times at level 1. Read as a bound: W sits above that floor at levels 1 and 2 (75 to 95 against 72; 66 to 74 against 43) and at chance at level 3, where the mean gap is within a nat of zero on every seed. So the recursion is in the probabilities one and two levels down and not three: he prefers to open a depth-4 group, prefers to open what it holds, and has no preference about the innermost pair. A length-matched alternative is the repair to this instrument; it was written the next day and reads the innermost level the other way (the amendment below).

The label-rank read (scripts/probe_rank.py, lever 6): the gold parse line teacher-forced against its single-label swaps over the same string and spans, so the question is given the cut, does he prefer the right label. On the depth-4 items the gold line is likelier than every single-label swap in 0.875 to 0.943 of cells at every seed, and at the innermost level, the one that does not exist in training, 96 of 96 on all six checkpoints, against B's 52 and a chance of 16. On this read the trunks are the other way from greedy: learned positions are above RoPE on parse.w_depth4 at every matched pair (+24, +26, +17 cells) and on parse.w_heldout at every pair (+8, +3, +5). The trunk

RoPE against learned positions, matched pairwise at three seeds: above on parse.w_span at every pair (+6, +8, +13) and on parse.w_entity (+10, +7, +8); the language rows better by the same three per cent as finding 132 at every pair; e2e.w_tree the other way (30/32/22 against 32/41/36). The rule the C0 read pre-registered — the mid size takes the trunk the six nano cells prefer on the parse rows — names RoPE, and the two mid runs are configs/nano100m_v3_funcworld_rope.py and its seed-2 twin.

What this licenses

The third row's consequence: rung 4 on the real carriers, with depth a runtime matter. The bindings of finding 131 carry to the real generators with a functional-label claim attached at last — a label learned as what a part does transfers to phrasings it never saw, on this world and across worlds — and the parse of a nesting deeper than trained is the stepped protocol's job (PLAN-DECOMPOSE arm D) rather than a bigger corpus's, until a length-matched structure read says the deep preference reaches level 3. The fourth row's consequence rides beside it: the labels for a depth-4 cut are in the weights (96 of 96 at the innermost level on every seed), so the next arm on depth is a constrained decode of the parse line — spans held to the copy binding, labels free — and not a corpus that shows him depth 4. (Run the next day, the amendment below: it lifts the copy slips and not the cut, so it ships for the parse line and the depth question moves to a dose arm.) The repair row goes to the runtime as it stands: after a bound call fails, hand the failure back and let him re-parse; he does, at nano, on a world where nothing else does. The mid size runs RoPE, two seeds, to say whether the read holds with scale (lever 5).

Instruments, and what was caught before it could count

  • The first build dropped half its draws to the block and lost the deep shapes — 0.7% of turns at depth 3; the plan is drawn before the words and re-phrased under the block, the deep and repaired shapes are lean, and the lookup observation carries its answer alone.
  • The scorer oracle refused the first e2e.w_tree items: a find inside a do is consumed and never said, and the gold answers had counted it.
  • probe_rank.py was serial; batched, held within 1e-4 nats of serial with a control (check_rank_batch.py), which took the rank stage from fifty minutes to ten.
  • The structure read's length confound, above: named, floored on B, and read as a bound.
  • Six checkpoints through check_decode.py, all parity.

Limits

Nano, on a dummy world, and the held-out gain is on held-out cores under trained modifiers: with both held out he reads at the floor, so a fragment still votes when nothing around it is familiar. The depth-4 items are an extrapolation from a thin base — 8.1% of turns at depth 3, one nesting seen more than once — and the structure read that says the preference reaches two levels down is unpinned. The cross-world row is 3 to 11 of 120 and is reported, not claimed. Nothing here touches the shipped checkpoint.

Amended 2026-09-14 — two reads with no training, on the same six checkpoints

The owner asked whether depth 4 had been shown to work, and the honest answer above rested on one unpinned instrument. Two more were built the same day, both decode-time, both run on the six W checkpoints and B beside the first mid-size run: the length-free structure read, and the parse line decoded under its own grammar.

The length-free structure read (probe_structure.py's div columns): the deep and flat lines share every token up to the first that differs — the deep span continuing where the flat one closes, or the group carrier opening where a member would — and at that one position a single distribution gives a probability to each. No length enters it; chance is a coin; B is the floor.

The grammar removes the copy slips and not the cut. Bound, every trained and held-out row rises by four to twelve cells at every seed — wrong_span misses become passes, and a span that is not in the request can no longer be written — and the innermost pair of a depth-4 tree, given its parent, reads 20 to 62 of 96. Level 3 does not move: 0 to 7 of 96 free and bound, with wrong_span on 63 to 95 of the misses — right arity, right label, the member's span where the group's should be, which is the flat cut the structure read measures from the other side. So the whole-tree parse.w_depth4 stays at zero under the grammar, and stays at zero for one reason that two instruments now agree on.

What this changes. The fourth row's consequence is narrowed: the constrained decode is worth shipping for the parse line (it buys the same four to twelve cells on the rows he can cut, for nothing), and it is not the next arm on depth. The label half of depth 4 is a readout failure; the cut half is an absence, learned as the depths shown rather than as a rule. Whether that absence is a dose — depth 3 is 8.1% of turns, almost every nesting once — or a limit of the size is the question the owner then put, and it is pre-registered as phase C5 in configs/mix_v3_funcworld_dose2.py and _dose3.py: the same world with the depth-3 share at 0.142 and 0.202 delivered, every holdout W's, read on level 3 of this instrument. The rule cell (train 1-4, hold out 5) does not fit a 512 block — a depth-4 row is 274-374 tokens before its calls — and waits on the block decision.

Instruments. The div read and the parse constraint were smoked on B and on three items before the runs; the seven structure cards took a minute each and the seven constrained decodes about twenty, beside the mid-size run's training, with no timing number read from either. The first mid-size seed is training as this is written; its second seed was dropped by the owner in favour of the dose arm, so scale is read from one seed as a direction.

Scale buys the trained world and the language rows, and nothing on the two rows this rung exists for. Trained shapes 94 of 120 against 60-66, unseen entities 84 against 41-42, the repair 45 of 60, the whole tree 56 of 60 under the binding where nano's best is 40, the language rows better by eleven to seventeen per cent — and parse.w_heldout at 14 of 140, inside the nano band, with the three carrier holdouts at 5, 4 and 5; and parse.w_depth4 at zero. The labels are finished at this size (367 of 384 depth-4 cells rank the gold first; level 4 is 96 of 96 at every size and seed) — read as a bound: the floor under every rank and structure number here is a 20M checkpoint, and a ranking instrument answers to size on its own; the 100M floor is nano100m_v1, which never saw a parse world, ranked and structure-read behind the dose chain. Read: two seeds of nano100m_v1 rank the gold depth-4 line first 218 and 214 of 384 against B's 189, so size alone buys the instrument about thirty cells and the trained 367 is mostly capability; at the innermost level the floor is already 84 and 86 of 96, so "96 of 96" at this size is a gain of a dozen over its own floor and not of forty-four; on held-out carriers the floor is 52 and 46 of 254 against 236. On the length-free structure read the 100M floor is a coin at every depth-4 level (46/64/59 and 40/73/52 of 96, gaps within a nat of zero), so the trained checkpoint's 4 of 96 at level 3 sits fifty-five cells below its own size's floor: the flat preference at the unseen depth is learned from the world, not carried in by the trunk, which is what a table of depths does with a depth it does not hold. The inversion at the unseen depth is stronger with scale, not weaker: at level 3 of a depth-4 tree the flat continuation is likelier by four nats, 4 of 96 against nano's 3 to 19, while levels 1 and 2 climb to 88 and 64. The constrained decode reads the same wall from the other side: level 3 stays at 2 of 96 bound with wrong_span on 92 of the misses, the member's span where the group's should be, while every level training showed him rises (the innermost pair to 82 of 96, trained shapes to 100 of 120).

So at one seed the size is not the lever on depth, and it is not the lever on held-out carriers either. What 3.4x the parameters and 2.9x the exposures bought is more of the depths shown and a sharper refusal of the one not shown, which is what a rule learned as a table of depths looks like as the table is learned better. The mid-size read would have been a result at two seeds; the owner spent the second seed on the dose arm (phase C5), which asks the question this leaves: whether the wall moves with how much depth 3 he sees. The trunk decision for PLAN-1-0 §10 gains one number it did not have: RoPE at 100M reads the language rows 11-17% under nano's, in line with finding 132's three per cent at equal size.
