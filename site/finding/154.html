The tag language · 7 October 2026

With no template he completes nothing; inside one, every task runs

The result, as the finding states it

G5, three templates nobody on the world's side wrote: with no template he completes 0 of 1,440 runs, inside one the runtime makes every task executable, and his one-place judgment rides into a two-place composition unchanged (45-47% a soft part against 28% for the rule filler, at the one seed that reads the wish); both registered gates fail as written, the kill does not fire, and choosing among unseen templates is chance

What it cost

zero training. GPU: 20 min for the five execution reads

(215-261 s a checkpoint), 2.2 min for the four selection reads (31-38 s), a 12-row smoke and one 7 s crash. CPU: the runtime, rows, probe and guard, about an hour (18:20-19:20 on 2026-10-07). End to end 18:20-19:55, against an estimate of 1-1.5 h of code and 1.5 h of card.

ROADMAP G5, PLAN-TAG-LANGUAGE rung 5 and §1's experiment: someone other than the author writes a template for a task the model has never seen; with zero retraining, the model with the template, the model without it, and the template with a rule-based filler. Built: corn/worker.py (a template file and its step language — find, send, note — a fresh frame per find showing him what rung 3's traces show before a decision, the runtime's own check: from the goal he wrote, the typed return, the call tree); scripts/make_evals_workers.py -> evals/workers.jsonl (288 rows); scripts/probe_workers.py, gates in its docstring before a decode (f78f00c); scripts/check_workers.py, guard 91. The templates (evals/g5_templates/*.tpl, c7bfd7c) were written by a separate agent session from BRIEF.md and corn/worker.py alone — the owner's call — and committed before the rows, the probe or a decode existed: share_a_place (find one place, text it to a friend), trip_stay_and_eat (a stay and a meal, noted together), day_out_with_friend (lunch then drinks, both texted). Zero training, the owner's call: execution on the five checkpoints that write rung 3's trace (arms E and E2), selection on the four that write use: by clause (arms US and USN). Cards: evals/results/nano20m_v3_fw_filter_*__workers{.json,_items.jsonl} and nano20m_v3_fw_templates_*__workers_select{.json,_items.jsonl}.

Caught before the read

  • The guard's first run: the gold decisions through the interpreter read every soft pick as unread, 386 of 386 — a decoded line comes back without the newline the bind's reader closes on. worker.find supplies it. Unguarded, the template column would have scored zero on judgment.
  • The rule filler's first CPU read: "under 2 km out" matched the price phrase "under {v}" first. Longest phrase first; its goal is then exact on 480 of 480 parts and its hard-only picks 94 of 94.
  • The 12-row smoke showed his goal errors entering the template column, so a pick_only column (the rule's exact goal, his bound pick) was added and committed (e31151b) before the full read — reported, never gated.
  • The first execution read died when a no-template conversation filled the 512 block; such a run now stops there, recorded.

Without a template he completes nothing: 0 of 288 rows at every checkpoint, 1,440 runs. He makes one listing call, usually for one part and sometimes mixing both parts' words ("a place for lunch in Lanilvholm" for a drinks part); he writes a send or note on 3-25 rows of 288 and never a right one — messages from the lookup world ("about 50,000", "about 4 stars") addressed to towns. Inside the template the runtime makes every task executable: the rule filler, with no model at all, completes 65.

His judgment rides into the composition unchanged. A frame is the shape the decision trained in, and a part's soft pick is right at the same rate whichever template holds it — E s2337 pick_only: 35 of 78 (45%) in the one-place template, 73 of 154 (47%) and 70 of 154 (45%) in the two-place ones, against the rule's 28%. On trained pairings he is above the rule's band (12.5) at four checkpoints of five (pick_only 81-99 against 55); on held-out pairings at E s2337 (79) and, with the rule's goal, barely at E2 s2337 (67 against 54 + 12.5). The others sit on the rule.

Where he falls below the rule is his goal. E2 s2337 writes the gold goal on 278 of 480 parts: the wrong field (rating>=50 for "at most 50 a head"), place words as the wish (soft[for a pint in Belorndnia]), an invented assume[...]; under the bind it copied a town as a value (goal: price<=Corondstad). Its template column falls to 43 and recovers to 98 with the rule's goal. The bind holds the form of goal:, not its meaning.

Read by hand (EXPERIMENTS §5.6), every condition on 4 shown rows a checkpoint and 8 held-out soft frames on E s2337: right picks copy the gold description whole ("a calm cafe"); wrong ones copy another ok item's, never an unmet one under the bind. The bound pick sometimes writes two brackets (because[a calm tavern] because[a busy bistro ...]), which the spec allows; the runtime takes the first.

Selection — of 288, chance 96

US s1337 95 US s2338 96 USN s1337 102 USN s2338 104 lexical selector 210

Chance, as predicted. Each seed has a favourite clause rather than a reading — US s1337 writes because[a trip away] on rows asking for lunch and drinks — and the one-place template is almost never chosen (4-5 of 96). A lexical selector over the clauses gets 210. These checkpoints trained use: on concept clauses in another vocabulary; arm U's zero-shot paraphrase test failed the same way (finding 149).

The registered reading

G5a fails as written: template task success above plain by 20 or more of 96 on every template at every checkpoint — it holds on all three at E s2337 and E2 s1337, and on share_a_place everywhere, and misses on a two-place template at the other three. Plain is 0 throughout, so the lift is the template's own task rate, and a two-place task needs two soft picks right: near the rule's 28% a part, about 8% of rows. The bar measured judgment twice over, not programmability, which the rule filler's 65 and plain's 0 answer on their own. G5b fails: above the rule's band at E s2337 (77 against 54 + 12.5) and not at E2 s2337 (47), whose goal is the reason (67 with the rule's goal — a column not gated). The kill does not fire: one checkpoint is above the band.

What it licenses

  • "Template over no-template" holds absolutely, and it is the runtime's. A template written by someone else turns a task he cannot do at all into one that runs, with no retraining — PLAN-TAG-LANGUAGE §1's smaller claim, "a very good local interpreter", read for the first time.
  • "Model over rule filler" holds where rung 3's judgment holds, and no further. The frame neither adds judgment nor loses it; what each seed brought from its single-place training is what it shows inside a composition. That is the design working — composition is call depth, the decision stays local (§5) — and it means the claim's second half rests on rung 3's split, not on anything G5 adds.
  • Selection among unseen templates is the runtime's. He picks at chance; the lexical selector does not. With D-A1.16 already giving the runtime every checkable clause, this extends it: a template's task-shape clauses ("text it to a friend", "two places for one outing") are for the runtime's retrieval, and his use: stays on the soft clause.
  • His goal is the weak line on one seed in five — the bind holds the form and not the field. A rule-written goal is exact on this world; whether the goal should be the runtime's where a rule can write it is §0's own test, and on this world a rule can.

Limits

Nano, zero training, two models (execution on E/E2, selection on US/USN); one model doing both is an arm not run. The rule filler is the strongest a writer of this world could have — its goal rules are the world's own phrase tables — so model over rule is a conservative read. Three templates by one LLM author from a one-page brief; a person's templates, or ones needing an op this language lacks (a fallback, a comparison across two lists), are not read. The plain run is free decoding under a probe-played runtime; a bound plain run is not read. Held-out cells as rung 3's (finding 148), with the set-only floor finding 148 owes still owed here. Every number is a bound through an instrument held by check_workers.py and nothing more.

Amended 2026-10-07 — why each failure happens, read off the items

The owner asked what the failures are made of. Read off the committed __workers_items.jsonl and rung 3's own full-option cards, CPU only.

Wrong picks are rung 3's, inherited unchanged. Seed by seed, the G5 held-out pick rate (pick_only) is the same checkpoint's rung 3 full-option rate on its own trace (__filter_full.json): E s1337 28% against 26%, E s2337 41% against 39%, E2 s1337 33% against 29%, E2 s2337 35% against 34%, E2 s2338 29% against 24%. The frame neither costs nor adds: a wrong pick here is a seed whose wish-reading never installed (finding 148), not the template or the composition. No position habit under the bind — wrong picks spread over first, middle and last ok items. Two seeds hedge, writing two brackets on 57 and 68 of 386 lines; the runtime takes the first, and a later bracket held the gold 16 and 19 times.

No template fails because he runs his one trained shape once and stops. On 276-286 of 288 rows the plain run ends on a say after a single list-goal-pick; of 960 two-place rows, one made a second listing call. Every trained document ends at its first say, so nothing after it — the second place, the send, the note — is ever written. Where he calls the message or note tool (7-97 rows), it is first, before any listing, with arm F's lookup answers as the message ("about 50,000") and a town as the recipient.

Selection does not read the request; it picks the clause shaped like the ones it trained on. Three of four checkpoints write because[a trip away] on 202-228 of 288 rows, and the split of choices barely moves with the gold template. The clauses chosen open on "a" and are short noun phrases — the form of every concept clause arms US and USN trained on ("a hushed interior", "a converted mill"); share_a_place's clauses all open otherwise ("one place to go", "text it to a friend", "tell someone where to meet") and are chosen 1-4% of the time. The register, not the meaning.

E2 s2337's goals default to the most common training shape. It writes a hard and a soft term on 61 of 94 hard-only parts (the soft one made of place words) and an assume[...] hard term on 36 of 94 soft-only parts; the other four checkpoints do either on 0-3 of 94. It writes the hs shape — 60% of traces — instead of counting what the request holds. Why this seed alone is not read here; whether rung 3's own goal column showed it was never scored by shape.

Amended 2026-10-07 — the two causes, tested

Both probes registered before they ran (7cc5da5's evals/g5_shape_probe/README.md; the goal read as stated to the owner), zero training, about 3.6 min and 20 min of the card.

Selection follows the clause's form, tested by changing only the form. evals/g5_shape_probe/: the author's templates copied, one template's use when reworded to open on "a" or not, meaning kept. Choices of 288 on the four US/USN checkpoints:

share_a_place trip_stay_and_eat as written 8-34 131-228 share's clauses opened on "a" 171-250 10-83 "a trip away" -> "going away on a trip" 8-33 0-3

When "a trip away" goes, its votes move to the other "a" clause ("a meal and then drinks with a friend", 243-275). Accuracy stays at chance in every variant (95-114 of 288). The form decides and the meaning does not, as predicted: arms US and USN trained use: on concept clauses that are all a short "a" noun phrase, and that is what they learned to look for. Cards: evals/results/nano20m_v3_fw_templates_*__workers_select_{a_share,no_a_trip}*.

E2 s2337's goal habit is its own, on its own task. Rung 3's probe (probe_filter.py, its 528 rows, bound) on the five checkpoints: E2 s2337 writes the gold goal on 284 of 528 against 522-527 for the other four; on hard-only rows it adds a wish on 67 of 96, on soft-only rows a limit or an assume[...] on 89 of 144, and it invents wishes no request holds (goal: km<3 soft[for an anniversary]). The others do either on 0-3. So G5 did not cause it: the seed trained into the majority hs shape. Cards: evals/results/nano20m_v3_fw_filter_*__filter_goal{.json,_items.jsonl}.

What the two say together, the owner's reading of them: where a failure has a cause here, it is a feature the training held constant — one clause form, a 60% goal shape — read as the cue. A corpus that varies every surface feature the label should not depend on denies those shortcuts; the 1.0 corpus is one run, so it is a design rule for every slice, not a fix for these arms.
