A checker that reads the candidate answer works; re-ranking does not

The result, as the finding states it

The verifier that reads the span it verifies clears the verifier bar, and the re-ranker cannot choose better than the span head

What it cost

0 s of new scoring on record; it reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m_v4_ft4g). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

Run 2026-09-02, after the eighty-fifth finding, on the frozen corn_jr_260m_v4_ft4g trunk. Three verifier heads on cross-fitted labels and cross-fitted features off the same fold span heads, one seed each, then the one that worked replicated on the second seed. The span head, reduce, corn/extract.py, every scorer and every frozen row are untouched; the verifier is a binary head that decides whether the span head's argmax is spoken.

The eighty-fifth finding ended with the honest-label verifier reading the say marker alone and seeing "he is about to be wrong" without seeing "he is about to be right", and named the next head: one that reads the argmax pair's own rows. This file is that head, the two arms beside it that say what its gain is made of, and the re-ranker that tests whether the same head can also choose which span.

The first cross-fitted verifier had an honest label and dishonest features. The eighty-fifth finding's --features std --label verifier arm read its three scalars off feats_reader_built_s1.pt — the deployed head's s_best on windows it memorised, mean well above what it reads on Population A — beside a label from fold heads. scripts/crossfit_hits.py now writes label, scalars and candidates from the fold heads in one pass, so every input the verifier trains on is out of fold, and the training s_best reads 11.3 +- 6.2 rather than a memorised head's. A gold pair is in the fold heads' top five on 82.1% of gold-bearing windows; their argmax hits on 51.4%.

Arm A is the head the eighty-fifth finding asked for, and on seed 1 it is the first reader on this record whose joint on Population A is 600 (seed 2 reads 575; §"Seed 2"). The previous best was 578. It gets there by refusing: unanswerable silent reads 233 of 282 (82.6%, against the plan's 85% target and the record's 207), precision 66.5% against 59.4%, acc | att 0.730 against 0.664, and RIVAL falls from 80 to 42 and LOCATION from 134 to 74 as the head declines the windows the span head would have read wrong. On the frozen file its joint(tight) is 267 against R1's bar of 270 — the closest any arm has come to the eighty-third finding's primary bar — with abstain 165, and (f) passes on both files on both readings: 0.776 / 0.781 frozen, 0.805 / 0.737 phrase.

At the joint optimum it fails PRIMARY's right by four (367 against 371) and under the bar protocol it passes both halves: 381 right at exactly 200 silent. This is the eighty-fifth finding's protocol point made concrete: a head that ranks better lets the joint optimum choose a conservative point, because a correct silence is worth exactly a correct answer to joint and this head can buy silences cheaply. The bar's own shape — right subject to the silence floor — reads the same head at 381, ten over the null head's 371 at the same floor. The protocol is the owner's call and it is now worth fourteen passages on this arm.

The species table says what the pair rows bought. Against B — the same head without the rows — A moves RIVAL 59 → 42 and LOCATION 101 → 74 while REFUSED_RIGHT goes 78 → 113 and REFUSED_WRONG 112 → 136 at the joint optimum. The rows make the head a better judge of wrong: with the span's own states in front of it, it refuses the same-type rival the say marker could not see. What it does not do is admit more of the right ones at that operating point; under the bar protocol, where the two arms are read at the same silence, A reads 381 against B's 375, so it does admit more of them once the threshold is allowed to spend the silence it saved.

Arm C, the re-ranker, fails, and the failure is informative. Its held-out pick rate — over windows with a gold among the fold head's top five, how often the verifier's favourite is gold — reads 0.649 at epoch 0 and never above 0.657, against the fold head's own argmax being gold on 62.6% of those same windows (51.4 of 82.1). The verifier ranks candidates almost exactly as the span head already ranks them. On Population A it speaks a different span on the windows where it disagrees and reads 348 right, CLIPPED doubling to 44 and AUC | attempted collapsing to 0.640, because its logit is now doing two jobs and is calibrated for neither. A concatenation MLP over [h_last; h_i; h_j] with the pair's scalars cannot tell the singer from the composer any better than the pair scorer with a pointer term can. The "which" question stays a representation question — R2's, PLAN-EXTRACTION-READER.md §7's — and the verifier's job is "whether". The re-ranker is retired as a design; its inference path stays in read_cached behind the head's rerank attribute so the reading can be reproduced.

What replicates, and it is the mechanism. RIVAL halves on both seeds (80 → 42, 85 → 47) and LOCATION falls by nearly half (134 → 74, 137 → 81); precision rises on both (59.4 → 66.5, 59.5 → 61.3) and acc | att with it (0.664 → 0.730, 0.666 → 0.701); the frozen joint(tight) reads 267 on both seeds against R1's bar of 270, and the frozen all-items verifier AUC reads 0.781 and 0.788, the first two readings above 0.75 on that floor. What does not replicate is the joint at the record's protocol. 600 on seed 1 is 575 on seed 2, and 575 is below the null head's 578: seed 2's joint optimum lands at the same 207 silences as the null head and three fewer right answers, where seed 1's landed at 233 silences and four fewer. The joint optimum is choosing a different trade on each seed of a head that can make either, which is the eighty-fifth finding's protocol point a third time. And the verifier bar (f) passes on both files on seed 1 and on neither attempted subset on seed 2 (0.732 frozen, 0.652 phrase), while the all-items frozen number passes on both; (f) is one seed's pass and one seed's near miss, and is reported as that.

So the honest statement about arm A is: a verifier that reads the span's own rows is a precision lever that replicates — it halves the same-type rival on both seeds by refusing it — and buys about ten passages at the silence floor on both seeds, twenty with the extent rule; its headline joint and its verifier-bar pass are seed 1's. It does not move REFUSED_RIGHT down on either seed at the joint optimum and does move it under the bar protocol (the ten it buys are those); the species the owner asked about is now bounded by the head's operating point rather than by its signal.

Two joint is not a tolerance, it is the whole trade. Across a band the objective cannot tell apart, a fold's right moves by up to 17 and its silences by the same 17 in the other direction; on the population that is plus or minus fifteen to thirty right answers at one and the same joint. The "optimum" the record has been quoting is where the lowest t in a flat band happened to fall — cross_validate takes the lowest t among ties, which is why the fold values differ so much between harvests that read the same joint. On the pair-rows head seed 1's fold 0 has a 134-threshold plateau whose two ends read 189 and 168 right on the held-out fold; that is the 600-against-575 gap in one row. So the three-protocol table in the eighty-fifth finding is not three preferences. joint does not choose; the bar's shape does, and R-T's one-SE rule chooses the wrong end. The plan's §6.2 recommendation stands, and it is now the only determinate option.

What ships and what does not

  • Arm A, the pair-rows verifier, is the arm with a claim on shipping under the bar protocol, +10 / +9 / +18 at the silence floor on three seeds (+18 / +13 / +27 with the extent rule) and the same-type rival halved on all three; under the record's joint protocol it reads +22 / -3 / +19, a protocol the plateau section shows cannot choose. The protocol decision is the shipping decision, and the two-fold label is the one that ships. It touches no weight of the span head and is a head file beside it; wiring is R3's, with read_cached's gather of the argmax rows as the reference.
  • scripts/crossfit_hits.py now writes label, scalars and top-K candidates from fold heads in one pass; train_null_head.py --cands --pair-rows trains arm A and --rerank arm C; read_cached reads both.
  • The re-ranker does not ship and no further arm in that family is proposed: its ceiling was measured high and its pick rate did not move off the span head's.
  • The threshold protocol is now the largest free variable on the shipping arm and is the owner's to settle once.

Limits

One trunk, fp32, one seed for arms B and C and for the four-fold label, three for A. Every fold head is weaker than the deployed head (held-out exact-pair 0.505 and 0.507 against 0.541), so the cross-fitted label and features are a harder verifier's, and the deployed head's argmax rows at inference are a slightly different distribution from the fold heads' at training — the stacking rule's usual cost, accepted rather than removed. The frozen and phrase files arbitrate nothing threshold-dependent (eighty-fourth finding); (f) is an AUC and does not depend on the threshold except through the attempted subset, and the all-items number is printed beside it every time. The selection metric for arm C is candidate-level balanced accuracy, and the pick rate that actually describes a re-ranker was printed beside it and not selected on; a re-ranker selected on its pick rate would be a different arm, and the curve (0.649 → 0.657 → 0.628) says it would not have been a better one. The extent stack's d is fixed at the value both seeds' curves chose in the eighty-fifth finding, not searched here.
