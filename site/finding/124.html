Refitting the reader on the social model reads 517 at runtime

The result, as the finding states it

The reader is re-fit on the social8 trunk and reads 514 of 721 on the harvest and 517 at runtime at its own threshold, nine under the ft4g adapter read as a proxy on the same trunk, and the pair ships with the swap guarded

What it cost

0 s of new scoring on record; it reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social8). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

2026-09-05, the seventh section's four steps on the last trunk that landed. scripts/train_reader_whole.py --ckpt checkpoints/corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social8/ckpt.pt --seed 1 --tag social8, run eagerly (--no-compile, bf16 autocast on; §3 for why), three epochs in 3,138 s at ~20k tok/s, epoch 1 selected on the held-out pooled metric, checkpoints/reader_whole_social8_s1.pt written with arm 8's sha256 (87c395d623e0) inside it. Then the script's own Population A read at the bar protocol, then probe_reader_runtime.py with the new file on its own trunk at the new constant.

The re-fit reads finding 112's 513 to the passage — the whole-passage arm's first read on ft4g before the grounding repair — and nine under finding 116's 523 for the same adapter on its own trunk under the repaired rule, at four fewer correct silences. Runtime is three over harvest, the same fp32-over-bf16 shape finding 117 (b) traced. The species is the whole-passage arm's: the three window species gone, RIVAL up, CLIPPED up, and RIVAL is where the nine went (111 against 94 on ft4g seed 1).

The number that has to be at the top: the ft4g adapter, read as a proxy on this exact trunk, reads 526 at 195. So on the social8 trunk, at one seed each, the delta fitted to ft4g's states reads nine more right answers than the delta fitted to social8's own states. Finding 118 said the old adapter survives the move; this says a fresh fit does not obviously beat it. Three readings are open and none is settled by one seed:

  • seed spread. Finding 112's two seeds on ft4g read 523 / 517 under the repaired rule, a spread of six; nine is one-and-a-half of that.
  • the eager run. The ft4g adapter was trained under torch.compile; this one was not (§3). Same autocast, same arithmetic, different kernel fusion; finding 117 put the compile-vs-eager inference gap at three passages, and no number exists for training.
  • the trunk. Four fine-tunes stacked on ft4g may have moved the states the head reads at layers {8, 12, 16} in a way that fits worse from scratch than the old delta ports. That would be the head-portability finding the seventh section named, in the direction nobody expected.

2. The pair ships, per protocol, with the comparison recorded

The seventh section's protocol is the re-fit, and the fingerprint is the design: load_adapter refuses the ft4g file on the social8 trunk by hash, and re-stamping it to serve the better number would be the rule the port built being bent on its first use. So the pair that ships is the re-fit at its own threshold, and the comparison above is the first thing the next session reads. What would settle it is a second seed of the re-fit (~50 min eager, ~55 compiled) read the same way; if it also reads under the proxy, the head-portability question is answered in favour of porting the delta and the design should say so.

The swap, each held by a guard:

corn/reader.py WHOLE_THRESHOLD = -1.280 (was -1.174) harness/serverconfig.py reader_adapter = checkpoints/reader_whole_social8_s1.pt reader_whole_threshold = -1.280 harness/backends/read.py WHOLE_ADAPTER = checkpoints/reader_whole_social8_s1.pt server.json ckpt = checkpoints/corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social8/ckpt.pt scripts/check_model.py "the shipped adapter names the shipped trunk" now reads the pair from ServerConfig and server.json rather than repeating it

check_extract.py holds ServerConfig().reader_whole_threshold equal to WHOLE_THRESHOLD and the two thresholds not equal to each other; check_model.py asserts the shipped adapter carries the shipped trunk's hash and still refuses a different trunk. reader stays generate — decision 7 is the owner's, unchanged by any of this — so what changed for a user tonight is the trunk server.json names, which carries the social class, the consolation family, the second turn and the depth generalisation of findings 119 to 123, and the adapter that is available behind --reader head_whole.

3. The compiled path, broken and fixed

The first launch died at torch.cat inside _trunk_states under torch.compile(dynamic=True): "cat expects at least one tensor". Reproduced on a four-layer toy trunk in a minute: with torch 2.11, dynamic=True treats the ints of a tuple argument as symbolic, i in layers never resolves true while tracing, the tapped list stays empty. The same function with layers closed over as a constant traces and matches eager on two shapes; dynamic=False also traces. GradStates now closes over its layers (commit a29b527e), so the plan's compiled recipe is back for the next trunk; this re-fit ran eagerly because the card clock mattered more than twenty minutes.

Limits

One seed of the re-fit against one seed of the old adapter, on one population, and the comparison that matters is nine passages at n = 721. The eager-versus-compiled training difference is unmeasured. Nothing here is a live session; packs are still unmeasured under any reader.
