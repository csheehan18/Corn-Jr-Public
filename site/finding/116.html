A fix to the copy check lifts the whole-passage reader to 523

The result, as the finding states it

grounded now tests the span as said, first novel word to last with the frame words inside it kept, as one verbatim run of the window; the two frozen checkpoints re-score byte-identically on every pass count, one refused reply a checkpoint moving between failure buckets, and the whole-passage adapter reads 523 and 517 with AGGREGATION at 2 and 1

What it cost

62 s of scoring on the 2 cards first cited here (corn_jr_260m_v4__grounded115, corn_jr_260m_v4_ft4g__grounded115); it also reads 3 cards scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m_v4, corn_jr_260m_v4_ft4g, nano20m_v3). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

corn/extract.py::grounded, scripts/check_extract.py, 2026-09-05. The owner took finding 115's repair the same day ("it was put in place when results were much worse"), so this is the rule-4 record: the change, the guard, the re-score of two frozen checkpoints with every moved row listed, and the reader numbers under the new rule. evals/results/corn_jr_260m_v4_ft4g__grounded115.json, corn_jr_260m_v4__grounded115.json (the --only extract,paired cards), whole_causal_s{1,2}_grounded115.json.

0. The change

Before, the contiguity test ran over the reply's novel words alone, the frame words having been stripped out from wherever they sat:

novel = [w for w in said if w in window_words and w not in question_words and w not in source_words and w not in FRAME] return len(set(novel)) == 1 or longest_span(" ".join(novel), window) >= 2

After, it runs over the span as said from the first novel word to the last, and the whole of that must be a verbatim run of the window:

core = said[novel_at[0]:novel_at[-1] + 1] return longest_span(" ".join(core), window) == len(core)

Two things moved, and the second was found by the guard written for the first. Keeping the inner frame words is what lets "best-of-seven", "Stephen A. Douglas" and "Coton in the Elms" through. Requiring the whole core rather than any two contiguous words is what keeps "1858 of Illinois" out: with the frame word kept, the old >= 2 would have passed it on the strength of "of Illinois" alone, and two window facts joined by a frame word is the invention the rule exists to refuse. The first draft of the change had >= 2, the new guard case failed on it, and the rule was tightened before any number was read. Frame words outside the span ("It's", "according to Wikipedia") are still stripped, which is what lets a one-word fact through; the no-invention test and the novel-word requirement are unchanged.

scripts/check_extract.py gains four cases: the three spans above are grounded, and "1858 of Illinois" is not. All prior cases still pass.

1. Rule 4: the frozen checkpoints, re-scored

python -m corn.evals --only extract,paired on corn_jr_260m_v4_ft4g and corn_jr_260m_v4, against their frozen cards:

row ft4g frozen -> now v4 frozen -> now extract.span 97/200 -> 97/200 94/200 -> 94/200 extract.abstain 111/200 -> 111/200 121/200 -> 121/200 extract.brevity 395/400 -> 395/400 399/400 -> 399/400 extract.paired 115/128 -> 115/128 117/128 -> 117/128

Every pass count is byte-identical. Inside extract.span's failure buckets one reply a checkpoint moves: invented 13 -> 12 on both, the reply now landing in wrong_span — a span the passage does contain, framed with a function word inside it, that the old rule refused as invention and the new one scores as what it is, a wrong answer. (The near_miss_*, span_loose and span_tight rows in the new cards are rows the harness has added since those cards were frozen, finding 82, and are not this change.) The generative reader's numbers do not move because it rarely emits a multi-word span with a function word inside it exactly.

3. The guards

scripts/check_extract.py passes with the four new cases. check_all.py reads 65 of 67 with the card idle: the two that fail, check_churn.py (seven nano20m_v3 extract attributions no longer re-derive to their scorecards' failure buckets) and check_copyable.py (relpairs.txt's window and search-query value counts), fail identically with this change stashed — both predate it and belong to other work, and are reported here so nobody attributes them to the rule.
