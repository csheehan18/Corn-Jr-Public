Ranking candidates together makes the checker worse on both seeds

The result, as the finding states it

The listwise verifier makes cross-window outranking worse on both seeds, so B2 fails the bar it was written for, and the metric it was pre-registered to be selected on ranks the three arms in exactly the reverse of how Population A ranks them, twice

What it cost

Six verifier heads, ~15 s an epoch on cached states. evals/results/null lw{00,05,10}_s{1,2}.json and their Population A harvests.

0. What the arm became, and why it is not the arm that was written

PLAN-READER-RESIDUE.md §7.4 records the redirection and the ninety-seventh finding measured it: the arm as first specified — hard negatives from the gold passage's own non-gold windows, a 50/50 mix pre-registered against one alternative — has no variable in it, because 56,092 of the verifier's 100,000 training nulls are already siblings, 96.8% of every absent_window. What was missing was not the negatives but the comparison: a pointwise binary loss over a shuffled pool never asks which of one passage's windows carries the answer, and that is the failure the ninety-third and ninety-sixth findings name at 25.5% and 22.2% of the answerable half.

So B2 is the listwise variant and only that:

loss = BCE(pointwise, pos_weight unchanged)

  • LAMBDA * softmax cross-entropy over the passage's own windows

with the epoch's order over passages so a group arrives whole in a batch, every row still seen exactly once, and the BCE kept because a listwise term has no absolute calibration and the unanswerable half is decided against a constant. The architecture, the features, the pair rows, the cross-fitted verifier label, the article split and the harvest are all the shipped arm's. 21,273 training passages carry a positive and a sibling (72,713 rows, mean group 3.42) and 1,158 are held out.

1. The selection metric, pre-registered, and the arm loses on it monotonically

Selection was pre-registered as held-out AUC on the sibling-negative split — positives against the siblings of their own passages — because balanced accuracy is pooled over a file whose negative half is 42% absent_topical and cannot see the comparison this arm changes. The one-variable control is the same recipe and the same selection rule with the listwise term removed:

lambda seed 1 seed 2 sibling AUC rank acc sibling AUC rank acc 0.0 ctrl 0.8371 0.7444 0.8047 0.7009 0.5 0.8307 0.7504 0.7944 0.7009 1.0 0.8278 0.7306 0.7890 0.6965

the shipped nullpr head, scored not retrained: 0.8343 / 0.7453 (seed 1)

Monotone decreasing in LAMBDA on both seeds. A loss term that exists to make the head rank a passage's own windows better makes held-out ranking of a passage's own windows worse, consistently, at both values. Under the pre-registered rule the arm that ships is LAMBDA = 0.5 — the better of the two — and no arm beats its control.

And the control is not a straw man. The shipped verifier already reads sibling AUC 0.8343 and within-passage rank accuracy 0.7453: on three quarters of held-out passages carrying both, every gold-bearing window already outscores every sibling. 1 - 0.7453 = 25.5%, which is Population A's own outranking rate to the tenth of a point. The head is not underperforming its training distribution. 25% is what that performance is.

LAMBDA = 1.0 is the best arm on both seeds and the worst arm on the metric it was to be selected by, on both seeds. +16 and +7 right against its control, -5 and -16 wrong, correct silence held at 197 and raised to 200, and acc | att up 0.015 and 0.024 at an attempt rate that does not rise. That last clause is the one that matters: this is not a willingness re-tune. Seed 1 reads +16 right and -5 wrong at an attempt rate 0.016 above its control's and 0.005 below the shipped head's; seed 2 reads +7 and -16 at an attempt rate 0.013 below its control's. More right answers and fewer wrong ones out of the same or fewer attempts.

The seed-2 control is the shipped head. LAMBDA = 0.0 reads 388 / 172 / 196 with an identical species table, so the sibling-AUC selection picked the same epoch balanced accuracy did and the control is not a second draw.

3. The mechanism bar fails, and it fails in the wrong direction

The bar PLAN-READER-RESIDUE.md §7.4 wrote for this arm before it ran was the unconditional outranking rate falls by at least half — probe_reader_rank.py read (b), the share of the 580 answerable passages carrying both kinds of window on which a no-gold window outranks every gold-bearing one. It is a property of the head's margins and does not depend on a threshold.

outranking rate seed 1 seed 2 nullpr (ships) 148 25.5% 129 22.2% lambda 0.0 ctrl 147 25.3% 129 22.2% lambda 0.5 161 27.8% 156 26.9% lambda 1.0 148 25.5% 145 25.0%

It does not halve. It does not fall. It rises on both seeds at both values — +2.5 and +4.7 points at LAMBDA = 0.5, +0.2 and +2.8 at LAMBDA = 1.0. The bar is missed by its own sign, and §1 says why it was always going to be: the loss made held-out within-passage ranking worse, and Population A is where that shows up unmasked.

So LAMBDA = 1.0's +16 and +7 are not the mechanism the arm claimed, and the arm's own failing branch is the reading: if right rises while the outranking rate is flat, the gain came from somewhere else and the arm has not shown what it claims. What did move, on both seeds, is AGGREGATION — 60 → 52 and 50 → 47 — and per-attempt precision. A within-passage contrastive term appears to have made the head more discriminating about whether a window carries the answer without making it better at ranking which one does, which is a different head from the one §7.4 argued for.

4. The bars, read as written

bar lambda 0.5 (selected) lambda 1.0 outranking rate halves MISSED, rises MISSED, rises right >= 420 MISSED (388 / 386) MISSED (405 / 395) u.silent >= 199 / 196 (each seed's floor) MISSED / MISSED MISSED by 2 / MET acc | att not below shipped MET / MISSED MET / MET

B2 does not ship. The pre-registered selection picks LAMBDA = 0.5, which is flat on Population A and worse on the mechanism; LAMBDA = 1.0 clears neither PRIMARY nor the mechanism bar, and taking it because it reads best on Population A would be choosing an arm on the population it is measured on — which is the error R3 explicitly refused when it declined to ship seed 3 on the strength of its Population A row.

What LAMBDA = 1.0 is, instead, is the strongest unexplained consistent signal in this workstream: +16 and +7 right, -5 and -16 wrong, silence held, acc | att up on both seeds, at no cost in attempt rate, from a loss term aimed at something it demonstrably did not move. It is recorded here as that and is not banked.

What this cannot say

It cannot say what LAMBDA = 1.0 did. §3 rules out the mechanism it was built for and names what co-moved (AGGREGATION, precision); it does not establish a cause. Two seeds agreeing on the direction of a 16-and-7-passage effect is worth recording and is not worth deploying.

The selection metric is not vindicated by being anti-predictive. Held-out sibling AUC could be the wrong metric, or Population A's right column could be reading a threshold effect the bar protocol did not remove; nothing here separates those. What is measured is that they disagree in the same direction twice.

One value of LAMBDA between 0 and 0.5 was not tried, and neither was one above 1.0. Two values were pre-registered and two were run; a sweep chosen after seeing this table would be a sweep on Population A.

The listwise groups are the cache's, not the runtime's. A passage contributes a group only when both a gold-bearing window and a sibling survived the 100,000-row null cap, which is 21,273 of the 57,064 passages with a positive window. A denser cache is a different arm.

Two seeds, one trunk, one population, Population A under hybrid at each head's own bar-protocol threshold, with the control retrained under the same selection rule so the comparison is one variable.

What follows

The admission lever is not reachable by a loss over these states, which is §7.4's own failing branch: if the outranking rate does not fall, a listwise loss over these states cannot rank a passage's windows, and the ninety-fourth finding's generated frame is the next thing aimed at admission, at a much larger cost. That branch now fires, and the ninety-eighth finding is the independent read on what a generated frame is worth.

The bound the arm was quoted against still stands and is still unspent. ONLY_GOLD reads 515 and 499 of 721 (finding 96); the best arm here reads 405 and 395, which is 21% and 6% of the gap between its control and that bound.

Left unexplained, and it is the first thing the next session should look at: why the arm that ranks a passage's own windows worst on held-out training data reads +16 and +7 on the population, twice, at a held attempt rate. Either the sibling-negative split is not the comparison Population A rewards, or the listwise term buys per-window calibration that the AUC over positives-against-siblings cannot see. A paired read of LAMBDA = 1.0 against its control on the 580 passages, split by whether the passage's gold window was admitted, would separate them and costs no GPU.
