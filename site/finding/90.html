A nearby-words rule can't tell a rival answer from the right one

The result, as the finding states it

The locality rule cannot tell a rival from a gold inside one window, so the sixty-sixth finding's runtime repair is closed and the eighty-ninth's revival of it was one inference too far

scripts/probe_locality.py, 2026-09-03. Replay only — no GPU, no decode, no weight read, no tokenizer — over evals/results/nullpr_s{1,2}_popa.json, the shipped pair-rows verifier. docs/NEXT-AFTER-89.md item 2. The baselines reproduce the eighty-ninth finding to the passage: 367 right / joint 600 and 368 right / joint 575, RIVAL 42 passages / 49 admitted windows and 47 / 62.

Forty-two arms — three readings of the rule, seven tolerances, two seeds — and not one of them is a gain on any column. The best joint in the whole grid is −33 and −29. The owner's precision-only trade does not arise, because the arm does not buy precision either.

What was being priced, and why it looked live

The sixty-sixth finding named a runtime repair — "reject an extraction whose span is not local to the question's distinguishing term" — and never defined the distinguishing term. The eighty-ninth finding revived it: on the shipped verifier RIVAL's spoken span is the earlier one 74.5% of the time against a measured positional-indifference null of 52–53%, +22 points, pooled p = 2.5e-07, where the superseded head had given +12 and the arm had been set aside. That is a real and replicated fact and it is not the fact an arm needs; §3 below is why.

The three readings, all of them runtime-computable, none of them the gold

Positions are content-word indices, corn.extract.content_words — the space span_of and longest_span already work in. Q the question's content words, S the spoken span's, W the window's.

absent_span D = (Q - S - FRAME) & W. The eighty-ninth finding §4's first reading and the cheapest: grounded already computes Q. unique absent_span, keeping only terms occurring ONCE in the window. The finding's own "once iron is discounted as shared", made computable without the gold, and the only reading that reproduces the sixty-sixth finding's example. all_words D = Q & W. The control §4 predicts is degenerate.

The rule is a mask edit and nothing else: admitted says which windows clear the margin, this silences some of them, and replies_for, corn.extract.reduce and score_extract_tight are the runtime's, untouched. A silenced window has the runtime author its decline exactly as an unadmitted one does. When D is empty the rule does not fire — no evidence to reject on is not a reason to reject.

The head-noun reading is unpriced rather than approximated. It needs a part-of-speech decision, there is no tagger in the repo, no dependency may be added, and every dependency-free heuristic tried picks the wrong word on the sixty-sixth finding's own example: first content word gives boiling, last gives iron, the token before of gives point.

1. The rule fires on the answers it must protect at the rate it fires on rivals

This is the read that decides it, and it is one number. A rejection rule is a lever only if it fires more on what it should silence than on what it must not. CORRECT is every admitted window on an answerable passage whose own span scores tight — 540 and 550 of them.

ratio of fire rates, RIVAL over CORRECT seed 1 seed 2 absent_span k = 3 1.17 1.05 k = 5 1.13 1.21 k = 8 0.98 0.99 unique k = 3 1.18 1.03 k = 5 1.07 1.11 k = 8 1.04 1.07 all_words every k ≤ 0.99 ≤ 0.99

Nothing in the grid separates the two populations, and the two seeds do not even agree on which cell comes closest. unique k=3 reads 1.18 on seed 1 and 1.03 on seed 2; absent_span k=5 reads 1.13 and 1.21 but 1.17 and 1.05 one tolerance down. A ratio of 1.2 against a base rate of 52–59% is not a usable trade: silencing 30 of 49 rival windows costs 279 of 540 correct ones on seed 1, and 34 of 62 costs 288 of 550 on seed 2.

all_words is worse than indiscriminate — it is anti-discriminative. Its ratio is below 1 at every tolerance on both seeds, falling to 0.31 and 0.47 at k=8: it preferentially silences the right answers. The eighty-ninth finding predicted this reading would be degenerate on long questions. It is, and the degeneracy has a sign.

Right, joint and acc | att fall on all 42 arms, and precision falls on 41 of them. The best joint in the grid is −33 and −29 and it belongs to all_words at the loosest tolerance measured — that is, to the setting closest to not applying the rule at all. Every column is monotone in the tolerance: this is the shape of a loss, not of a trade with an optimum somewhere inside it.

One cell beats a baseline precision anywhere and it does not survive contact. all_words k=0 on seed 1 reads 66.7% against 66.5% — +0.2 points bought with 335 of 367 right answers, 32 right at an attempt rate of 6.2% — and the same cell on seed 2 reads 60.4% against a 61.3% baseline, at the identical 32 right. It is the arm silencing almost everything, and what survives is not more accurate: acc | att reads 0.711 on both seeds against 0.730 and 0.701.

3. Why the eighty-ninth finding's +22 points did not carry, and it is not a contradiction

Both statements are true and they are about different objects:

  • Across windows. RIVAL's spoken span sits earlier than the gold 74.5% of the time against a 52–53% null. That compares RIVAL windows to a positional-indifference process and to REFUSED_WRONG, and it is what forbids one bar reading both species.
  • Inside one window. A rejection rule has to say this span rather than that span, on evidence available before the gold is known. Position relative to a question term is that evidence, and §1 measures it at a ratio of 1.0–1.2.

A population-level tilt of twenty-two points is compatible with almost no per-window separation, and that is what happened: the gold sits early too — the eighty-ninth finding §3 measures golds at mean relative position 0.394 and 62.5% of first-quartile windows carrying the gold. Both the rival and the gold are near the front, so "is the span near a question word" hits both. The eighty-ninth finding's revival of this arm inferred a per-window discriminator from a cross-window statistic, and that inference is what this finding refutes.

The ceiling that was known before the run also holds: 13 of 49 and 16 of 62 RIVAL windows put the spoken span at or after the gold, where locality cannot tell the two apart in principle.

What this cannot say

It is not a test of the rule the sixty-sixth finding imagined. content_words does no stemming, so on that finding's own fixture — "the boiling point of iron" over "...melts at 1538 °C and boils at 2862 °C" — boiling is not boils and the distinguishing term is absent from W under every reading built here. absent_span gives D = {point, iron} and unique gives D = {point}. A stemmer is a new dependency and a hand-written suffix table is a scorer-shaped object needing its own guard, so what is priced is the rule this repo can build today. A stemmed version could read differently and nothing here forecloses it; what §1 forecloses is the unstemmed family, and §3 gives a reason to expect a stemmed one to fail the same way.

Two seeds, one trunk, one population. Everything is Population A at the shipped hybrid reduce and the CV'd threshold. The tolerance grid is 0,1,2,3,5,8,12 content words and a finer grid would not change a monotone column.

It says nothing about the corpus arm. The sixty-sixth finding named two repairs and called them not-alternatives: the runtime rejection priced here, and paired-property windows where the gold is the second value often enough that "take the first" scores badly. Only the first is closed.

What follows

Close item 2 and do not spend GPU on A4's locality half. The eighty-ninth finding left this arm "live for those 42/47 once its distinguishing term is defined". It is defined three ways here and priced, and it is dead.

RIVAL has no cheap lever now. 42 and 47 passages, positional across windows and not separable within one, and the re-ranker was already retired by the eighty-sixth finding for being unable to choose better than the span head's own argmax. What is left for it is A4's trunk arm, restated — which is handoff item 3 and is now the only thing item 2 could have replaced.

The ceiling does not move and that is the point. The locality rule was never in PLAN-READER-RESIDUE.md §2's arithmetic — it was the cheap route to A4's target, a no-GPU rejection that could have bought some of RIVAL without touching the trunk. Closing it leaves the 65.5%/65.3% ceiling where the shipped-head re-run put it and removes the only way of approaching it that cost nothing. REFUSED_WRONG at 136/139 is still the plurality and still has nothing aimed at it — handoff item 4, and now the only item whose value has gone up.

Left unexplained: absent_span and unique cross a ratio of 1.0 from above at k=8 on both seeds and fall below it by k=12, while all_words is below 1 everywhere. Something about mid-range tolerances is weakly right-signed on both seeds and it is not strong enough to use; whether it is the same thing on both is unmeasured.
