A generated question frame invents relations more than a hand-written one

The result, as the finding states it

The generated frame names a relation the question never carried on three quarters of the frames that carry an anchor, and inverts 29.3% there against the hand-written lexicon's 17.4%, so the frame moved the confabulation upstream; it also fires on no window at all three times as often as the lexicon, makes a call on 10.7% of the population against the lexicon's 18.8%, and misses both pre-registered bars

The frame moved the confabulation upstream. 75.7% of the frames that carry an anchor at all name a relation the question never used, and on the subset of those that make a call the frame inverts 29.3% against the ninety-fourth finding's hand-written lexicon at 17.4% and against the shipped verifier's 23.9/21.1 — the first line the pre-registration asked for if that number came back worse, and it did.

scripts/gen_frames_ft4g.py (one generation pass, 1,003 questions, 25 s) and scripts/probe_frame_gen.py (replay, both seeds), 2026-09-03. evals/results/frames_ft4g_popa.jsonl, evals/results/framegen_s{1,2}.json, and the control at frames_ft4g_popa_rot.jsonl / framegen_rot_s{1,2}.json. Population: the ninety-third finding's read (e), the 580 answerable passages carrying both a gold-bearing and a non-gold-bearing window. anchor_score, the inversion definition and the cross-fitted AUC read are imported from probe_frame_window.py, not restated, so "the same statistic" is enforced by the import.

Nothing was trained, no arm ran, no threshold moved, and the frame never chose or restricted a span. The frame ranks windows and that is the whole of what was tested.

0. The bars, written before the pass, and both are missed

bar generated lexicon coverage makes a call on >= 60% 62/580 10.7% 109/580 18.8% FAIL precision inverts <= 20% where it calls 17/62 27.4% 19/109 17.4% FAIL AUC printed against +0.02, not a gate

cross-fitted AUC, 'this window carries the gold' seed 1 seed 2 margin alone 0.8000 0.8147 margin + generated anchor +0.0008 +0.0006 margin + lexicon anchor -0.0014 -0.0000 margin + both (vs margin + lexicon) +0.0007 +0.0006

Both bars fail on both seeds, and the failing branch the pre-registration wrote for "both fail" applies: the frame idea is closed at the window-ranking level, which was the last position the ninety-third finding left it holding.

The frame's own numbers are identical across the two seeds and that is construction, not replication. One generation pass serves both seeds — the two harvests carry the same 1,003 ids in the same order, asserted at startup — and gold-bearing membership does not depend on the seed either, so 62/580 and 17/62 cannot differ. What the seed moves is the margin, the verdicts and therefore the residue. The ninety-fourth finding's lexicon had the same property and the same caveat.

1. Generation bought raw reach and lost it again at the window

Generation did the thing the ninety-fourth finding said it would, once:

frames carrying at least one anchor generated 416/1003 41.5% lexicon 263/1003 26.2%

And then, on the 580, the reach evaporates:

of the 580 passages generated lexicon no anchor produced at all 350 397 anchor fires on NO window 159 52 anchor fires on EVERY window 9 22 anchor makes a call 62 109

That middle row is the finding. The generated anchor is a word that is not in the passage, three times as often as the hand-written one is. The ninety-fourth finding predicted the opposite in as many words — a model "can produce the passage's own phrasing rather than a guessed synonym" — and the measurement is that the trunk writes in the question's register, not the passage's. where does the this is us family live came back as "the family lives in ___": lives is a perfectly good frame word and the passage says Pittsburgh in a sentence that never uses it.

The +47 passages generation buys on the "no anchor" row are more than paid back by the +107 it adds to "fires on nothing".

2. Most of what the trunk writes is the question again

frames carrying the blank ___ 683/1003 68.1% frames with a token stripped 38/1003 3.8% frames leaving no anchor at all 587/1003 58.5%

Three frames in five are a restatement: every content word already in the question, or in STOP. "what states do not allow daylight savings time" → "the states do not allow daylight savings time". The blank is dropped a third of the time as well, which is the same failure seen from the other side.

The strip rule fired on 38 frames of 1,003 (3.8%), 17 of them in the population. That is rule 3 of the pre-registration doing its job — "when does the new adventure time come out" came back as "...comes out on 25th September 2018" with the gold being April 21, 2017 — and it is a small number because the trunk rarely gets far enough to invent a date. It is not evidence the frames are clean; §3 is the read that matters and it is not small.

3. The confabulation read, which is the first line

An anchor set is grounded when at least one anchor shares a stem with a question content word, under crude suffix stripping plus a closed irregular table (sang/sung, wrote/written, born/birth). The table is small on purpose: one grown until everything matched could not find a confabulation.

frames with anchors that name NO question relation 174/230 75.7% of those, how many make a call 41 the frame inverts there 12/41 29.3% where the relation IS in the question 5/21 23.8% the margin on that same novel-relation subset 10/41 24.4% / 7/41 17.1%

the ninety-fourth finding's lexicon, its own decisive set 19/109 17.4%

29.3% against 17.4%, and worse than the margin it was supposed to help on both seeds. Rule 3 catches a frame that supplies a fact. It does not catch one that supplies a relation, and that is where the trunk's invention went: "what is cain and abel software used for" is framed as "the abbreviation cain is a software program used to ___", and abbreviation then scores every window that happens to contain the word.

4. The control: it is not the exemplar block

The eight-exemplar prompt echoes its final exemplar. 129 of the 1,003 frames came back about abbreviations and dna, stands, abbreviation are the three most common anchors in the file by a factor of six. On the population, 90 of the 230 anchor-bearing frames (39.1%) have an anchor set that is nothing but exemplar vocabulary. That echo inflates coverage with anchors the idea did not earn, so the primary run is the generous one.

The control rotates the exemplar order by k % 8 so no exemplar is systematically last. Second pass, 30 s, same rule, same reads:

primary rotated control coverage 62/580 10.7% 66/580 11.4% precision 17/62 27.4% 20/66 30.3% pure exemplar echo 90/230 39.1% 104/232 44.8% AUC delta, s1/s2 +0.0008/+0.0006 -0.0003/+0.0006 novel-relation inv 12/41 29.3% 13/43 30.2%

Removing the systematic echo does not rescue coverage and makes precision worse. The failure is not a prompt artefact and a better exemplar block is not the missing piece.

What this cannot say

It does not close the frame idea for a bigger model, and it does not open it either. What is measured is a 260M trunk at iter 800, prompted eight-shot, greedy. Every number here is that vehicle's. What the finding does establish is that the failure mode is not the one the ninety-fourth finding diagnosed: the shortfall is not "the model cannot be asked", it is that when it is asked it writes the question's words back, and when it reaches past them it reaches for a relation the question did not carry.

The exemplar block is a hand-written artefact and this finding is partly about it. Eight examples chosen on generation sanity over ten questions before any window was scored — recorded in gen_frames_ft4g.py's docstring, including the four-example version that was rejected and the one exemplar swapped out because its question is row-for-row in this eval set. §4 is the control that bounds how much of the result belongs to it, and it bounds it at "none of the direction".

Nothing here re-prices the admission lever. The ninety-sixth finding's ONLY_GOLD at 71.4%/69.2% against a 53.9%/56.7% baseline is why a window ranker was worth a test; that +126/+90 of room is still there and is still unclaimed. This finding removes one candidate for claiming it.

The population is the ninety-third finding's read (e) — passages with both kinds of window — so it says nothing about passages where every window carries the gold or none does. And the unanswerable half is untouched by construction, which is the ninety-third finding's §6 caveat applying for the fourth finding running.

Precision is measured on 62 passages. McNemar against the margin on the same passages reads p = 0.664 and p = 1.00 — the frame is not shown to be worse than the margin where it calls, it is shown to miss a pre-registered 20% bar. The whole-word sensitivity variant reads 22.6% rather than 27.4% and misses the bar too.

What follows

The frame is closed as a window scorer and should not be retried by generating better frames. Both branches the pre-registration wrote for a single failure are ruled out by the two failing together: coverage did not fall because the trunk declined to speak (it spoke on 41.5%, more than the lexicon), and precision did not fall because coverage was bought (the control buys less coverage and loses more precision).

The one thing the generated frame did buy is not a frame. 47 passages gained an anchor where the lexicon had none. If anything survives here it is that the trunk can be prompted into a declarative restatement 68% of the time; that is a text-shape capability and no read in this file suggests it carries information about which window holds an answer.

The ninety-fourth finding's §2 parity result stands as the ceiling on the whole idea, and it was never a demonstrated win — p = 0.32 and 0.62. A hand-written lexicon at 17.4% on 109 passages is the best any anchor has done on this population, and it missed the AUC bar by a factor of four.

Left unexplained: the generated anchor's own window-level AUC is 0.5104, which is chance, against the lexicon's 0.5288 — yet on the decisive subset it inverts at 27.4% while a coin would sit near 50%. Something in the 62 is informative and the 2,498 windows are not, and no read here separates which 62 they are.
