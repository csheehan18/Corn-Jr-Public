Averaging three seeds' readers barely helps

The result, as the finding states it

The three seed heads averaged rank the gold first on four more passages than the best of them, while any one of them being right would be sixty-one more, so the pick's seed variance is real and averaging does not harvest it

scripts/harvest_popa_pairs.py --span-head s1 s2 s3, 2026-09-04. No head was trained: the three shipped built heads (reader_built_s{1,2,3}.pt) read Population A's 3,893 windows off the popa cache, their pair logits averaged before the argmax, 34 s on the GPU. evals/results/pairs_ens_s123_popa.json and reader_funnel_ens_s123.json. corn/reader.py is untouched.

0. Why this read, and what it was to decide

Finding 102 left the span head at training loss 0.03 and held-out exact-pair 0.54 with more epochs buying nothing: a generalisation gap, not an under-trained head. The levers for a gap split by what the gap is made of. If the three seed heads err on different windows the gap is variance, and weight averaging, ensembling and stronger regularisation are the cheap next arms. If they err on the same windows the gap is bias in what the frozen states carry, and only data, labels or representation can move it. The cheapest instrument that separates the two is the ensemble the project already owns: three heads of one recipe on three seeds, on a population none of them trained on.

Pre-stated before the run (this session's recommendation to the owner): the pick stage moves by fifteen or more passages, and averaging pays; under ten, and the next arms are labels and data.

1. The pick stage, four ways

probe_reader_funnel.py --pairs on the ensemble's pairs file, with the seed-1 reader harvest supplying the gold windows. The first two funnel stages read the pairs file alone, so they are the ensemble's own.

The mean-logit ensemble is +4 passages over the best single head and +8 over their mean. Majority vote across the three is 493, the best head's number. The pick rate over windows whose ten holds the gold is unchanged: 739 of 1,109 (66.6%) against seed 1's 731 of 1,097 (66.6%); the ensemble's whole gain is that its ten catches twelve more windows and three more passages, which is a generation gain and not a pick gain. The winner's median lead over the gold falls from 3.44 to 3.06 logits, still a confident miss.

And the oracle says the variance is there. Some one of the three heads ranks the gold first on 555 passages, sixty-one more than the best head; all three agree on only 423. At the window level 837 against 731. The seeds disagree on about one gold-bearing window in five, and on those windows the ensemble sides with the wrong head as often as the best head is wrong alone.

2. Reading

This is the shape of disagreement that averaging cannot help: when the heads split, the one that is wrong is not less confident than the one that is right, so the mean inherits the error. Whatever separates 498 from 555 is a choice among heads on the windows where they differ, and a choice among candidate spans by a second scorer is the pick problem itself, which the re-ranker (finding 86), the listwise verifier (finding 99) and the encoder (finding 100) have each failed to make.

By the rule set before the run, four is under ten and the cheap variance-reduction arms — EMA or SWA of the weights, a dropout and weight-decay sweep, state noise — are not the next thing. They would reduce each head's variance around a mean that is itself wrong on the same windows. They remain cheap (about three minutes a point) and never-asked, and are left as an option and not a recommendation.

What is left on the pick is data, labels and representation. Labels: train_reader.py's loss already sums the probability of every annotated occurrence of the gold (spans carries more than one on 7,842 of 100,084 positives), and only 1,752 positives (1.75%) hold an unannotated further occurrence of the answer string, so "credit every occurrence" is already pulled; the label lever that remains is the EQUIVALENT class finding 95 measured at 15% of refused-wrong golds, which needs relabelling and waits on the owner's spot check (docs/SPOT-CHECK-REFUSED-WRONG.md). Data: SQuAD 2.0 as minority supervision (audit L18) was blocked on disk at 19 GB free; the states drive now has 50 GB free against a 38 GB positive cache, so it is blocked on the owner's licence line only. Representation: the layer band (row 36) and the prompt-form variants (L16, L17), each a fresh state harvest.

3. On disk

harvest_popa_pairs.py --span-head now takes several heads and averages their pair logits; one head is byte-identical to before, and --check-against is refused for an ensemble whose spans are nobody's. pairs_ens_s123_popa.json is in the single-head pairs format with span_head and seed as lists, so every replay tool reads it unchanged.

4. Limits

One ensemble rule, the mean of raw logits; the three heads share one recipe and 95% of their training articles, so this is a lower bound on what a more diverse ensemble would show and an upper bound on nothing. The any-of-three oracle is handed the answer and is quoted as a bound on variance, not as a reachable number. Population A only; the held-out slices differ by seed so no held-out exact-pair for the ensemble exists. The downstream funnel stages (admission, vote) for the ensemble were read with seed 1's verifier and are not quoted because that verifier was cross-fitted to seed 1's head.
