Given only the right window, the reader reaches 93%

The result, as the finding states it

Admitting only the gold's own window reads ninety-three per cent whatever the threshold, so the ninety-fifth finding's eighty-three was an oracle that left every rival in the vote, and the residue is two coupled levers of similar size

scripts/probe_reader_rank.py read (g), 2026-09-03. Replay only — no GPU, no decode, no weight read, no tokenizer, and no new harvest — over nullpr_s{1,2}_popa.json joined to pairs_built_s{1,2}_popa.json, the same join the ninety-fifth finding checked and this run re-checks: 3,893 of 3,893 windows on both seeds. evals/results/rank_s{1,2}.json.

This finding corrects the one before it and takes the number up, not down. The ninety-fifth finding read ORACLE_JOINT at 82.8% and 81.0% and called it "the ceiling on everything downstream of candidate ranking". It is not, and the reason is one line of the code it was read from.

0. What ORACLE_JOINT hands over, and the thing it leaves alone

verdict_oracle's fix_window adds the best gold-bearing window to the real admitted mask:

mask = list(admitted(row, t)) ... mask[max(pool, key=lambda i: row["margins"][i])] = True

Every window that cleared the threshold on its own is still in that mask, and reduce still counts all of them. So a passage whose gold window is forced in and then outvoted by two admitted repetitions of a rival fails under ORACLE_JOINT exactly as it fails under the shipped reader. That is not a hypothetical: it is AGGREGATION, which the ninety-fifth finding measured at 66 and 60 passages at SHIPPED and left unexplained in its own last paragraph.

ORACLE_JOINT is therefore a ceiling on locating and picking, conditional on the rest of the vote staying as it is. It is not a ceiling on the reader.

1. The two oracles that remove the rivals instead

Read (g), added to the same file, admits exactly one window:

ONLY_GOLD the mask is the highest-margin gold-bearing window and nothing else; the head keeps its own pick on it. ONLY_GOLD_PICK the same single window — preferring, since the pick is also handed over, a gold window whose ten actually hold the gold — with the gold substituted wherever they do.

Everything else is verdict_at's own code: the hybrid fallback's second reduce call, local=False, the same judge. Unanswerable rows have no gold-bearing window, so they keep the real mask and therefore their real verdict — the correct-silence column below is unchanged by construction and not by merit, which is the ninety-third finding's §6 caveat applying for the third finding running and not getting smaller for being repeated.

Both read the answer. Both are ceilings and neither is an arm.

2. Seventy-one and ninety-three, and the second one does not depend on the threshold

ONLY_GOLD_PICK reads 672 and 670 at both operating points, to the passage. That is not a coincidence and it is the structural point of the read: once the mask is one forced window and the span on it is the gold, an answerable passage's verdict cannot depend on the threshold, because the threshold only decides admission and admission has been taken away. The extent rule cannot reach it either, for the same reason — the pick is already the gold. 93.2% and 92.9% is a ceiling on the whole stack below candidate enumeration, invariant to both of the constants corn/reader.py ships.

ONLY_GOLD does move between the points, by +14 and +10, and the only thing that differs is the extent rule. That is the extent rule's worth under a perfect window, and it is small, which is what the eighty-fifth finding measured it as under a real one. ONLY_GOLD is threshold-invariant too, for the same reason and with the extent rule held: its mask is also exactly one forced window. §2a is the check.

2a. Seed 2 at its own operating point, added after the ninety-seventh finding

The seed-2 column above is read at -2.02, and -2.02 is seed 1's constant. The ninety-seventh finding puts the bar protocol in a committed script and measures what that costs: seed 2's own cross-validated mean is -1.6730, and at -2.02 it reads 409 right at 170 correct silences, thirty below the floor PRIMARY names. So the seed-2 column above is not a point this project would ship. Re-read at seed 2's own constant:

seed 2 at -1.6730 (its own bar-CV mean) right of 721 baseline 390 54.1% ORACLE_PICK 454 63.0% +64 ORACLE_WINDOW 454 63.0% +64 ORACLE_JOINT 581 80.6% +191 ONLY_GOLD 499 69.2% +109 ONLY_GOLD_PICK 670 92.9% +280 what ONLY_GOLD_PICK misses 51: ECHO_BLOCKED 21, OUTSIDE_TEN 26, neither 4

Every conclusion in this file survives and two of them tighten. ONLY_GOLD reads 499 at both -2.02 and -1.6730, and ONLY_GOLD_PICK 670 at both, which is §2's invariance claim demonstrated at two thresholds rather than argued from the code. What moves is the baseline (409 to 390) and therefore the deltas, and the two levers become +109 admission-alone and +64 pick-alone against +280 jointly on seed 2 — still super-additive, still of comparable size, and now with admission the larger on both seeds rather than on one.

Against the owner's 85% = 613 of 721: ONLY_GOLD is 98 and 114 passages short; ONLY_GOLD_PICK is 59 and 57 passages over. So the target is not ruled out by anything measured here, and it is not reachable by admission alone.

3. Two levers of similar size, and they are coupled

what it is worth seed 1 seed 2 at -2.02 at its own pick alone ORACLE_PICK - base +60 +84 +64 admission alone ONLY_GOLD - base +126 +90 +109 pick after perfect admission +157 +171 +171 admission after perfect pick +223 +177 +216 both ONLY_GOLD_PICK - base +283 +261 +280

Admission is the larger lever on both seeds once seed 2 is read at its own threshold — +126 against +60 on seed 1 and, from §2a, +109 against +64 on seed 2 — and the sum of the two alone (186 and 173) is well under the joint 283 and 280. (Read at seed 1's constant, seed 2's two levers come out level, +90 against +84; that apparent parity is the wrong-threshold artefact §2a removes, and it is left here because the row above it is.) Strongly super-additive, for the same reason the ninety-fifth finding gave and with a sharper edge on it: fixing the pick on a window that never gets admitted buys nothing, and admitting the right window buys nothing if the head then speaks a rival from it. The correct reading of the residue is not "admission is the lever". It is two levers of comparable size that have to move together, and an arm that moves one while the other stands still will read a fraction of its own bound.

4. The pick failure is confident, not a near miss

On the RANK_2_10 passages — the plurality of the residue, 168 and 182 at SHIPPED — how far the head's argmax leads the gold, in span-head logits, on that same window:

seed 1 seed 2 median 3.72 3.05 quartiles 1.99 / 5.90 1.53 / 5.83 within 1.0 22 of 168 13.1% 33 of 182 18.1%

Three and a half logits is not a tie a re-ranker breaks. Seven passages in eight sit outside a one-logit band, which is why the eighty-sixth finding's re-ranker never left the span head's own argmax rate and was retired for it, and why the pick lever is a head problem and not a threshold problem. It also says what §3's 157 and 171 passages would cost: something that changes the score by several logits on the right span, not something that reorders ties.

5. What survives the strongest oracle, and it is almost entirely accounted for

ONLY_GOLD_PICK still misses seed 1 seed 2 ECHO_BLOCKED 19 21 OUTSIDE_TEN 29 26 neither 1 4 total 49 51

Forty-eight of 49 and 47 of 51 are the two deductions already on the record: the grounded anti-restatement rule's 22 (PLAN-READER-RESIDUE.md §3, the owner's open call) and a gold the pair enumeration never offered on that window. ECHO_BLOCKED reads 19 and 21 rather than 22 because the oracle's chosen span is not always the one the guard refuses. One passage on seed 1 and four on seed 2 are unexplained by anything in this file, and they are printed rather than rounded away.

So the ceiling decomposes with nothing material left over, which is the property ORACLE_JOINT did not have: its 162 and 148 misses were 22 guard passages and about 130 of something, and the something was the rival windows it had left in the vote.

6. Two corrections to the ninety-fifth finding, made where they were made

  • §2's claim that ORACLE_JOINT is "the ceiling on everything downstream of candidate ranking" is withdrawn, and a note saying so is in that file beside the claim. It is fourteen points below the oracle that removes the rivals.
  • "The 58% ceiling was an artefact" overstates it, and that is withdrawn too. ORACLE_PICK's 58.1% and 58.9% are correctly computed over a different conditioning set — admitted windows — and the ninety-fifth finding's own §1 says exactly that two paragraphs earlier. The honest statement is that 58% is a differently conditioned oracle, and that quoting it as a bound on the reader was reading the conditioning off. That file's title is left as it was written, because a finding records what was claimed and the note records the correction.
  • Two transcription errors in §1's by-species table are fixed: seed 1's CLIPPED OUTSIDE_TEN is 1 and not 4, and its UNGROUNDED is 4 and not 3, per rank_s1.json. No total, share or conclusion in that file moves.

What this cannot say

Every arm here is handed the answer, twice. ONLY_GOLD is told which window carries the gold; ONLY_GOLD_PICK is additionally told which of the ten candidates it is. The ninetieth finding's locality rule had a plausible mechanism and reached a negative fraction of its bound, and the ninety-third, ninety-fourth and ninety-fifth findings are a record of real mechanisms reaching small fractions of such numbers. 93% is what perfect information buys and it is not a forecast.

The correct-silence column is unchanged by construction on every row of §2. A real window scorer admits and refuses on both halves of the population and would pay in the unanswerable column, as every threshold move in the ninety-second finding did. Nothing here prices that.

ONLY_GOLD is a stronger hand-over than ORACLE_WINDOW, not a weaker one, and the two are not a range. Removing every rival window from the vote is not something a scorer can do without also being right about which windows to remove on the unanswerable half.

The 23% label-noise floor from the ninety-fifth finding §6 still sits under all of this, is still one species and one model auditor, and the owner's spot-check of 20 is still outstanding.

Two seeds, one trunk, one population, Population A under hybrid, at both operating points, with each read's point stated.

What follows

The plan's arms are re-sized at SHIPPED in PLAN-READER-RESIDUE.md §7, and their ceilings are quoted against ONLY_GOLD's 515 and 499 rather than against ORACLE_WINDOW's 444 and 449, which is a different and smaller thing.

The two arms this decomposition names are the two levers, and they are pre-registered in that file rather than here: a verifier trained on the gold passage's own non-gold windows as hard negatives (admission, bound 515 / 499), and a bidirectional encoder over the cached window states feeding the existing pair scorer (pick, bound 672 / 670 jointly). The second was declined in the ninety-fifth finding and the rationale for declining it does not apply to the new claim. It was declined because its stated mechanism — a causal trunk cannot enumerate the gold, so the gold is outside the ten — is contradicted by the 89%. The claim now is the opposite one: the gold is in the ten, the head ranks it second to tenth on the plurality of the residue, and it does so by a confident three and a half logits. That is a scoring failure over an enumeration that already contains the answer, and it is what a richer state over the same candidates is for.

Left unexplained: the ninety-fifth finding's AGGREGATION note is now half answered and half open. §0 says why those 55 and 51 RANK_1 passages fail — the outvoting windows were in the mask and ORACLE_JOINT left them there — but nothing here says whether the outvoting is one rival repeated across overlapping windows or several different ones, which is the difference between a dedupe and a scorer.
