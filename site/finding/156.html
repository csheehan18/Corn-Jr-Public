The tag language · 8 October 2026

He can't build a goal from two observations at one pass

The result, as the finding states it

G6's first rung, combine: at one pass he does not build the goal from two observations; both twins right on 5 and 0 of 288 held-out rows and 1 and 1 of 144 trained ones, against controls that never saw a note at 4-6 and 2-3; the request does not choose the clause even on the traces he trained on or when the note repeats the request's own words, and what forms instead is a fixed ranking of the note's phrasings, a different one at each seed

What it cost

two nano runs, 1,154 s and 1,263 s, contended (the card at 35-39%

before every job, so the times are bounds); probes about 2 min a checkpoint; eight control reads while the rows were built, about 6 min; three cause probes, about 3 min. Built and read 12:52-15:20 on 2026-10-08, against a registered ~50 min of card time.

ROADMAP G6, the first rung of PLAN-TAG-LANGUAGE's ladder after filter. Combine, defined by the owner on 2026-10-08, is using two results together. A request asks for a place someone would like ("somewhere to eat in Garathmere under 30 that Jorendbourne would like"). He looks the person up and gets a note with one clause per kind of place ("for a meal they like somewhere low-key; for a sundowner, somewhere with a buzz; on holiday, somewhere homely"). Then he lists the places. The goal's soft[...] must be the clause the request's kind of place names. Arm E2's mix plus 6,300 such traces at one pass (configs/mix_v3_funcworld_combine.py, pre-registered at 60d98b8); arm E2's three seeds are the controls. Cards: evals/results/nano20m_v3_fw_combine*__combine.json, *__copy.json, and the controls' nano20m_v3_fw_filter_ev2*__combine.json.

Caught before the read

The controls were read before anything trained, and four times a model that had never seen a note scored above chance. Each time the gold was readable without the request: it was the only clause with a place in the list; a phrasing-family familiarity prior; rung 3's rule that a concept never sits beside its near-synonym; and a skew in which concepts could be decoys (cosy was the gold on 85% of the rows it appeared on). Removing the list or the note left that last read where it was, 112 and 111 of 288 (probe only). Each was fixed in the generator (corn/combinegen.py's comment carries all four), and the registered gate became paired: both twins of a row right, where the twin moves only the request's kind of place. A model with any fixed preference that ignores the request scores zero there by construction. scripts/check_combine.py holds all four fixes on the bytes, and it caught five cue words that shared a stem with something else in the world before anything trained.

The standing kill fires as written: tool.copy reads 100 and 102 of 120, under 118 at both seeds. The bar was inherited from arm E2's config, and E2 seed 1337's own card reads 102. So seed 1337 matches its control, and seed 2337 fell from E2's 119 to 102.

Why it fails — the cause probes

The probes ran on both arm seeds and two control seeds (probe only, scratchpad scripts):

checkpoint right on his own training traces (of 400) paired with the request's words in the note combine 1337 136 6 combine 2337 155 5 E2 1337 146 5 E2 2337 129 5 chance 133 32 (1/9)

  1. Not learned on the text he trained on. On the payload's own first 400 traces he picks the right clause at the controls' rate. This is not memorised pairings that fail to generalise; the routing never formed.
  2. Not a problem of meaning. With each clause's cue rewritten to repeat the request's own phrase ("for a place for dinner they prefer somewhere scenic" beside "look for a place for dinner"), paired stays at 6 and 5, the controls' level. He does not route even when the words match exactly, so the wall is not rung 3's synonym association.
  3. Not the list. His chosen clause's place in the list is spread just like the gold's (first ok place 247-259 of 864 against the gold's 247), so rung 3's first-ok habit is not what drives it.
  4. A habit formed instead: by note position seed 1337 takes the last clause on 411 of 864 prompts (48%, against 269-308 for the controls), and seed 2337 leans to the middle (337). The request moves his choice on 14-23 of 288 twins, fewer than the controls' 26-35. The hand read below shows the habit is a ranking of the phrasings, which position only shadows.

The form installs. His free-decoded goal is exactly the gold on 96 and 86 held-out rows, the full-option count to within one, and it is written from the note's own phrasings. He learned to copy a clause from the first observation into goal:. What he did not learn is to let the request choose which.

Read by hand

The probe prints six rows whole, but the first read of them covered seed 2337 only; this section first claimed six rows a seed. The real hand read (2026-10-08, after the owner asked; probe only, a scratchpad script): 24 held-out rows spread across the cell, each with its twin, greedy free decode, at both seeds, so 96 decodes:

  • Every one is a well-formed goal line copying one of the note's three phrasings verbatim (96 of 96), with the request's hard term right. The form installed completely.
  • On all 24 rows at both seeds, the twin writes the identical line. Asked for "a place for dinner" and then "a place for drinks" against the same note, he writes the same soft[...] both times.

  • What decides it is the phrasing itself, a fixed ranking per seed. Seed 1337 takes "quiet" 4 times of 4 offered and "in the open air" 3 of 3, and never "scenic" (0 of 5) or "with a buzz" (0 of 4). Seed 2337 takes "scenic" 5 of 5 and "lively" 5 of 5, and never "for the little ones" (0 of

    1. or "full of energy" (0 of 4). The cue in front of the phrasing ("for a meal", "on holiday") does not move it: picks per cue sit between 0 of 7 and 6 of 10 at both seeds.

The note-position counts in the probe section are this ranking seen from the side: the last clause wins more often at seed 1337 because the rows put its favoured phrasings there more often, not because position is the rule. So the position habit in the title and the cause probes is better read as a phrasing habit. Because every concept is the gold on a third of the rows it appears in (the fourth control fix), a phrasing ranking scores chance, and paired scores it zero.

What it licenses

  • Combine at nano and one pass reads zero, and the cause is that the choice never conditioned on the request. That holds on trained text, on held-out text, and with the words matching. This is the shape finding 148 recorded for filter: a choice that depends on a distant span does not form at this scale and dose, and a position habit forms in its place.
  • The next variable is the dose, as pre-registered. If a higher dose also reads zero, the routing goes to the runtime (a clause-to-domain table) and the note's text reaches him already chosen. That is SPEC §0's rule applied: a choice the runtime can make by lookup is the runtime's.
  • The bind's wider copy source (request plus a prior observation) is not adopted. The form installs, but nothing is gained by it until the choice does.

Limits

Two seeds, one dose, nano only. The probe scores three teacher-forced goal lines; the free decode is a side column. The cause probes are hand-built and unpinned, so their numbers are bounds (CLAUDE.md). Rung 3's held-out cells are not readable on this arm (the mix config says why).

Amended 2026-10-08 — four times the dose, and combine goes to the runtime

The dose arm finding 156 pre-registered (configs/mix_v3_funcworld_combine4.py, at fd5a41d): the same payload at four times its weight by repetition, 8.3% of the mix, 6,231 iterations; same rows, probe and gate. Two nano runs, 1,367 s and 1,373 s, contended. Read by hand first: 24 held-out rows and their twins, free decode, both seeds (probe only, the same scratchpad script):

Every decode but one is a verbatim note clause; the one is a note span that is not a clause. The cards (committed, evals/results/ nano20m_v3_fw_combine4*):

"Nothing installs at four times either", the registered row: combine goes to the runtime — the clause is chosen by lookup (the request's kind of place against a clause-to-domain table) and he is shown the one clause, already chosen, as SPEC §0 has it. The rebased copy kill does not fire: 120 and 102 against the one-pass arm's 100 and 102. Four times the dose moved the twins on 6 of 24 rows at seed 2337 against 0 of 24 at one pass, and took no row of 288 to both twins right. The habit is the same phrasing ranking, re-drawn.
