Fine-tuned, he reads every condition word like 'not' and 'unless'

The result, as the finding states it

By fine-tune the one-slot binding line reads every flip word with the pick out of his loss (0.984, 0.999, 1.000 at three starts; runtime-resolved quads 136-144 of 144), so the runtime's move needs nothing of RULES 21; "the other one" never forms with the pick trained (0.50, 0.76, 0.76 against 0.90); and on hand-written sentences the line reads new words in the trained structures and fails new structures

What it cost

six fine-tunes of 650 iterations at 20M (159, 144, 144, 145, 149 and 156 s, contended — the card at 39-41%, a bound), three start reads and six three-way reads at 87-135 s, six slot reads at 7-8 s and six copy cards at 8 s: one chain of 1,919 s, 19:25-19:57 on 2026-10-18, registered ~45 min; the real-wording probe 18 s a pass of three checkpoints; the build 19:21-19:25 and the probe's 19:40-19:50 on CPU.

The owner's direction, 2026-10-18: make branch excel at both halves — the binding line and the move — without training him on a line the runtime writes (RULES 21). Two arms, pre-registered in configs/mix_v3_bind_ft_pick.py at 60ce6bd before a byte trained: finding 175's one-slot payload (if: raining -> X) by finding 172's fine-tune recipe (two passes beside arm E2's replay, 650 iterations) from arm E2's three starts, one variable, the pick line's loss:

A1 pick the pick under loss — can dose form "the other one", so the pick is his and the line keeps the pick's boost? B2 nopick the pick masked (loss="no_pick") — can dose alone build the line, so the runtime resolves the move from it?

check_branch.py section 14 holds the pair (a mutant that trains B2's pick fails it). Then, at the owner's go, a real-wording probe (scripts/probe_bind_real.py, committed at 1494930 with its prediction before it read a checkpoint): 29 hand-written sentence shapes, most in wordings no list in corn/branchflipgen.py holds, each at two condition values and read with its two continuations swapped — a pair right only when both are.

Read by hand

The line. The three-way probe prints his line beside the gold on eight quads a run; every shown line is exact, flip words included ("provided it isn't snowing", "except if it's foggy"). Of the few wrong lines (36, 0 and 8 on A1; 28, 2 and 0 on B2), every one names the same continuation as on its action twin — the request with its two continuations swapped — so the swap did not reach that line.

The complement. On an unmet condition, A1's pick from the right line takes the first menu option 596, 472 and 441 times of 864, and the line's continuation 434, 206 and 209: neither a copy of the line nor "the other one" — a lean that the dose moved without forming the operation. Finding 175's pretraining read 342 and 474 of 864, flat across the eight families.

The copy row, a side column: tool.copy_wide 298, 253, 292 (A1) and 298, 249, 293 (B2) against the starts' 298, 295, 293 — "not readable" by finding 170's rule. Both arms from the second start fall ~45, which no other start does; the misses were not read by hand, and that read is owed before a fine-tune from that start is quoted on the copy row.

New words in a trained structure transfer, negation included: "when", "whenever", "should it be", "except on the off chance", "at all" — none in any list — read at ceiling where the clause order and connectives are one of the eight families'. A structure the families never had fails: a default plan with a late switch ("go with B, though if it's foggy make it A"), a condition stated with a negative tail ("if it doesn't", "but if it's not then"), a negated condition introducing the second continuation ("go for B, but if it's not snowing, A", where he writes if: snowing -> A — attaching the condition to the continuation beside it and dropping the "not"). Each failing shape fails the same way on both orders, so it is a stable misread, not noise. And two words inserted into a trained P2 sentence ("if by chance it's raining, A; if not, B") take it from the family's held-out level to 3 of 12.

The vocabulary column, apart: on near-trained shapes with a word he never saw, the continuation is right (pairs 4-6 of 6) and the value is not: "shut" and "packed" are copied as written, and "pouring" is written "sunny" — a training value substituted for one he was not handed, GENERATORS §5's first signature.

What it licenses

  • Branch on the one-slot line with the runtime resolving the move is supported at three starts of three, with no byte of the runtime's in his loss. The fine-tune lifts the line from 0.89 (pretraining, pick masked) to 0.98-1.00, and his first slot on negated training traces to 1.00 — the pick's boost (finding 178's 0.86 to 0.99) was exposure, and the dose replaces it. RULES 21 is kept, not amended.
  • The pick as his is not shown: "the other one" did not form at any start (0.50-0.76). Route A's next arm, if wanted, is A2 (the runtime lists his line's continuation first, the pick a result-to-position read).
  • The line is a reading of the eight clause structures with open word slots, not a reading of conditionals. It is finding 169's table one level up: the flip words were varied (2,997 wordings) and transferred; the clause structures were not (eight) and did not. GENERATORS D8 applies to structures as well as wordings: a branch slice for the 1.0 corpus needs many clause structures — real conditionals mined from text, the default- plus-switch, the negative tail, the condition in either clause — or the person's ninth way of building the sentence gets the wrong branch.
  • The condition's value word is a vocabulary hop he does not make ("pouring" -> "sunny"). The runtime matches the value against the lookup's field (it already computes check:), or the slice carries synonyms.

What it does not settle. Whether a slice with many structures installs at nano or a fine-tune; the copy row at the second start; A2; and whether 20 hand-written structures stand for real speech — they are one writer's, and a mined set would read it better.
