Fine-tuning doesn't overwrite what pretraining installed

The result, as the finding states it

The fine-tune does not overwrite what the mix installed, and the re-ask band comes back whole

What it cost

3.8 min of scoring on the 2 cards first cited here (nano20m_v3_turn2, nano20m_v3_turn2_joint); it also reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (nano20m_v3). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

Two nano arms trained 2026-08-28 from configs/nano20m_v3_turn2_joint.py and _seed2.py: the mix_turn2_ft recipe exactly as shipped — same data dir, same schedule, same seeds — with init_from pointed at nano20m_v3_turn2{,_seed2}/ckpt.pt, the MIX arm's own checkpoints, instead of the payload-blind nano20m_v3. Ordered by the owner on 2026-08-27 and pre-registered the same evening in docs/PLAN-TURN-2.md, with its readings written before a config existed. Controls are the four existing nano20m_v3 seeds and the four turn-2 cards from finding 74; nothing retrained and nothing was re-scored. All three arms are eager — the signed compile=True is scoped to the H200 family precisely so a read like this one never straddles the boundary.

The question, and the honest limit stated first

Finding 74 read React and Chain at parity across the two vehicles and Re-ask four times better on the mix. The owner declined to sign the §7a deferral that would have dropped React and Chain from mix_v4_5b, stated the prior the arm would test — pretraining exposure is the substrate a capability survives on, and fine-tuning amplifies what the mix installed — and ordered the deferral's own reopening condition run first.

The limit the pre-registration put in writing: turn2.react and both chain cells sit at or near ceiling on both existing vehicles, so additivity is unreadable there. The two cells that separate cleanly are turn2.reask (MIX 57-66 against FT-alone 15-17) and turn2.reask_yn (33-38 against 8-11). Those two carry the read; the ceilings are checked for damage, not for gain.

The result

last.pt reproduces ckpt.pt on every cell of both joint cards, and that is a tautology here rather than a measurement: both runs selected best-val at iteration 551, which is their last, so the two files are the same weights. It is worth saying out loud because it is not what happened on the arm this one copies — three of finding 74's four fine-tunes selected below their final iteration (525, 525, 455 of 551) and scoring both was not a formality there. Both cards were scored anyway, on the sixth finding's rule that a selection argument is answered by measurement; the column is folded in rather than printed twice.

The joint arm sits in the MIX band on both readable cells. turn2.reask reads 52 and 68 against a MIX band of 57-66 — one seed five items below its floor, one two items above its ceiling, straddling it. turn2.reask_yn reads 32 and 37 against 33-38: one item below the floor and one below the ceiling, which at n=2 on a 40-item row is the same number. Against FT-alone the gap is not close: 52 and 68 against 15 and 17, and 32 and 37 against 8 and 11.

Against the pre-registered table this is "the mix survives", on both of the clauses that outcome was written with: the joint arm holds the MIX band on reask and reask_yn, and it lands above FT-alone by far more than the seed spread — thirty-five items on a row where the FT arm's own two seeds differ by two. The ceilings are matched rather than spent: react 112-116 against FT-alone's 107-108 and the mix's 112-117, chain_result at a clean 50/50 on both seeds, chain_ellipsis 43-46 against 44 and 47-48, which is a two-item shortfall on the one cell where the mix was strongest and is not a separation at this spread.

What it does not show is addition. The joint arm does not beat the MIX arm on reask; it equals it. So the reading is the narrower of the two the owner's prior could have produced: base exposure is not overwritten by a fine-tune over the same payload — which is what the deferral turned on — rather than base exposure plus a fine-tune buying more than either. On these two cells, with the fine-tune's payload identical to the base's, there was no headroom left for it to buy.

turn2.chain_pronoun is not readable and is reported as such, again: the control spans 0-17 over four seeds, joint reads 12-17, and finding 61's rule binds.

One cell moves, and it is not a turn-2 cell

tool.selection /200 control 109-130 MIX 174 FT 181-185 JOINT 187-188

Every arm that reads this payload routes better than the control, the fine-tune vehicle more than the mix, and the joint arm most of all — above both of its parents on both seeds. It is a small margin over FT-alone (two to seven items on a 200-item row) and it is the only place in this experiment where mix-plus-fine-tune reads above fine-tune-alone at all. Worth recording as the one additive-looking cell and not worth more than that: it is not a registered cell, and finding 74's own turn-2 arms already moved it 109-130 → 174-185 without a joint arm anywhere.

Read against the FT-alone cards, with the ~10-item tool.copy wobble floor:

No bill. The 114 is six items inside the wobble floor and its seed-1 twin is at ceiling; copy_wide is above the control range on both seeds, as it was for every turn-2 arm in finding 74. refusal.say at 260 is one item below the control's four-seed floor of 261, on a row whose own control spread is seven — noise, and recorded rather than rounded away.

What this licenses, and who decides it

The joint arm was ordered as the input to a decision and the decision is not the implementer's. docs/PLAN-5B-SUPPLY.md §7a's turn-2 deferral is HELD OPEN with its reopening condition ordered, and this finding is that condition's output. The §7a entry is untouched.

What the pre-registration says follows from "the mix survives", stated here so the owner can check the numbers against the sentence they signed rather than against a paraphrase: base exposure is not overwritten, the owner's prior holds, React and Chain ride mix_v4, and the held-open deferral dies. Note what does not change either way, and it is the thing that matters for the freeze: turn2_v1.txt rides whole at one pass regardless — that is SIGNED, on Re-ask's strength alone, and the classes share the file by ground rule 5. The deferral decides what the finding record says Phase 3 fine-tunes must re-install, not what the corpus contains.

Limits

Two seeds per arm, so directions rather than sizes (rule 6). The two readable cells are the only ones this design can read, and the pre-registration said so before the arms existed — everything else here is a damage check. The joint arm's fine-tune payload is identical to its base's, which is the cleanest form of the question and also the one least likely to show addition; a joint arm over a payload the base had only partly seen is a different cell and is not this one. And these are nano numbers on a policy class where the 260M baseline carries a substrate the nano control does not (finding 74's chain_pronoun note), so the sizes transfer to the H200 run by argument and not by measurement.
