Reading question fit improves refusals but not right answers

The result, as the finding states it

The verifier that also reads the window's fit to the question buys a better refusal on all three seeds and no more right answers, so it clears both floors, misses the bar, and its one mechanism win does not reproduce

What it cost

0 s of new scoring on record; it reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m_v4_ft4g). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

scripts/train_null_head.py --window-features, 2026-09-04 (seed 3; seeds 1 and 2 on 2026-09-04 in commit 86da847c, whose message was the only record until this file). Three verifier heads on the frozen corn_jr_260m_v4_ft4g trunk with no weight of it moved, the shipped span head loaded frozen and never trained, ~4 s an epoch on cached states, one Population A harvest a seed. evals/results/nullwf_s{1,2,3}.json and _popa.json; paired_nullwf_s{1,2,3}.json; reader_funnel_nullwf_s{1,2,3}.json and reader_funnel_s3.json. corn/reader.py is untouched.

0. The arm

The shipped verifier (finding 86, arm A) reads the span head's argmax pair, that pair's two boundary rows and three scalars about its score — s_best, margin, length — and never the window. The hundredth finding's audit noticed what that leaves out: a passage carrying "sung by Matt Monro" verbatim scored below two windows holding no answer, because the verifier judges the pick and not the window the pick sits in.

scripts/window_features.py is the five numbers that were missing, defined once and read from the cache's own meta in training and from the prompt at inference so the two cannot drift:

overlap question words present in the window, after a crude suffix strip with an irregular-verb table ("sang" = "sung") overlap_frac that overlap as a share of the question's words win_len the window's length in tokens rel_pos the window's position in its passage, 0 to 1 n_windows how many windows the passage was cut into

Training-set moments on seed 3: overlap 1.72 ± 1.37, overlap_frac 0.36 ± 0.27, win_len 38.1 ± 6.1, rel_pos 0.46 ± 0.37, n_windows 6.2 ± 4.0. The head widens from three scalars to eight; everything else — architecture, pair rows, the cross-fitted verifier label, --feat-norm std, the article split, eight epochs, selection on held-out balanced accuracy, the harvest — is the shipped recipe. A checkpoint declares whether it carries the five, and train_reader_cached.read_cached computes them at inference only for one that does, so every head trained before this reads exactly as it did.

Pre-registered, from A1's protocol (PLAN-READER-RESIDUE.md §6): three seeds; the bar protocol, extent on, hybrid reduce, each seed at its own cross-validated threshold; the 420-right bar with the silence floor (199 / 196 / 201) and the acc | att floor (0.674 / 0.693 / 0.692) held. The mechanism bar, written before seed 3 ran: AGGREGATION falls by at least a third, as it did on seed 1.

2. Paired on the same passages, none of it is significant

McNemar over the passages both harvests hold, at each harvest's own CV'd threshold:

seed 1 seed 2 seed 3 pooled joint bit +12 (74/86, .385) +9 (32/41, .349) +3 (25/28, .784) +24 (131/155, .174) right alone +4 (53/57, .775) +3 (22/25, .771) -2 (21/19, .875) +5 (96/101, .776) correct silence +8 (21/29, .322) +6 (10/16, .327) +5 (4/9, .267) +19 (35/54, .056)

(lost / won, then p.) The only line that approaches significance pooled is the silence line, at p = 0.056 over 89 moved verdicts. The right line is +5 over 197 moved verdicts on 2,163 answerable reads, and the fold noise the ninety-second finding measured is ±7 a seed. Nothing here is a direction on right.

Where the answerable verdicts moved, seed by seed:

s1 s2 s3 wrong -> silent 31 29 14 right -> silent 36 9 14 silent -> right 27 17 11 wrong -> right 30 8 8 right -> wrong 17 13 7 silent -> wrong 27 15 6

Seed 1 moved 168 answerable verdicts to net +4; seed 3 moved 60 to net −2. The arm's action is largest where its threshold moved most (seed 1's CV'd constants went from −1.98 / −2.06 to −0.26 / −0.49, a re-scaling of the head's logit that the bar protocol absorbs) and smallest where the head's scale stayed put (seed 3, −1.88 / −2.33 to −1.73 / −2.26).

3. The mechanism does not reproduce, and the funnel says why

scripts/probe_reader_funnel.py on the six harvests. The first three stages are the span head's and do not move — same head, same ten, same argmax:

lost at admission 75 105 77 75 62 64 lost at the vote and extent 57 33 43 44 59 58

On seed 1 the arm admitted 30 fewer gold-first windows and lost 24 fewer at the vote — a stricter admission that also silenced rivals, which is why AGGREGATION fell 64 → 43 and REFUSED_RIGHT rose 78 → 91 on that seed. On seeds 2 and 3 admission and the vote each move by at most two passages. AGGREGATION reads 50 → 49 and 64 → 63. The mechanism bar — a third off AGGREGATION — is met on one seed of three and fails by its size on the other two. Seed 1's 21-passage fall was the fold threshold moving, not the five features re-ranking anything: the species tables underneath move in different directions on each seed.

species, shipped -> arm s1 s2 s3 REFUSED_WRONG 93 -> 84 110 -> 113 99 -> 107 REFUSED_RIGHT 78 -> 91 80 -> 78 71 -> 76 AGGREGATION 64 -> 43 50 -> 49 64 -> 63 RIVAL 63 -> 74 59 -> 59 49 -> 44 CLIPPED 18 -> 23 23 -> 20 25 -> 22 UNGROUNDED 16 -> 13 11 -> 11 13 -> 11

This is the result the hundredth finding's audit predicted for every hand feature: removing the question from the prompt drops in-window accuracy from 59% to 19%, so the trunk already reads each candidate with the question in context, and five numbers about lexical overlap are a cruder restatement of what the boundary rows already carry. Where they help is the unanswerable half — a window with no question words in it is a little easier to refuse — which is the one place the boundary rows of a wrong pick say least.

4. The selection metric disagrees with itself across seeds

Held-out balanced accuracy at the selected epoch, shipped → window:

shipped + window AUC shipped -> window seed 1 0.7666 (ep 3) 0.7525 (ep 3) 0.8500 -> 0.8298 seed 2 0.7520 (ep 2) 0.7497 (ep 1) 0.8311 -> 0.8305 seed 3 0.7612 (ep 2) 0.7715 (ep 2) 0.8436 -> 0.8532

86da847c recorded that both heads were worse on the metric that selected their epoch. Seed 3 is better on it, by a point of balanced accuracy and a point of AUC, and reads the fewest passages differently of the three. So the held-out metric and Population A disagree in direction on seed 3 as they did in the ninety-ninth finding on both seeds, and the "worse at its own task" reading was two seeds' noise. Neither direction is a pattern.

5. What is on disk that was not before

scripts/probe_harvest_paired.py — two reader harvests of the same passages, read at the bar protocol with extent on and hybrid reduce, each at its own CV'd threshold, aligned by passage id (asserted), paired by McNemar on the joint bit, on right alone and on correct silence alone, with the verdict moves and the species tables side by side, and — added for §7 — each harvest read at the other's attempt rate by shifting both of its fold thresholds together, as the willingness control. The seed-1 and seed-2 reads in 86da847c's message were done by hand and lived in no file, which is how the eighty-fifth finding's A2 table was lost; this is that read committed, and probe_reduce_shipped.py's bar protocol is imported rather than re-implemented.

evals/results/paired_nullwf_s{1,2,3}.json, reader_funnel_s3.json (the shipped seed-3 funnel, which reader_funnel.json did not hold) and reader_funnel_nullwf_s{1,2,3}.json.

6. Verdict and limits

Closed as an arm aimed at right: the 420 bar failed on three seeds, the paired right line is +5 pooled at p = 0.78, and the mechanism it was written for reproduces on one seed of three. Under the audit's rule — a pre-registered bar failed on two seeds — it is closed.

Not closed as a precision improvement, and not banked either. §1 and §2 read it as a better refusal — fewer wrong on every seed (−17 / −9 / −9), more correct silences on every seed (+8 / +6 / +5, pooled p = 0.056), attempt rate down 0.9 to 1.8 points. §7, written after this section, says the silence half of that is the threshold and not the head: read at the shipped head's own attempt rate the arm has the same correct silences and is right on 8 / 5 / 4 more passages and wrong on 8 / 6 / 4 fewer. That is the arm's whole content — about one point of acc | att at equal willingness, same sign on three seeds, each inside fold noise. If the owner wants it, it costs moving scripts/window_features.py under corn/ and re-pointing corn/reader.py at nullwf_s*.pt and its thresholds; nothing else changes. This file does not make that call.

Limits. The extent rule at d = 2.0 and the hybrid reduce are the shipped constants and were not re-tuned for the arm. The pooled McNemar treats the three seeds' passages as independent trials when they are the same 1,003 passages read by three heads, so the pooled p-values are optimistic. Seed 3's shipped baseline, 400 right, is eleven above seeds 1 and 2, and its shipped funnel (reader_funnel_s3.json) was not in the record before this file.

7. The willingness control, run after §6 was written: the silence gain is the threshold, and what is left is one point of precision at equal willingness

§6 named the read that would settle whether §1's shape was more than a re-tune of willingness. It is one sweep and it was written the same session: probe_harvest_paired.py now moves both of a harvest's CV'd fold thresholds together until its attempt rate matches the other harvest's, and reads the table there. Shifting the pair keeps the fold structure and the bar protocol's own constants; only willingness moves.

s2 shipped 388 172 161 196 86 0.777 0.693 50 s2 shipped @ arm 386 169 166 199 83 0.770 0.695 48 +0.05 s2 + window 391 163 167 202 80 0.768 0.706 49 s2 window @ shpd 395 165 161 199 83 0.777 0.705 50 -0.05

s3 shipped 400 178 143 201 81 0.802 0.692 64 s3 shipped @ arm 394 173 154 206 76 0.786 0.695 61 +0.08 s3 + window 398 169 154 206 76 0.786 0.702 63 s3 window @ shpd 404 173 144 197 85 0.800 0.700 64 -0.13

At the arm's attempt rate the shipped head has exactly the arm's correct silences on seeds 1 and 3 (207, 206) and three fewer on seed 2 (199 against 202). §1's "more correct silences on every seed" was the arm's CV'd threshold sitting 0.05 to 0.12 logits stricter in effect, and the bar protocol choosing it because the stricter point held the silence share; the shipped head moved to the same willingness refuses the same passages. The pooled p = 0.056 on the silence line in §2 is therefore not evidence about the five features.

What does not go away when willingness is equalised:

arm minus shipped right wrong u.silent at the arm's attempt rate +8 -8 0 seed 1 +5 -6 +3 seed 2 +4 -4 0 seed 3 at the shipped attempt rate +9 -9 0 seed 1 +7 -7 +3 seed 2 +4 -5 -4 seed 3

The arm's content is 4 to 9 more right and as many fewer wrong a seed at the same willingness, same sign on all three seeds and in both directions of the match, +17 / −18 pooled at the arm's rate and +20 / −21 at the shipped rate. Each seed is inside the ±7 fold noise; three of the same sign and size are not nothing and are not a bar. On seed 1 AGGREGATION still falls at matched willingness, 57 → 43, so part of that seed's mechanism win was the head and not the threshold — and it still does not appear on seeds 2 and 3 (48 → 49, 61 → 63).

Two cautions on the control itself. window @ shipped on seed 3 reads 197 correct silences, four under that seed's floor of 201, so that row is not a point the reader would ship and is quoted only as the mirror of the match. And the match is on attempt rate over the answerable half; a match on correct silences would move the thresholds by a different amount and was not run.

§6's verdict, restated with this in hand. Closed on right at the bar. What the arm actually buys is about one point of acc | att at equal willingness — the same right-for-wrong trade in every seed — and not the refusal §1 described. That is the number the owner's rule would count, with the attempt rate unchanged by construction; it is smaller than §1 made it look, and it is the honest one.
