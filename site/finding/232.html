Understanding at small scale · 14 November 2026

He uses far words like the 260M; his gap is close in

The result, as the finding states it

On 4,800 held-out meetings of word pairs — 2,400 the ruler-blind book ties and his text shows 30 times or more, 2,400 it says nothing about — what an earlier word is worth to the 20M at the next piece falls with distance no faster than it does for the 260M. A tied word 9 to 16 words back takes 0.28 and 0.29 nats off the word it is tied to at the two 20Ms and 0.33 at the 260M; the 20Ms keep 0.85 and 0.88 of their standing against the 260M from one word back to there, where the row held at 0.5 and failed at 0.8. With the context cut to the last k pieces, pieces 11 to 40 back are worth 0.66 and 0.63 of what they are worth to the 260M and pieces 2 to 10 back 0.66 and 0.71: the same two thirds near and far, 0.99 and 0.90 on a row with the same lines. D, T and Z fail. Where the 20M does fall short is close in: 0.65 and 0.70 of the 260M's pair worth two words back and 0.56 and 0.51 at three to four, and of the 2.15 nats between them on these words with the whole context, 1.6 are already there with only the last five pieces in view. The two 5Ms read like the 20Ms on every row; the 100M reads like the 260M on every row and reads the ruler's fields like the 20M.

The question (owner, 2026-11-14: "I feel as though we are close but are missing something", then "Yes let's do it"). Finding 228 left distance as the clue under the fields stall — a count answers more fields the further it looks — and named the read nobody made: how far back he actually reads. Findings 229 to 231 then tried three ways to teach him from afar. The reading offered to the owner was that those tries changed the question he is asked and none changed the path by which a far word gets credit, so that what an earlier word is worth to him should fall away with distance faster than it does for the 260M. This is that read. The pre-registration is scripts/probe_far_read.py's docstring.

The pre-registered rows

  • R holds. Every bucket has 400 and 400, and the 260M's pair worth is six standard errors or more above zero in each.
  • D fails. q is 0.85 and 0.88, over the 0.8 line at both seeds; the intervals (0.67 to 1.08, 0.68 to 1.12) do not reach 0.5. Predicted 0.3 to 0.6: wrong.
  • T fails. tq is 0.99 and 0.90. Predicted between: wrong. t(2-10) was called near 0.7 and read 0.66 and 0.71; t(11-40) was called near 0.45 and read 0.66 and 0.63.
  • Z fails, as predicted: the 20Ms' pair worth is over nine standard errors above zero at 9-16 and at 17-30.
  • The 5Ms share the 20M's shape, as predicted. The 100M does not: it stands level with the 260M at every distance.

Where he does fall short (a cause read, not gated)

  • Two to four words back. r is 0.65 and 0.70 at two words and 0.56 and 0.51 at three to four, with intervals clear of 1, and back at 0.8 to 0.9 beyond. The gap is a few meetings each worth a great deal: the 40 meetings of 400 where the 260M gains most carry 37% of the bucket's gap at two words and 61% at three to four.
  • Read by hand, those meetings are set phrases. shed to light (the 260M's cost for light is 0.02 nats, the 20M's 6.96), making to comeback (0.13 against 7.31), threatened to extinction, ring to bell, serve to purpose, within to limits, differences to similarities, doctors to nurses, unless to otherwise, hasn't to yet, interesting to boring, winds to mph. In each the 260M has the word all but written and the swap costs it 5 to 13 nats; the 20M gets a nat or two from a and still pays 5 nats or more for b. What he lacks there is the earlier word together with the one or two between.
  • Most of the gap is in the last five pieces. On the tied meetings the 260M is 0.41 nats ahead of the 20M (seed 1337) with one piece of context, 1.58 with five, 1.91 with ten and 2.15 with all 128: three quarters of what separates them is made out of five pieces.
  • Far back the two models differ meeting by meeting, not in amount. The two 20Ms' worths agree at 0.82 to 0.92 across meetings in every bucket; a 20M and the 260M at 0.34 to 0.65. At 9-16 words the ten read by hand move little in either model (heart to kidney, content to website, search to answer: under 0.1 in both), and where one moves it is as often the 20M (written to novel, +1.44 against 0.00).
  • By how often the pair met, at 9-16 words: 0.24 against the 260M's 0.22 for pairs met 30 to 100 times, 0.33 against 0.41 for 100 to 400, 0.27 against 0.48 for 400 or more (62 meetings). Printed; not a result.

What it licenses

By the pre-registration, its second outcome: distance is not where he falls short.

  • The credit-path reading loses its evidence. It predicted a 20M that loses its standing against the 260M as the earlier word moves back, and he does not, on either read. The wide lane beside his blocks was to be built on D holding and is not built on this. Its remaining support is finding 227's cause read of the book speaking on his scores.
  • Finding 228's clue was about the count, not about him. A count answers more fields the further it looks because the text's ties barely fade (+3.46 to +2.40 nats over thirty words). That says where the knowledge is on offer. It does not say he reads far worse than a model that has it: he uses a tied word nine to sixteen words back about as well as the 260M does. Finding 228 is amended.
  • What separates a small model from the 260M on these words is what it makes of the last few pieces: a word two to four back bound with the ones between into a phrase. That is the near question, which the next-piece lesson pays for directly, and it is where size and text buy the most.
  • This instrument does not see the ruler's gap. The 100M is level with the 260M on every row here and reads 80 fields to the 260M's 101, beside the 20M's 78 and 76 (finding 221). Whatever the fields ask for, it is not what an earlier word is worth by distance in running text.
  • The read is an instrument worth keeping. 800 meetings a bucket give standard errors of 0.02 to 0.04 nats from five words back, the two seeds of a size agree, and it trains nothing. It is unguarded: its own checks are the count against a plain loop, the batched read against five single windows and each swap changing only a's pieces, so by rule 28 these numbers are bounds through scripts/probe_far_read.py.

What it does not settle. Why the fields stall: this removes distance and puts nothing in its place. The ruler's own pairs — a phrase's content word and the field's name — read this way in running text, which would say whether he holds those ties and cannot use them through the ruler's frame, or does not hold them. Whether the 260M ever read this held-out text (it read other tokens; unchecked). The 100M at the same text: nano100m_v1 read another mix. Contexts past 128 pieces. Stand-ins of the same length and commonness, two a meeting: a third would say how much of the near buckets' spread is the stand-in.
