Search returned 'capital punishment' for 'capital of Mongolia'; fixed

The result, as the finding states it

"capital of Mongolia" means capital punishment until the sort stops helping, and a surname counts toward its whole name

What it cost

0 s of new scoring on record; it reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m_ftextract). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

The first live --extract session, 2026-08-27, and the two runtime repairs it priced. corn_jr_260m_ftextract behind harness.server --search --extract, real Wikipedia, four turns. No training run, no eval change: both defects and both fixes live in harness/backends/search.py and corn/extract.py, which no frozen scorecard reads.

What the live session found: the failure is one link upstream of everything finding 65 measured

All four turns routed correctly — including the bare proper name that used to degenerate into a run-on query — and none produced garbage. Two of them answered the wrong page:

what is the capital of Mongolia -> "Capital punishment, according to wikipedia.org." what is the capital of France -> the Capital punishment in France lead, deferred verbatim

Extraction never had a chance: the wrong passage arrived. Findings 55-65 are all about link four; this is link two handing link four the wrong document, which no amount of extraction quality can repair.

The sort was the bug, measured at the API

_wiki_search ranked candidate titles by shared-term count, written for "the boiling point of water" ranking High-altitude cooking first. Probed live against the MediaWiki API on the four session queries, the engine's own first result was the right page four times out of four — Ulaanbaatar, List of capitals of France (Paris at rank 4), Icelandic króna, Boiling point — and the shared-term sort is what inverted it:

query engine rank 1 sort promoted the capital of Mongolia Ulaanbaatar (0 shared) Capital punishment in Mongolia (2 shared) the currency of Iceland Icelandic króna (0) Economy of Iceland / Crown (currency) (1)

"Icelandic króna" shares zero terms with "the currency of Iceland" under exact set intersection — the answer page is routinely titled by the answer, which the query's words cannot contain. And the coverage gate cannot save the wrong page, because "capital" prefix-covers "Capital punishment".

The repair is a demotion, not a smarter sort. What separates the wrong pages is not missing query words but extra ones: a title mixing the query's terms with content words the query never said (Capital punishment in Mongolia, Economy of Iceland, List of capitals of France) names a compound subject that merely contains the asked-about words. Mixed titles go to the back of the engine's own order; everything else stays where the engine put it. Demoted rather than dropped, so a mixed title still answers when nothing cleaner passes the lead gate — scripts/check_search.py asserts both directions. The High-altitude cooking case needs no sort at all now: the lead-sentence gate added since rejects it on its own. srlimit rises 3 → MAX_RESULTS (5) because Paris is the engine's fourth result for "the capital of France", behind three mixed titles.

After the change, live: Ulaanbaatar. / Paris, according to wikipedia.org. / The króna or krona is the currency of Iceland. — all three property questions correct on the same checkpoint, same day.

The tally now knows a surname is its whole name, and what that cost was measured at zero

The handoff had already flagged reduce's disagreement rule discarding a correct extraction, and the live session reproduced the shape on its first proper-name question. The specific defect: a reply "Alexander Fleming." keys alexander fleming and a reply "Fleming." keys fleming, and the tally read one fact as two — the exact whole-name-against-surname reading corn/extract.py's _overlaps documents, unused by the tally.

_canonical merges a key into the one maximal key that word-contains it. The instrument is the _span_key docstring's, rebuilt over data/raw/nq-dev.jsonl (721 answerable passages, replies in the corpus's own SAY_ANSWER framings), merge off against merge on in one process:

same fact framed differently agrees 131/343 -> 131/343 unchanged gold against a same-type rival agrees 0/606 -> 0/606 unchanged whole in one window, tail in another 4/206 -> 83/206 the buy

Of the 85 part-against-whole cases the locality gate admits at all, the merge delivers 83; the other 121 fail identically with the merge off — they are property_local (finding 66's rule) holding, not this change failing. The answer returned carries the whole statement in every merged case, whichever window said it. (The 131/343 same-fact rate is not comparable to the _span_key docstring's 678/678: that measurement predates property_local sitting in the reduce path, and the rate is identical in both arms here.)

Two rules keep it honest, and the guard caught the first draft violating one. A key may only merge when every reply carrying it asserted exactly that key — _span_key keys a truncated reply by the longest run a window actually contains, so "It's the AFC NFL." over windows that only say "NFC NFL" keys down to nfl, and the first draft merged it into nfc nfl, counting a contradiction toward the fact it contradicts. check_extract.py's NFC/AFC case went red before the code ever ran live. And a part contained in two different maximal keys — fleming against both alexander fleming and fleming institute — stays its own group rather than guessing.

What stands, deliberately

The live Ada Lovelace turn still falls back with 4 windows disagree (augusta ada, lord byron, lovelace, ockham) — augusta ada and lovelace share no run, and merging them needs entity knowledge the conservative rule refuses; the guard pins that as correct. For a bare "look up X" turn the lead sentence is the right answer anyway; that the model then cut it to "Looks like Augusta Ada." is a weights matter, not a runtime one. And property_local treating question stopwords as pointing terms — "the" beside "Nobel Prize" rejecting alexander fleming, hit while building the fixture — is measured machinery (finding 70 scored with it) and is left for its own measured cell.

Artefacts

The guard additions are scripts/check_search.py (the Mongolia inversion and the demoted-not-dropped case) and scripts/check_extract.py (the surname merge, both reply orders, the no-shared-run abstention, the two-wholes ambiguity, and the NFC/AFC truncation case). Both green, and the full sweep with them. Session log rows for the before and after turns are in Saved Sessions/turns.jsonl, 2026-08-27.
