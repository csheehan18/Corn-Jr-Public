Two decisions: whole-passage reader by default, life questions declined

The result, as the finding states it

Decisions 7 and 8 are taken: the whole-passage reader is the default, and a routed question about his life is answered at the runtime by a decline that says he is a model, which catches all sixteen held-out phrasings and none of twenty requests

2026-09-05, evening, the owner's two decisions after finding 124, both runtime-only and both guarded. No card. Nothing trained. The eval rows are unchanged in what they measure.

1. Decision 7 — reader defaults to head_whole

harness/serverconfig.py reader: str = "head_whole". The pair it serves is finding 124's: reader_whole_social8_s1.pt on the social8 trunk at WHOLE_THRESHOLD -1.280, keyed by hash. The number the decision was taken on, Population A through the real read_passage:

reader right wrong silent | u.silent wrong precision ms/passage generate (the gate, 117 a) 230 99 392 | 256 26 64.8% — head_whole (this pair, 124) 517 147 57 | 197 85 70.6% 23.5

More than twice the right answers at higher precision, spending 59 correct silences of 282. --reader generate and --reader head stay as arms, so the comparison remains paired; check_extract.py now asserts the default is head_whole and that every head has its flag. What a user sees change: a question a pack or a search can answer is answered from the passage read whole, in one forward.

2. Decision 8 — the routed question about his life

The gap. ABOUT_HIS_LIFE is a whole-utterance set and the hatch answers its 31 members from NO_LIFE_REPLIES; the sixteen held-out phrasings in evals/social_self.jsonl ("so how old are you anyway", "any pets", "where are you based") reached the router by design so social.self_heldout could read the weights. Finding 122 §4 read what the weights do there on the arm 7 trunk: they speak on twice as many and half of what they say is SODA's life ("I'm 23", "I work ..."). The plan's decision 8 named three repairs; the owner took the router-side rule, with the reply he specified — a decline that says he is a model with no concept of the thing asked.

The rule, corn.agent.life_question, tried inside identity_reply after the whole-utterance sets and the two-clause split have missed. Three conditions, all required:

addressed to him you / your / yourself, or a bare possession question ("any pets", "got any kids", "how's the family") a life topic age, work, home, family, pets, habits, a body — a closed list, and nothing wider no request no tool-register verb, no "can/could/would you", no "my" (a question about the user's things routes)

The reply, NO_LIFE_ROUTED_REPLIES, six hand-written lines held to the self pool's bars (finished sentence, no digit, passes social_rejection, name only through Persona.pool), each saying he is a model and handing the turn back: "That one's not a thing for me. I'm a model, not a person." / "I got trained, not born, so that's out. What can I do for you?" Identity answers are never rephrased by the weights (_own_words runs on the social path only), so the line is served as written.

The reads, all on the CPU:

the 16 held-out phrasings of social_self.jsonl 16 of 16 caught 20 must-route turns ("can you call mom", "do you know the capital of france", "what's my dog's name", "how are you", "what day is it", "note that i'm tired", "can you sleep", "how long does a dog live") 0 of 20 caught check_hatches' self battery 13 -> 15 of 16 the one miss is "I love you buddy", which is no question check_toolgrid: no grid A/B/C request reaches any hatch unchanged, green check_confirm: no orphan yes/no answer is caught unchanged, green

What it does and does not change. At the runtime, a question about his life in any of these shapes is answered by the pool and never reaches the weights; the exposure finding 122 measured (four of sixteen held-out phrasings given a life) is closed at the boundary the rule draws. The eval rows social.self and social.self_heldout still send prompt_route to the model directly and still read the weights — the ruler is unchanged, and the residue it measures (finding 122 §4) is still in the weights, unrepaired, which is what §3 rule 1 says it must be. check_evals.py group 32 now asserts every held-out phrasing takes the identity hatch's rule and none the social or temporal hatch.

3. Held

The owner held two things: a second seed of the re-fit (finding 124's open question) and §7's speech-temperature sweep (decision 5). Both are on the plan's current-order block as item 14.

Limits

The rule is lexical and the topic list is closed; a phrasing about his life that names no listed topic ("what's your story", "tell me about yourself") routes as before, and "I love you buddy" is not a question the rule can see. The twenty must-route turns are a hand-written battery, not a population. Nothing here is a live session.
