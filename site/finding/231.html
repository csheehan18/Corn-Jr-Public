Understanding at small scale · 13 November 2026

A far lesson in training takes, costs reading, and lifts nothing clearly

The result, as the finding states it

A 5M trained from its first step with the far lesson beside its reading — 2,048 pairs a micro-batch off the reading's own scores, which of two pieces stands 2 to 40 ahead, a piece's general likelihood cancelling — learns the lesson at weight 0.5 (far pairs right on held-out text 0.711 and 0.700 against its controls' 0.631 and 0.627) and reads 80 and 83 of the ruler's 141 fields: a direction by the registered row, and inside what twelve 5Ms with no far lesson read (65 to 84, the controls 79 and 75). It pays 0.160 and 0.156 nats a token on held-out text, three and a half times the gap between a 5M and a 20M (0.045, finding 197), on every source. At weight 0.1 the text costs 0.017 and 0.019, the lesson is half learned (0.665 and 0.663) and the fields read 71 and 74. The one read nobody called — the light cell's wishes at 72 and 80 of 192, at and over the top of the no-lesson runs' 61 to 72 — was registered for replication and failed it: two more seeds read 69 and 61.

What it cost

lesson, guard 109, four configs and reader committed with the pre-registration at 16:35 on 2026-11-13 (81507f7), after a read of the twelve no-lesson runs (164.3 s of card) and a 250-iteration smoke in a scratch directory (58.6 s); the four runs trained 380.8, 381.8, 381.8 and 384.2 s (the controls 350.0 and 353.9), 1,763.5 s for the chain with its read; the replication, registered at cf7025a after the four were read, 354.7 and 354.6 s, 934.4 s with its read; the repetition cause read 74.4 s, contended. ETA about thirty minutes of card for the four runs and their read; 29.4.

The question (owner, 2026-11-13: "Yes do the 5M"). Findings 229 and 230 asked a trained 20M for a word from where its neighbour stands and found two stores: what a far step writes, the far question reads, and the next-piece question does not. No step on a trained trunk can say whether the stores are two when both questions are asked from the first step. This is that arm, at the size that reads the ruler as the 20M does (finding 197) and trains in six minutes. The pre-registration is configs/nano5m_v3_webblend_far.py's docstring; the mechanism is corn/far.py.

The replication (registered after the four were read)

LIGHT's wishes, 72 and 80, sat at and over the top of the twelve no-lesson runs, and the pre-registration had called them flat. Two more seeds, one row — both at 73 or more — and a prediction that it would not hold:

W2 seeds 1339 and 1340: 69 and 61 wishes -> FAILS

Four seeds of the light lesson read 72, 80, 69 and 61. It was the draw.

Read by hand

  • What he writes. Six openers a run, one seed. FAR's chat replies read like the control's. Its web continuations loop on what was just said: "The cheapest way to travel across Europe is in New York, New York, and New York. / Martnet, Illinois, a professor of professor of science and science, is a professor of psychology"; "The first study was developed to the science of Earth, which was the first study, has an important study". LIGHT reads like the control.
  • Counted afterwards (a cause read). Over 40 held-out web openers, words of four letters or more that repeat one of the twenty before them: the text itself 5.7%; the controls 25.4% and 26.0%; FAR 31.9% and 28.3%; LIGHT 27.2%, 27.2%, 28.9% and 25.8%. A 5M already says things again four times as often as the text does; the heavy lesson adds two to six points. The lesson pays him to score what is in the air, and what is in the air is what he has just said.
  • The fields that change side at seed 1337: 18 gained, 17 lost — a retrain's worth of churn (finding 222), one item net. The gains are the phrase's content ("that fits in a small pocket" to size, "I can play with three friends" to players); so are the losses ("near the beach" from location to rating).
  • Stock answers. The controls' commonest wrong field is price (8 and 7 of 62 and 66 misses); each of the four registered far runs' is rating (5 to 7). The default moved; it did not go.

What it licenses

By the pre-registration: the lesson takes, the fields are a direction inside the no-lesson range, and the reading pays — the two questions compete for the same scores.

  • Asked both questions from the first step, he does not learn the fields a count learns. The heavy lesson may be worth a few items (81.5 over two seeds against a no-lesson mean of 74.5 with sd 5.0); it is not worth 85, and it is bought with more reading loss than separates a 5M from a 20M. A lesson a fifth as heavy is nearly free and buys nothing.
  • Sharing the scores is the cost, and not sharing them is two stores. Read off the reading's own scores, "coming within forty" and "coming next" pull the same numbers two ways, and the reading loses. A separate head would spare the reading and is, by construction, the second store of findings 229 and 230.
  • This closes the training side of finding 221's idea for a lesson of this shape. Seven reads in one day — the press weighted by the book (222), one hard press (224), the press held against the swap (225), kept (226), asked from afar (229), kept from afar (230), and from the first step (231) — and each time what he keeps is what the next-piece question pays for. The book's knowledge is real (91 wishes and 95 fields, ruler unread, finding 227), it is far-apart knowledge (finding 228), and nothing here put it in him by gradient.
  • At 5M, two seeds are not an instrument for small effects. The no-lesson spread is 19 fields wide. The light cell's wishes looked like a result at two seeds and were gone at four; the replication cost thirteen minutes and belongs in any 5M arm that reads the ruler.

What it does not settle. A weight between 0.1 and 0.5. A separate head on a shared trunk, read at the next piece. A 20M, where the reading has more room to pay from. Whether the few items at weight 0.5 are real: four more seeds would say, and at this price nobody needs to know.
