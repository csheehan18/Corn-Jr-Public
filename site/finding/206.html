The reply judge · 22 October 2026

A matching lesson at a tenth of conversation reading did not take

The result, as the finding states it

Graded from the first step on whether a reply belongs to its message — two quads a micro-batch from the 717,092 message-reply pairs of his own conversational reading, scored lp(r1|m1) + lp(r2|m2) - lp(r2|m1) - lp(r1|m2) so a reply's general likelihood cancels, weighted like one token of the reading — the 5M matches no better afterwards: same-dialogue quads right 0.873 and 0.867 against the control's 0.872 and 0.858 (the gate was 0.887 at both), the pre-registered row (u), the exercise did not take at this weight. Its margins rose a little at both seeds (11.00 / 10.30 against 10.43 / 9.69) and its training log climbed to 0.885 by step 5,000; language, the rulers and the swap test are level; the blind hand read gives 73 and 63 of 148 against 70 and 55. By hand, a large part of what the control gets "wrong" has no right answer: two turns of one conversation often carry replies that fit either message

What it cost

corn/contrast.py, its guard (scripts/check_contrast.py, 103 guards), scripts/probe_contrast_take.py, a 500-iteration smoke run, the floor and the pre-registration 13:41-13:47 on 2026-10-22 (5c48510); the chain 13:47-14:06, 19.1 min (both runs 417 and 414 s, contended, a bound); the quad hand check and the blind hand read of 592 replies inside it. ETA 25 min of card, actual 19.1.

The question (owner, 2026-10-22: teach him from wrong answers; "this should come from pretraining"; "no shortcuts"; about a tenth of his conversational reading). Next-word training only ever says what to write. Findings 204-205: the plain 5M recognises a fitting reply far better than it writes one. This adds a matching lesson to pretraining from step one — corn/contrast.py argues every setting — at 2 quads a micro-batch (three same-dialogue to one cross), weighted like one token each, on the control's reading byte for byte (its own generator). Predicted (t): it takes, writing is level.

The gates, as registered. Took: no — 0.873 and 0.867 under 0.887. (u), whatever else moved. Read beside: fluency holds; the hand read is inside the control pair's spread (a seed-for-seed +3 and +8, not the pre-registered direction, which needed both above 70).

Read by hand

The quads. Six same-dialogue quads drawn fresh and decoded with both checkpoints' margins: the instrument scores what it should ("Hi there! I'm going to wash your face and hair" -> "Okay!" against "This feels good! Thank you, Jerry" -> "You're welcome, sweetie...": +21.5 on the control). But the hard ones are hard because nothing decides them: "It's no problem. I want to help you" -> "That means a lot to me" against "I'm happy to offer my protection" -> "Thank you, Rylei. I really appreciate it" — either reply fits either message (+1.5 / +0.9); in another, "Really? I never thought of it that way" and "Exactly! And that's what makes art so interesting" each fit both turns. Two turns of one conversation share the topic, the people and the mood, and their replies are often interchangeable; the 13% the control gets wrong is part error and part a coin with no right side.

The replies (592, blind; the control's labels came out 70 and 55 for the third time) fail as before: "That's great!" to a spilled coffee, the self-doubt line to open questions.

Corrected by finding 207 (2026-10-22). The six quads above were not a count. Read blind with a baseline, a person decides 42 of 50 same-dialogue quads the control gets wrong, 38 correctly; undecidable pairs are about a sixth of the misses, not "a large part". The reading (u) stands; the reason given for it below does not, and the licence's "if few" branch is the one that did not happen.

What it licenses

  • At this weight the lesson does not move him. It honoured the cap the owner set (~1.6% of the reading's weight); quads already right by a wide margin teach almost nothing, and the ones left are partly undecidable.
  • Before a heavier weight, measure the headroom by hand: read 50 same-dialogue quads the control gets wrong and count how many a person could decide. If most cannot be decided, a heavier weight trains on noise and the exercise needs harder decidable pairs (a reply that answers a question against one that answers a statement; a reply that fits the mood against one that fits the topic) rather than more of these.
  • The machinery stands: pairs from his own reading, a margin no reply prior can move, the reading untouched — guarded and ready for either.

What it does not settle. Any heavier weight; any size above 5M; whether a lesson that took would carry into writing (finding 205's open question).
