Measurement and noise · 24 August 2026

Two of my checks were scoring the harness, not the model

The result, as the finding states it

The envelope decides the output too, and two of my checks were reading the harness as the model

What it cost

0 s of new scoring on record; it reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

corn/extract.py wired into a turn, run 2026-08-24 against corn_jr_260m on the 3080 with a stubbed search backend, no network and nothing trained. The work was plumbing — World.reader, the chunk-and-reduce in the web_search executor, fell_back carrying the reduce reason. It was expected to yield nothing and it produced two instrument defects, both of which had already consumed published numbers.

Both are the same mistake in two places, and the fifty-second finding's neighbour explains why. The previous session established that this model is sensitive at the marker level on the way in: the same window as bare prose, as a JSON payload, and as a whole <|call|> → payload → <|say|> transcript gives conversational drift, an invented number and a correct extraction. What this finding adds is that the same is true on the way out — his reply is drawn from the corpus's own sentence templates, and a check written from taste rather than from the corpus refuses it.

The frame vocabulary was written from three of four templates

corn/toolgen.py's SAY_ANSWER is the complete set of sentences the corpus ever wraps a search answer in:

{a}. It's {a}. {a}, according to {s}. Looks like {a}.

grounded's FRAME — the closed set of words a reply may add that came from neither the window nor the question — covered the first three. It had it, s, according, to. It did not have looks or like.

Over a six-sentence iron abstract, three windows:

windowreplyverdict
1Iron is a chemical element … a soft, silvery-white metal …refused, correctly — soft, silvery, white are not in the passage
2Looks like 1538 degrees Celsius.refused, wrongly — on looks and like
3Looks like 1538 degrees Celsius.refused, wrongly — same

Two of three windows produced the correct answer and the acceptance test called both inventions. With the two words added, the same run reads 2 of 3 windows agree on '1538 degrees celsius' and the user hears the melting point instead of the atomic number, in 465 ms.

The fix that generalises is not the two words. scripts/check_extract.py now asserts FRAME ⊇ SAY_ANSWER's framing words, so a fifth template added to the corpus fails a guard rather than silently costing an extraction rate. The class of defect — a runtime rule about what the model may say, written without consulting the corpus that taught it — has now cost four checks in two sessions.

Four of five windows never stop, and that was being reported as "nothing found"

The previous session had already found this and separated it: an unfinished reply is withheld rather than allowed to vote, "otherwise the token budget decides the answer". That separation lived in its probe script and not in corn/extract.py, and the three files it committed did not carry it. Wiring the chain into a turn therefore reproduced the bug one commit after it was fixed.

BatchedReader runs the full budget for every row deliberately. A reply that reached no marker used all 32 tokens and gave the opening of a long answer, not a short one — and handed to grounded its cut-off last word reads as an invention. Live, on the first three passages:

the melting point of iron 1 of 3 windows never finished the capital of Laos 1 of 1 never finished the population of Laos 1 of 1 never finished

All three had been reported as no window extracted an answer. Four of five windows across the run, against 5 of 12 in the offline probe. The reason strings now carry the count on both arms, so the untrained failure mode is a live number on every turn rather than something someone has to go and measure.

It is not "wrong", it is "unfinished", and the distinction sets the corpus target. grounded already enforces the safety half — nothing ungrounded has ever reached the reduce, including the distractor case. What is missing is stop when the answer is out, which is not a property a runtime can fake, and which MAX_NEW is deliberately not raised to hide: the corpus's post-observation say is at most 79 characters over 1,629,766 of them, so 32 tokens is already generous against the distribution being asked for.

What this retires

0/6 and 1/12 are lower bounds with a known bias, not measurements. Both published extraction baselines were taken through a FRAME that refused the reply form this model prefers; the windowed one also predates the truncation separation reaching the runtime. Marked as bounds in docs/PLAN-WEB-SEARCH.md and docs/EXTERNAL-RESEARCH.md. Nothing here says the untrained rate is good — it says it has not been measured, and that both numbers were partly measuring the instrument.

What this cannot say

Three passages, one checkpoint, no seeds. The 2-of-3 result is one abstract of one subject shape, and the melting point of … is the worst shape in the fifty-second finding's grid at 1/25 on real subjects. A better rate on an easier shape would not be a surprise and would not be a measurement either.

Nothing about the chain. Of five phrasings of the same question, one routed to web_search and four declined. The extraction path runs on the turns that reach it, and most turns do not — link 1 is unchanged and is still where the loss is.

Nothing about abstention. Every fallback here was an unfinished worker. Whether he can report a genuine absence is untested, and it is the one output grounded cannot verify.

What follows

The transferable form of the envelope claim is a method, not an inspection. "Check that the markers are on-distribution" is only actionable for someone who owns the corpus, which is what EXTERNAL-RESEARCH.md §8's papers do not:

Hold the content fixed, vary the structural envelope, and see whether the answer changes. If it does, the number was never a capability measurement.

That runs against any black-box model, it is what the call-line probe actually did, and no entry in §8 reports having tried it before concluding that utilization is a capability problem. Given §8's SmolLM2-360M at 100% and Qwen2.5-7B at 85.4% under an oracle passage, it is worth someone running.

Two conventions in docs/HACKING.md §4 came out of this and are the durable part: a number in a commit message says whether it was committed or probe-only, and a measurement made through an unguarded check measures the check.
