The tag language · 13 October 2026

Naming the flexible limit outright splits the seeds

The result, as the finding states it

Recover with the field named outright ("though the price can stretch a bit"): the seeds split — paired 8 and 288 of 288 — but both learn whether the person bent at all, which neither did when the limit was named by meaning, so the position is not a wall; one seed learns which limit and the other forms a fixed ranking; and the standing copy kill fires at both seeds, a copied number one digit off

What it cost

41.4 min of training two nano runs (1,243 s each, contended — the card at 38-39%, a bound; registered ~45 min end to end, actual 42.4); 38 s of probing, two tool_copy_wide cards in 18 s, the two control reads 58 s and the hand read 11 s on the card; the build about 5 min of CPU (15:13-15:18 on 2026-10-13), the read 16:01-16:10.

Finding 167's split, at the owner's go, pre-registered in configs/mix_v3_funcworld_recover_named.py's docstring and committed at 84e9a2d before a byte trained — after the owner pointed at findings 158-159, whose rule (a wording seen more than ten times that settles the answer is labelled from memory) said recover should have been learned as a table and was not. One change from recover: the flex clause's noun is the field's own key word (recovergen.NAMED_NOUNS: "the price", "the km", "the rating"), so the clause matches relax[...] by word; same generator seed, frames, lists, dose (8,100 traces, 2.21% of the mix, 5,843 iterations), arm E2 underneath. Every field x frame pairing trains (450 each); the rows are 288 fresh trained relax rows with twins and 96 ask rows; the gate is paired (both twins right) more than 42 of 288 at both seeds. Cards: evals/results/nano20m_v3_fw_recover_named{,_seed2}__recover_named.json, __copywide.json; the controls' nano20m_v3_fw_filter_ev2{,_seed2}__recover_named.json.

The registered read

seed paired (of 288) twin moved relax rows right ask rows right (of 96) 1337 8 14 140 of 288 96 2337 288 288 288 of 288 94

Seeds split on the gate: the registered row "a direction; no third seed". The prediction on the record — the gate passes at both seeds — held at one. Controls (arm E2): ask on every row, paired 0 at both seeds; readable.

The standing kill fires at both seeds: tool.copy_wide 265 of 300 against arm E2's 298 at seed 1337, 290 against 295 at seed 2337 (the half-width 4.4; below by 5). Read by hand, the misses are one species: a copied number with its first digit moved — "4,086 degrees Celsius" written "5,086", "4,514 metres" written "5,514" — 26 wrong answers and 9 trained answers at seed 1337. Recover's own arm, whose payload differs from this one only in the clause's noun, read 298 and 287 on the same instrument (finding 167). A cost on the copy band at both seeds is the kill as registered; that it moves this far between two payloads a word apart is finding 139's caution about the copy band at nano, and is said beside it.

Read by hand — both seeds learned more than the gate shows

A scratch read of every row (probe only — not on disk; recover's columns from finding 167 beside):

the pull toward ask with a clause with none (nats: nll(ask) - best relax) recover seed 1337 +0.55 +0.51 no difference recover seed 2337 +0.20 +0.19 no difference named seed 1337 +2.80 -4.18 flips sign named seed 2337 +4.68 -2.48 flips sign

Both named seeds learned whether the person bent at all — relax when a clause is there, ask when it is not — which neither recover seed did. So a decision made after the ~250-token observation does come to depend on the request: the position is not the wall finding 167 left open.

Which limit: seed 2337 prefers the gold on 288 of 288 relax rows by 5.33 nats on average, at every list length. Seed 1337 prefers it on 142 of 288 — chance — and relaxes by a fixed ranking instead, price over rating over km (price/rating: price 99 of 102; km/rating: rating 79 of 86), finding 167's shape.

What it licenses

By the table: a direction, no third seed. Read with finding 167, the two arms bracket the cause: with the limit named by meaning, nothing formed at either seed — not even whether the person bent; with the key word in the request, the bend formed at both seeds and the limit at one. The meaning hop is the larger wall, and the choice of limit is fragile at nano even without it. For recover that points one way: the flex reading is the runtime's — a table of flex phrasings names the limit, as combine's clause became a lookup — and the move can be the runtime's too, since the one part that installed at both seeds (bent or not) is the same lookup's other answer. For the ladder's other menu rungs (branch, loop, replan): a pick after the observation can depend on an earlier key when the key is the option's own word; when it must be read by meaning, two rungs in a row (findings 156, 167) formed a fixed ranking instead. The copy kill is the third reason not to put this payload in the run as it stands.

What it does not settle. Why seed 1337 formed a ranking with the word right there; whether a second pass or a lower share of ask traces would steady it; and whether the digit shift in the copy band is this payload's or nano's noise between payloads — a third seed of either arm would say, and none was registered.

Amended 2026-10-14 — the copy kill is not readable (finding 170)

The standing kill above was read with a binomial half-width of 3-4 items. Seeds of one arm on identical data spread 0-54 items on tool.copy_wide (median 8, 90th percentile 20), so by EXPERIMENTS §5.2, and §5.7 as amended, the gap here is not readable: every seed of this arm would have to sit below arm E2's lowest seed (293) by more than 20 items. The hand-read misses are real outputs and stay as written. What does not stand is "the copy kill fires at both seeds" as a reason against the payload (finding 170).
