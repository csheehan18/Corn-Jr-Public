Half textbook, half everyday web keeps the word gain at lower cost

The result, as the finding states it

At 20M, a web share half FineWeb-Edu and half plain FineWeb with the junk out reads the understanding ruler's wishes 0.38 at both seeds against the Edu band's 0.28-0.32 and Edu's held-out text at 0.906 and 0.907 nats per byte against 0.884-0.887 (the all-plain web costs 0.963) — keeps and recovers, the pre-registered blend outcome; the all-plain filtered cell misses its wish gate at both seeds (0.35, 0.33 against 0.36) and the filter is not why: the pages it dropped hold 2.35% of every wish word, their share of the text; on 40 hard everyday words no 20M checkpoint answers one in its own words

What it cost

four nano runs of 867, 870, 893 and 900 s (2026-10-20 21:16 to 2026-10-21 08:20, the chain paused overnight at the owner's word), cards 246-276 s and rulers ~10 s each; the junk-page count 141.7 s on CPU; the everyday-words read on ten checkpoints 56.9 s; the card at 31-41%, a bound.

The question (pre-registered at 2db41ec, configs/mix_v3_webblend.py). Finding 185: plain FineWeb in place of FineWeb-Edu raised the wish read 0.28-0.32 -> 0.40 on one seed and cost Edu's own held-out text 0.886 -> 0.963. Two cells: P, the same swap with the junk filter's 16,932 pages out (2be3af2) — does the filter take understanding with the junk? — and B, the web weight split to the token between Edu and the filtered file — does half the plain web keep most of the gain at less of the cost? Two seeds each; mix_v3's budget and iterations.

The read

run fields wishes lm web behaviour rows (of 84 with three controls) Edu: nano20m_v3 / _seed2 / 0.50- 0.28- 0.884- — _compiled 0.54 0.32 0.887 finding 185 (unfiltered) 0.58 0.40 0.963 77 in band, 3 below, 4 above P nano20m_v3_plainclean 0.54 0.35 0.964 76 in band, 0 below, 8 above P _seed2 0.56 0.33 0.963 80 in band, 3 below, 1 above B nano20m_v3_webblend 0.55 0.38 0.906 76 in band, 4 below, 4 above B _seed2 0.54 0.38 0.907 72 in band, 7 below, 5 above

B keeps and recovers at both seeds (wishes >= 0.36, lm web <= 0.925): the pre-registered row "a blend keeps the everyday web's gain at under half its cost". It recovers more than half the cost — 0.906 sits 0.019 above the Edu band where the all-plain web sits 0.076 above — and its wishes sit above both P seeds.

P's fields keep (0.54, 0.56) and its wishes miss at both seeds (0.35, 0.33 against a gate of 0.36): the pre-registered row "the filter took understanding with the junk — read the dropped pages by kind before any blend is called".

The dropped pages, read

scripts/probe_junk_wishes.py counts every word the wish read turns on — the 36 wish labels and the content words of its 96 descriptions — in the pages the filter dropped and the pages it kept:

dropped pages 16,932, 12,715,211 words — 2.37% of the text the 36 wish labels 37,817 of 1,612,085 dropped (2.35%; 1.0x the text's share) description words 1,371,716 of 56,235,392 dropped (2.44%; 1.0x)

The labels most over-represented in the junk are cheap (4.2x), thrilling (2.9x), romantic (2.3x) and exciting (1.6x) — the casino and dating pages — and even cheap lost only 3,228 of 32,256 occurrences. The filter took the wish words in proportion to the text and no more, so it cannot be why P reads lower. What is left is the read itself: 96 items put one run's wish rate within about +-0.05 of its mean (binomial), finding 185's 0.40 is one seed, and P's 0.33-0.35 against B's 0.38 is no larger than that spread. Read as a pair, P and B sit 0.03 and 0.07 above the Edu mean (0.31) — the opposite order to their plain shares, inside the spread: at this resolution the wish gain does not grow with the share, which is what makes the blend cheap.

Costs

Finding 185's rule (each behaviour row within the three Edu controls' range widened by 5% of n). No row falls at both seeds of a cell, so no cost replicates. The largest single drops are B seed 2's note rows (tool.note_fidelity, notefid.* 1/120 against 10-19), read off the card's recorded reasons: 117 of 120 wrong_tool, the controls' own dominant failure (89 of 120 at 11/120) — a row near its floor falling further, not a new failure; B seed 1 reads 6/120 on the same row. turn2.second reads 130 and 132 on B seed 1 and P seed 2 against 157-175 and 166 on B seed 2: one seed in each cell. These are bounds — read from the cards' reasons, not decoded by hand. The prediction ("a row or two in each cell") was right for P and low for B (4 and 7 below, none shared).

Hard everyday words (scripts/ruler_words.py, ce0f247)

Finding 188's 40 hard words and phrases, scored on what he writes after "what does X mean? It means" — right only when an accepted meaning appears that he could not copy from the prompt:

every 20M here (Edu, plain, P, B) free 0/40 pick 0.30-0.42 (chance 0.25) corn_jr_260m_v4 free 1/40 pick 0.53 corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social8 free 1/40 pick 0.45

The free read is the number; the pick is printed to show how much it flatters. The 260Ms' one credit each, read by hand: social8 bucks -> "the amount of money that is spent" (right) and v4 ubiquitous -> "not as common as they were" (the inverse; a false credit). Their answers also carry two partial meanings the list does not credit ("on the cards" -> "that the rain is coming"; "staycation" -> "staying in one place for a while") and the 20Ms none. The instrument was fixed during this read: its first pass credited gas guzzler -> "the gas is not a gas", dirt cheap -> an echo of the request and low-key -> "a quiet catch-up" copied from the request; an accepted meaning that shares a content word with the target or its request no longer counts. Predicted: slang stays near zero at 20M — held. Whether the everyday web moves these words is a 260M question.

What it licenses

  • For D1: the web share is a blend. Half Edu, half the filtered plain web keeps the everyday web's word gain at about a quarter of its cost on Edu's own text, at both seeds; the pilot's ruler (and now ruler_words.py) sets the split at 260M.
  • The filtered file is the plain source from here: it took junk and no share of the wish words beyond its share of the text.
  • The wish read's spread is wider than its gates assume. At 96 items a gate 0.04 above a band misses on noise; a read that decides a share wants more items or more seeds.

What it does not settle. Any size above 20M; any split but half; the hard words, which no 20M knows; the cost rows, read off the cards and not by hand.
