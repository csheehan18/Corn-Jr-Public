Measurement and noise · 26 August 2026

The phrasing test itself has noise of sixty items

The result, as the finding states it

The instrument built to read this has a floor of sixty, and four seeds made every floor wider

P3-B1, run 2026-08-26. Four arm seeds against four control seeds, 1,112s each, paired seed for seed (1337–1340). configs/mix_v3_p3b1.py carries the pre-registration; this is what it read.

The treatment. data/raw/tools_p3b1.txt is tools_names.txt with the 2,992 web_search carriers re-drawn from 85 hand-written phrasings instead of 25, and nothing else changed — same subjects, same calls, same observations, 6,775 non-search transcripts byte-identical, seven of eight slices hashing identical to mix_v3's after prepare_mix. It attacks the fifty-second finding's defect: 25 phrasings offered, about four kept.

Not one row separates, and the floors are why

row control x4 arm x4 c spr a spr search.route_base [169, 190] [163, 195] 21 32 search.route_wide [134, 180] [152, 193] 46 41 search.route_heldout [135, 162] [124, 184] 27 60

Midpoints move +4.0, +14.5 and +11.5 against floors of 32, 46 and 60. Every cell overlaps. Ground rule 6 is not close to satisfied on any of them.

Every floor got wider going from two seeds to four, and not one narrowed:

search.route_base ctl 8 -> 21 arm 18 -> 32 search.route_wide ctl 35 -> 46 arm 23 -> 41 search.route_heldout ctl 17 -> 27 arm 44 -> 60

That is the forty-third finding reproducing on a corpus family it was never measured on. It said mix_v1's two-seed spreads were not luck in the convenient direction and got wider at four seeds; this says the same about mix_v3, on a row built three days ago. A two-seed spread is a lower bound on a floor and should never be quoted as the floor.

The absolute size is the part that outlives this arm. search.route_heldout spans 60 of 200 items on the arm alone — 30% of the row — and route_wide 46 on the control. A treatment would have to move a third of the row to be readable here.

The one encouraging pattern, and the two controls that take it away

search.route_wide — the row the treatment targets, carrying the 60 phrasings trained only in the arm — is positive at all four paired seeds: +18, +6, +13, +21, mean +14.5. That is the only registered row with a consistent sign, and it is the one the arm was built to move. It is not enough, for two reasons that were found by looking rather than by argument.

A 4-of-4 sign is what this data produces by chance. Twenty-three untreated rows carry a nonzero paired difference. Under a fair coin, P(all four the same sign) is 0.125, so ~2.9 of 23 should be sign-consistent by noise alone. Observed: exactly 2 — tool.validity all-positive and turn2.second all-negative, neither of which the treatment has any mechanism to move. Sign consistency at four seeds is therefore not evidence in this data; it is the expected rate.

And there is a per-seed offset favouring the arm. Summed across the 23 untreated rows, arm-minus-control reads +32, +8, +72, +118 at seeds 1337–1340 — a mean per-row offset of +1.4, +0.4, +3.1 and +5.1 items. The two later seeds put the arm broadly ahead on rows the corpus change cannot touch, so the paired differences are contaminated by something seed-shaped rather than treatment-shaped. The arm's val loss is marginally worse than its control at every matched seed (2.3786 vs 2.3761, 2.4688 vs 2.4658), so this is not the arm being a better model.

route_wide's per-seed advantage does exceed that offset at every seed (+18 vs +1.4, +6 vs +0.4, +13 vs +3.1, +21 vs +5.1), which is the honest reason not to call it zero either. It is a candidate, not a result.

What is registered, and what is not claimed

The registered report is configs/mix_v3_p3b1.py's own: not readable. Not outcome 1, not outcome 2, not outcome 3. In particular:

  • Not "the dose was installed" — route_wide overlaps its control.
  • Not the honest null — that needs a non-movement on route_heldout, whose arm seeds span 60 of 200. A non-movement inside a 60-wide spread is what the sixtieth finding refused to conclude anything from.
  • Not a cost. tool.selection looked like a 7-item separation at two seeds against a two-seed spread of 1 and 4. At four it reads ctl [109, 130] against arm [117, 144] and overlaps outright, with the arm higher. The nineteenth finding's measured floor for that row is 44. The two-seed reading was the thirtieth finding's trap and nothing else.

Rule 10's regression watch is clean and is the one thing this run establishes. temporal.copy 353/353 on all eight checkpoints. tool.copy_wide midpoint identical to the control's at 290.8. tool.copy reads arm [100, 120] against control [118, 120] — a 20-item arm spread that is one seed reading 100, and the sixtieth finding's method note already measured 9 of 120 moving across five iterations of a single run. Every lm row sits inside its control range, as it must for a transform touching 0.62% of the mix that changed no subject.

What this costs the plan

docs/PLAN-PHASE-3.md says the fine-tune-or-mix question is "cheap to settle at nano". That was true and it was settled. It does not follow that the phrasing question is cheap to settle at nano, and this run is the evidence that it is not. Two instruments have now been measured unable to read a phrasing treatment at 20M: harness/searchgrid.py (sixtieth finding, seed-noise-dominated) and search.route_* (this one, floors of 27–60 on a 200-item row). Eight nano runs and eleven scorecards bought a null about the instrument both times.

The next move is therefore a decision about where this gets measured rather than another nano arm. Three options, none costed here:

more seeds the floors widened at four and there is no reason to expect eight to help. The forty-third finding is the precedent and it points the wrong way a bigger row 200 items over 200 subjects; a 1,000-item row would cut the sampling component but not the checkpoint-to- checkpoint component, which is what these floors are measure at 260M where the fifty-second finding was measured in the first place, and where the phrasing effect it found was large. This is the honest place for the question and it is not cheap

Nothing here says the widened pool is worthless. It says this project cannot currently tell, and that the sentences cost a morning to write and sit on disk either way.
