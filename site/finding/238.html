The reply judge · 15 November 2026

He writes worst exactly where he reads worst, message by message

The result, as the finding states it

Over finding 208's 148 one-off messages, a message's mean swap margin on the shipped 260M — how strongly he prefers a hand-written fitting reply over a swapped one — predicts whether his own greedy reply to that message was hand-labelled coherent at AUC 0.713, and at 0.713 counting only pairs inside one of the ten message categories. Message length predicts the label at 0.54 and taking it out of the margin leaves 0.713. Pre-registered row R: linked (0.65 or more). On the plain 5M the same read is 0.544 (0.586 within category): near chance. The share of pairs he gets right predicts nothing at either size (0.544, 0.516) — the 260M gets nearly every pair right, so only the margin's size carries the signal.

What it cost

pre-registered and committed at 21b9000 before either read was computed; started 15:11:55 on 2026-11-15, read 1 on CPU in under 2 s, hand read and length check under 1 min; written 15:15-15:20. No card.

The question (owner, 2026-11-15: "Run both probes", after a brainstorm on how a model can understand words while still generating text). Findings 204 and 208 measured that he recognises the fitting reply far more often than he writes one — 99-100% of swap pairs against 123 of 148 written at 260M. Is that one weak signal read two ways, or two competences that fail in different places? It had never been read message by message. The pre-registration is scripts/probe_read_write.py's docstring, read 1.

The read

A message's read score is the mean margin (finding 204's swap score, a reply's general likelihood cancelled) over every pair it sits in, either side, across and within category pooled: 7 to 18 pairs a message. Its write label is finding 208's blind hand label of that run's own greedy reply. The parse reproduces 208's totals (123 and 70) by assertion.

run writes R AUC Rc within category share-right AUC corn_jr_260m (shipped) 123/148 0.713 0.713 0.544 nano5m_v3_webblend 70/148 0.544 0.586 0.516

Not registered, read after: message length (words) predicts the 260M's label at 0.540 and correlates 0.25 with the read score; the read score with length regressed out still reads 0.713. Prediction was wrong: it said 0.58-0.68, linked more by category than by item; it is linked by item.

The hand read

The eight messages he reads worst, with the fitting reply he was shown and the one he wrote:

138 "There's a spider in my bath and I'm not going in there." F shown: I'd leave it for a while too. wrote: Okay, we'll send someone over right away. Can you tell me where you are? 118 "Maybe." F wrote: It's about 5,000. 61 "Any ideas for a cheap date night?" F wrote: I don't know, what did you have in mind? 147 "My neighbour gave me a huge bag of lemons." P wrote: That's great! How many did you get? 120 "Never mind." F wrote: I don't mind. 116 "Hmm." F wrote: That's interesting. 137 "I accidentally called my teacher mum." P wrote: I'm sorry to hear that. What happened? 127 "Wait, what?" F wrote: I'm sorry, I didn't mean to. I was just trying to get your attention.

Six of eight fail. What they share is that the meaning is not in the words: a spider in the bath is a joke-sized complaint and not an emergency, "Never mind" withdraws a request, "Wait, what?" asks for a repeat. Where the message's words carry its meaning he reads it well and writes well; where the meaning is in what the words imply, both fail together.

What it licenses

Reading and writing are one competence in the 260M, not two. The messages his recognition finds hard are the ones he writes badly on, beyond what the kind of message or its length explains. So the recognise-ahead-of- write gap of findings 204 and 208 is mostly the same weak signal read two ways — recognition with the prior subtracted and the whole reply in view, writing committed at the first word — and a fix that strengthens understanding of a message should move both. At 5M the link is near chance, which fits finding 214's weak recognition there: little signal to share. One checkpoint per size, one hand labeller, 148 messages; the 5M's second seed and exam 2 on the 260M have no swap read and are not in it.
