A checker on the whole-passage reader doesn't beat its own confidence

The result, as the finding states it

The cross-fitted pair-rows verifier on the whole-passage adapter separates right picks from wrong three points better than the arm's own margin on held rows, and on Population A it is that margin's own curve to the passage on the answerable half and forty correct silences worse on the other, so it closes at its willingness control

scripts/train_reader_whole.py --fold {0,1} then --verifier, 2026-09-05, seed 1 only. Two fold arms of finding 112's recipe (three epochs each on half the articles, 41,707 / 41,946 passages), each reading the argmax pair's two rows, six scalars and the label "the argmax is a gold pair" on the half it never saw (83,653 out-of-fold rows, 38.4% / 38.9% positive); an MLP over [LN(h_i); LN(h_j); standardised scalars] selected by AUC on a tenth of the articles; then the full seed-1 arm's Population A argmax judged by it. evals/results/whole_causal_s1_ver.json, reader_whole_causal_s1_ver_popa.json, reader_whole_causal_s1_stack_popa.json; checkpoints/verifier_whole_causal_s1_ver.pt and the fold arms (gitignored); features at F:/corn-jr-data/states/whole_feats_whole_causal_s1_fold{0,1}.pt.

0. Why

Finding 86's pair-rows verifier was worth +10 / +9 / +18 right at the silence floor on the window reader, by halving the same-type rival. Finding 112's arm speaks 132 wrong answers and holds 84 right ones back on seed 1, and its speak-or-decline decision is one null logit in the span softmax. This is the same head, cross-fitted the same way (finding 85: the label is unreadable in fold), on the new arm.

1. The verifier separates, on the rows it was fitted to

held rows 8,418 AUC span-head margin 0.8041 verifier (epoch 3) 0.8327 margin + verifier stack 0.8359

Three points over the margin, and the two-feature stack adds a third of a point over the verifier alone.

2. On Population A it does not, and the willingness control says why

Bar protocol, extent on, each at its own CV'd threshold, seed 1:

right wrong silent u.silent attempt acc|att arm, own margin (112) 505 132 84 198 0.883 0.793 arm, verifier 465 90 166 198 0.770 0.838 arm, margin+verifier stack 496 126 99 198 0.863 0.797

paired vs the margin: verifier right -44 (lost 49, won 5, p 4e-10), silence 0 stack right -9 (lost 14, won 5, p 0.06), silence 0

At matched attempt rate (0.781, both fold thresholds shifted together):

margin @ 0.781 right 462 wrong 101 u.silent 237 verifier right 461 wrong 102 u.silent 198

On the answerable half the verifier is the margin's own precision curve to within a passage. On the unanswerable half it is 39 to 43 correct silences worse at every willingness. Its precision at the bar (0.838) was the threshold protocol spending silences it did not have, and the stack, which lets the margin back in, gives back the silences and reads −9 right.

3. What it says

The window verifier's edge was structural: the window pipeline's null was a separate head on the say marker that had never seen the picked span, and the pair rows told it something new. Here the null already sits in the same softmax as the pair scores on a trunk that was trained for it, so the boundary rows carry nothing the margin does not, and the verifier's three points of held-out AUC are on a label (argmax is gold, unanswerable rows all negative) whose operating point the bar protocol does not read at. Finding 107's shape on the other side of the fine-tune.

Closed at the willingness control on one seed. A second seed would cost 95 minutes to replicate a −40 on silences; not run. The verifier flag stays in the script.

4. What it leaves

The 132 wrong and 84 withheld are still there. What the read rules out is a second head over the same rows; what it does not touch is the null itself (its own head on the say marker, or a class-weighted term, are untried on this arm), the schedule (both seeds' pooled metric was flat across epochs 1 and 2), and CLIPPED at 38 / 34.
