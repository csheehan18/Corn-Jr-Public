6. What this does not claim

Not that the fold is wrong to ship: the depth it buys is measured and the copies it fails to remove were never going to move on a checkpoint that copies at depth 1 with nothing to fold. Not that the reserve is unnecessary: the probe's 61–78% second-round cut is real; it is harmless on these columns at this depth, and the frame row cannot see a sliced declaration that a later round recovers from. Not that social.self's 16 held-out phrasings are the whole residue — they are the sixteen a session wrote, and the web-search share on them (12 of 16 on ft4g) is the number to widen the hatch against, not a bound. Not a seed replicate on anything: one seed, one grid, and the between-arm differences on social_answered are one to two items out of six.
