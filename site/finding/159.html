More ways to phrase each move helps unseen phrasings modestly

The result, as the finding states it

A3b and finding 158's pair: more ways of saying each move holds unseen phrasings at 40 and 38 of 100 at both seeds (A3: 32 and 20) and installs at both, a direction by A3b's table; capping how often any wording is seen buys nothing on far phrasings — level at one seed, unreadable at the other, both arms running on the seed's default label — so finding 158's lever is not shown at nano, no guard is built, and its family contrast fails

What it cost

93.9 min of training six nano runs (15.1-16.5 min each, contended — the card sat at 35% before the chain, so a bound); 7.4 min of scoring two standard cards; probe_move.py 6.4 min, the tag scorings 1 min and the repeat probe 0.5 min over the six; the chain 11:27-13:17 on 2026-10-10 end to end, against a registered ~45 + ~65 min of card.

Two pre-registrations read together because one chain ran them: A3b's (configs/mix_v3_a3b.py, finding 157's experiment B — the move line over 1,021 hand-written phrasings, share, a trained goodbye, a funded consolation), whose first two attempts died on 2026-10-09 with no finished checkpoint; and finding 158's one-variable pair (configs/mix_v3_a3b_cap.py, committed at fd71ddd before a byte trained): A3b's slice cut two ways at the same size move for move, cap with no wording seen more than five times and rep keeping the transcripts whose wordings A3b repeats most — a whole-line table over 0% of the move lines against 38%, read alike by a word counter (53 and 53 of 100 unseen). Every run resumable (--resume-every 500), both seeds (1337, 2337). Readers committed before any result (ead7e93): probe_move.py --read-b, --read-pair, --read-contrast.

Finding 158's family contrast fails

seed consolation + farewell wellbeing + thanks the seed's default, and where it fell 1337 21 / 40 16 / 40: +5 consolation (36 of 120): 23 in the low pool, 5 in the high 2337 17 / 40 15 / 40: +2 fail farewell (23 of 120): 13 low, 1 high

The registered bar was +5 at both seeds. The one pass sits on the confound the registration named: the default label landed in the pool it lifts. Per family, unseen accuracy follows the seed's default, not the lookup mass.

Why, read by hand

On far phrasings every arm runs on a default. The unseen probe was filtered against every trained carrier by exact match, containment and letter-trigram similarity (check_moveline.py section 8), so it measures reach to phrasings unlike anything trained. There, each checkpoint writes one or two labels for most items — cap open 41 and 69 of 120, rep open 44 and share 57 — and what reads as accuracy is which family those defaults happen to match: cap's +8 at seed 2337 is its second default, farewell, written 20 times, scoring 9 of the 20 goodbyes. Side by side at seed 1337 the arms differ item by item ("that adds up", "duly noted": cap open, rep ack; "I'm running on empty", "it's all a bit much": cap consolation, rep share / open) in the direction of each one's defaults.

Near phrasings are a third regime, and the cap bought it at one seed. On the 104 trained phrasings — stock forms, close kin of lines both arms trained on — cap at seed 1337 labels a phrase contained in ten or fewer of its lines right 47 of 67 times (70%), where finding 158's curve puts a wording seen ten or fewer times near the unseen rate. It did not memorise them (no wording past five sightings); it labelled near variants. At seed 2337 the same cell reads 33 of 67 and cap wrote open on 69 of 120 unseen: that checkpoint installed less of everything. So there are three regimes, not two — exact recall past about ten sightings, interpolation to near variants, and reach to far phrasings — and finding 158 read "not memorised" as "learned the idea"; capping repetition moves the second, not the third.

The curve's misses sit at its line. Every miss is a wording seen 11-16 times (rep's most is 25, where A3's top bucket was 61+ at 98-100%), and the two repeated at both seeds are ack phrasings with a thanks word in them ("that's perfect", "that's helpful", 14 sightings each, labelled thanks).

What it licenses

  • For D7 and the move line: A3b's direction — the wider user side holds the unseen five at 38-40 of 100 where A3 read 20-32, installs at both seeds, no routing kill. It is a bundle (variety, share, a trained goodbye, a funded consolation) and the read names the arm. It is still under the word counter on its own slice (53), and on natural openings under the first token it is mixed (44 and 38 of 62 against A3's 48 and 30).
  • For finding 158: capping repetition is not a lever for reach at nano. What survives is a description, not a recipe: a decision whose wording recurs past about ten sightings in a nano pass is labelled from memory, and a slice that removes those recurrences loses nothing measurable here (cap's trained cell ties or beats rep's at one seed, falls at the other; routing holds in both). No guard is built.
  • Where the limit is not: the table (this pair), the word evidence (the word counter reads both arms at 53), and same-sentence rivals for extraction (finding 158's amendment). Where it might be, untested: the model's size (D11's pilot at 260M), and whether a move's idioms ("consider it heard", "running on empty") need meaning a nano pass over this mix does not carry.

What it does not say

That variety is the cause of A3b's lift — the arm is a bundle. Anything at 260M (finding 51: nano flips sign there). That the pair's level reading holds at a second seed — the second was unreadable. That near-variant interpolation is a stable property of a capped slice — one seed showed it. harness/results/nano20m_v3_a3b{,_seed2}__depth.json (the repeat probe) and the standard cards' social rows are on disk and not read here.

Not the opening either (the guess the hand read prompted, checked and dropped): on items whose first word opens five or more lines his label is the opening's move on 22-38% and the word counter's on 25-44%, and where the two disagree he follows the words a little more often at six of eight checkpoints (rep's two the exceptions); his default is the largest single share, 20-73%. Amended 2026-10-11: read from inside — finding 161

Probe C read the far-phrasing limit at his decision point on the same eight checkpoints. A linear reader on his own state reads 46-55 of 100, at the word counter's level and well over an untrained net of his shape, in every arm; what differs between arms is how much of it the written label uses (A3b 32 of 47-48, the others 11-27). So this finding's "size more than meaning" has two layers: a readout that writes a default over a move the state holds (A3, cap, rep), and, at A3b, a state that does not hold the move on about 45 of 100 far phrasings. A3b's lift above is the first layer closing.
