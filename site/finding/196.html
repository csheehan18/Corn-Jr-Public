Understanding at small scale · 21 October 2026

Loop fails because the small model lacks the knowledge, not the format

The result, as the finding states it

On loop's own 436 answer-bearing batches, asked in plain sentences outside any trace, which place best fits the wish is near chance at 20M and grows with size — the place that most raises the wish phrase is the right one 0.45 at loop's 20M start, 0.48 after the balanced fine-tune, 0.55 at 100M and 0.63 at 260M, against a chance of 0.41 — while the trace format neither hides nor adds a judgment the model has (trace pick 0.45 at the 20M start, 0.52 after the balanced fine-tune); so loop's collapse (findings 184, 195) is a fine-tune with no signal learning the base rate, and the missing signal is knowledge of what a description implies, which 20M does not hold for these items

What it cost

20:55-21:13 on 2026-10-21 (the owner: "investigate the failures yourself and get back to me"); four reads of scripts/probe_loop_formats.py, 6.3 and 5.4 min of the four that ran whole, contended (the card at 37-40%, a bound).

The question. Findings 184 and 195: loop's stop reads nothing from the batch — he writes none on every batch when 71% of decisions are none, and stops on every batch when half are — and the place he names is near chance. The owner: "I still don't fully understand why... Is it truly internal reasoning being too small? 20M should be able to do this." Three candidates: the control is too much for 20M; the trace format hides a judgment he has in plain language; the judgment is not in him at all.

  • 287 of 436 batches; the rest overrun a 512-token block, and these checkpoints never trained on the trace format

Not the format. At loop's 20M start the judgment reads the same inside the trace as outside it (0.45 and 0.45). There is nothing in plain language for the format to hide.

Not the control. The stop cannot read a judgment the model does not have: the stop's AUC sits at 0.49-0.55 at every size, and the place-fit judgment it would need is near chance at 20M.

The knowledge, and it grows with size. In the ruler's direction the right place reads 0.45 at 20M, 0.55 at 100M and 0.63 at 260M. The plain pick (scoring whole descriptions, whose neutral tails — "by the market square" — add noise) shows the same order more faintly. The 20M fine-tunes move the trace pick 0.45 -> 0.50-0.52: the fine-tune teaches some of it, not enough to supply a stop.

The yes/no read is unreadable, 0.48-0.51 at every size including 260M: a bare yes/no question is not a frame these models answer by meaning, so it says nothing about absolute judgment either way.

What it licenses

  • The collapse is a fine-tune learning the base rate because the batch gives it no signal. No signal, because what "a play area" says about "good for kids" is knowledge 20M does not hold for these items. It is not the reasoning the stop needs and not the trace format.
  • For the owner's loop call (finding 184): a stop the runtime can check tests loop's control at 20M with the knowledge taken out of the question; the "sounds right" stop is a size question — 100M reads 0.55 and 260M 0.63 on these items before any loop training — to be read at the pilot.
  • Size buys this knowledge faster than the web text did: 20M -> 260M moves the lift read +0.18 on these items, where every 20M web mix moved the wish read by at most +0.08 (findings 185, 189-191).

Size and text are confounded. The checkpoints that read higher also read more: the 20M starts trained on 129-140M tokens, the 100M on 398M and the 260M on 5B (about 4, 4 and 19 tokens a parameter). Some of "grows with size" is "grows with text read".

What it does not settle. Whether a 100M or 260M loop fine-tune installs the soft stop; absolute judgment ("is any of these good enough?"), which no frame here can read; the yes/no frame itself.
