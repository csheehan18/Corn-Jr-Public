Routing and declining · 19 August 2026

Too noisy to read; underneath, wrong contact names fell 19 to 2

The result, as the finding states it

The row cannot read this arm, and underneath it the substitution fell from 19 to 2

What it cost

4.4 min of scoring on the 1 card first cited here (nano20m_v1_seed3). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

configs/mix_v1_toolnames.py ×2 against mix_v1 ×2. One file swapped in a 0.62% slice: tools.txt → tools_names.txt, same weight to the token, contact book 28 hand-written names → 4,000 composed ones at 1.81 uses each.

The row is a null, and the reason is the baseline rather than the arm

mix_v1 s1mix_v1 s2toolnames s1toolnames s2
tool.name_fidelity /12064335339

Baseline [33, 64], arm [39, 53]. Fully overlapping, and the arm's range sits inside the baseline's.

The baseline's own two-seed spread is 31 of 120. That is wider than any effect this arm could plausibly have had, so the row cannot read it — this is the thirtieth finding's rule arriving on a new row: a floor is a property of the arm, not of the row, and nobody had measured one here. The plan quoted "56/120 wrong at 29.6M" from a single seed. The second seed reads 87/120 wrong. That number should not have been quoted without an interval and this finding is the correction.

The controls are no better behaved: tool.copy reads [100, 100] baseline against [77, 100] arm, and tool.copy_wide [276, 279] against [252, 289]. One arm seed dips on each, different seeds each time. Nothing here supports the pre-registered "bought fidelity with copy discipline" trade, and nothing here rules it out either.

Underneath the row, the mechanism moved and is measurable

score_name_fidelity's failure buckets are defined against corn/tools.py's CONTACTS, which is the training book for mix_v1 and not the training book for this arm. So a substitution out of the composed book lands in wrong_name and the buckets are not comparable across the two corpora. Re-running the 120 items and classifying each emitted contact against both books directly:

19 → 2 and 10. That is the failure the eval exists for, and it is the dangerous one: the runtime texted Omar Silva and reported success. Fluent, schema-valid, wrong person. tool.selection and tool.validity both pass it.

What replaced it is neither book, 27 → 54 and 53 — strings that are nobody. Those fail visibly, and CopySpec blocks them at inference. So the arm changed which way he is wrong without changing how often, and the direction of that change is the operationally useful one.

The honest position

  • The pre-registered question reads null and is reported as null. The arm did not move tool.name_fidelity.
  • The mechanism it was aimed at moved by an order of magnitude, on a row the pass rate cannot see, in a bucket the frozen scorer cannot separate for this corpus.
  • Both statements are true at once, and the second does not rescue the first.

This is the same shape as temporal.outcome: a row that sums outcomes with opposite operational meanings reports a wash, and the split is where the result is. The difference is that temporal.outcome is now a first-class row and this is a script run by hand.

What this does not settle

  • Whether it is a cardinality effect at all. The plan pre-registered an unmoved row as "the fourth test of the fourteenth finding's rule and the first to fail it". That reading is not available, because the substitution count did move — what failed is the row, not the rule.
  • ~~Two seeds against a baseline whose spread is 31. More baseline seeds are the only thing that makes tool.name_fidelity quotable at all, and they cost 17 minutes each. Nothing on this row should be quoted until they exist.~~ Done, and they cost nothing — the forty-third finding. nano20m_v1_seed3 and _seed4 were already trained and had simply never been scored on the frozen set with the three --add files present. At four seeds the baseline reads 33, 37, 44, 64, spread still 31, and the arm's [39, 53] is still strictly inside it. This finding's conclusion is unchanged and is now measured rather than inferred from two points.
  • The scorer's buckets are corpus-relative and silently so. substituted_nearest and substituted_other mean "a name from CONTACTS", which stops being the training book the moment an arm changes it. A row whose failure labels depend on a table the arm is allowed to move is a row that will mislead again. Fixing it means a new bucket rather than a redefinition, on the --add terms, and it is not done here.
  • The neither book strings were not inspected. Whether they are near-misses of the asked-for name, garbled composed names, or something else is a question this data can answer and nobody has asked.
