The tag language · 13 October 2026

He can't learn which limit a person will relax

The result, as the finding states it

G6's recover rung does not install at one pass: when nothing fits he relaxes a limit by a fixed ranking of the field names, a different ranking at each seed, and the limit the person said they would bend on never reaches the choice — paired 3 and 0 of 288 held-out, 0 and 0 of 144 trained, and his state at the pick carries the clause no better than an untrained net's

What it cost

41.5 min of training two nano runs (1,244 s and 1,249 s, contended — the card at 36-41%, a bound; registered ~40 min), 52 s of probing, four tool_copy_wide cards in 34 s, the three control reads 117 s and the three hand reads 52 s on the card; the build — generator, guard, probe, rows, configs — about 15 min of CPU (12:47-13:02 on 2026-10-13), the read 13:45-13:55; registered ~50 min of card, actual ~46.

ROADMAP G6, PLAN-TAG-LANGUAGE §6's ladder, its second rung ("recover when nothing fits"), in the form SPEC-LANGUAGE §6 fixed (D-A1.8): when check: marks every listed place unmet, the runtime writes options: relax[a] relax[b] ask and his pick: is one move. Pre-registered in configs/mix_v3_funcworld_recover.py's docstring and committed at a092e65 before a byte trained. One variable from arm E2: 8,100 recover traces (corn/recovergen.py) at combine's dose to the token, 2.21% of the mix, 5,843 iterations; seeds 1337 and 2337. On two traces in three the request names one limit as flexible by meaning ("though the budget can stretch a bit"), with nouns that share no stem with price, km or rating (scripts/check_recover.py, which caught "budget" against a firm clause's "budge" before anything trained); the gold is the first-named limit on 50.0% of relax traces and the nearest-miss limit on 51.1%. Cards: evals/results/nano20m_v3_fw_recover{,_seed2}__recover.json and __copywide.json; the controls' nano20m_v3_fw_filter_ev2{,_seed2,_seed3}__recover.json and nano20m_v3_fw_filter_ev2{,_seed2}__copywide.json.

Read by hand (EXPERIMENTS §5.6) — what the pick follows

A scratch read of every row on both seeds (probe only — not on disk).

A fixed ranking of the field names, different at each seed. Seed 1337 relaxes rating over km over price, almost without exception:

pair seed 1337 seed 2337 km / price relax[km] 148, relax[price] 6 relax[km] 84, relax[price] 60, ask 10 km / rating relax[rating] 133, relax[km] 3 relax[km] 65, relax[rating] 64, ask 7 price/rating relax[rating] 142 relax[price] 58, ask 71, relax[rating] 13

right near half on the relax rows is that ranking landing on the gold half the time, and the twin — the clause moved to the other limit, every other byte the same — moves the pick on 0-6 of 288 rows.

The person's words do not move the choice at all. Where the clause names neither limit (the ask rows) and where it names one, the margin of ask against his best relax is the same: +0.55 against +0.51 nats at seed 1337, +0.20 against +0.19 at 2337. The gold relax is the likelier of the two on 221 and 230 of 432 relax rows. List length is not it either (seed 1337: 81 of 151 right at three places, 70 of 142 at four, 70 of 139 at five).

Inside him, at the token where he names the field (after pick: relax[), a linear reader in finding 161's protocol — fit on the trained rows and their twins, the layer chosen on a fifth of them, read on the held-out rows and twins (576):

label seed 1337 seed 2337 arm E2 (control) random-init floor which limit (first / second) 0.509 0.503 0.500 0.486 which field (price/km/rating) 0.627 0.670 0.653 0.733

The relative label — the one the choice needs, the noun's field placed against the goal's order — is at chance in every net. The absolute field is readable well above chance (0.33), but a randomly initialised net of his shape reads it best: that is the noun's identity mixed forward, which any net carries and which a reader can map to a field because every noun appears in its fit. His trained state carries it no better than an untrained one. So the words reach the pick as words, at the floor's level, and training formed no mapping from "the budget" to price and no use of one: the evidence never reaches the decision, finding 161's first reading, not its second.

Amended 2026-10-13: the raw rows, and his own training traces

The raw rows (the probe's printed rows, read after the numbers — the order CLAUDE.md says to avoid): on every printed row all three menu lines sit at 0.6-1.7 nats, near a blind three-way guess (1.10), and the lowest is the seed's favourite field whatever the clause says — "I'm easy about the budget" and "I'm easy about the trip there" both put relax[km] lowest at seed 1337 (0.78, 0.63). He learned the menu's format and its one-in-three prior, and almost nothing that depends on the request.

His own training traces (the owner asked for this before any further arm; probe only — not on disk): the same full-option scoring on 300 relax and 150 ask traces taken from the part of the payload file that trained, verbatim:

seed relax right gold's NLL among the three ask right 1337 147 of 300 1.01 nats (blind 1.10) 0 of 150 2337 126 of 300 1.50 nats 51 of 150

Chance on what he trained on. Each trace trained once, so no trace was expected to be memorised; but each noun x frame pairing trained about 200 times and each noun about 600, and the choice depends on none of them. This is not a transfer failure — the request-dependent choice never formed, even in distribution. What is still open is why: too little gradient on a decision of three tokens in a ~400-token trace (combine read the same at four times the dose, which argues against dose alone), or the two hops — the clause's noun to a field, the field to a menu move — across the observation between them. An arm that names the field outright in the request ("I'm flexible on price") splits the two.

What it licenses

The table's row: recover at nano is not his at one pass; the dose is the next variable. The same shape as combine (finding 156): a request-dependent choice over a small menu of values he sees on every trace forms a fixed ranking of the values instead, different at each seed, and combine read the same at four times the dose. So the cheap next arm is recover at four times the dose; the decision it would feed is D7's row for recover. The alternative this finding makes available without training: the flex reading is the runtime's — the runtime holds a table of flex phrasings (as combine became the runtime's, by lookup) and either picks the move itself or shows him the menu already narrowed; what stays his is nothing on this rung.

What it does not settle. Nano, one pass, one world (two limits, three fields). Whether a menu read at a later layer, a shorter distance between the clause and the pick, or a format where the flexibility is written on the goal: line (a term he writes while the request is in view, e.g. soft[...]-style) would install — none was tested. The 96 ask rows read 0 and 18: he did not learn to ask when the person bent on nothing, either.
