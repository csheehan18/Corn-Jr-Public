Balanced keep-looking odds flip loop from never stopping to always stopping

The result, as the finding states it

Finding 184's loop fine-tune on a payload balanced so pick: none is the right line on half the batches at every batch position, with the answer's slot, the wish, the wording and the places' concepts spread so no wish-blind reader beats 0.59, lands on the pre-registered row "the constant flips": at all three starts he now stops on every batch — none on 0.004, 0.010 and 0.006 of the balanced held batches (gate 0.35-0.65; finding 184's fine-tunes 1.000, 0.999, 1.000) — and stop-or-not reads 0.499, 0.501, 0.500 on the balanced train batches (gate 0.60; always none 0.500), so every gate fails at three of three (installs 27, 30, 24 of 240; gives up 0 of 48; reads it 73, 81, 55 of 148; by meaning 14, 12, 12 of 120); inside, his stop lean is the same number on batches that hold the answer and batches that do not (+0.22 to +0.24 nats, AUC 0.48-0.51 on the balanced rows), where finding 184's sat at -1.48 on both — the decision is the payload's base rate and nothing else, and greedy decoding turns a 56% coin into a constant; no new slot or batch constant appears, and the place he names on an answer batch is right 0.58, 0.67, 0.53 of the time on trained pairings and 0.52, 0.56, 0.48 on held ones (chance about 0.42)

What it cost

the CPU build and reads 16:51-17:43 on 2026-10-21 (the generator, the guard, the fit of the batch draws 17 s, the mix 12 s); the zero-training control 785.8 s (17:29-17:42); pre-registered at aaf7536 (17:43:35); one chain under the card's lock 17:43-18:04, 1,224.5 s — three fine-tunes of 159, 161, 161 s, on each a split read of finding 184's rows 80 s and of the balanced rows 159-160 s, a copy row 8 s — registered ~16 min, actual 20.4 min; the inside read on nine checkpoints and both row sets 342.5 s (18:04-18:10); all contended (the card at 36-39% before every job, a bound).

The owner's instruction, 2026-10-21: balance the payload properly — "make sure the picks are spread properly too so he doesn't pick the first one consistently or second. All should be spread properly so he doesn't learn a shortcut." Finding 184's payload put the answer on batch 1, 2, 3, 4 or nowhere a fifth each, which balances traces and not decisions: pick: none was right on 14,858 of 20,795 batch decisions (71%), and findings 184 and 194 collapsed to it at every start. Pre-registered at aaf7536 (configs/mix_v3_loop_ft_bal.py), after the control and before a byte trained.

The balanced payload, and a route found on the way

corn/loopgen.py BalancedSchedule (its docstring is the design), through the same build, lexicon and holdout: a geometric stop at p = 1/2 — of every 16 traces a pairing gets, the match on batch 1 in 8, batch 2 in 4, batch 3 in 2, batch 4 in 1, nowhere in 1 — so stop and keep looking are 0.500 each at batches 1, 2, 3 and 4 (12,288 traces, 23,040 decisions, batch 4 decided 1,536 times); the answer's slot cycled per size and stop batch (0.500/0.500 and 0.333/0.333/0.333); every wish the answer exactly as often as a keep-looking; no key of the wording above 0.507. Finding 184's file regenerates byte for byte.

The places needed more than a shuffle. No distractor may be of a concept that clashes with the wish (no near answer), so a stop batch holds the wish and none of its clash partners and a keep-looking batch holds neither. Drawn evenly, as finding 184's were, the batch's set of place concepts, the wish unread, settles stop-or-not at 0.66 with two places and 0.72 with three, and given a stop names the answer at 0.56 and 0.58 (exact, chance 0.50 and 0.33). A first fix — weighting the draw so each concept is the answer on a fifth of its showings — made the set route worse (0.76). With eight concepts and five clashes the routes cannot all reach chance: at three places the set route and the slot route trade along a front (scripts/fit_loop_draws.py --grid). The payload's places come from a fitted per-(size, outcome, wish) draw (corn/loopgen_draws.json) that takes them to 0.54 / 0.59 and 0.53 / 0.47 on the bytes, each wish's distractor concepts alike in stop and keep-looking batches.

Read before the GPU (scripts/check_loop.py §7-8, a reader per route and six mutants, each failing its check): every content-free policy 0.500 on stop-or-not; the places' concept set 0.561 (payload), 0.565 and 0.568 out of sample on the train and held rows; novelty (places new to the trace) 0.550, 0.563, 0.568; a bag of the places' words 0.529 and 0.531; a bag of words with the wish 0.992 on trained pairings — the lexicon as a table, a route by design — and 0.565 on the held ones. Twenty traces read by hand: each gold the only right answer, nothing in the wording, the batch or the position that a table could answer from.

Every gate fails at three of three, the other way round from finding 184. What he writes is a stop on every batch, the place chosen by the soft read.

The cause, named. The stop lean is one number per checkpoint, the same on batches that hold the answer and batches that do not (AUC 0.48-0.51 on the balanced train and held cells at every fine-tune, both arms). On finding 184's payload it settled at -1.48 nats — the stop at about 0.19 — and here at +0.23, the stop at about 0.56: in each case the payload's base rate, with a small lean, and nothing read from the batch. Greedy decoding then takes the larger side every time, so a calibrated coin prints as a constant, and which constant follows the base rate: 71% none gave always none, 50% gave always stop. Balancing the share moved the coin; it could not give the coin anything to read, because the input that should move it — the soft read of the places against the wish — does not reach the stop. Why the balanced lean sits on the stop side rather than at zero is not named (a candidate, untested: the replay's other pick: lines, every one followed by a place).

The soft read under it, unchanged where it counts. The place ranked first on answer batches moves little: on finding 184's rows 102, 116, 91 of 192 against 184's 94, 116, 90; on the balanced train rows 420, 485, 381 of 720 against 372, 477, 366; on the held rows 185, 200, 171 of 360 against 171, 204, 168 — level (chance about 81 of 192, 300 of 720, 150 of 360). It is a little better than the starts' (the format) and no better than finding 184's at the start that read the wish (2337).

Read by hand before it was quoted

Fourteen balanced rows on the 2337 fine-tune, eight held and six trained, every batch's free line against its gold. Every keep-looking batch gets a stop, the place's description copied exactly with its filler cut, so the line is well formed and the decision is the only thing wrong. On answer batches he names the gold on 7 of 14; the misses read as meaning misses — "for the little ones" -> "a hotel with blankets on every chair" over "a hostel with a puppet show on sundays", "where we can talk" -> "a boarding house with a play corner and toys" over "a serene lodge", "outdoors" -> "a pub with high chairs" over "a bar with a beer garden". On none batches the place he stops on is not the most wish-like one ("outdoors" -> "a candlelit cocktail bar"). The instrument agrees with the text: the split read, his free line and the inside read say the same thing three ways. The full-line read (finding 184's registered one, a side column) disagrees — installs 57, 67, 51 — because its length bias favours the two-token none: it picked none on 177, 157 and 153 of 436 answer batches where his free line wrote the gold, the hole finding 184 named.

What it licenses

  • Finding 184's collapse was the base rate, and so is this one. The owner's balancing did what it was built to: no none majority, no batch, slot, wish, wording or place-set route above 0.59, and he took none of them. With every cheap route shut, the decision fell to the one thing left — the share — because the meaning hop under it is not there to read. GENERATORS §2's line holds a third time: a low lookup route is necessary and not sufficient.
  • A balanced decision has to be read by its margin, not its argmax. Where the input does not move the decision, greedy decoding prints the base rate as a constant of either sign; the inside read (d, AUC) is the read that tells "reads nothing" from "reads and leans". For loop's decision every read so far, two payloads and two web sources, says it reads nothing.
  • For the owner's loop call — a checkable stop, or the soft read itself as the work. This takes away the reading that the payload's share was hiding a decision: no balancing of the soft-stop format forms one at this size. A stop the runtime can check would split the iteration format from the soft read, as branch did; the soft read as the work is the meaning hop findings 148, 156, 167-168, 184 and 194 stopped at, and this adds no evidence it forms from the loop format at 20M. The call stays the owner's.
  • For any future generator on filtergen's lexicon: the clash rule makes the batch's set of concepts a wish-blind route (0.66-0.72 drawn evenly). Finding 184's payload, filtergen's own lists and anything built on them carry it; check_loop.py §7's set and slot readers are the measure.

What it does not settle. Why the balanced lean is on the stop side; whether the soft read forms at 260M or with real descriptions; whether loop's control installs on a checkable stop; the second start's copy species.
