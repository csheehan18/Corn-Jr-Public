Fine-tuning installs extraction at 260M from zero

The result, as the finding states it

Extraction installs by fine-tune at 260M, and the sixth finding's boundary does not hold here

What it cost

12.5 min of scoring on the 1 card first cited here (corn_jr_260m_ftnarrow); it also reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

WS-G, run 2026-08-26. One fine-tune from the frozen corn_jr_260m, 786 iterations, ~15 minutes. configs/mix_wsg_ft_extract.py carries the pre-registration. It lands on outcome 1, which the config registered as the least likely of its three.

The question, and why a null was the expected answer

corn_jr_260m had never seen an extraction transcript: mix_v3_5b carries eight sources and extract is not one of them. So unlike P3-B2, which surfaced a routing capability the base already had at ~35 passes, this asked a fine-tune to install a capability that was not there — the sixth finding's failure mode, registered as the expected outcome rather than the feared one.

The argument for trying anyway was that the half finding 6 lost is already at ceiling here: tool.copy 120/120, tool.copy_wide 300/300, temporal.copy 353/353 on the base. Reading an answer out of an observation rather than out of the weights is fully installed; what extraction adds is a longer observation and a span-selection step that nothing in the corpus teaches.

The control is corn_jr_260m_ftnarrow — P3-B2's narrow arm, trained from this same base on this same tool payload with no extraction in it, already scored. It cost no card time and it is what separates "extraction data did this" from "any fine-tune does this".

It installs, and the control shows most of it is the extraction data

row base ftnarrow ftEXTRACT attributable extract.span 9/200 22/200 70/200 +48 extract.abstain 0/200 1/200 141/200 +140 extract.brevity 133/400 243/400 397/400 +154

The control moves the rows a little — a tool fine-tune alone takes span 9 to 22 and brevity 133 to 243 — and the extraction arm goes far past it on all three. extract.abstain is the sharpest: 0 and 1 against 141. The base and the control essentially never produce a miss sentence; the arm produces one on seven of ten unanswerable windows.

Nothing was spent. Rule 10's watch is clean at the ceiling, which is the strongest form this project can run it:

tool.copy 120/120 unchanged temporal.copy 353/353 unchanged tool.copy_wide 300/300 unchanged temporal.route 400/400 unchanged

The sixth finding lost temporal.copy to a fine-tune under 1:1 replay, down to 48.2–66.3%. This corpus replays the whole v3 mix on mix_p3a_ft.py's terms and temporal.copy does not move by one item. So finding 6's boundary is not where it was drawn. Installing deference by fine-tune failed in a TinyStories-based model that had never seen a <|call|>; installing extraction over deference that is already at ceiling does not.

And the routing gains survive on half the tool exposure: search.route_* reads 200/200, 200/200, 200/200 against the control's 200/199/200, with tool.selection identical at 187 and tool.validity 179 against 181.

The fifty-seventh finding's caveat reproduces, and is milder

WS-B's nano arm bought extract.span by declining three fifths of the windows that did contain the answer. That happens here too and must be read with the row rather than after it:

ftEXTRACT span failures abstained 80 wrong_span 37 invented 11 ftnarrow span failures invented 63 wrote_own 43 continued_payload 35

Confabulation collapses — invented 63 → 11, which is WS-B's result again (105 → 7). And the arm declines on 80 of 200 answerable windows, 40%, against the nano arm's ~59%. Better, and still the dominant failure mode.

It is not the degenerate cheat, and this is the number that says so. He attempts an answer on 120 of 200 answerable windows (60%) and on 59 of 200 unanswerable ones (29.5%) — twice as likely to try when the answer is actually there. WS-B measured 70–79% against 59–60% on the same comparison, so the discrimination here is wider, not narrower.

Do not pool extract.span and extract.abstain. Pooled they read 211/400 against the base's 9/400 and look like a rout. The fifty-seventh finding's instruction stands and this run does not weaken it.

What this cannot say

One seed. Nobody has measured seed variance at 260M — the sixty-first finding's whole subject. The effects here are large (+140 on abstain, +154 on brevity) and not plausibly noise; anything small in this table is not readable.

The payload is half tools and half extraction, so the arm saw half the tool exposure the control did. That is a confound for the routing rows and not for the extraction rows, which the control has no extraction data to move at all.

It says nothing about a pretraining mix. This is a fine-tune result, and the sixtieth finding measured that vehicle delivering 61% of a mix's effect on a different payload.

And the 40% decline on answerable windows is unexplained here as it was there. Findings 58 and 59 retired four hypotheses for it — class balance, gold position, corpus type frequency, the scorer — without finding a cause. The other answer-type decline sat at 86–92% across six checkpoints and nothing in this run addresses it.

What follows

The chain has four working links for the first time. Recognition was 53/200 on real subjects and is 198/200 (sixty-third finding); retrieval was never broken; reduction was throwing away a third of what it retrieved and now keeps it (_best_answer, same day); and extraction was 9/200 and is 70/200 with abstention at 141/200.

Two things follow immediately, neither costing a training run:

  • --extract now has a model behind it. The flag, PASSAGE and read_passage were all built against a capability that did not exist. It does now, and a live read is the obvious next measurement.
  • The 40% decline is the binding constraint on this link and is the thing worth an instrument rather than an arm — WS-F's territory, parked in PLAN-WEB-SEARCH.md on the argument that link one was failing. That argument has expired twice over.
