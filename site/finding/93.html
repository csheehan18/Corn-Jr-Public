A perfect sentence finder would be worth only 15-38 passages

The result, as the finding states it

A perfect locator is worth fifteen to thirty-eight passages because two thirds of what it could reach is already in the right sentence, and a perfect choice among the head's own ten caps the whole family at fifty-eight per cent

scripts/probe_frame_ceiling.py, 2026-09-03. Replay only — no GPU, no decode, no weight read, no tokenizer — over nullpr_s{1,2}_popa.json and pairs_built_s{1,2}_popa.json, whose spans are verified aligned on all 1,003 rows rather than assumed. Baselines 367 right / joint 600 and 368 / 575.

The frame proposal, 2026-09-03: let him sketch the answer's shape before he looks for it — "the theme from Russia With Love was sung by ___" — and use the sketch to find the matching text, near "sung by" rather than near some other name in the paragraph.

This finding does not test that idea. It bounds it, which is cheaper and comes first. Whatever a frame is for, its job as a locator is to say which part of the window to read. So hand the head the gold's own sentence for free and see what it then gets right: no locator can beat that, and the number is what the proposal should be read against. The three arms that died earlier today all had a one-line version of this read that would have preceded them.

1. Two thirds of the addressable residue is already in the right sentence

Over admitted windows that carry the gold — and only those; see the correction below:

seed 1 (127 windows) seed 2 (157 windows) SAME_SENTENCE 82 64.6% 110 70.1% OTHER_SENTENCE 40 31.5% 42 26.8% SPLIT_LOST 5 3.9% 5 3.2%

by species, seed 1 / seed 2 SAME OTHER SPLIT RIVAL 21 / 32 28 / 30 0 / 0 AGGREGATION 33 / 45 4 / 3 5 / 5 CLIPPED 24 / 30 1 / 0 0 / 0 UNGROUNDED 4 / 3 7 / 9 0 / 0

SAME_SENTENCE is the dominant case and a locator cannot touch it. He is already reading the sentence that contains the answer and picking the wrong span out of it — the composer standing next to the singer in the same clause, where pointing at "sung by" does not separate two names that both sit beside it.

(The frame proposal's own worked example is not of this shape and is not of OTHER_SENTENCE's either — §5 traces it and finds a third thing again. That is the finding's most useful passage and it is why §5 exists.)

OTHER_SENTENCE — the whole addressable set — is 31.5% and 26.8%, and it is almost entirely RIVAL. SPLIT_LOST is negligible, so the sentence splitter is not distorting this.

A correction, because the first version of this read said 55%. It counted every admitted window and labelled one with no gold in it GOLD_ABSENT, which pooled "the sentence split lost it" with "the answer was never in this window". The tell was that all of REFUSED_WRONG and REFUSED_RIGHT landed in that bucket — species defined by having no admitted gold-bearing window. Restricted properly, the splitter accounts for 4% and 3%, not 55%.

2. The gold is not among the head's ten on seventy per cent of the residue

gold reachable in the head's top ten, on some admitted window seed 1 95 of 354 residue passages 26.8% seed 2 111 of 353 31.4%

A locator can only promote a candidate the head already produced. On roughly seventy per cent of his failures the correct answer is not among the ten things he considered, and no amount of better choosing reaches those.

3. The ceilings, and they are monotone in how tightly you can point

Each arm replaces an admitted window's span with the head's own best candidate inside a chosen region, then hands the result to an untouched reduce and score_extract_tight. Every oracle consults the gold and is therefore a ceiling and never an arm.

arm right s1 / s2 delta joint delta baseline 367 / 368 — — ORACLE (sentence) 382 / 386 +15 / +18 +15 / +18 ORACLE_RAD r = 20 371 / 375 +4 / +7 +4 / +7 ORACLE_RAD r = 12 382 / 385 +15 / +17 +15 / +17 ORACLE_RAD r = 8 384 / 390 +17 / +22 +17 / +22 ORACLE_RAD r = 5 389 / 398 +22 / +30 +22 / +30 ORACLE_RAD r = 3 393 / 403 +26 / +35 +26 / +35 ORACLE_RAD r = 2 398 / 406 +31 / +38 +31 / +38 ORACLE_PICK 419 / 425 +52 / +57 +52 / +57 Q_OVERLAP 275 / 280 -92 / -88 -92 / -84

Every oracle is 0 lost / all won — they cannot lose, by construction — and ORACLE reads p = 6.1e-05 and 7.6e-06.

A sentence-accurate locator is worth 15 and 18 passages, about two points of the answerable half. Pointing to within ±2 content words of the gold — a precision no frame will reach, since it means naming the answer's neighbourhood to the word — is worth 31 and 38.

Q_OVERLAP is the floor and it is a catastrophe: −92 and −88, 100 lost against 8 won. Choosing the sentence with the most question words in it is the cheapest locator anyone would write, and it is far worse than not locating at all. That is the ninetieth finding's result in a second form — the gold and the rival sit in the same neighbourhood, and question overlap picks the wrong one systematically.

4. The number that bounds the whole family

ORACLE_PICK takes the gold whenever it is anywhere in the head's ten candidates. That is a perfect locator and a perfect re-ranker and a perfect verifier at once. It reads 419 and 425 of 721 — 58.1% and 59.0%.

Nothing built on this candidate list can beat that: not a frame, not a re-ranker (the eighty-sixth finding already retired one), not a verifier, not a locality rule (the ninetieth). Against the owner's 85% — 613 of 721 — the entire "choose better among what he produced" family is capped twenty-six points short.

And it puts the frame idea in proportion: as a locator it captures 29% and 32% of that ceiling at sentence granularity, 60% and 67% at ±2 words. As a verifier — my own reframe of the proposal, that the sketch is better used to check a candidate than to find one — the ceiling is the full +52 / +57, which is three times the sentence-locator's and is the stronger reading of the idea.

5. The proposal's own example says the failure is neither of those

"who sang the theme song from russia with love", gold Matt Monro, spoken Monty Norman. Traced through the harvest:

w0 no gold spoken 'Monty Norman' margin -1.23 <- admitted, spoke w1 no gold spoken 'David Arnold' margin -3.80 w2 GOLD spoken 'David Arnold' margin -3.81 <- ranked last

and window 2's own sentence is "The theme song was composed by Lionel Bart of Oliver! fame and sung by Matt Monro."

The frame the proposal predicts is literally the text of the window the verifier ranked below two that contain no answer at all. The mechanism is vindicated and the deployment is not: the failure here is neither localisation nor within-window selection. The wrong window won.

And it is not one anecdote. How often a window with no gold outranks every gold-bearing one, over the whole answerable half rather than the residue, because measuring it on failures alone conditions on the outcome:

seed 1 seed 2 all 148 of 580 25.5% 129 of 580 22.2% he got it right 8 of 287 2.8% 8 of 285 2.8% residue 140 of 293 47.8% 121 of 295 41.0%

2.8% where he succeeds against 47.8% and 41.0% where he fails, with the right-half rate identical on both seeds. That is the cleanest failure signature measured in this workstream, and it is a cross-window ranking failure, which no species in probe_reader_species.py names.

6. Repairing that is outside every ceiling above

ORACLE_WINDOW forces the highest-margin gold-bearing window into the mask and changes nothing else:

seed 1 367 -> 460 right, joint 600 -> 693 +93 p = 2.0e-28 seed 2 368 -> 442 right, joint 575 -> 649 +74 p = 1.1e-22

460 and 442 of 721 — 63.8% and 61.3% — against ORACLE_PICK's 58.1% and 59.0%. It is not bounded by §4 because §4 conditions on the candidates of admitted windows and cannot see a window that never cleared the threshold.

The caveat is structural and it is large. An unanswerable passage has no gold-bearing window, so the force fires only on the answerable half: the attempt rate goes to 96.1% and 95.7% while correct silence is unchanged by construction, not by merit. A real window scorer has no such licence and would lift the attempt rate on both halves, paying in the unanswerable column the way every threshold move in the ninety-second finding did. So +93 is not comparable to ORACLE_PICK's +52 as stated; what is comparable is the signature in §5, which is unconditional and replicated.

What this cannot say

It is a ceiling, not a forecast. Every oracle here is handed the answer. A real frame will reach some fraction of ORACLE, and the ninetieth finding's locality rule reached a negative fraction of a similar bound.

It bounds locators over this candidate list only. A frame that changed what the head proposes — used as a decoding constraint rather than a post-hoc filter — is not bounded by §4, because §4 conditions on the ten candidates. That is a real gap and it is the version of the idea worth protecting: §2 says the gold is missing from the ten on seventy per cent of failures, so a mechanism that changes the ten is aiming at the part §4 cannot see.

Sentence granularity is one choice among many and the radius sweep is why it is not the only one reported. A frame might imply a region finer than a sentence and coarser than two words, and the table gives its ceiling directly.

Two seeds, one trunk, one population, Population A at the CV'd threshold under hybrid, with the extent rule off.

What follows

The proposal survives, at a stated size, and the reframe is the stronger half. As a locator it is worth at most 15–18 passages at the granularity a sentence frame implies, against a residue of 354. As a verifier it is worth at most 52–57. If it is built, build it to check.

Test its own first question against §1 before building either. The proposal asks whether he can produce a useful outline at all. The prior question is whether a useful outline would help on SAME_SENTENCE, which is two thirds of the reachable residue — and the answer looks like no, because both candidates sit beside the same anchor. OTHER_SENTENCE is 40 and 42 windows. That is the honest size of the target.

And §4 is the number this workstream has been missing. Four findings went into the threshold, three arms died today, and all of them live inside a family whose ceiling is 58–59%. The remaining twenty-six points to 85% require the gold to enter the candidate list at all, which is the head and the trunk — PLAN-EXTRACTION-READER.md §7 — and no post-hoc selection reaches them.

What follows from §5 and §6, and it is the session's strongest lead. The frame's best use is neither locating within a window nor re-ranking a window's candidates: it is scoring which window to admit. That is the 70% of the residue §2 says is unreachable by candidate selection, it is where the proposal's own example fails, and the verifier that would do it is the same component the ninety-second finding showed cannot be improved by passage-shape features — but a frame is a semantic feature, not a shape one, and nothing measured yet speaks to it. The next test is whether frame-match separates a gold-bearing window from a non-gold-bearing one, which is read (e)'s population and needs no decode if the frame is hand-written first.

Left unexplained: ORACLE_RAD is monotone in the radius across six values and both seeds, and at r = 12 it exactly equals the sentence oracle on seed 1 (+15) and nearly on seed 2 (+17 against +18). Whether a sentence just is a twelve-content-word neighbourhood in this corpus, or the agreement is coincidence, is unmeasured and would say whether "sentence" is the right unit for a frame at all.
