The last unmeasured data share costs nothing; web gains are linear

The result, as the finding states it

The last unpriced share costs nothing, and the web response is linear over two doses

configs/mix_v1_temp5.py ×2 against mix_v1 ×4. One pair of slices moves: temporal 12,821,624 → 6,546,719 tokens (9.79% → §4's declared 5.00%), web taking all 4.79 points (40.00% → 44.79%). Every other source is mix_v1 verbatim, total held at 130,934,381, no repeat anywhere.

This is the sixth of §4's six shares. It was written, pre-registered and committed on 2026-08-18 and then not run; it trained on 2026-08-19.

temporal.route is the row that could have failed and the arm's real question: it reads 399/400 on mix_v1 against a floor of 3, so it had one item of headroom up and 396 down. It did not move. temporal.copy holds at 353/353, which is what §11's cardinality-over-exposure rule predicted from a count taken before the run — 1,531 of 1,555 distinct date values survive the cut while exposure halves from 117.3 uses each to 60.8.

The one non-overlap is schema.conform by one item, on a row whose measured floor is 8. effect.py reads it churn unmeasured and that is the right reading: it is not a result.

Six of six. mix_v2 is now a build rather than a research question. And the result is the same shape the other five had: §4's declared share was right and the inherited one was generous. Every share this project has priced has been too big, and none has ever been too small.

The web response is linear, and this is the first reading of that

Both mix_v1_narr10 and this arm give their freed budget to web, at different sizes, so together they are two doses on one receiver — which the config pre-registered as "the first linearity reading this project can take".

corpusweb sharelm web
mix_v1 ×440.00%0.9020, 0.9023, 0.9024, 0.9034
temp5 ×244.79%0.8920, 0.8930
narr10 ×249.58%0.8844, 0.8844

Full dose: −0.01809 over 9.58 points, −0.001888 per point. Half dose: −0.01001 over 4.79 points, −0.002090 per point.

The linear prediction for this arm was 0.8935 and it read 0.8925 — an error of −0.00097 against a floor of 0.0038. The response is linear to a quarter of the resolution this design can measure, slightly superlinear at the smaller dose in a direction the noise cannot separate.

That is worth having because it is what licenses the arithmetic mix_v2 needs: web's share can be set by interpolation rather than by another arm.

lm temporal reads 0.0966 and 0.0981 against a baseline of 0.0939-0.0973 — the pre-registered degradation, in the right direction and not separated, one seed inside and one just outside. The config permitted a degradation here on the grounds that the row reads ablations even though it is saturated as a capacity instrument; what it got is a move too small to claim.

The prediction was quoted as a result before it was one

mix_v1_daylog's docstring pre-registers its own temporal.copy on the grounds that "mix_v1_temp5 halved this slice's read and held 353/353 with 1,531 of 1,555 values surviving". mix_v1_conv10 opens with "mix_v1_narr10 and mix_v1_temp5 closed both". At the time both were written, mix_v1_temp5 had never trained: the count was real and in its docstring, and the hold was a pre-registration.

The prediction turned out right, which is the least useful way for this to end — it means the error cost nothing this time and the habit is intact. It is the same shape as the forty-first finding's "56/120 wrong" and the seven share-claims the thirtieth finding withdrew: a number stated once without its status attached, then read as settled by everything downstream. The two docstrings are corrected to cite this finding.

What this does not settle

  • Whether 5% is right, only that 9.79% was not buying anything over it. The same limit mix_v1_narr10 states about narrative. The marginal-rate framing that would settle it needs a third dose, and the cheap one is 2.5%.
  • The tool.copy question is still open and this arm cannot close it. Pre-registered as such: the seventeenth finding's signal was 9 items and the thirtieth's floor on that row is 28 frozen. 119 and 111 against 100-120 is exactly the unreadable overlap predicted.
  • Linearity is two points on one row. Two doses define a line; they cannot show it is one. The reading is that 4.79 and 9.58 points are consistent with linear, not that the response is linear everywhere — and both doses are small, on one receiver, at 29.6M.
  • schema.conform +1 with churn unmeasured is the only cell not plainly null, and the honest form is that nobody has measured churn on it rather than that it moved.
