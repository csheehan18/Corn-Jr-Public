Conversation and the second turn · 5 September 2026

One unrelated exchange in history stops repeated calls

The result, as the finding states it

With one unrelated exchange in front of the bound pair on half the two-turn pairs, nothing reissues at depth 3, copied_from reads none on all 72, the social probe rises, the turn-2 rows hold, and novel decline returns to 32 of 32

4. Reading

§6d's go reading, and the last arm of the plan lands. Arm 8 carries everything the arms before it bought — the declared social turn at 111/112, the consolation family at 13/28 from twenty lines, the second turn with nothing reissuing — and adds the one thing no arm before it could show: on the grid where "the turn before this one" and "turn 1" are different strings, he copies from neither. It is the trunk the reader is re-fit on (the seventh section's four steps, running as this is written) and the shipping candidate, with the two decisions that stay the owner's: the reader flag (7) and the routed say on questions about his life (8).
