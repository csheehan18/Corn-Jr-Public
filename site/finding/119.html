Fine-tuning from the shipped base: frozen scores hold, small social costs

The result, as the finding states it

Arm 2's payload from the ft4g init lands inside arm 2's band on every social row with every frozen row unmoved, the square says arm 3's family drop was the hello anchoring and the extract lean is the init, and the init's cost is two novel declines of 32 and seven social moves of 36 after a tool turn

What it cost

corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social4, 2026-09-05. PLAN-SOCIAL-SLICE's 2026-09-03 arm, re-specified for the shipped reader in PLAN-CONVERSATION's seventh section: init_from ft4g, data_dir arm 2's own data/mix_v4_ft_social2/ (arm 2's payload to the byte; verified in the checkpoint's config before anything was scored), every other field arm 2's verbatim. 1,200 iterations in 1,498 s on an idle 3080 (ENVIRONMENT's 1,502 s for this shape), best at 1,000 (val 0.5873, mean-log-per-source), social per-source loss 0.6554 -> 0.3794. Full batched card, grid A table-backed, historygrid depth 1 under --multi, and the ft4g adapter read on the trunk as a proxy through probe_reader_runtime.py --adapter-trunk (finding 118). Card serialised: the grids ran on a server at port 8766 with an empty --config, no packs, --no- log; the probe and the next arm's launch waited for each other.

5. Reading

Arm 4 lands on the first pre-registered reading for the rows and misses grid A's novel bar by two. The ft4g init is free on every eval row, costs two novel declines and seven post-history social moves on the grids, and reproduces arm 3's extract lean without arm 3's payload — so that lean is the init's and is inside the floor. The square attributes arm 3's family drop to its greeting tag, which arm 5's generator bounds instead of anchors.

What this does not decide is which checkpoint ships: arm 2 is still the stronger social checkpoint on the two grids, and the reason to prefer an ft4g-init trunk — the reader's heads were fit on ft4g — is weaker than it was, because finding 118 read the adapter on arm 2's trunk at 524/199. The plan's rule stands (every arm inits from ft4g and stacks on the one before), arm 5 is training on it, and the shipping choice is the owner's after the arms are read.

Limits

One seed per arm. Grid A's novel column is 32 items and moved by 2; the historygrid social probe is 36 and moved by 7; neither is a number to quote without the second seed the config family has asked for since arm 1. The vague-tail battery was not run on this arm — its expected reading is arm 2's 5 of 22, and the battery has no script. No per-family split of social.family was decoded for this arm; the species column is what says its residue is arm 2's shape.
