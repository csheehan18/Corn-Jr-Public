The answer-length rule is already at its best setting

The result, as the finding states it

The extent rule is at its optimum: neither a wider tolerance nor a wider reach buys the fuller answer the owner asked for on either column, and dropping "the" before a proper noun costs ten right answers a seed

scripts/probe_extent_sweep.py and harvest_popa_pairs.py --radius 8, 2026-09-04. Replay off the pairs harvests plus three 19-second GPU passes to widen the stored grid; no head trained. evals/results/extent_sweep_s{1,2,3}.json, extent_sweep_r8_s{1,2,3}.json, pairs_built_s{1,2,3}_r8_popa.json. corn/reader.py is untouched.

0. What the owner asked for, and what it turned out to be

Finding 104 §7 recorded four speech preferences from the owner's read of the lenient credits: say the whole name; extend the answer with the passage's qualifying phrase ("It occurred in Gilbert Inlet in Alaska", not "It's Gilbert Inlet"); give both names on a who ... and question; and no "the" before a bare proper noun ("It's Chernobyl", not "It's the Chernobyl"). This was the first thing recommended after the ruler question closed, because it looked like the cheapest change a user would notice: a template fix and a constant.

It is not. The first three are the same thing — the reader's span is shorter than the natural phrase — and that is the extent rule's business. The fourth is not a template problem at all.

1. Dropping "the" is wrong on ten passages a seed

On the answerable passages where the reader speaks It's the <Capital>... (23 / 20 / 22 a seed), the strict verdict before and after removing the article:

spoken 'the <Cap>' strict right right after stripping seed 1 23 14 4 seed 2 20 15 6 seed 3 22 14 3

the Soviet Union, The Maginot Line, the Intolerable Acts, the Battle of Antietam, The Royalettes need their article and the ruler knows it. It's the Chernobyl — the case the owner heard — is not an article error: the head picked the Chernobyl out of "The Chernobyl disaster" in the first sentence when the answer, Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant, is in the second. That is a pick on the wrong occurrence with a clipped noun phrase, and no template rule can tell it from the Soviet Union. Closed as a template change.

2. The tolerance is flat, on the record's grid

corn.reader.extend speaks the longest pair within EXTENT_D of the argmax over EXTENT_RADIUS = 3 candidate rows on either side; EXTENT_D = 2.0 was chosen on the strict ruler. Swept at the bar protocol, hybrid reduce, each d at its own CV'd threshold, on two columns — strict, and a lenient proxy that also credits a spoken span which wraps an accepted answer (the gold or any span finding 104's labels accepted for that passage) while staying answer_sized:

d s1 right / lenient* s2 right / lenient* s3 right / lenient* mean words 0.0 381 / 416 384 / 425 391 / 433 4.1 1.0 387 / 422 387 / 429 399 / 441 4.2 2.0 ships 389 / 424 388 / 430 400 / 442 4.2 3.0 390 / 424 386 / 428 403 / 441 4.2 4.0 389 / 423 386 / 428 406 / 444 4.3 6.0 389 / 423 383 / 425 406 / 443 4.4 8.0 387 / 420 391 / 431 404 / 439 4.5

The rule is worth +8 / +4 / +9 over no rule (d = 0), which is finding 86's number again. Above d = 2 the strict column moves by at most +6 on one seed and −5 on another, the lenient proxy by at most +2, and the mean spoken answer lengthens by a third of a word. There is no fuller answer in the grid the rule searches: within three rows of the argmax, the head does not score a longer span close enough to be taken.

3. A wider reach does not help either

The grid is the harvest's. harvest_popa_pairs.py --radius 8 re-wrote the three seed heads' grids to eight rows on either side (19 s each), and the sweep re-run over them:

radius 8, d s1 right / lenient* s2 right / lenient* s3 right / lenient* mean words 2.0 389 / 424 393 / 431 402 / 442 4.2 3.0 388 / 423 389 / 427 402 / 438 4.3 4.0 386 / 416 391 / 427 396 / 427 4.5 6.0 381 / 409 379 / 410 388 / 415 4.8 8.0 372 / 399 375 / 403 380 / 406 5.2

At the shipped tolerance the wider reach is +0 / +5 / +2 strict and +0 / +1 / +0 on the proxy — noise. Every wider tolerance loses on both columns, steadily, while the answers lengthen: by d = 8 the reader is 17 / 13 / 20 right answers worse and the lenient proxy 25 / 28 / 36 worse. Widening past the head's own near-ties does not find "in Gilbert Inlet in Alaska"; it finds neighbouring clauses that fail answer_sized or, admitted wider, tip the vote to another window.

4. Verdict

The extent rule as shipped is at its optimum on both rulers. The preferences the owner voiced are real and are not reachable by the rule's two constants or by the say template. What they need is either a span head that ranks the natural phrase higher — training, where every arm on frozen states has failed to move the pick — or a syntactic completion rule (finish the noun phrase the argmax sits in) that finding 82 measured on the generative path and found broke more than it fixed. Neither is cheap, and this finding's purpose is to have spent forty minutes rather than a day learning that.

Not closed: the who ... and preference was not measured separately. It is a subset of extension (the second name is a wider span) and the sweep says the head does not offer it, but a count of how often a two-name gold has its second name in the head's ten would size it, and was not run.

5. On disk

scripts/probe_extent_sweep.py (new): the rule replayed at any tolerance and reach off a pairs harvest, read on the strict column and the wrapping proxy, with the mean spoken length beside them. probe_reader_rank.apply_extent takes a radius, default the shipped 3. pairs_built_s{1,2,3}_r8_popa.json are the radius-8 grids and read identically to the radius-3 files at radius 3.
