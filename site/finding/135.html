More depth-3 training data doesn't teach depth 4

The result, as the finding states it

Raising the depth-3 share from eight to twenty per cent of turns, at a shape coverage every corpus already saturates, deepens the preference to open a group at the depths shown, leaves the one depth never shown at the floor with the two seeds of the larger dose spanning the band, and costs unseen entities; the dose is exposure and exposure is not the lever on depth

PLAN-TAG-SPINE phase C5, the dose arm, read 2026-09-14. Four nano runs pre-registered in configs/mix_v3_funcworld_dose2.py and _dose3.py: the functional world under corn/funcgen.py's DEPTH_PROFILES, the delivered depth-3 share raised from W's 0.081 to 0.142 and 0.202 of turns with every holdout W's (depth 4, the two pairings, the carrier twins), at W's pledge, iterations and policy, RoPE, two seeds a cell. Controls: the three W-rope cells, re-scored never retrained. check_funcworld.py --corpus <file> --min-depth3 <bracket> green on both files before a byte trained; a trainer smoke on the dose2 mix; decode parity four of four. Full cards, the bound partials, the label-rank read, both structure reads, the constrained decode, and one read taken off the corpora before any cell was scored.

The question

Finding 133 and its amendments: on the functional world he cuts phrasings he never saw, holds the labels for a fourth level (96 of 96 at the innermost line), and does not cut the depth he never saw — at the token where the deep and flat cuts part he opens a group at depth-4 levels 1 and 2 and prefers the flat cut at level 3 by two to four nats on every seed and at 100M. The owner asked whether that is a corpus gap: too little and too narrow a showing of the recursion. Depth 3 was 8.1% of W's turns. This arm moves that one number, twice.

What was read before the cells, and what it changed

A review pass asked whether a raised share buys more distinct nestings or more repetitions of the same ones, since the four cells cannot tell. scripts/probe_shapes.py (card evals/results/funcworld__shapes.json): the generator's drawable depth-3 shape space — the tree with the words removed — is 54 shapes (200,000 plans a profile, the count flat from the first quarter), depth 2 is 100, and every corpus carries every shape at both depths: W at 41 uses a depth-3 shape, dose2 at 72, dose3 at 102. Finding 133's "almost every nesting once" counted trees with their words. So the dose is exposure, not coverage, the configs were amended to read it in that word before a card was scored, and the outcome table's null was given its causes (below). Two more facts off the builds: depth 1 is flat across the three (0.563, 0.557, 0.560 of turns) and depth 2 is what shrank (0.356, 0.301, 0.239), so the dose trades exposure to the more diverse level (100 shapes) for repetition at the less diverse one (54); and the delivered repair share falls with it (0.082, 0.069, 0.054), reported and not read.

The pre-registered second row lands: level 3 sits inside W's band at both doses, and the larger dose's two seeds span it — 24 at one seed, five above W's high, and 4 at the other, at W's floor — so on the one number the arm exists for, the dose3 pair disagrees in direction and the config's own unreadable clause makes that a direction and not a result. The flat cut stays likelier at every seed of every cell, by 1.8 to 3.3 nats. What the exposure did move is the depths training showed: level 1 climbs monotonically with the dose (65-76, then 74-76, then 82-85) and level 2 with it (58-62, 61-66, 64-67), which is what a table of nestings looks like as it is learned harder — the same shape the mid size read (finding 133, second amendment: 88 and 64 at levels 1 and 2 and 4 at level 3). More of the same 54 nestings buys more of the same 54 nestings.

And it costs. parse.w_entity reads 33 and 34 on the larger dose against W's 41-42, below the band at both seeds — unseen entities are the row a raised share of long, lean, entity-dense turns displaces — and e2e.w_tree reads 18 at one dose3 seed against W's low of 22. The kill clause (w_heldout or w_span under the band at both seeds) did not fire: w_span 66/63 is inside, w_heldout 9/12 straddles W's low of 10. The copy band held with one dip (tool.copy_wide 277 on dose2 seed 1), the pattern every arm on this mix has shown once. The language rows are unmoved to the third decimal, as they should be on a byte-identical mix_v3.

What the null names, and does not separate

The config wrote it before the read and it stands: neither repetition (this arm) nor size (the 100M run, one seed) moves the cut at the depth never shown, and three causes remain, none eliminated here. (a) At 54 shapes and 41 to 102 uses each, memorising the table of nestings is cheaper than inducing a rule, so nothing pressures him toward one; the depth-4 cut, and w_pairing's inner level at a coin on every cell (53-65 of 96, a held-out shape at a trained depth), are what a table reads on what it does not hold. (b) Effective depth of the trunk — against which the 100M read is a direction, not a result. (c) The design's own trade: the dose shrank the more diverse level to feed the less diverse one, and if a recursive rule is induced from seeing one composition applied across depths, the arm moved the corpus the wrong way for it.

What this licenses

Nothing in the 1.0 share table for the depth-3 share: raising it buys the trained depths harder and costs unseen entities, and the dose stays where W had it. Depth stays the runtime's — the stepped protocol, with the parse grammar shipping for the copy slips it removes (finding 133's amendment) — until an arm changes the shape space rather than the share: PLAN-TAG-SPINE §11 C6, a generator with hundreds of depth-3 shapes each used a handful of times and a held-out shape set, finding 13's construction one level up from carriers, gated on the block decision (NOW.md decision 2) because a 512 block holds four or five leaves a turn. The companion layers-at-fixed-data cell (8 to 12 layers on W's own corpus) separates (b) from (a) without a generator. Both are the owner's to order.

Instruments

  • scripts/probe_shapes.py: the shape read that turned "dose" into "exposure" before a cell was scored; its follow at depth 1 is the rebind conversation's second turn, which the plan sampler never draws and the corpus carries, and is the one shape outside the sampled pool.
  • check_funcworld.py --corpus --min-depth3: the base brackets on a file that differs in one table; dose3's consumer-first share sits exactly on its bracket's edge (0.30), a third consequence of the same budget, recorded in the config.
  • The outcome table amended for an interpretable null and the depth-2 trade, both before any card, both from a review pass and judged against the record: the review's "no held-out-shape row exists" was wrong (w_pairing is that row and reads the negative), and its remaining point — two shapes held out of 54 is a thin instrument — became C6.
  • Four cells at 944-960 s; probes uncontended; nothing timed.

Limits

Two seeds a cell and the deciding pair disagrees, so directions. One world whose groups are inert (a flat cut with the right labels executes the same lookups), so the row measures the shape and not the task — the random depth-4 item decoded by hand this day flattened a group at level 3, which the runtime would have run identically, and slipped one find to ask, which it would not. Exposure was varied over a saturated shape space; coverage was not varied at all, and cannot be on this generator. Nothing here touches the shipped checkpoint.
