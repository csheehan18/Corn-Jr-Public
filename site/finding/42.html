Routing and declining · 19 August 2026

He now declines 118 times, with routing untouched

The result, as the finding states it

He declines 118 times, and the routing underneath is untouched

configs/mix_v1_toolspace_decline.py ×2 against mix_v1_toolspace ×2. The absent class stops being a silence and becomes a route: decline on every declaration line and always last, he calls it, the runtime returns {"ok": false, "reason": "not_covered", "say": ...}, and he relays the observation's own words. He composes nothing.

This is the first time absent has moved in this project. It read 0 in twelve of fourteen cells across three corpora and two vocabularies, survived three corpus repairs, and the thirty-ninth finding showed the grammar could not hand it over either — with decline declared, rendered identically, positioned last and left open as the only escape, untrained checkpoints took it 3 times in 442.

Every single pass is pass_declined. Not one is "he stopped calling" — the bucket exists precisely so those two cannot be averaged, and the answer is unambiguous. The behaviour arrived through the channel the plan predicted: routing, then deference to the observation.

The routing capability is undamaged — and that needed the right control to see

The obvious worry is that he learned to decline by declining more. The declared cell answers it: same items, same narrowing, but decline is not on offer, so declining is not available and what is left is pure tool choice.

Identical, both seeds. The corpus did not cost him any routing.

But the policy is miscalibrated, and that is the cost

In the decline cell — where declining is available — he takes it on items where a real tool was declared:

checkpointname passover-declined
toolspace s1235/3790
toolspace s2209/3790
toolspace_decline s1178/37998
toolspace_decline s2182/37970

This is the pre-registered trade branch, and it gets its own statement rather than an average: he gained 115 correct declines and paid 84 wrong ones.

The two cells together say something more precise than "route falls". The capability is intact — he still picks the right tool at baseline rate when declining is off the table. What moved is the policy: offered an escape, he over-uses it. That is a calibration problem, not a lost skill, and it is a different repair from the one a damaged-routing result would have called for.

What made the arm readable, recorded because none of it was free

  • decline on all 200,000 declaration lines, not only the 69,919 where it is the answer. Otherwise its presence predicts its use and the arm measures a cue.
  • The say pool at 2.75 uses each — 25,426 distinct refusals composed from the domain's own verb and noun. One sentence at ~36,000 uses failed; 128 refusals at 163 uses each failed; temporal_sayvar worked at 4.5. The target was sayvar's regime, not a round number.
  • A decline-aware scorer written one commit before anything could emit a decline, with its dormancy proved by zero-diff re-score. The thirty-seventh finding's arm scored its own success as unparseable for want of exactly that.

What this does not settle

  • The calibration. 70–98 over-declines of 379 is the headline cost and nothing here prices a fix. The obvious dose is the 35% ABSENT_SHARE, which was chosen to be far from the local optimum rather than tuned, and is now the first thing to bracket.
  • Whether this transfers to bare prompts. chat.abstain_oos asks whether an utterance is a request at all, with no declaration to read. The thirty-seventh finding proved turn-1 and turn-2 abstention are not one behaviour; nothing licenses assuming decline-with-declarations and abstain-without-declarations are either. They are measured separately and stay that way.
  • Two seeds, spreads of 17 on absent and 28 on the over-decline count. The move off zero needs no interval — its floor is zero by construction and it cleared it by two orders of magnitude — but every size quoted above does.
  • 29.6M only. Nothing here says what a larger model does with a declared escape, and the thirty-eighth finding is a standing warning that a treated arm can read differently from an untreated one on the very instrument used to price it.
