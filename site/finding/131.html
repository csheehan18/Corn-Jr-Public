The tag spine · 12 September 2026

Once the runtime enforces the label, a forged one is obeyed 36/36

The result, as the finding states it

The tag becomes the door, and the door is the runtime's: the role grammar alone takes the whole tree from eighteen and twenty-four to forty-five and fifty-one of sixty on an unmasked checkpoint, the structural mask costs nothing and adds nothing measurable, a forged tag is obeyed thirty-six of thirty-six once the copy is bound, and a follow-up composes what no copy slot can source

PLAN-TAG-SPINE rung 1 and the tail of rung 0, read 2026-09-12. Two nano arms, S at two seeds, trained on arm B's file byte for byte under the structural mask (configs/mix_v3_decomp_s.py, pre-registered before a byte trained; a first seed discarded, below). Read against B's own cards, and — because every piece of the binding except the mask is a decode-time constraint — read on B as well, so that mask, grammar and copy separate on the same checkpoints. Nothing else retrained. Twelve readings of the decomposition rows, seven forged-structure probes, four rung-0 probes.

The question

Finding 130: a tag he may read is never read while the words decide the call, at either layer, with or without the request under loss. PLAN-TAG-SPINE §4 made the tag the door instead: while a call line is written the request tokens of every other part are masked out of attention (trained and served), the grammar admits only the declared tools of the focused leaf's role, and the copy slot admits only the leaf's words and the last answer. Does the mask cost anything on trained rows, and how much of the door is the mask?

The answer: the door is the grammar and the copy, and both are the runtime's

+role is --role-bind (the grammar admits the focused leaf's tools); +full adds --copy-bind (the string slots copy from the leaf's words and the last answer). S- is the masked checkpoint read under the mask alone.

The grammar alone, on the unmasked B, takes the whole tree from 18 and 24 to 45 and 51 of 60, and the route from 96 and 97 to 114 and 114. The copy binding adds two on the route and takes move.args to 120 on every checkpoint. The mask adds nothing measurable: S- sits inside B's band on every row, and S under the same bindings reads what B does under them, seed for seed. The parse rows are untouched everywhere, as they should be — parse positions are unmasked.

The whole tree is near the world's ceiling under the binding: sixteen of the sixty tree items declare two tools of one role, the generator always takes the first declared, and the label cannot choose between them. The wrong_tool that remains — 9, 12, 6 — lives inside that set.

The band. tool.copy 120/120 on both S seeds, tool.copy_wide 299 and 291, temporal.* flat. tool.selection reads 99 and 115 against B's 128 and 118, on the row whose seed spread has run to 35 on every arm that carries the world (finding 129, 130): direction down, size unreadable at two seeds, and it is the world's 5% declared-tools slice again, not the mask — S- and B share the file.

The mask's cost. 9% a training step compiled, measured before the run (probe only — not on disk); on the payload's own validation split the masked call lines are easier, decomp 0.343 against 0.454, with every other source within a hundredth — a distractor removed, which is the inductive bias doing its one job.

The forged structure: invention, then obedience by construction

probe_depth.py --forge label --structure: the two children of a do swapped, so the first call executes the span the forgery calls a lookup. Readable denominator 36 of 60 — the other 24 are items whose first call was never the forged part, and they are exactly the "neither" every arm since B has carried on this probe.

Three readings, in order. The first mask was porous: it hid the request's other parts and left their copies on the parse line, and he took the gold span from the line above 31 times — caught by this probe on the first checkpoint, the streams rebuilt so a part's words carry its id wherever they appear, and on the porous checkpoint under the fixed mask the gold span reads 0 of 30. Under the tight mask he invents: handed a send-span with no subject in it, he composes one around the visible name — the founding year of Kelunlvstone from fire it over to Kelunlvstone — finding 98's mechanism arriving when the words are taken away. Under the copy binding invention is impossible and he follows the forged tag 36 of 36 (seed 2: 31, five unparseable), copying a fragment of what he was shown (fire, along, send) — provenance guaranteed, sense not, because the forgery gave the lookup nothing sensible to copy. And B, which never saw a mask, reads 36 of 36 under the same three constraints.

So "load-bearing by construction" is literal, and it is the runtime's construction. The weights contribute the parse. Everything that makes the parse govern the call — which tool, which words — is a constraint at decode that any checkpoint with the parse obeys.

The one thing the copy slot could not hold: the follow-up

Under the copy binding as first written, e2e.rebind read 5, 28, 5, 28 of 50 where it reads 48-50. A follow-up's argument is the previous turn's frame with a new entity bound in — the capital of spain from the capital of france and what about spain? — which is a contiguous span of nothing (PLAN-DECOMPOSE §4 says so; this is what it costs). So the label decides which binding applies: find, send and note copy and are held to the span and the answer; follow composes and is role-bound only. Under that rule the table above holds and the re-bind is back at 48-50 everywhere.

Rung 0's tail, corrected

The A1 probe (scripts/probe_rolebind.py) read the role door at +2 on move.next because it kept its own leaf table — no do leaf, no follow, surface order — and bound the wrong role where those govern. The scorer's --role-bind on the same checkpoints is the read above: +18 on the route and +27 on the tree. The probe now takes its tables from the one place that defines them. A2 (the state line halving the repeat under the route header, 40 to 19 of 192) and A3 (his label a bag of words at 0.641 held out) stand as read.

What this licenses

PLAN-TAG-SPINE §8, rung 1's row: S inside B's band on trained rows and the copy band; copy under forged structure at or above 80% — both clauses hold, the second under the full binding. The gate to rung 2 is open, with the reading sharpened: the mask is not the door, the grammar and the copy are, and they ship as runtime rules with no training run. What rung 2 asks is whether a label learned as a function can be written for a shape he was never shown; nothing here touches that — parse.heldout and parse.depth3 are zero on every reading in this finding, as on every arm before it.

Instruments

  • The porous mask, caught by the forged probe on the first checkpoint and refused by check_decomp_scorer.py group 7, which now reads the visible text of a focused query and rejects any other leaf's span in it.
  • CopySpec keys a slot by (tool, slot), and the first copy binding passed bare slot names, which matched nothing; the walk test that caught it (invented: accepted) is the kind of negative control this repo requires.
  • The probe's readable denominator is 36 of 60 and always was; every "neither" count on this probe since finding 129 is that set.
  • structure_for_text disagreed with the generator's execution order on 115 of 399 turns (follow) and then 16 (do as a leaf) before it agreed on 535; the guard holds the agreement.

Limits

Two seeds per arm, so directions (rule 6). A dummy world whose labels are lexical (A3), so a bound tag is only as right as the parse, and on unseen carriers the parse is wrong. The mask's contribution is unmeasurable here because the words never fail; a world where they do is rung 2's. The tree ceiling from duplicated roles is the world's, and a description binding (finding 34's) would lift it. The copy binding guarantees provenance and not sense: under a wrong structure he copies a fragment, and the runtime that ships this should treat a call whose argument is a lone word as a refusal rather than an answer.
