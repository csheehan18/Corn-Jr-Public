The reader survives the base model being fine-tuned underneath it

The result, as the finding states it

The shipped reading adapter survives the trunk moving under it: fitted to ft4g, it reads 521 of 721 at 196 correct silences on arm 3 (ft4g fine-tuned once) and 524 at 199 on arm 2 (a sibling trunk from the base), against 526 at 199 on its own trunk, with 27 and 9 passages moving and nothing paired significant

What it cost

16.0 min of scoring on the 2 cards first cited here (corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social2, corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social3); it also reads 1 card scored for an earlier finding (corn_jr_260m_v4_ft4g). Backfilled 2026-09-26 from the cards' seconds; training and probe time were never recorded, so this is a floor.

Run 2026-09-05, docs/PLAN-CONVERSATION.md §4i, pre-registered the same day before the number existed. Nothing trained. Two reads of Population A through the real runtime, ~23 s of card each, plus the pairing off the JSONs on the CPU. The adapter is checkpoints/reader_whole_causal_s1.pt exactly as finding 117 shipped it; the trunks it is read on are corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social3 (ft4g init, one 1,200-iteration fine-tune) and corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social2 (v4-base init, ft4g's recipe with one source swapped). corn/, the server, every scorer and every frozen row are untouched; the runtime still refuses this pairing by hash.

0. The question, and why it is a one-minute question now

PLAN-SOCIAL-SLICE's 2026-09-03 section called head portability "the number nobody has": no measurement existed of how far a reader fitted on ft4g's states degrades when the trunk beneath it moves by one fine-tune, and every reader result on disk was ft4g and only ft4g. It priced the number at a harvest and a re-fit. Finding 117 changed the price: the reader is now a delta keyed to the trunk's sha256, scripts/probe_reader_runtime.py reads Population A through the real read_passage in 23 s, and arm 3 — ft4g moved by exactly one fine-tune of arm 4's shape — is already on disk.

The social plan's join turns on this number. If the delta survives a trunk move, arms 4-8 can be read with the shipped adapter as a proxy and the ~55 min re-fit is paid once, on the trunk that ships. If it does not, every arm that wants a reader number pays the re-fit, and the social plan's "under an hour of card" an arm becomes about two.

1. The instrument, and the one flag it needed

corn.model.load_adapter refuses a trunk the adapter file did not name, and harness/backends/read.py always hands it the served --ckpt, so there was no way to put the ft4g delta on another trunk without either editing the fingerprint or bypassing the check. Neither was done. The probe gained --adapter-trunk <path>: the checkpoint the adapter's hash is checked against, defaulting to --ckpt. Passing ft4g there while --ckpt is arm 3 loads the adapter with the fingerprint check passing against the trunk it names, prints both hashes, and records adapter_trunk in the JSON. The mismatch is declared on the command line rather than hidden. The runtime has no such flag, and scripts/check_model.py — which drives load_adapter against a different trunk and asserts the refusal — is green after the change.

trunk sha256 init what it is corn_jr_260m_v4_ft4g 985240095e80 v4 base the trunk the adapter names; server.json corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social3 7b5edfaa78bc ft4g ft4g + one fine-tune (arm 4's shape) corn_jr_260m_v4_ft_social2 02e5d681e7cd v4 base ft4g's recipe, one source swapped: a sibling

Everything else is finding 117 (a) verbatim: --reader head_whole at the shipped -1.174, extent d = 2.0 radius 3, fp32, the same 1,003 passages, probe_reader_passages.judge on reduce's output. The threshold is held at ft4g's constant on purpose. A re-derived threshold would be the re-fit's protocol; the question here is whether the file that ships reads on a trunk it was not fitted to, and that is a fixed-threshold question — so the attempt rate is reported beside every right, as §4i required.

Pre-registered reading one fires on both trunks: "arm 3 within ~10 right of 526 with correct silence within ~5 of 199". Arm 3 is 5 right and 3 silences under; arm 2 is 2 right under and level on silence. The attempt rate moves by half a point — the fixed threshold is very slightly more willing on a moved trunk, which is the direction a margin perturbation shows when the constant was fitted elsewhere, and it is inside what finding 117 (b) measured between the harvest's bf16 trunk and the runtime's fp32 one. Latency is the same 23 ms a passage; the hybrid fallback fired on 0.

3. Paired, by passage

Verdicts paired against finding 117's per-passage record (reader_runtime_whole_s1.json), exact two-sided binomial:

against ft4g's own read moved right lost/won p silence lost/won p joint lost/won p arm 3 27/1003 8 / 3 0.227 6 / 3 0.508 14 / 6 0.115 arm 2 9/1003 2 / 0 0.500 1 / 1 1.000 3 / 1 0.625

Nothing is significant and nothing is close to it. 27 passages of 1,003 move under a one-fine-tune trunk move, and 9 under the sibling. For scale, finding 117 (b) measured 17 spans differing between the harvest's trunk and the runtime's identical weights under a different autocast; a whole fine-tune moves 9 to 27.

The species of the movement, arm 3:

answerable right -> silent 4 right -> wrong 4 silent -> right 2 silent -> wrong 6 wrong -> right 1 wrong -> silent 1 unanswerable silent -> wrong 6 wrong -> silent 3

Eleven of the 27 are a silence becoming speech or the reverse — margins on either side of a constant fitted on another trunk — and the rest are the head's pick moving on a passage it speaks on either way. That is the same two-species shape finding 117 (b) traced to a small perturbation of the head's inputs, and no third species appears. Arm 2's nine are five silent -> wrong on answerable passages and four singletons.

4. What this says, and what it licenses

The delta is portable across the trunk moves this project actually makes. A rank-32 adapter and a 6.3M-parameter head fitted to ft4g's layers {8, 12, 16} read within the seed spread (finding 112 put it at ~5 passages) on a trunk ft4g was fine-tuned into and on a trunk that shares ft4g's recipe but not its init. The states the reader depends on are a property of the family's training rather than of one checkpoint's last 1,200 iterations.

So the join in PLAN-CONVERSATION's seventh section takes its cheap branch:

  • Arms 4-8 are read with the ft4g adapter as a proxy — probe only, through --adapter-trunk, never served — and a reader number on an arm costs 23 s rather than 55 min. The number is a proxy in exactly the sense measured here: within a handful of passages at a fixed threshold, attempt rate reported beside it.
  • The re-fit is reserved for the trunk that ships. It still runs, once, at that trunk's own CV'd threshold, with whatever recipe PLAN-READER-WHOLE-NEXT §2 settles; nothing here replaces it, because a fixed threshold is not the bar protocol and 3 silences is not 0.
  • Packs and every later trunk inherit the same expectation, stated as an expectation: a one-fine-tune move on this recipe costs the reader single digits of passages.
  • The conversation-as-adapter alternative the seventh section named — the social class as a second delta on a frozen ft4g, so the fingerprint never breaks — is not worth pricing on this evidence. It would buy protection against a cost that reads as 2 to 5 passages.

Not licensed: serving the ft4g adapter on a moved trunk. The fingerprint stays, the server has no override, and this finding is the reason the override lives in a probe: the number is small because it was measured, and a trunk that moves further than one fine-tune of this recipe has not been measured at all.

5. Limits

One adapter seed, one population, fp32, a fixed threshold. Two trunks, both one recipe step from ft4g in different directions; a trunk two or three social arms downstream is a different distance and is the next place to read this, for 23 s, when arm 5 exists. The arm 3 versus arm 2 ordering (the descendant reads slightly worse than the sibling) is 3 passages and is not a result. The moved passages were read by verdict transition and not by margin; the JSONs carry no margins, and a margin-level account of the 27 would need the probe to write them. Nothing here is a live session and nothing measures packs.
