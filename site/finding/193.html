DCLM's stronger stock answer is not in its text

The result, as the finding states it

The thirds' stronger pull toward a stock wish label (finding 192) is in all three of the ruler's phrasings — top-two default share 0.31-0.42 against the blend's 0.25-0.32, frame by frame — and not in DCLM's words: the label it favours, relaxing, sits in the answer slot (after feels, is, so, very, pretty, really, call it) 0.5 times per million words in DCLM against 0.9 in plain FineWeb, 0.2% of all slot uses against 0.5%, while DCLM carries more "so funny" and "so sad" (10.1 and 10.5 against 6.0 and 6.2) and the thirds do not favour either; both registered predictions failed, so the cause is not a text pattern and two seeds cannot name it

What it cost

13:48-13:52 on 2026-10-21; the per-frame default read on six checkpoints 121.8 s (contended, the card at 38%, a bound); the slot count over the first 1,000 MB of each web source 215 s on CPU.

Finding 192: both thirds seeds lean on a stock label hardest of any mix, relaxing above all, and the label's plain frequency in DCLM says they should not.
