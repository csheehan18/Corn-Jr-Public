The parse and depth

Why depth reads zero, and what of the parse the weights can hold.

9 findings, 137 to 146, filed 14 September 2026 to 26 September 2026

The findings, in order

  1. 137
    The depth-3 failure doesn't track copy length 14 September 2026 ·

    The level-3 floor is flat against every covariate that moves the trained levels: the span the deep cut must write drives the in-distribution cell from 0.968 to 0.132 and leaves the unseen depth between 0.03 and 0.11 across its whole range, where the spans are already the shortest on the item, so copy burden is not what pins it and the greedy zero is one failure and not two

  2. 138
    He knows where the next request part starts, 80 to 93 percent 16 September 2026 ·

    The level-1 cursor is in the weights and not at the ceiling: with everything before it gold he opens the next part where the last one ended at 80 to 86 per cent of interior boundaries at nano and 93 at 100M against a floor of 1, flat in offset, a lost cursor lands mid-part and no line that loses one passes, while the close is his at 98 per cent and a quarter of level-1 failures tile the utterance and fail on the decision alone

  3. 140
    Depth-4 failure is position alone; moved to line one it works 16 September 2026 ·

    The depth-4 zero is a position and nothing else: the identical cut that reads 3 to 19 of 96 at line 3 and 9 to 45 at line 2 reads 71 to 89 of 95 at line 1 on six W seeds and 88 at 100M, survives padding back to length, and sits where trained depth-3 shapes sit, so the content is not the block and a position-free expansion format is the arm on depth

  4. 141
    Written as one nested line, he builds a structure never trained 16 September 2026 ·

    With the parse written as one nested line he writes a three-member group inside a group, a structure the corpus never contains, eighteen times across two seeds and 5.8 times more often where the gold calls for one than where it does not, on the leg that had never given anything

  5. 142
    Removing four-member groups from training broke nesting another way 25 September 2026 ·

    Arm G took its four-member groups out of the pairs, and he stopped reading an unmarked pair as a group: he writes a lookup-and-deliver with an invented consumer instead, so the trained nesting falls and the pre-registered null on nested four is confounded rather than read

  6. 143
    Nesting at 20M breaks two different ways; too fragile to build on 25 September 2026 ·

    Arm G2 held every pair arm F had and the trained nesting fell anyway, this time because he flattens the nested pair into the outer group; two differently matched arms broke the same base two different ways, so nesting at nano is too fragile to carry the arity ladder, while the outer four-carrier installs cleanly at both seeds

  7. 144
    Showing the request step by step fixes short chains, not long ones 26 September 2026 ·

    Arm S's stepwise views land in the null band at both seeds: they make the short chain reliable and cure the view read as social, and they do not train the one decision a long chain needs at every step — opening a nested group inside the view — which the payload shows 78 times

  8. 145
    The depth limit is the linking word; re-showing uncovered text fixes it 26 September 2026 ·

    The depth wall under the windowed runtime is the carrier word, not the depth: a chain built on a carrier he reads as a group reaches depth ten at 52-54 of 54 on arm S, one built on a carrier he does not stops at level one, and a runtime that re-presents the text he left uncovered takes arm S from 0.29 and 0.30 to 0.95 and 0.89 — where arm F, never trained on views, stays at 0.19 and 0.37

  9. 146
    A runtime gap-filling rule breaks nothing but fixes little on real requests 26 September 2026 ·

    The gap rule breaks nothing on requests it was not designed on and fixes almost nothing: every firing on a parse he had right comes back a connective, the few groups it opens at a non-carrier all land on parses already wrong, and natural requests rarely hold the flat carrier that made it worth 0.95 on the chains
