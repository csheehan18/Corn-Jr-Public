  1. 160
    2048-token blocks cost 1.2x training time and fit in memory 10 October 2026 ·

    The block's costs: at the 260M step 2048 trains at 1.20x the 512 wall clock and 1024 at 1.06x, a fine-tune fits at 2048 in the memory it uses at 512, decode is free on a short turn and 100 tok/s deep in a full window as shipped, and a 2048 window holds ten documents with a third of its attention inside one

  2. 166
    Halving the tool data leaves tool use intact 13 October 2026 ·

    The tool share (ROADMAP TS): with tools and toolspace at half their share the call surface holds under the door at both seeds — tool.selection 190 and 180 against 186 and 182, the routing rows 199-200 against 188-200 — and nothing is a kill; the one registered cost is refusal.say at both seeds, read by hand as the relayed refusal cutting the copied query's last words short on four of the row's six strings; a quarter splits on search.route_heldout and is a direction

  3. 185
    Everyday web text taught him more word meanings at little cost 20 October 2026 ·

  8. 193
    DCLM's stronger stock answer is not in its text 21 October 2026 ·
