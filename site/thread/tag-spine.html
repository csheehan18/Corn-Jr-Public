The tag language

The tag spine

The tag as a door the runtime holds: labels, the forged tag, the state line.

13 findings, 129 to 153, filed 12 September 2026 to 7 October 2026

The findings, in order

  1. 129
    He learns to label his own request; forcing labels doesn't change calls 12 September 2026 ·

    He learns to write the parse, half of it is label and not position, and not one of two hundred and forty forced tags moves a call

  2. 130
    A label in the text is a hint, never the decision 12 September 2026 ·

    The tag in the stream is read as a prior and never as the decision: zero of seven hundred and twenty forged maps at the embedding and at block 2, with and without the request under loss, while the pass schedule costs a fifth of the parse rows and the map gives none of it back

  3. 131
    Once the runtime enforces the label, a forged one is obeyed 36/36 12 September 2026 ·

    The tag becomes the door, and the door is the runtime's: the role grammar alone takes the whole tree from eighteen and twenty-four to forty-five and fifty-one of sixty on an unmasked checkpoint, the structural mask costs nothing and adds nothing measurable, a forged tag is obeyed thirty-six of thirty-six once the copy is bound, and a follow-up composes what no copy slot can source

  4. 132
    Rotary position embeddings beat learned positions on most tests 14 September 2026 ·

    RoPE against learned positions on the dummy world: above on the trained parse at both seeds, three per cent better on three of four language rows, two below on the bound tree, and the held-out rows zero as on every arm before it, so rung 2 trains both trunks at nano and its mid size takes the one the parse prefers

  5. 133
    On a synthetic world he generalises labels but not one level deeper 14 September 2026 ·

    On a world where the phrasing is useless for the label he cuts phrasings he never saw at every seed, repairs a wrong label after its bound call fails, parses a world he never trained on, holds the right label for a fourth level ninety-six of ninety-six times when the cut is given, and still writes no cut one level deeper than he was shown

  6. 134
    He learns to write a summary line of conversation state 14 September 2026 ·

    He learns to write the state line at nano, sixty-seven and a hundred and one of a hundred and twenty from a floor of one, a value comes from whichever slice carries it, a line merely present moves the repeat on the cells trained on it and not on the others, the line he writes reads as a hand-inserted one on the cell that writes it whole, and the first scoring read zero because the prompt ended on a space token the corpus never wrote

  7. 135
    More depth-3 training data doesn't teach depth 4 14 September 2026 ·

    Raising the depth-3 share from eight to twenty per cent of turns, at a shape coverage every corpus already saturates, deepens the preference to open a group at the depths shown, leaves the one depth never shown at the floor with the two seeds of the larger dose spanning the band, and costs unseen entities; the dose is exposure and exposure is not the lever on depth

  8. 136
    The depth-4 failure is a position never seen, not missing recursion 14 September 2026 ·

    The depth-4 zero is a position the corpus pinned at zero, not an absence of recursion: a parse line at index 3 holds a group in 0 of 2,236 blocks, he predicts a fourth level 96 of 96 when the deep cut is given, and the identical cut scored at line 2 instead reads 8 to 27 of 96 on four checkpoints and survives being padded back to the original length

  9. 139
    Aligning training blocks to transcript starts helps parsing, short of target 16 September 2026 ·

    Arm A at one seed: blocks aligned to transcript starts move the level-1 cursor from 558 to 639 of 772 and cut invented spans by a fifth, short of the pre-registered third, lift unseen entities by nine and trained shapes by one, cost held-out carriers four and the deep cells ten, and put the copy band under its kill line on one of six answers, where a copied person's name comes back as an invented entity

  10. 147
    When the runtime holds the structure, invented words vanish; grouping stays his 2 October 2026 ·

    The nested bind owns the form and the copy pointer, not the count: invented spans, repeats, splices and swapped number words go to zero, legal lines go from 516 to 2,917 of 3,456, and a marked chain passes the three-level depth wall for the first time, while where a group closes, whether a nested group opens at all and which request words he drops stay his — he skips words where he used to invent them, and the struct column's rise on nested rows is half a broken line becoming readable

  11. 148
    Picking a template by letter never installed; one seed in three learned 2 October 2026 ·

    Rung 3's letter-first pick never installed, not even on the pairings he trained on, at one dose or four; written evidence first, one seed of three reads the wish — held-out 113 and 97 of 288 against a gate of more than 95, and swapping the request's soft term moves the pick onto the new wish's item — while the other two sit at chance, so rung 3 reads seeds split, a direction and not section 7's kill

  12. 150
    On real requests the runtime's machinery works but his own parse doesn't 4 October 2026 ·

    On the real carriers the door works and his cut does not: given the gold parse, the two-note row reads 18 and 26 of 48 from 0 and 2, with his own parse 2 and 7; the forced card's zeros are his parse line where the prompt has none, and the one cost that survives staging is a chat label read off the tools: header

  13. 153
    He writes a summary of the conversation state but doesn't use it 7 October 2026 ·

    The state line's second seed: he writes the line again, 106 of 120, and the repeat does not fall under it — at seed 2 a line present raises it, 59 to 74 hand-inserted and 66 under his own record, and averaged over four draws seed 1's fall shrinks from 21 to 8; the pre-registered read fails at both seeds, so the record is written and not read
