# The fields stall is not the word-pieces, and not hidden in him

**Full result:** Three reads of why the 20M answers 76 to 78 of the ruler's 141 fields where a count of the same text answers 95. Split by piece count, his shortfall from the book is the same whether the gold label and the phrase's content words are one piece or several (-4.5 and +0.6 points where 15 was asked): the cut into pieces is not why. A book counted over his own 8,192 pieces, the ruler never read, answers 83 fields at the registered window and so does one tied table of 512 numbers a piece, the shape of his word table, where his own trained table answers 58 to 61 by the same read -- a direction on both rows, and 83 is inside the spread of seven 20M runs (76 to 87). A ridge reader from his state to the book's 512-number home, fitted on words the ruler never uses, answers 70 and 72 fields at the middle of the net and 77 and 80 at its best depth: what he holds is about what his framed answer already gives, so it is not sitting in him unread. One thing nobody predicted: the piece book answers more fields the further it looks -- 83, 86, 86 and 91 at windows of 5, 10, 20 and 40 pieces -- while its wishes fall (33, 30, 30, 28 of the first 96), and the wide-window knowledge does not squeeze (79 tied at 512 numbers).

**Cost:** probe and pre-registration committed at 14:31 on 2026-11-13 (8e6c67f), nothing run before it but a compile. Read 1 0.3 s, no card. Read 2 12.4 s of card after a first run died at 116 s in the squeeze (a piece never met gave 0 / 0 in its lines; fixed, the settings half printed the same before and after). Read 3 69.4 s after a 40.4 s smoke at a twentieth of the text. The cause read at window 40, 12.3 s. ETA about five minutes of card for reads 2 and 3; 81.8 s.

**The question** (owner, 2026-11-13: "it feels like there's a real strong
lead here we don't fully understand", then "Yes let's run through these
three"). The fields half reads flat from 5M to 100M -- 0.56 and 0.53 at 5M
(finding 197), 78 and 76 at 20M, 80 at 100M -- and a count of the 20M's own

The pre-registration is `scripts/probe_fields_stall.py`'s docstring. The

## The cause read: the window (not registered)

The registered rule chose the window on the first 96 wishes, and it chose
the one worst for the fields. At 40 pieces (about 23 words):

    the piece book, window 40, whole     26 wishes of the 2nd 96    91 fields
        squeezed to 512                  25                         81
        tied, 512                        26                         79
        whole, the label's first piece   32                         84

B1's bar (88) is passed here by a window the rule did not pick: a cause read.
What it shows is the shape. **The fields are far-apart knowledge**: each step
out adds items (83, 86, 86, 91), the wishes go the other way, and what the
wide window adds needs the whole table (91 whole, 79 tied at 512 numbers a
piece, where the narrow window loses nothing to the same squeeze).

## What it licenses

By the pre-registration this is its fourth outcome with two directions in
it: X fails, R fails, B1 and B2 are directions.

* **The pieces are not why he stalls.** Whole-word tokens, or a whole-word
  signal at a word's last piece, are not the fix for this.
* **It is not in him unread.** Finding 223 said so with a cosine and this
  says so with a trained reader on words it never met. On the fields the
  20M's shortfall is knowledge he does not hold in any form read so far.
* **A counted table in his word table's shape is a real but modest start.**
  It holds 83 where his own holds 58 to 61, and 83 is a good 20M, not a
  better one. The one-hour arm that starts him from it is a test of *sooner*
  (the ruler at 10% and 25% of the run), not of *higher*.
* **The lesson stands, and it has a shape now: distance.** The count
  answers more fields the further it looks, and he is paid only for the next
  piece. Where he misses, three separately trained 20Ms give the noun's
  stock label -- the phrase a few words back has not moved him. This is
  elimination plus a clue, not a measurement of him.

**What it does not settle.** How far back he actually reads: his loss with
his context cut to the last 5, 10, 20 and 40 pieces, beside the 260M's, is
the direct read, and nothing here made it. The word book by window on the
fields (finding 221 printed its windows for the wishes). Whether a table
started from the count stays there through training, or is pulled back to
what next-piece prediction wants.

## Amended 2026-11-14 -- the direct read was made

"How far back he actually reads" was read in finding 232, on 4,800 held-out
meetings of pairs the book ties and pairs it does not, beside the 260M. He
does not fall short from afar: a tied word 9 to 16 words back is worth 0.28
and 0.29 nats to the 20Ms and 0.33 to the 260M, and with the context cut
the pieces 11 to 40 back are worth the same two thirds of the 260M's that
the pieces 2 to 10 back are. So the fourth licence above -- "the lesson
stands, and it has a shape now: distance" -- holds for the count (the
text's ties barely fade over thirty words, which is why a wider window
answers more fields) and not for him: distance is not where he falls short
of a model that reads the fields. Where he does is two to four words back.
